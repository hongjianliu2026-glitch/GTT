## bloodshed

**英音:** _[\'blʌdʃed]_ **美音:** _[\'blʌdʃɛd]_

**译义:** *n.流血*

### 短语

+ **avoid bloodshed:** _避免流血事件_

+ **cause bloodshed:** _造成流血事件_

+ **stop bloodshed:** _制止流血事件_

+ **witness bloodshed:** _目睹流血事件_

+ **end bloodshed:** _结束流血事件_

**单词释义:** 流血事件；杀戮

**单词释义:** 流血；杀戮

### 背诵小故事

> In a dark forest, a fierce battle occurred. Swords clashed and people
screamed. There was a lot of bloodshed. Blood flowed freely, staining
the ground. It was a terrifying scene of violence and death.
