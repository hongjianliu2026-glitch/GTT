## boarding

**英音:** _[\'bɔːdɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'bɔrdɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.寄膳宿,木板*

### 短语

+ **boarding school:** _寄宿学校_

+ **boarding pass:** _登机证；登机牌_

+ **boarding house:** _供膳寄宿处_

+ **boarding time:** _登机时间；上船时间_

+ **boarding gate:** _登机口；登机门_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The boarding time for the flight is 3:00 PM.**

**中文翻译:** 这趟航班的登机时间是下午 3 点。

**单词释义:** 登机

#### 例句2

> **She sent her child to a boarding school.**

**中文翻译:** 她把孩子送到了寄宿学校。

**单词释义:** 寄宿

#### 例句3

> **The boarding of the windows was necessary for security.**

**中文翻译:** 为了安全，给窗户装上木板是必要的。

**单词释义:** 用木板封住

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was at the airport and saw a sign that said
\'Boarding\'. He realized it meant getting on the plane. He followed the
instructions and successfully boarded the flight to his dream
destination.
