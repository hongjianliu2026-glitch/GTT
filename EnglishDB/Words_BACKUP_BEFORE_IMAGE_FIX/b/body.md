## body

**英音:** _[\'bɒdɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'bɑdi]_

**译义:** *n.身体,肉体,人,尸体,主要部分,团体,大量vt.赋以形体*

### 短语

+ **body language:** _身体语言；肢体语言_

+ **human body:** _人体_

+ **body temperature:** _体温_

+ **body part:** _身体部位_

+ **whole body:** _全身_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The body of the car was damaged in the accident.**

**中文翻译:** 事故中车身受损。

**单词释义:** 物体的主体；车身

#### 例句2

> **Exercise is good for the body and mind.**

**中文翻译:** 锻炼对身体和心灵都有好处。

**单词释义:** 身体

#### 例句3

> **The main body of the report is very detailed.**

**中文翻译:** 报告的主体内容非常详细。

**单词释义:** 主体；主要部分

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little ant was lost. It searched everywhere and finally
found its way back to the body of its ant colony. The body of the colony
was like a big family, protecting and supporting each other.
