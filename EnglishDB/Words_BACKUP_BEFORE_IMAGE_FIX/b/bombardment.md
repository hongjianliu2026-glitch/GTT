## bombardment

**英音:** _[bɒm\'bɑːdm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[bɑm\'bɑrdmənt]_

**译义:** *n.炮击,轰击*

### 短语

+ **aerial bombardment:** _空中轰炸_

+ **constant bombardment:** _持续的轰击_

+ **heavy bombardment:** _猛烈的炮击_

+ **nuclear bombardment:** _核轰击_

+ **artillery bombardment:** _炮火轰击_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The city endured a constant bombardment during the war.**

**中文翻译:** 战争期间，这座城市遭受了持续的轰炸。

**单词释义:** 轰炸

#### 例句2

> **The bombardment of advertisements makes it hard to focus.**

**中文翻译:** 广告的轰炸让人难以集中注意力。

**单词释义:** 大量的（信息）冲击

#### 例句3

> **The enemy\'s bombardment of our positions was intense.**

**中文翻译:** 敌人对我军阵地的炮击十分猛烈。

**单词释义:** 轰炸

### 背诵小故事

> In a war, the enemy\'s city was under bombardment. Bombs were
constantly falling from the sky, causing chaos and destruction. The
sound of the bombardment was deafening. Remember, bombardment means a
continuous attack with bombs or shells.

**在一场战争中，敌人的城市遭到轰炸。炸弹不断从天上落下，造成混乱和破坏。轰炸的声音震耳欲聋。记住，bombardment
意思是用炸弹或炮弹的连续攻击。**
