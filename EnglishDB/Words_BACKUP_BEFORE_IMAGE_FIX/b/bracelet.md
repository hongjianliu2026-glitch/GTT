## bracelet

**英音:** _[ˈbreɪslət]_ **美音:** _[ˈbreslɪt]_

**译义:** *n.手镯*

### 短语

+ **gold bracelet:** _金手镯_

+ **silver bracelet:** _银手镯_

+ **beaded bracelet:** _串珠手链_

+ **leather bracelet:** _皮革手链_

+ **bracelet charm:** _手链坠_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She wore a beautiful gold bracelet on her wrist.**

**中文翻译:** 她手腕上戴着一只漂亮的金手镯。

**单词释义:** 手镯

#### 例句2

> **The bracelet was a gift from her mother.**

**中文翻译:** 这只手镯是她母亲送的礼物。

**单词释义:** 手镯

#### 例句3

> **He bought a silver bracelet for his girlfriend.**

**中文翻译:** 他给他女朋友买了一只银手镯。

**单词释义:** 手镯

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Alice was very sad because she lost her favorite bracelet.

bracelet was shiny and made her happy again.
