### 背诵小故事

> Imagine two political parties constantly fighting. But one day, they
decide to work together for the common good. This act of coming together
and cooperating is called bipartisanship.

**想象两个政党一直在争斗。但有一天，他们决定为了共同的利益一起合作。这种走到一起并合作的行为就被称为两党合作。**
