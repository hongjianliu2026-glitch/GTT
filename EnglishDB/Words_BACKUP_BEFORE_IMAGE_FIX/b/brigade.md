## brigade

**英音:** _[brɪ\'geɪd]_ **美音:** _[brɪ\'ged]_

**译义:** *n.旅,队*

### 短语

+ **fire brigade:** _消防队_

+ **brigade commander:** _旅长_

+ **brigade major:** _旅参谋长_

+ **military brigade:** _军事旅_

+ **brigade of guards:** _禁卫旅_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The fire brigade was called to put out the blaze.**

**中文翻译:** 消防队被叫去扑灭大火。

**单词释义:** 消防队

#### 例句2

> **The soldiers were organized into a brigade.**

**中文翻译:** 士兵们编成一个旅。

**单词释义:** 旅（军队编制单位）

#### 例句3

> **A brigade of volunteers helped clean up the park.**

**中文翻译:** 一队志愿者帮助清理了公园。

**单词释义:** 队；组

### 背诵小故事

> A fire broke out in the forest. The brave brigade of firefighters
rushed to the scene. They worked together, using hoses and tools to put
out the fire and save the forest. Remember, \'brigade\' is a group
working for a common goal.

**森林里发生了火灾。勇敢的消防队迅速赶到现场。他们齐心协力，用消防水管和工具灭火并拯救了森林。记住，\'brigade\'
就是为了共同目标一起工作的团体。**
