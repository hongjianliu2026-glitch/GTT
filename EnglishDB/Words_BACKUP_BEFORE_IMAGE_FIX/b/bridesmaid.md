## bridesmaid

**英音:** _[\'braɪdzmeɪd]_ **美音:** _[\'braɪdzmed]_

**译义:** *n.女傧相*

### 短语

+ **chief bridesmaid:** _首席伴娘_

+ **bridesmaid dress:** _伴娘礼服_

+ **junior bridesmaid:** _年少伴娘_

+ **honorary bridesmaid:** _名誉伴娘_

+ **maid of honor bridesmaid:** _主伴娘_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The bridesmaid wore a beautiful pink dress.**

**中文翻译:** 伴娘穿着一件漂亮的粉红色连衣裙。

**单词释义:** 伴娘

#### 例句2

> **She was chosen as the bridesmaid for her best friend\'s
wedding.**

**中文翻译:** 她被选为她最好朋友的婚礼的伴娘。

**单词释义:** 伴娘

#### 例句3

> **The bridesmaid carried a bouquet of flowers.**

**中文翻译:** 伴娘手捧一束鲜花。

**单词释义:** 伴娘

### 背诵小故事

> A beautiful bride was very nervous before the wedding. Her
bridesmaids, dressed in lovely gowns, held her hands and gave her
courage. They were like her loyal companions on this important day.

**一位美丽的新娘在婚礼前非常紧张。她的伴娘，身着漂亮的礼服，握住她的手给她勇气。在这个重要的日子里，她们就像她忠诚的伙伴。**
