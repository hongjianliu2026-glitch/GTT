## offender

**英音:** _[ə\'fendə(r)]_ **美音:** _[ə\'fɛndɚ]_

**译义:** *n.罪犯,冒犯者*

### 短语

+ **habitual offender:** _惯犯_

+ **juvenile offender:** _少年犯_

+ **first-time offender:** _初犯_

+ **serious offender:** _重犯_

+ **repeat offender:** _累犯_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The offender was sentenced to five years in prison.**

**中文翻译:** 罪犯被判处五年徒刑。

**单词释义:** 罪犯

#### 例句2

> **The police are searching for the offender who committed the
robbery.**

**中文翻译:** 警方正在寻找实施抢劫的罪犯。

**单词释义:** 罪犯；违法者

#### 例句3

> **First-time offenders may receive a warning instead of a fine.**

**中文翻译:** 初犯可能会收到警告而不是罚款。

**单词释义:** 违法者；犯罪者

### 背诵小故事

> One night, a thief broke into a house. The police caught him and
called him the offender. Because he violated the law and did something
wrong by stealing from others.

**一天晚上，一个小偷闯入一所房子。警察抓住了他，并称他为罪犯。因为他违反了法律，通过偷别人的东西做了错事。**
