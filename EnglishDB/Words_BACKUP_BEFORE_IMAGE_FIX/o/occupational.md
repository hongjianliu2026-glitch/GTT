## occupational

**英音:** _[ɒkjʊ\'peɪʃ(ə)n(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,ɑkju\'peʃənl]_

**译义:** *adj.职业的,占领的*

### 短语

+ **occupational disease:** _职业病_

+ **occupational hazard:** _职业危害_

+ **occupational therapy:** _职业疗法_

+ **occupational training:** _职业培训_

+ **occupational safety:** _职业安全_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Occupational health and safety is of paramount importance in the
workplace.**

**中文翻译:** 职业健康和安全在工作场所至关重要。

**单词释义:** 职业的

#### 例句2

> **She is pursuing an occupational therapy degree to help people
with disabilities.**

**中文翻译:** 她正在攻读职业治疗学位，以帮助残疾人。

**单词释义:** 职业的

#### 例句3

> **The government is implementing new policies to improve
occupational standards.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在实施新政策来提高职业标准。

**单词释义:** 职业的

### 背诵小故事

> A young man was confused about his future. One day, he met a wise old
man who told him to focus on occupational choices that made him happy.
He followed the advice and found success in his occupational path.

**一个年轻人对自己的未来感到迷茫。有一天，他遇到了一位睿智的老人，老人告诉他要专注于能让自己开心的职业选择。他听从了建议，在职业道路上取得了成功。**
