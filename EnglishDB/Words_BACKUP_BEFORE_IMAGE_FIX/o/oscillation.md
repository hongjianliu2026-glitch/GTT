## oscillation

**英音:** _[,ɒsɪ\'leɪʃn]_ **美音:** _[,ɑsɪ\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.摆动,振动*

### 短语

+ **thermal oscillation:** _热振荡_

+ **oscillation frequency:** _振荡频率_

+ **electrical oscillation:** _电振荡_

+ **mechanical oscillation:** _机械振荡_

+ **oscillation amplitude:** _振荡幅度_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The oscillation of the pendulum was mesmerizing.**

**中文翻译:** 钟摆的摆动令人着迷。

**单词释义:** 摆动

#### 例句2

> **There was an oscillation in the stock market prices.**

**中文翻译:** 股市价格出现震荡。

**单词释义:** 波动

#### 例句3

> **The oscillation of her mood was obvious.**

**中文翻译:** 她的情绪波动是显而易见的。

**单词释义:** 起伏

### 背诵小故事

> A little monkey was swinging on a rope, going back and forth in
oscillation. It was like a pendulum, constantly changing its position.
Just like this movement, \'oscillation\' means the act of swinging or
vibrating regularly.

**一只小猴子在绳子上荡来荡去，来回摆动。这就像一个钟摆，不断地改变位置。就像这种运动一样，\'oscillation\'意思是有规律地摆动或振动。**
