## oppose

**英音:** _[ə\'pəʊz]_ **美音:** _[ə\'poz]_

**译义:** *vt.反对,使对立,使对抗,抗争vi.反对*

### 短语

+ **oppose to:** _反对；反抗_

+ **be opposed to:** _反对；与\...\...对立_

+ **strongly oppose:** _强烈反对_

+ **oppose vehemently:** _激烈反对_

+ **oppose actively:** _积极反对_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I oppose your plan because it\'s not feasible.**

**中文翻译:** 我反对你的计划，因为它不可行。

**单词释义:** 反对

#### 例句2

> **The citizens oppose the construction of the new factory due to
environmental concerns.**

**中文翻译:** 由于环境问题，市民们反对新建工厂。

**单词释义:** 反对

#### 例句3

> **He strongly opposed the idea of giving up at the first
difficulty.**

**中文翻译:** 他强烈反对一遇到困难就放弃的想法。

**单词释义:** 反对

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was determined to oppose the evil dragon. The dragon
wanted to destroy the village, but the knight stood in its way, showing
his strong will to oppose the threat.
