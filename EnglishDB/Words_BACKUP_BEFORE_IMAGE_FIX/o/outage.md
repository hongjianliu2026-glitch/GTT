## outage

**英音:** _[\'aʊtɪdʒ]_ **美音:** _[\'aʊtɪdʒ]_

**译义:** *n.储运损耗*

### 短语

+ **power outage:** _停电；电力中断_

+ **service outage:** _服务中断_

+ **internet outage:** _网络中断_

+ **communication outage:** _通信中断_

+ **telephone outage:** _电话中断_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The power outage lasted for several hours, leaving the entire
neighborhood in darkness.**

**中文翻译:** 停电持续了几个小时，整个街区一片漆黑。

**单词释义:** 停电；断电

#### 例句2

> **There was an outage of the internet service due to a technical
glitch.**

**中文翻译:** 由于技术故障，互联网服务中断。

**单词释义:** 中断；停止服务

#### 例句3

> **The water outage disrupted the normal operation of the
factory.**

**中文翻译:** 停水扰乱了工厂的正常生产。

**单词释义:** 停水；供水中断

### 背诵小故事

> One night, the whole town was plunged into darkness. It turned out
there was an outage of electricity. People had to use candles. Later,
the power was restored. Remember, \'outage\' means a disruption or
failure of supply, like this power outage.

**一天晚上，整个城镇陷入黑暗。原来是停电了。人们不得不使用蜡烛。后来，电恢复了。记住，\'outage\'意思是供应的中断或故障，就像这次停电。**
