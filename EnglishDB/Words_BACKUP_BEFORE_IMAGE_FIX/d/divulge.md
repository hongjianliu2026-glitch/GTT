## divulge

**英音:** _[daɪ\'vʌldʒ; dɪ-]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'vʌldʒ]_

**译义:** *vt.泄露,暴露*

### 短语

+ **divulge secrets:** _泄露秘密_

+ **divulge information:** _透露信息_

+ **divulge details:** _披露细节_

+ **divulge the truth:** _揭露真相_

+ **refrain from divulging:** _忍住不泄露_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The spy refused to divulge any secrets to the enemy.**

**中文翻译:** 间谍拒绝向敌人泄露任何秘密。

**单词释义:** 透露；泄露

#### 例句2

> **She wouldn\'t divulge the details of her plan.**

**中文翻译:** 她不愿透露计划的细节。

**单词释义:** 透露；泄露

#### 例句3

> **Journalists are often pressured not to divulge sensitive
information.**

**中文翻译:** 记者经常面临不泄露敏感信息的压力。

**单词释义:** 泄露；公开

### 背诵小故事

> A spy was captured and threatened with punishment. Finally, he
couldn\'t bear the pressure and divulged the secret mission. Remember,
\'divulge\' means to reveal something that was supposed to be kept
secret.

**一名间谍被抓获并受到惩罚的威胁。最终，他无法承受压力，泄露了秘密任务。记住，\'divulge\'意思是透露本应保密的事情。**
