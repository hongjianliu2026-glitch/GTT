## dissatisfied

**英音:** _[dɪs\'sætɪsfaɪd]_ **美音:**
_[dɪs\'sætɪsfaɪd]_

**译义:** *adj.不满意的,不高兴的*

### 短语

+ **be dissatisfied with:** _对\...\...不满_

+ **dissatisfied customer:** _不满的顾客_

+ **feel dissatisfied:** _感到不满_

+ **dissatisfied look:** _不满的表情_

+ **show dissatisfaction:** _表现出不满_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She was dissatisfied with the service at the restaurant.**

**中文翻译:** 她对这家餐厅的服务不满意。

**单词释义:** 不满意

#### 例句2

> **The customers are increasingly dissatisfied with the quality of
the products.**

**中文翻译:** 顾客们对产品的质量越来越不满意。

**单词释义:** 不满意

#### 例句3

> **He looked dissatisfied when he heard the result.**

**中文翻译:** 当他听到结果时，他看起来不满意。

**单词释义:** 不满意

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom went to a restaurant. The food was bad and the service
was poor. He felt very dissatisfied. He complained to the manager and
decided never to come back again.

**有一天，汤姆去了一家餐厅。食物很差，服务也不好。他感到非常不满。他向经理抱怨，并决定再也不来了。**
