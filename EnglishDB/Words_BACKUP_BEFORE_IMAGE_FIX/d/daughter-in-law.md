### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a man introduced a lady to his parents and said,
\'This is my daughter-in-law.\' The parents understood that she was the
wife of their son. So, daughter-in-law means the wife of one\'s son.

**从前，有个男人向他的父母介绍一位女士说："这是我的儿媳。"父母明白她是他们儿子的妻子。所以，daughter-in-law
意思是儿子的妻子。**
