## doctrine

**英音:** _[\'dɒktrɪn]_ **美音:** _[\'dɑktrɪn]_

**译义:** *n.教条,学说*

### 短语

+ **religious doctrine:** _宗教教义_

+ **political doctrine:** _政治学说_

+ **military doctrine:** _军事学说_

+ **economic doctrine:** _经济理论_

+ **doctrine of precedent:** _判例原则_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctrine of evolution is widely accepted in the scientific
community.**

**中文翻译:** 进化论这一学说在科学界被广泛接受。

**单词释义:** 学说；教义

#### 例句2

> **The company operates under a strict doctrine of quality
control.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司在严格的质量控制原则下运营。

**单词释义:** 原则；信条

#### 例句3

> **His political doctrine emphasizes social justice and
equality.**

**中文翻译:** 他的政治学说强调社会正义和平等。

**单词释义:** 学说；主义

### 背诵小故事

> A young monk was confused about the monastery\'s doctrines. One day,
an elder monk explained them clearly. The young monk finally understood
that doctrine means a set of beliefs or principles. Now he follows them
with a clear mind.

**一个年轻的和尚对寺院的教义感到困惑。有一天，一位年长的和尚清楚地解释了它们。年轻的和尚终于明白，doctrine
指的是一套信仰或原则。现在他头脑清晰地遵循着它们。**
