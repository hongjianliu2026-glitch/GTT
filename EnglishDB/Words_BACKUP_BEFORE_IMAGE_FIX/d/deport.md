## deport

**英音:** _[dɪ\'pɔːt]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'pɔrt]_

**译义:** *vt.举止,驱逐*

### 短语

+ **deport oneself:** _举止，表现_

+ **be deported from:** _被从\...\...驱逐出境_

+ **deport illegally:** _非法驱逐_

+ **deport sb. for sth.:** _因某事驱逐某人_

+ **deport to:** _驱逐到\...\..._

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government decided to deport all illegal immigrants.**

**中文翻译:** 政府决定驱逐所有非法移民。

**单词释义:** 驱逐

#### 例句2

> **He was deported from the country for criminal activities.**

**中文翻译:** 他因犯罪活动被驱逐出该国。

**单词释义:** 驱逐

#### 例句3

> **The authorities have the power to deport individuals who pose a
threat to national security.**

**中文翻译:** 当局有权驱逐对国家安全构成威胁的个人。

**单词释义:** 驱逐

### 背诵小故事

> A criminal was caught and deported from the country. He had committed
many serious crimes and was no longer welcome. The police took him to
the airport and sent him away. Remember, \'deport\' means to force
someone to leave a country.

**一名罪犯被抓获并被驱逐出境。他犯下了许多严重罪行，不再受欢迎。警察把他带到机场并送走了他。记住，'deport'意思是强迫某人离开一个国家。**
