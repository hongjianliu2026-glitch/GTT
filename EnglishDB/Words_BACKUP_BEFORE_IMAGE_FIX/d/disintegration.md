## disintegration

**英音:** _[dɪs,ɪntɪ\'greɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[dɪs,ɪntə\'greʃən]_

**译义:** *n.瓦解*

### 短语

+ **nuclear disintegration:** _核分裂_

+ **social disintegration:** _社会解体_

+ **molecular disintegration:** _分子分解_

+ **rapid disintegration:** _快速瓦解_

+ **gradual disintegration:** _逐渐崩解_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The disintegration of the Soviet Union was a significant event in
history.**

**中文翻译:** 苏联的解体是历史上的一个重大事件。

**单词释义:** 解体；分裂；瓦解

#### 例句2

> **The old building is in a state of disintegration.**

**中文翻译:** 这座旧建筑处于崩解的状态。

**单词释义:** 崩解；破碎

#### 例句3

> **The disintegration of the rock was caused by weathering.**

**中文翻译:** 岩石的崩裂是由风化造成的。

**单词释义:** 崩裂；分解

### 背诵小故事

> A powerful magician tried to keep a precious gem intact, but his evil
rival cast a spell of \'disintegration\'. The gem broke into pieces.
Remember, \'disintegration\' means the process of breaking into pieces.

**一位强大的魔法师试图保持一颗珍贵宝石的完整，但他邪恶的对手施了一个"瓦解"的咒语。宝石碎成了几块。记住，"disintegration"意思是破碎成块的过程。**
