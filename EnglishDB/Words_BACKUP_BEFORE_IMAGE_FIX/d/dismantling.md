## dismantling

**英音:** _[dɪsˈmæntlɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[dɪsˈmæntlɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n. 拆卸；废除；分解 v. 拆除；取消；解散（dismantle
的现在分词）*

### 短语

+ **dismantling work:** _拆除工作_

+ **dismantling process:** _拆解过程_

+ **dismantling equipment:** _拆卸设备_

+ **dismantling of structures:** _结构的拆除_

+ **system dismantling:** _系统拆除_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The workers are engaged in the dismantling of the old
factory.**

**中文翻译:** 工人们正在拆除旧工厂。

**单词释义:** 拆除

#### 例句2

> **The government is considering the dismantling of some unnecessary
regulations.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在考虑废除一些不必要的规定。

**单词释义:** 废除

#### 例句3

> **He spent the weekend dismantling the broken machine to see if it
could be repaired.**

**中文翻译:** 他周末把那台坏机器拆开，看看是否能修理。

**单词释义:** 拆开

### 背诵小故事

> A factory was undergoing dismantling. Workers carefully took apart
the machines, piece by piece. It was a complex process, but they were
determined. \'Dismantling\' means to take apart or break down something.

**一家工厂正在被拆除。工人们小心翼翼地把机器一块一块地拆开。这是一个复杂的过程，但他们很坚决。'dismantling'意思是拆开或拆除某物。**
