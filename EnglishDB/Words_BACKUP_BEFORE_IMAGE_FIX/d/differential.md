## differential

**英音:** _[,dɪfə\'renʃ(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[\'dɪfə\'rɛnʃəl]_

**译义:** *adj.微分的n.微分*

### 短语

+ **differential equation:** _微分方程_

+ **differential calculus:** _微分学_

+ **differential gear:** _差速齿轮_

+ **differential pressure:** _压差_

+ **differential diagnosis:** _鉴别诊断_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The differential between the two prices is significant.**

**中文翻译:** 两个价格之间的差异很大。

**单词释义:** 差额；差价

#### 例句2

> **Differential calculus is an important part of mathematics.**

**中文翻译:** 微分学是数学的重要组成部分。

**单词释义:** 微分（的）

#### 例句3

> **The car has a differential gear to improve its performance.**

**中文翻译:** 该车配有差速器以提高其性能。

**单词释义:** 差速器；差速齿轮

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working on a complex math problem. The key to solving
it was understanding the differential part. It was like finding the tiny
differences that made a big impact. Finally, he cracked it!

**一位科学家正在研究一个复杂的数学问题。解决它的关键在于理解微分部分。这就像找到那些产生重大影响的微小差异。最终，他解决了！**
