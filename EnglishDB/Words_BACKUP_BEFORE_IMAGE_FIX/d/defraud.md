## defraud

**英音:** _[dɪ\'frɔːd]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'frɔd]_

**译义:** *vt.欺骗*

### 短语

+ **defraud of:** _骗取，诈取_

+ **defraud someone out of something:** _骗某人失去某物_

+ **defraud by false pretenses:** _用欺诈手段诈骗_

+ **attempt to defraud:** _企图诈骗_

+ **be defrauded into doing something:** _被骗去做某事_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He was accused of defrauding the company of millions of
dollars.**

**中文翻译:** 他被指控诈骗该公司数百万美元。

**单词释义:** 诈骗

#### 例句2

> **The salesman defrauded customers by selling fake products.**

**中文翻译:** 该推销员通过销售假冒产品来欺骗顾客。

**单词释义:** 欺骗

#### 例句3

> **She was found guilty of defrauding the government of tax
revenue.**

**中文翻译:** 她被判犯有诈骗政府税收罪。

**单词释义:** 骗取

### 背诵小故事

> A thief planned to defraud a rich merchant. He pretended to be a
noble and gained the merchant\'s trust. But in the end, his scheme was
exposed. Remember, defraud means to cheat someone out of money or
property.

**一个小偷计划欺骗一位富商。他假装是个贵族并取得了富商的信任。但最终，他的阴谋被揭穿了。记住，defraud
意思是骗取某人的钱财或财产。**
