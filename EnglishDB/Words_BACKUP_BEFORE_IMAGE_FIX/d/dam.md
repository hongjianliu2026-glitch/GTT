## dam

**英音:** _[dæm]_ **美音:** _[dæm]_

**译义:** *n.水坝,障碍v.控制,筑坝*

### 短语

+ **dam up:** _to block or obstruct (a river, etc.) with a dam_

+ **dam site:** _the location where a dam is built_

+ **dam breach:** _a failure or break in a dam, often causing
flooding_

+ **dam construction:** _the process of building a dam_

+ **dam safety:** _measures taken to ensure the structural
integrity and safe operation of a dam_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The beavers built a dam across the river to create a
reservoir.**

**中文翻译:** 海狸在河上筑坝以形成一个水库。

**单词释义:** 水坝

#### 例句2

> **The dam burst after days of heavy rain, causing flooding.**

**中文翻译:** 连续多日的大雨过后，大坝决堤了，引发了洪水。

**单词释义:** 水坝

#### 例句3

> **They are planning to construct a new dam to generate
electricity.**

**中文翻译:** 他们正计划建造一座新的大坝来发电。

**单词释义:** 水坝

### 背诵小故事

> A beaver was working hard to build a dam across a river. It carried
logs and branches to stop the water flow. The dam was its masterpiece to
create a peaceful pool. Remember, \'dam\' means a barrier to hold back
water.

**一只海狸正在努力地在一条河上建水坝。它搬运原木和树枝来阻止水流。水坝是它创造平静水池的杰作。记住，\'dam\'意思是阻挡水流的障碍物。**
