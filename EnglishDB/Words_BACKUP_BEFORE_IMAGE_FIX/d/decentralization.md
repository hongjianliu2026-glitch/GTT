## decentralization

**英音:** _[diː,sentrəlaɪ\'zeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[ˌdiˌsentrəlaɪ\'zeɪʃn]_

**译义:** *n.分散,地方分权,排除集中*

### 短语

+ **decentralization policy:** _分权政策_

+ **economic decentralization:** _经济分权_

+ **administrative decentralization:** _行政分权_

+ **decentralization process:** _分权过程_

+ **system of decentralization:** _分权制度_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The decentralization of power has led to more local
decision-making.**

**中文翻译:** 权力下放导致更多的地方决策。

**单词释义:** 分散；分权

#### 例句2

> **Decentralization is often seen as a way to increase efficiency in
large organizations.**

**中文翻译:** 权力下放通常被视为提高大型组织效率的一种方式。

**单词释义:** 分散化；去中心化

#### 例句3

> **The company is considering the decentralization of its operations
to different regions.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在考虑将其业务分散到不同地区。

**单词释义:** 分散；下放

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a big kingdom where all the power was held in one place. But
then, a wise leader decided to give power to different regions. This
spreading of power is called decentralization.

**想象一个大王国，所有权力都集中在一处。但后来，一位明智的领导者决定将权力赋予不同地区。这种权力的分散就叫做权力下放。**
