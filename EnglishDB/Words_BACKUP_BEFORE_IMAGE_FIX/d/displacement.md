## displacement

**英音:** _[dɪs\'pleɪsm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[dɪs\'plesmənt]_

**译义:** *n.移置,转移,取代,置换,位移,排水量*

### 短语

+ **displacement activity:** _替代活动；移位活动_

+ **displacement current:** _位移电流_

+ **displacement vector:** _位移矢量_

+ **horizontal displacement:** _水平位移_

+ **vertical displacement:** _垂直位移_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The displacement of the population due to the war was a
humanitarian crisis.**

**中文翻译:** 由于战争导致的人口迁移是一场人道主义危机。

**单词释义:** 迁移；流离失所

#### 例句2

> **The engine\'s displacement is an important factor in determining
its power.**

**中文翻译:** 发动机的排气量是决定其功率的一个重要因素。

**单词释义:** 排气量

#### 例句3

> **The displacement of the object was measured accurately.**

**中文翻译:** 这个物体的位移被精确测量了。

**单词释义:** 位移

### 背诵小故事

> A man was forced to leave his home due to a flood, experiencing
displacement. He had to find a new place to live. This sad event made
him understand the meaning of \'displacement\' - the act of moving
something from its usual or original position.

**一个男人因洪水被迫离开家，经历了流离失所。他不得不找新的地方居住。这个悲伤的事件让他明白了'displacement'这个词的意思------将某物从其通常或原来的位置移动。**
