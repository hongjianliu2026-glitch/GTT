## discriminate

**英音:** _[dɪ\'skrɪmɪneɪt]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'skrɪmɪnet]_

**译义:** *v.歧视,区别,区别待遇*

### 短语

+ **discriminate against:** _歧视；排斥_

+ **discriminate between:** _区分；辨别_

+ **discriminate in favor of:** _偏袒；优待_

+ **be able to discriminate:** _能够辨别_

+ **fail to discriminate:** _未能辨别_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The law prohibits employers from discriminating against employees
based on their race.**

**中文翻译:** 法律禁止雇主基于种族歧视雇员。

**单词释义:** 歧视

#### 例句2

> **She has the ability to discriminate between right and wrong.**

**中文翻译:** 她有辨别是非的能力。

**单词释义:** 辨别；区分

#### 例句3

> **The new test can discriminate patients with the disease from
those without.**

**中文翻译:**
新的测试可以区分患有这种疾病的患者和未患有这种疾病的患者。

**单词释义:** 区分；鉴别

### 背诵小故事

> A king had to discriminate between two knights. One was brave and
honest, the other was sly. By observing their actions in battles, he
finally chose the right one. Here, \'discriminate\' means to make a
clear distinction.

**一位国王必须区分两位骑士。一位勇敢诚实，另一位狡猾。通过观察他们在战斗中的表现，他最终做出了正确选择。这里，'discriminate'意思是区分、辨别。**
