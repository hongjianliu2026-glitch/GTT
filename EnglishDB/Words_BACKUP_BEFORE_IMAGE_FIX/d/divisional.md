## divisional

**英音:** _[dɪˈvɪʒənl]_ **美音:** _[dɪˈvɪʒənl]_

**译义:** *adj.分割的,分区的,师的除法的,部门所有的*

### 短语

+ **divisional manager:** _部门经理_

+ **divisional structure:** _事业部结构_

+ **divisional headquarters:** _分区总部_

+ **divisional director:** _部门主管_

+ **divisional playoff:** _分区季后赛_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The divisional manager is responsible for the performance of the
entire division.**

**中文翻译:** 部门经理对整个部门的绩效负责。

**单词释义:** 部门的；分区的

#### 例句2

> **The company\'s divisional structure was reorganized to improve
efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 公司部门架构进行重组，提高效率。

**单词释义:** 划分的；分割的

#### 例句3

> **The divisional championship was fiercely contested by several
teams.**

**中文翻译:** 分区冠军的争夺由多支球队展开激烈争夺。

**单词释义:** 分区的；分部的

### 背诵小故事

> A divisional manager was in charge of several teams. But there was a
big problem. The teams were not working well together. The manager had
to find a way to make them united. So \'divisional\' means related to a
division or a part of a whole.

**一位部门经理负责几个团队。但有个大问题，这些团队合作不佳。经理得想办法让他们团结起来。所以'divisional'意思是与划分或整体的一部分有关。**
