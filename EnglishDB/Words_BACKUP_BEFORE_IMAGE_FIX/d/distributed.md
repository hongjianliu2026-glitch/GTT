## distributed

**英音:** _[dɪ\'strɪbjʊtɪd]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ\'strɪbjʊtɪd]_

**译义:** *adj.分布式的*

### 短语

+ **distributed computing:** _分布式计算_

+ **distributed system:** _分布式系统_

+ **distributed database:** _分布式数据库_

+ **widely distributed:** _广泛分布_

+ **evenly distributed:** _均匀分布_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The books were distributed evenly among the students.**

**中文翻译:** 这些书在学生中被均匀分发。

**单词释义:** 分发；分配

#### 例句2

> **The information was distributed widely through the internet.**

**中文翻译:** 信息通过互联网被广泛传播。

**单词释义:** 传播；散布

#### 例句3

> **The company\'s profits are distributed among the
shareholders.**

**中文翻译:** 公司的利润在股东之间分配。

**单词释义:** 分配；分发

### 背诵小故事

> A group of kind kids distributed candies to all the little friends in
the neighborhood. They made everyone happy. \'Distributed\' here means
to give things to different people or places.

**一群善良的孩子把糖果分发给了小区里所有的小朋友。他们让每个人都很开心。这里的'distributed'意思是把东西分给不同的人或地方。**
