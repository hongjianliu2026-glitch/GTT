## dictatorship

**英音:** _[dɪk\'teɪtəʃɪp]_ **美音:** _[dɪk\'tetɚ\'ʃɪp]_

**译义:** *n.专政*

### 短语

+ **military dictatorship:** _军事独裁_

+ **totalitarian dictatorship:** _极权独裁_

+ **tyrannical dictatorship:** _暴虐的独裁_

+ **despotic dictatorship:** _专制独裁_

+ **authoritarian dictatorship:** _威权独裁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The country suffered under the dictatorship for many years.**

**中文翻译:** 该国多年来遭受独裁统治。

**单词释义:** 独裁统治；专政

#### 例句2

> **The overthrow of the dictatorship was a great victory for the
people.**

**中文翻译:** 推翻独裁政权是人民的伟大胜利。

**单词释义:** 独裁政权；专制制度

#### 例句3

> **He spoke out against the dictatorship and its brutalities.**

**中文翻译:** 他公开反对独裁政权及其暴行。

**单词释义:** 独裁；专制

### 背诵小故事

> In a faraway land, a cruel leader seized all the power and ruled with
an iron fist. This leader\'s absolute control was called dictatorship.
People lived in fear and had no freedom. Remember, dictatorship means
absolute power by one person.

**在一个遥远的地方，一个残酷的领导者夺取了所有权力，用铁拳统治。这位领导者的这种绝对控制权就被称为独裁统治。人们生活在恐惧中，毫无自由。记住，dictatorship
意思是独裁统治。**
