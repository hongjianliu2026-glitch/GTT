## dissemination

**英音:** _[dɪ,semɪ\'neɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ,sɛmə\'neʃən]_

**译义:** *n.分发*

### 短语

+ **dissemination of information:** _信息的传播_

+ **wide dissemination:** _广泛传播_

+ **effective dissemination:** _有效的传播_

+ **rapid dissemination:** _快速传播_

+ **dissemination channels:** _传播渠道_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The dissemination of information through social media is very
rapid.**

**中文翻译:** 通过社交媒体传播信息非常迅速。

**单词释义:** 传播；散布

#### 例句2

> **Effective dissemination of knowledge is crucial for educational
progress.**

**中文翻译:** 有效的知识传播对于教育进步至关重要。

**单词释义:** 传播；散播

#### 例句3

> **The dissemination of false rumors can cause social unrest.**

**中文翻译:** 虚假谣言的传播会引起社会动荡。

**单词释义:** 传播；扩散

### 背诵小故事

> A group of scientists were dedicated to the dissemination of
knowledge about space. They held lectures and wrote articles, making the
wonders of the universe accessible to everyone. Finally, their efforts
led to wide public interest.

**一群科学家致力于传播有关太空的知识。他们举办讲座并撰写文章，让每个人都能了解宇宙的奇妙之处。最终，他们的努力引起了公众的广泛兴趣。**
