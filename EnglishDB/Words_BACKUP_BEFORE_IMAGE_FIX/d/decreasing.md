## decreasing

**英音:** _[dɪˈkriːsɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[dɪˈkriːsɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.减少的,渐减的*

### 短语

+ **decreasing function:** _递减函数_

+ **decreasing sequence:** _递减序列_

+ **decreasing rate:** _递减率_

+ **decreasing trend:** _下降趋势_

+ **decreasing order:** _降序_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The number of COVID-19 cases is decreasing gradually.**

**中文翻译:** COVID-19 病例数量正在逐渐减少。

**单词释义:** 减少

#### 例句2

> **The company\'s profits have been decreasing over the past few
years.**

**中文翻译:** 过去几年，该公司的利润一直在下降。

**单词释义:** 下降

#### 例句3

> **The demand for traditional products is decreasing as technology
advances.**

**中文翻译:** 随着技术的进步，对传统产品的需求正在减少。

**单词释义:** 降低

### 背诵小故事

> A king had a huge treasure chest. But every day, the number of jewels
inside was decreasing. He was very worried and tried to find out why.
Finally, he discovered a thief was stealing them.

**一位国王有一个巨大的宝箱。但每天，里面珠宝的数量都在减少。他非常担心，试图找出原因。最后，他发现有个小偷在偷珠宝。**
