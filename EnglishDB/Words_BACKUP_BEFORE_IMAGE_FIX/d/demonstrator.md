## demonstrator

**英音:** _[\'demənstreɪtə]_ **美音:**
_[\'dɛmən\'stretɚ]_

**译义:** *n.论证者,示威者,证明者,指示者*

### 短语

+ **peaceful demonstrator:** _和平示威者_

+ **professional demonstrator:** _专业的示范员_

+ **violent demonstrator:** _暴力示威者_

+ **street demonstrator:** _街头示威者_

+ **protest demonstrator:** _抗议示威者_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The demonstrators marched through the city center to protest
against the new law.**

**中文翻译:** 示威者在市中心游行，抗议新法律。

**单词释义:** 示威者

#### 例句2

> **The product demonstrator showed how to use the new device
effectively.**

**中文翻译:** 产品演示员展示了如何有效地使用新设备。

**单词释义:** 演示员

#### 例句3

> **She was a passionate demonstrator for environmental
protection.**

**中文翻译:** 她是一位热衷于环境保护的倡导者。

**单词释义:** 倡导者

### 背诵小故事

> A group of people were protesting in the street. One passionate
individual stood out as the demonstrator. He held a sign high, clearly
showing his demands and leading others to voice their concerns.

**一群人在街上抗议。有一个充满激情的人作为示威者脱颖而出。他高举着一个牌子，清楚地展示着他的要求，并带领其他人表达他们的担忧。**
