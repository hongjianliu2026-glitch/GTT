## disappearance

**英音:** _[dɪsə\'pɪərəns]_ **美音:** _[,dɪsə\'pɪrəns]_

**译义:** *n.不见,消失*

### 短语

+ **sudden disappearance:** _突然消失_

+ **complete disappearance:** _完全消失_

+ **mysterious disappearance:** _神秘消失_

+ **disappearance of species:** _物种消失_

+ **disappearance from sight:** _从视线中消失_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The disappearance of the valuable painting caused a great stir in
the art world.**

**中文翻译:** 这幅名贵画作的失踪在艺术界引起了轩然大波。

**单词释义:** 消失

#### 例句2

> **The police are investigating the disappearance of the young
girl.**

**中文翻译:** 警方正在调查这名年轻女孩的失踪事件。

**单词释义:** 失踪

#### 例句3

> **The sudden disappearance of the stars from the sky puzzled the
astronomers.**

**中文翻译:** 天空中星星的突然消失让天文学家感到困惑。

**单词释义:** 消失

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a famous magician performed on the stage. But suddenly, he
vanished without a trace. The mysterious disappearance of the magician
shocked everyone. No one knew how he did it.

**有一天，一位著名的魔术师在舞台上表演。但突然，他毫无踪迹地消失了。魔术师的神秘消失震惊了所有人。没人知道他是怎么做到的。**
