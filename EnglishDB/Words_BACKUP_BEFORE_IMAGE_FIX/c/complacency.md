## complacency

**英音:** _[kəm\'pleɪs(ə)nsɪ]_ **美音:**
_[kəm\'plesnsi]_

**译义:** *n.满足,安心*

### 短语

+ **fall into complacency:** _陷入自满_

+ **shake off complacency:** _摆脱自满_

+ **a sense of complacency:** _自满感_

+ **warn against complacency:** _告诫不要自满_

+ **overcome complacency:** _克服自满_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His complacency led to a series of mistakes in the project.**

**中文翻译:** 他的自满导致了项目中的一系列错误。

**单词释义:** 自满

#### 例句2

> **We must avoid complacency and keep working hard.**

**中文翻译:** 我们必须不自满，继续努力。

**单词释义:** 自满

#### 例句3

> **The team\'s complacency cost them the championship.**

**中文翻译:** 球队的自满让他们失去了冠军。

**单词释义:** 自满

### 背诵小故事

> A king became complacency after winning many battles. He stopped
training his army and ignored his people\'s needs. Soon, his kingdom was
attacked and he lost everything. Remember, complacency can lead to
downfall.

**一位国王在赢得多次战役后变得自满。他停止训练军队，也不理会人民的需求。很快，他的王国遭到攻击，他失去了一切。记住，自满会导致失败。**
