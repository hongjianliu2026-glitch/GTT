## certification

**英音:** _[,sɜːtɪfɪ\'keɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[,sɝtɪfɪ\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.证明*

### 短语

+ **quality certification:** _质量认证_

+ **professional certification:** _专业认证_

+ **product certification:** _产品认证_

+ **certification process:** _认证过程_

+ **obtain certification:** _获得认证_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The certification of the product ensures its quality and
safety.**

**中文翻译:** 该产品的认证确保了其质量和安全性。

**单词释义:** 证明；鉴定

#### 例句2

> **She received a professional certification in accounting.**

**中文翻译:** 她获得了会计专业认证。

**单词释义:** 证书；执照

#### 例句3

> **The company is seeking ISO certification to improve its
competitiveness.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在寻求 ISO 认证以提高其竞争力。

**单词释义:** 认证；资质证明

### 背诵小故事

> A young scientist worked hard for years to get the certification for
his new discovery. He faced many challenges but finally received the
official document, proving his success. The certification made his work
recognized worldwide.

**一位年轻的科学家多年来努力工作，为他的新发现获取认证。他面临诸多挑战，但最终收到了官方文件，证明了他的成功。这个认证让他的工作得到了全世界的认可。**
