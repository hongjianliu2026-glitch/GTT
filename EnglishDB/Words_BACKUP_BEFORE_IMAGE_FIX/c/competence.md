## competence

**英音:** _[\'kɒmpɪt(ə)ns]_ **美音:** _[\'kɑmpɪtəns]_

**译义:** *n.能力*

### 短语

+ **core competence:** _核心竞争力_

+ **professional competence:** _专业能力_

+ **language competence:** _语言能力_

+ **competence assessment:** _能力评估_

+ **competence development:** _能力发展_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He demonstrated great competence in handling the difficult
situation.**

**中文翻译:** 他表现出了处理困难局面的出色能力。

**单词释义:** 能力；胜任

#### 例句2

> **The company is looking for employees with the necessary
competence for the job.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在寻找具备该工作所需能力的员工。

**单词释义:** 能力；技能

#### 例句3

> **Her linguistic competence allowed her to communicate effectively
with people from different countries.**

**中文翻译:** 她的语言能力使她能够与来自不同国家的人进行有效的沟通。

**单词释义:** 能力；才能

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a fierce job competition, John showed his
competence in programming and problem-solving. He got the job easily
because of his outstanding skills. Competence means the ability to do
something well.

**从前，在一场激烈的求职竞争中，约翰展现了他在编程和解决问题方面的能力。他因出色的技能轻松获得了工作。Competence
意思是做某事的能力。**
