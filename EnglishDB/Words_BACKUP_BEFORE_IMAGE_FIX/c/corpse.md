## corpse

**英音:** _[kɔːps]_ **美音:** _[kɔrps]_

**译义:** *n.尸体*

### 短语

+ **corpse candle:** _鬼火_

+ **identify a corpse:** _辨认尸体_

+ **dispose of a corpse:** _处理尸体_

+ **examine a corpse:** _检查尸体_

+ **unclaimed corpse:** _无人认领的尸体_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The corpse was found in the abandoned house.**

**中文翻译:** 尸体是在废弃的房子里发现的。

**单词释义:** 尸体

#### 例句2

> **They had to deal with the stench of the decaying corpse.**

**中文翻译:** 他们必须应对腐烂尸体的恶臭。

**单词释义:** 尸体

#### 例句3

> **The detective carefully examined the corpse for any clues.**

**中文翻译:** 侦探仔细检查尸体，寻找任何线索。

**单词释义:** 尸体

hidden room, he found a corpse. It was pale and still, giving off a
