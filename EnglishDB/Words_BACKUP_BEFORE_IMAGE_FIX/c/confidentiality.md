## confidentiality

**英音:** _[,kɒnfɪ,denʃɪ\'ælɪtɪ]_ **美音:**
_[,kɑnfɪ,dɛnʃɪ\'æləti]_

**译义:** *n.机密性*

### 短语

+ **breach of confidentiality:** _违反保密规定_

+ **maintain confidentiality:** _保持机密_

+ **confidentiality agreement:** _保密协议_

+ **ensure confidentiality:** _确保机密性_

+ **violate confidentiality:** _侵犯保密性_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctor is bound by confidentiality and cannot disclose
patient information.**

**中文翻译:** 医生有保密义务，不能泄露患者信息。

**单词释义:** 保密；机密性

#### 例句2

> **We must ensure the confidentiality of the trade secrets.**

**中文翻译:** 我们必须确保商业秘密的保密性。

**单词释义:** 保密；机密性

#### 例句3

> **The company has strict policies on confidentiality to protect its
intellectual property.**

**中文翻译:** 公司有严格的保密政策以保护其知识产权。

**单词释义:** 保密；机密性

### 背诵小故事

> A spy was entrusted with a top-secret mission. The key to success was
maintaining confidentiality. If the information leaked, it would cause a
disaster. So, the spy guarded the secret carefully.

**一名间谍被委托执行一项绝密任务。成功的关键在于保密。如果信息泄露，将会造成灾难。所以，这名间谍小心翼翼地保守着秘密。**
