## counteract

**英音:** _[kaʊntər\'ækt]_ **美音:** _[,kaʊntɚ\'ækt]_

**译义:** *vt.抵消,中和,阻碍*

### 短语

+ **counteract the effect:** _抵消影响_

+ **counteract a force:** _对抗一种力量_

+ **counteract a trend:** _抵制一种趋势_

+ **counteract a poison:** _解毒_

+ **counteract an influence:** _抵制一种影响_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The medicine is designed to counteract the effects of the
poison.**

**中文翻译:** 该药旨在抵消毒药的影响。

**单词释义:** 抵消；对抗

#### 例句2

> **Exercise can counteract the negative effects of a sedentary
lifestyle.**

**中文翻译:** 锻炼可以抵消久坐生活方式的负面影响。

**单词释义:** 抵消；中和

#### 例句3

> **We need to take measures to counteract the rising crime rate.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要采取措施应对不断上升的犯罪率。

**单词释义:** 对抗；抵制

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was on a quest to counteract the evil spell cast by a
witch. He used his magic shield and sword to oppose and neutralize the
dark power. With determination, he finally counteracted the spell and
