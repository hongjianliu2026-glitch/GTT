## coronary

**英音:** _[\'kɒr(ə)n(ə)rɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'kɔrənɛri]_

**译义:** *adj.冠的,花冠的,冠状的*

### 短语

+ **coronary artery:** _冠状动脉_

+ **coronary heart disease:** _冠心病_

+ **coronary thrombosis:** _冠状动脉血栓形成_

+ **coronary bypass:** _冠状动脉旁路手术_

+ **coronary care unit:** _冠心病监护病房_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctor diagnosed him with coronary heart disease.**

**中文翻译:** 医生诊断他患有冠心病。

**单词释义:** 冠状动脉的

#### 例句2

> **Coronary bypass surgery is a common treatment for severe
cases.**

**中文翻译:** 冠状动脉搭桥手术是严重病例的常见治疗方法。

**单词释义:** 冠状动脉的

#### 例句3

> **He suffered a coronary thrombosis last year.**

**中文翻译:** 他去年患了冠状动脉血栓。

**单词释义:** 冠状动脉的

### 背诵小故事

> A king had a serious heart problem. The doctor said it was a coronary
issue. The king was worried. But with proper care, he got better.
Remember, \'coronary\' is related to the heart!

与心脏有关！**
