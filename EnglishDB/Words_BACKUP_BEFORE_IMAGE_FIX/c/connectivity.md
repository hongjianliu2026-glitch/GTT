## connectivity

**英音:** _[kɒnek\'tɪvɪtɪ]_ **美音:** _[,kɑnɛk\'tɪvɪti]_

**译义:** *n.连通性*

### 短语

+ **network connectivity:** _网络连接性_

+ **data connectivity:** _数据连接性_

+ **wireless connectivity:** _无线连接_

+ **global connectivity:** _全球连接_

+ **inter-device connectivity:** _设备间连接_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new network provides enhanced connectivity for
businesses.**

**中文翻译:** 新网络为企业提供增强的连接性。

**单词释义:** 连接性；连通性

#### 例句2

> **Poor connectivity can cause delays in data transmission.**

**中文翻译:** 连接不良可能会导致数据传输延迟。

**单词释义:** 连接；连通

#### 例句3

> **The connectivity between the two cities has improved
significantly.**

**中文翻译:** 两座城市之间的互联互通显着改善。

**单词释义:** 连通性；连接

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a group of islands. Each island has its own special
resources. But they are isolated. Then, magic bridges appear, connecting
them. These bridges represent \'connectivity\', allowing the flow of
resources and communication between the islands.

**想象有一群岛屿。每个岛屿都有自己独特的资源。但它们是孤立的。然后，神奇的桥梁出现了，将它们连接起来。这些桥梁就代表着"连通性"，允许资源的流动和岛屿之间的交流。**
