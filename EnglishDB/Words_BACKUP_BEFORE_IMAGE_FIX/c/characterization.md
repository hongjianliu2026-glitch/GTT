## characterization

**英音:** _[ˌkærəktəraɪˈzeɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[ˌkærəktərəˈzeɪʃn]_

**译义:** *n.描述,人物之创造*

### 短语

+ **characterization method:** _表征方法_

+ **characterization test:** _特性测试_

+ **characterization analysis:** _特性分析_

+ **precise characterization:** _精确表征_

+ **systematic characterization:** _系统表征_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The characterization of the main character in the novel is very
vivid.**

**中文翻译:** 小说中对主要人物的刻画非常生动。

**单词释义:** 性格刻画；描绘

#### 例句2

> **Scientists are working on the characterization of this new
virus.**

**中文翻译:** 科学家们正在研究这种新病毒的特征。

**单词释义:** 特征描述

#### 例句3

> **The film\'s poor characterization led to a lack of emotional
connection with the audience.**

**中文翻译:** 影片的人物塑造不佳导致与观众缺乏情感联系。

**单词释义:** 人物刻画

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was trying to solve a mystery. The key was the
characterization of each suspect. He carefully observed their behaviors
and words to figure out their true nature. Finally, he caught the
criminal based on accurate characterization.

**一位侦探试图解开一个谜团。关键在于对每个嫌疑人的性格刻画。他仔细观察他们的行为和言语来弄清楚他们的真实本性。最终，他依据准确的性格刻画抓住了罪犯。**
