## catastrophic

**英音:** _[kætə\'strɒfɪk]_ **美音:** _[,kætə\'strɑfɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.悲惨的,灾难的*

### 短语

+ **catastrophic event:** _灾难性事件_

+ **catastrophic failure:** _灾难性故障_

+ **catastrophic damage:** _灾难性破坏_

+ **catastrophic consequence:** _灾难性后果_

+ **catastrophic situation:** _灾难性局面_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The earthquake was a catastrophic event that caused widespread
destruction.**

**中文翻译:** 地震是一场灾难性事件，造成了广泛的破坏。

**单词释义:** 灾难性的

#### 例句2

> **The company\'s financial situation has become catastrophic due to
poor management.**

**中文翻译:** 由于管理不善，该公司的财务状况已变得灾难性。

**单词释义:** 极为糟糕的

#### 例句3

> **The flood had a catastrophic impact on the local agriculture.**

**中文翻译:** 洪水给当地农业带来了灾难性影响。

**单词释义:** 灾难性的

### 背诵小故事

> A huge earthquake hit the city. Buildings collapsed, roads were
destroyed, and many people were injured. It was a catastrophic event
that changed everything. But people united to overcome the difficulties.

**一场巨大的地震袭击了这座城市。建筑物倒塌，道路被毁，许多人受伤。这是一场灾难性的事件，改变了一切。但人们团结起来克服困难。**
