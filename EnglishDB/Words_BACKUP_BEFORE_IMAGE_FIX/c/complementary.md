## complementary

**英音:** _[kɒmplɪ\'ment(ə)rɪ]_ **美音:**
_[\'kɑmplə\'mɛntri]_

**译义:** *adj.补充的,补足的*

### 短语

+ **complementary color:** _互补色_

+ **complementary medicine:** _补充医学；替代医学_

+ **complementary angle:** _余角_

+ **complementary goods:** _互补商品_

+ **complementary function:** _余函数_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The colors of the dress are complementary, creating a harmonious
look.**

**中文翻译:** 这条裙子的颜色是互补的，营造出一种和谐的外观。

**单词释义:** 互补的

#### 例句2

> **These two products have complementary functions and can be used
together effectively.**

**中文翻译:** 这两种产品具有互补的功能，可以有效地一起使用。

**单词释义:** 互补的

#### 例句3

> **Her skills are complementary to his, making them a great
team.**

**中文翻译:** 她的技能与他的互补，使他们成为一个很棒的团队。

**单词释义:** 互补的

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor told a patient that the new medicine and the old one were
complementary. They worked together perfectly to cure the disease. Just
like two pieces of a puzzle, they fit and made the treatment successful.

**一位医生告诉一位病人，新药和旧药是互补的。它们完美地协同作用来治愈疾病。就像拼图的两块，它们契合并使治疗成功。**
