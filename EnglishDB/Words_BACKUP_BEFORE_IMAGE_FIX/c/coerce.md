## coerce

**英音:** _[kəʊ\'ɜːs]_ **美音:** _[ko\'ɝs]_

**译义:** *vt.强制,强迫*

### 短语

+ **coerce into:** _强迫（某人）做某事_

+ **coerce by force:** _用武力强迫_

+ **coerce through threat:** _通过威胁强迫_

+ **be coerced to do:** _被迫做_

+ **coerce compliance:** _强制服从_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government coerced the company into accepting the new
regulations.**

**中文翻译:** 政府强迫这家公司接受新的规定。

**单词释义:** 强迫

#### 例句2

> **He was coerced by his parents to study medicine.**

**中文翻译:** 他被父母逼迫去学医。

**单词释义:** 逼迫

#### 例句3

> **The criminal coerced the witness into giving false testimony.**

**中文翻译:** 罪犯胁迫证人作伪证。

**单词释义:** 胁迫

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was captured by an evil king. The king tried to coerce
the knight to reveal the secret of the treasure. But the knight remained
firm and refused to be forced. Finally, he escaped.

**一位勇敢的骑士被邪恶的国王俘虏了。国王试图胁迫骑士说出宝藏的秘密。但骑士坚定不移，拒绝被强迫。最后，他逃脱了。**
