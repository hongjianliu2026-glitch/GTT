## cordless

**英音:** _[\'kɔːdlɪs]_ **美音:** _[\'kɔrdləs]_

**译义:** *n.不用电线的*

### 短语

+ **cordless phone:** _无绳电话_

+ **cordless drill:** _无绳电钻_

+ **cordless mouse:** _无线鼠标_

+ **cordless vacuum cleaner:** _无线吸尘器_

+ **cordless kettle:** _无绳电水壶_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I use a cordless drill to assemble furniture.**

**中文翻译:** 我用无绳钻来组装家具。

**单词释义:** 无绳的

#### 例句2

> **The cordless phone allows me to move freely around the house
while talking.**

**中文翻译:** 无绳电话让我在通话时能在房子里自由走动。

**单词释义:** 无绳的

#### 例句3

> **This cordless vacuum cleaner is very convenient for cleaning the
car.**

**中文翻译:** 这个无绳吸尘器对于清洁汽车非常方便。

**单词释义:** 无绳的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was looking for a phone but couldn\'t find it. Then I
remembered I had a cordless one and started searching everywhere without
being limited by the cord. Finally, I found it under the sofa. Cordless
means without a cord!

**有一天，我在找电话但找不到。然后我想起我有一部无绳电话，开始到处找，不受电线的限制。最后，我在沙发下面找到了它。Cordless
意思是无绳的！**
