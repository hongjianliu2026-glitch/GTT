## carp

**英音:** _[kɑːp]_ **美音:** _[kɑrp]_

**译义:** *n.鲤鱼vi.吹毛求疵*

### 短语

+ **carp at:** _找茬；挑剔_

+ **carp about:** _抱怨；发牢骚_

+ **carp diem:** _及时行乐_

+ **sweetwater carp:** _淡水鲤鱼_

+ **grass carp:** _草鱼_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He always carp at his colleagues\' work.**

**中文翻译:** 他总是挑剔他同事的工作。

**单词释义:** 挑剔；找茬儿

#### 例句2

> **Don\'t carp about the small mistakes; focus on the overall
progress.**

**中文翻译:** 别老是对小错误吹毛求疵；要关注整体的进展。

**单词释义:** 吹毛求疵；找茬

#### 例句3

> **She carp ed constantly about the food at the restaurant.**

**中文翻译:** 她不停地抱怨餐厅的食物。

**单词释义:** 抱怨；发牢骚

### 背诵小故事

> A carp is a big fish. Once, a little boy went fishing and caught a
carp. But the carp struggled hard and escaped. The boy was sad but
learned that carps are smart and strong.
