## calibration

**英音:** _[kælɪ\'breɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,kælɪ\'breʃən]_

**译义:** *n.标度,刻度,校准*

### 短语

+ **calibration curve:** _校准曲线_

+ **calibration standard:** _校准标准_

+ **calibration equipment:** _校准设备_

+ **calibration factor:** _校准因子_

+ **calibration process:** _校准过程_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The calibration of the instrument is crucial for accurate
measurements.**

**中文翻译:** 该仪器的校准对于精确测量至关重要。

**单词释义:** 校准；标定

#### 例句2

> **We need to perform a calibration on the scale to ensure its
reliability.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要对秤进行校准以确保其可靠性。

**单词释义:** 校准

#### 例句3

> **The calibration process of this equipment is quite complex.**

**中文翻译:** 这种设备的校准过程相当复杂。

**单词释义:** 校准过程

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was conducting an important experiment. The success
relied on the precise calibration of the instruments. He spent hours
adjusting and checking to ensure the calibration was perfect. Finally,
the experiment went smoothly.

**一位科学家正在进行一项重要的实验。实验的成功依赖于仪器的精确校准。他花了数小时进行调整和检查，以确保校准无误。最终，实验顺利进行。**
