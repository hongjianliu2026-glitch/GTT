## concealment

**英音:** _[kən\'siːlm(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[kən\'silmənt]_

**译义:** *n.隐藏,隐蔽,隐蔽处*

### 短语

+ **concealment device:** _隐藏装置_

+ **concealment strategy:** _隐藏策略_

+ **concealment method:** _隐藏方法_

+ **concealment area:** _隐藏区域_

+ **concealment technique:** _隐藏技术_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The thief\'s concealment behind the bushes was eventually
discovered by the police.**

**中文翻译:** 小偷藏在灌木丛后面最终被警察发现了。

**单词释义:** 隐藏；隐匿

#### 例句2

> **The company was accused of the concealment of financial
information from its shareholders.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司被指控向股东隐瞒财务信息。

**单词释义:** 隐瞒；掩盖

#### 例句3

> **The darkness provided concealment for the soldiers as they moved
towards the enemy camp.**

**中文翻译:** 黑暗为士兵们向敌营前进提供了掩护。

**单词释义:** 掩护；遮蔽

### 背诵小故事

> A spy was on a mission to obtain secret documents. To avoid being
noticed, he used various tricks for concealment, like wearing disguises
and hiding in dark corners. Finally, he succeeded in his concealment and
got the important information.

**一名间谍正在执行获取秘密文件的任务。为了避免被发现，他使用了各种隐藏手段，比如乔装打扮和躲在黑暗角落。最终，他成功地隐藏了自己并获得了重要信息。**
