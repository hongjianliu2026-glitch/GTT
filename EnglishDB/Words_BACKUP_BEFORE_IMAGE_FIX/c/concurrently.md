## concurrently

**英音:** _[kənˈkʌrəntli]_ **美音:** _[kənˈkɜːrəntli]_

**译义:** *adv.同时发生地；并行地；同时进行地*

### 短语

+ **concurrently executed:** _并发执行_

+ **concurrently running:** _同时运行_

+ **concurrently processing:** _并发处理_

+ **concurrently available:** _同时可用_

+ **concurrently working:** _同时工作_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The two projects were developed concurrently.**

**中文翻译:** 这两个项目是同时开发的。

**单词释义:** 同时地

#### 例句2

> **She was concurrently studying French and Spanish.**

**中文翻译:** 她当时在同时学习法语和西班牙语。

**单词释义:** 同时地

#### 例句3

> **The company is concurrently expanding its domestic and
international markets.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司正在同时拓展国内和国际市场。

**单词释义:** 同时地

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to study concurrently for the exam. They
sat together, sharing notes and helping each other. It was a great way
to learn at the same time and achieve good results.

**一群朋友决定同时为考试学习。他们坐在一起，分享笔记并互相帮助。这是一种同时学习并取得好成绩的好方法。**
