## commercialization

**英音:** _[kəˌmɜːʃəlaɪˈzeɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[kəˌmɜːrʃələˈzeɪʃn]_

**译义:** *n.商业化；商品化*

### 短语

+ **commercialization process:** _商业化进程_

+ **accelerate commercialization:** _加速商业化_

+ **commercialization strategy:** _商业化战略_

+ **successful commercialization:** _成功的商业化_

+ **prevent commercialization:** _防止商业化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The commercialization of space travel is becoming a reality.**

**中文翻译:** 太空旅行的商业化正在成为现实。

**单词释义:** 商业化

#### 例句2

> **The commercialization of this new technology has brought great
profits to the company.**

**中文翻译:** 这项新技术的商业化给公司带来了巨大的利润。

**单词释义:** 商业化

#### 例句3

> **We need to be cautious about the excessive commercialization of
cultural heritage.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要对文化遗产的过度商业化保持谨慎。

**单词释义:** 商业化

### 背诵小故事

> A young entrepreneur had a brilliant idea. He worked hard to turn it
into a product. With marketing and sales efforts, his idea achieved
commercialization and became popular in the market, making him
successful.

**一位年轻的企业家有一个绝妙的想法。他努力将其转化为产品。通过营销和销售的努力，他的想法实现了商业化，并在市场上大受欢迎，使他获得成功。**
