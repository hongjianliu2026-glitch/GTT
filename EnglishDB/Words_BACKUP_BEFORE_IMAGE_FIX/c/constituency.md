## constituency

**英音:** _[kən\'stɪtjʊənsɪ]_ **美音:**
_[kənˈstɪtʃʊənsɪ]_

**译义:** *n.(选区的)选民,(一批)顾客,支持者,赞助者*

### 短语

+ **constituency service:** _选区服务_

+ **electoral constituency:** _选区；选举区_

+ **constituency boundaries:** _选区边界_

+ **constituency opinion:** _选区意见_

+ **constituency meeting:** _选区会议_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The politician visited several constituencies to listen to the
concerns of the voters.**

**中文翻译:** 这位政治家访问了几个选区，倾听选民的关切。

**单词释义:** 选区

#### 例句2

> **The constituency is mainly composed of middle-class families.**

**中文翻译:** 这个选区主要由中产阶级家庭组成。

**单词释义:** 选区

#### 例句3

> **The candidate won a large majority in his constituency.**

**中文翻译:** 这位候选人在他的选区赢得了绝大多数选票。

**单词释义:** 选区

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, the local mayor was worried about pleasing the
constituency. The constituency included farmers, workers, and
businesspeople. He needed to understand their needs to win their
support. So, \'constituency\' is the group of people a politician
represents or serves.

**在一个小镇上，当地的市长担心不能取悦选区民众。这个选区包括农民、工人和商人。他需要了解他们的需求以赢得支持。所以，\'constituency\'就是政客所代表或服务的人群。**
