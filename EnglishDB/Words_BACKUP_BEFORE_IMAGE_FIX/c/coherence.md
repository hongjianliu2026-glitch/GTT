## coherence

**英音:** _[kə(ʊ)\'hɪər(ə)ns; kə(ʊ)\'hɪərəns]_ **美音:**
_[ko\'hɪrəns]_

**译义:** *n.一致*

### 短语

+ **semantic coherence:** _语义连贯性_

+ **textual coherence:** _篇章连贯性_

+ **coherence theory:** _连贯论_

+ **coherence length:** _相干长度_

+ **coherence time:** _相干时间_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The coherence of his argument was impressive.**

**中文翻译:** 他的论点的连贯性令人印象深刻。

**单词释义:** 连贯性

#### 例句2

> **The novel lacks coherence and is hard to follow.**

**中文翻译:** 小说缺乏连贯性，难以理解。

**单词释义:** 连贯性

#### 例句3

> **We need to ensure the coherence of our marketing strategy.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要确保营销策略的一致性。

**单词释义:** 连贯性

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was solving a mystery. He found various clues, but they
seemed scattered at first. However, as he kept digging, he discovered
the coherence among them and finally solved the case. Coherence means
the quality of being logical and consistent.
