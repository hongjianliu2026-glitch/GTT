## conceptualization

**英音:** _[kən\'septjʊəlaɪ\'zeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[kən\'septʃuːələ\'zeɪʃən]_

**译义:** *n.化为概念,概念化*

### 短语

+ **conceptualization process:** _概念化过程_

+ **complex conceptualization:** _复杂的概念化_

+ **abstract conceptualization:** _抽象的概念化_

+ **creative conceptualization:** _创造性概念化_

+ **systematic conceptualization:** _系统的概念化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The conceptualization of this project was very complex and
required a lot of brainpower.**

**中文翻译:** 这个项目的概念化非常复杂，需要大量的脑力。

**单词释义:** 概念化；构思

#### 例句2

> **His conceptualization of the problem was unique and
insightful.**

**中文翻译:** 他对这个问题的概念化是独特且有深刻见解的。

**单词释义:** 概念化；构想

#### 例句3

> **The conceptualization process of the new theory took several
years of research.**

**中文翻译:** 新理论的概念化过程花了好几年的研究。

**单词释义:** 概念形成；概念化

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist had many ideas floating in his mind. He worked hard to
bring them together and form a clear understanding, which was the
process of conceptualization. Finally, he made a great discovery!

**一位科学家脑海中有许多想法在飘荡。他努力将它们整合起来形成清晰的理解，这就是概念化的过程。最终，他有了重大发现！**
