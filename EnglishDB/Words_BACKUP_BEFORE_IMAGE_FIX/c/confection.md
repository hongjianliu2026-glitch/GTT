## confection

**英音:** _[kən\'fekʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[kən\'fɛkʃən]_

**译义:** *n.糖果,蜜饯,调制,[药]糖膏(剂),精制工艺品*

### 短语

+ **confectionery shop:** _糖果店_

+ **chocolate confection:** _巧克力糖果_

+ **fruit confection:** _水果糖_

+ **elaborate confection:** _精心制作的甜食_

+ **traditional confection:** _传统的糖果_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The confection she made was a chocolate cake.**

**中文翻译:** 她做的甜点是巧克力蛋糕。

**单词释义:** 甜点

#### 例句2

> **The store sells a variety of confections.**

**中文翻译:** 这家商店出售各种糖果。

**单词释义:** 甜食

#### 例句3

> **The confection on the plate looked too good to eat.**

**中文翻译:** 盘子里的甜点看上去好吃得让人舍不得吃。

**单词释义:** 点心

### 背诵小故事

> A little girl went to a candy store. She saw many beautiful
confections, like colorful candies and sweet cakes. She was so happy and
couldn\'t wait to taste them. Confection means a sweet food, especially
a candy or cake.
