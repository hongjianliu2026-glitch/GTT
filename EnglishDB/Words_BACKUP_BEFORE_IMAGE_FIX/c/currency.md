## currency

**英音:** _[\'kʌr(ə)nsɪ]_ **美音:** _[ˈkɜrənsi]_

**译义:** *n.流通*

### 短语

+ **foreign currency:** _外币_

+ **domestic currency:** _本国货币_

+ **currency exchange:** _货币兑换_

+ **currency devaluation:** _货币贬值_

+ **currency appreciation:** _货币升值_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The currency of this country is very stable.**

**中文翻译:** 这个国家的货币非常稳定。

**单词释义:** 货币

#### 例句2

> **The dollar is a strong currency in the international market.**

**中文翻译:** 美元在国际市场上是一种强势货币。

**单词释义:** 货币

#### 例句3

> **The exchange rate of the local currency has fluctuated
recently.**

**中文翻译:** 当地货币的汇率最近波动了。

**单词释义:** 货币

### 背诵小故事

> A traveler was in a strange country. He needed to buy things but had
trouble understanding the local currency. Finally, he learned that
currency is like a key to unlock purchases and make transactions.

**一位旅行者在一个陌生的国家。他需要买东西，但难以理解当地的货币。最后，他明白了货币就像一把钥匙，能解锁购买行为并完成交易。**
