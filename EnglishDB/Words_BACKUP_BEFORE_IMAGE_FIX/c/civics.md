> A group of students were confused about civics. But one day, they
visited a courthouse and saw a fair trial. They understood that civics
is about how our society works and our rights and duties.

**一群学生对公民学感到困惑。但有一天，他们参观了法院并看到了一场公平的审判。他们明白了公民学是关于我们的社会如何运作以及我们的权利和义务。**
