## high-ranking

**英音:** _[ˌhaɪ ˈræŋkɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[ˌhaɪ ˈræŋkɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj. 高级别的；职位高的*

### 短语

+ **high-ranking official:** _高级官员_

+ **high-ranking position:** _高级职位_

+ **high-ranking executive:** _高级主管_

+ **high-ranking officer:** _高级军官_

+ **high-ranking diplomat:** _高级外交官_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The high-ranking official attended the important meeting.**

**中文翻译:** 这位高级官员参加了重要会议。

**单词释义:** 高级的；地位高的

#### 例句2

> **She is a high-ranking executive in the company.**

**中文翻译:** 她是公司里的高级主管。

**单词释义:** 职位高的；高层的

#### 例句3

> **The high-ranking military officers were discussing the
strategy.**

**中文翻译:** 高级军官们正在讨论战略。

**单词释义:** 高级别的；军衔高的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a big company, there was a high-ranking
official. Everyone respected him. He had a lot of power and made
important decisions. His position was high above others, just like the
word \'high-ranking\' means having a high and important position.

**从前，在一家大公司里，有一位高级官员。每个人都尊敬他。他权力很大，做重要决定。他的职位高高在上，正如'high-ranking'这个词意味着拥有高且重要的职位。**
