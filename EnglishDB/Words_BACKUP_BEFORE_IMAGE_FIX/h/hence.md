## hence

**英音:** _[hens]_ **美音:** _[hɛns]_

**译义:** *adv.因此,从此*

### 短语

+ **henceforth:** _从今以后；自此以后_

+ **henceforward:** _今后；从今以后_

+ **and hence:** _因此；所以_

+ **hence why:** _这就是为什么_

+ **hence come:** _由此而来_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The weather was bad, hence we stayed at home.**

**中文翻译:** 天气不好，所以我们呆在家里。

**单词释义:** 所以；因此

#### 例句2

> **He is very busy, hence he can\'t come to the party.**

**中文翻译:** 他很忙，因此不能来参加聚会。

**单词释义:** 因此；所以

#### 例句3

> **She was ill for a long time; hence, she fell behind in her
studies.**

**中文翻译:** 她病了很长时间；因此，她在学习上落后了。

**单词释义:** 因此；由此

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was on a long journey. He reached a crossroad and saw
a sign that said, \'Go right hence to the castle.\' So, he followed the
direction and finally reached his destination. Hence means \'therefore\'
or \'for this reason\'.
