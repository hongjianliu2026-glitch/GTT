## historiography

**英音:** _[hɪ,stɔːrɪ\'ɒgrəfɪ; -,stɒrɪ-]_ **美音:**
_[hɪ,stɔrɪ\'ɑɡrəfi]_

**译义:** *n.编史,历史之编纂*

### 短语

+ **ancient historiography:** _古代史学_

+ **modern historiography:** _现代史学_

+ **historiography research:** _史学研究_

+ **traditional historiography:** _传统史学_

+ **critical historiography:** _批判性史学_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The field of historiography has undergone significant changes in
recent decades.**

**中文翻译:** 史学领域在近几十年发生了重大变化。

**单词释义:** 史学，编史工作

#### 例句2

> **Her research focuses on the historiography of ancient
civilizations.**

**中文翻译:** 她的研究侧重于古代文明的史学。

**单词释义:** 历史编纂学

#### 例句3

> **The book provides a comprehensive overview of modern
historiography.**

**中文翻译:** 这本书对现代史学做了全面的概述。

**单词释义:** 历史学的写作

### 背诵小故事

> A historian named Tom was passionate about historiography. He spent
years studying and documenting the past, always striving to present
accurate and detailed accounts. His work became a model in the field of
historiography.

**一位名叫汤姆的历史学家对编史工作充满热情。他花费多年研究和记录过去，总是努力呈现准确和详细的叙述。他的工作成为了编史学领域的典范。**
