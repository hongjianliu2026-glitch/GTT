## handlebar

**英音:** _[\'hænd(ə)lbɑː]_ **美音:** _[\'hændlbɑr]_

**译义:** *n.手把,八字胡*

### 短语

+ **handlebar grip:** _车把把手_

+ **bicycle handlebar:** _自行车车把_

+ **motorcycle handlebar:** _摩托车车把_

+ **adjust the handlebar:** _调整车把_

+ **hold the handlebar firmly:** _紧紧握住车把_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The handlebar of the bicycle was loose and needed tightening.**

**中文翻译:** 自行车的把手松了，需要拧紧。

**单词释义:** 自行车的把手

#### 例句2

> **He gripped the handlebar tightly as he rode down the steep
hill.**

**中文翻译:** 他骑下陡峭的山坡时紧紧握住车把。

**单词释义:** 车把

#### 例句3

> **The motorcycle\'s handlebar was decorated with chrome
accents.**

**中文翻译:** 摩托车的把手用镀铬装饰。

**单词释义:** 摩托车的把手

### 背诵小故事

like the normal ones. The unique handlebars made his ride very special.
