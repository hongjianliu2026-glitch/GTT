> One day, Mary was in a hurry to go shopping. She forgot her handbag
at home. When she realized it, she rushed back. In the handbag were her
keys and wallet. Without it, she couldn\'t buy what she wanted.

**有一天，玛丽匆忙去购物。她把她的手提包忘在家里了。当她意识到的时候，她急忙跑回去。手提包里有她的钥匙和钱包。没有它，她就买不了想要的东西。**
