## headlight

**英音:** _[\'hedlaɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'hɛdlaɪt]_

**译义:** *n.前灯,桅灯*

### 短语

+ **headlight beam:** _前灯光束_

+ **headlight cover:** _头灯罩_

+ **headlight assembly:** _前灯总成_

+ **headlight switch:** _前灯开关_

+ **headlight reflector:** _前灯反射器_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The headlight of the car was broken in the accident.**

**中文翻译:** 这辆车的前灯在事故中坏了。

**单词释义:** 汽车的前灯

#### 例句2

> **He turned on the headlight to see the road clearly at night.**

**中文翻译:** 他在晚上打开前灯以便看清道路。

**单词释义:** 前灯

#### 例句3

> **The new model of the motorcycle has very bright headlights.**

**中文翻译:** 这款新的摩托车模型有非常亮的前灯。

**单词释义:** 前灯

### 背诵小故事

> One night, I was walking in the dark. Suddenly, a car came with its
headlight shining bright, guiding its way. The strong beam from the
headlight helped me see clearly and avoid danger. So, remember,
headlight is the light on a vehicle\'s front!

**一天晚上，我在黑暗中行走。突然，一辆车开过来，它的前灯亮堂堂的，照亮了道路。前灯强烈的光束帮助我看清并避开危险。所以，记住，headlight
就是车辆前面的灯！**
