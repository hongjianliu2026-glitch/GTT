## harassment

**英音:** _[\'hærəsm(ə)nt; hə\'ræsm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[hə\'ræsmənt]_

**译义:** *n.折磨*

### 短语

+ **sexual harassment:** _性骚扰_

+ **workplace harassment:** _工作场所骚扰_

+ **verbal harassment:** _言语骚扰_

+ **psychological harassment:** _心理骚扰_

+ **cyber harassment:** _网络骚扰_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She filed a complaint against the harassment at work.**

**中文翻译:** 她对工作中的骚扰提出了投诉。

**单词释义:** 骚扰

#### 例句2

> **The athlete suffered from constant harassment by the
paparazzi.**

**中文翻译:** 这位运动员经常受到狗仔队的骚扰。

**单词释义:** 骚扰

#### 例句3

> **The company has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any
kind.**

**中文翻译:** 公司对任何形式的骚扰采取零容忍政策。

**单词释义:** 骚扰

### 背诵小故事

> A mouse was constantly being bothered by a cat\'s harassment. The
mouse had no peace, always running away. Just like people, being
harassed makes life difficult. Remember, harassment means disturbing or
annoying someone repeatedly.

**一只老鼠不断受到猫的骚扰。老鼠不得安宁，总是逃跑。就像人一样，被骚扰会让生活变得艰难。记住，harassment
意思是不断地打扰或烦扰某人。**
