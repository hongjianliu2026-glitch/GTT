## hindrance

**英音:** _[\'hɪndr(ə)ns]_ **美音:** _[\'hɪndrəns]_

**译义:** *n.妨碍,障碍*

### 短语

+ **be a hindrance to:** _对\...\...是个阻碍_

+ **without hindrance:** _毫无阻碍地_

+ **put a hindrance in the way of:** _妨碍，阻碍_

+ **overcome hindrances:** _克服障碍_

+ **remove hindrances:** _排除障碍_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The bad weather was a hindrance to our travel plans.**

**中文翻译:** 恶劣的天气阻碍了我们的旅行计划。

**单词释义:** 阻碍；妨碍

#### 例句2

> **His lack of experience proved to be a hindrance in the new
job.**

**中文翻译:** 事实证明，他缺乏经验是新工作的一个障碍。

**单词释义:** 妨碍；阻碍

#### 例句3

> **Traffic congestion is a major hindrance to the city\'s
development.**

**中文翻译:** 交通拥堵是城市发展的一大障碍。

**单词释义:** 阻碍；障碍

### 背诵小故事

> A little rabbit was running in the forest. But there were many fallen
trees, which were hindrances to its speed. It had to jump over them to
continue its journey. The fallen trees here represent hindrance, meaning
something that slows or stops progress.

**一只小兔子在森林里奔跑。但有很多倒下的树木，这对它的速度造成了阻碍。它不得不跳过它们继续前行。这里倒下的树木就代表着
hindrance，意思是阻碍进展的事物。**
