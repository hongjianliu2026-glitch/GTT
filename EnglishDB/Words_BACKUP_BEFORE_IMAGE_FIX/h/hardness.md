## hardness

**英音:** _[\'hɑːdnəs]_ **美音:** _[\'hɑrdnɪs]_

**译义:** *n.硬,硬度,艰难,难度*

### 短语

+ **degree of hardness:** _硬度程度_

+ **surface hardness:** _表面硬度_

+ **rock hardness:** _岩石硬度_

+ **hardness test:** _硬度测试_

+ **material hardness:** _材料硬度_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The hardness of the diamond makes it extremely durable.**

**中文翻译:** 钻石的硬度使其极其耐用。

**单词释义:** 硬度

#### 例句2

> **We need to test the hardness of the metal before using it in
construction.**

**中文翻译:** 在建筑中使用金属之前，我们需要测试金属的硬度。

**单词释义:** 硬度

#### 例句3

> **The hardness of the rock prevented us from easily drilling
through it.**

**中文翻译:** 岩石的硬度使我们无法轻松地钻穿它。

**单词释义:** 硬度

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a brave knight was trying to break a stone. But the
stone showed great hardness and resisted all his efforts. Finally, the
knight understood that hardness means the quality of being difficult to
break or change.

**从前，有一位勇敢的骑士试图打破一块石头。但这块石头展现出极大的硬度，抵抗了他所有的努力。最后，骑士明白了
hardness 指的是难以打破或改变的性质。**
