### 背诵小故事

> A young artist traveled to Florence and was deeply inspired by the
city\'s beauty. He decided to call his new masterpiece \'Florentine\' to
honor the place. Later, the word was used to describe anything related
to Florence or having its style.

**一位年轻的艺术家去了佛罗伦萨，被这座城市的美深深打动。他决定将自己的新杰作命名为"Florentine"以纪念这个地方。后来，这个词就被用来形容与佛罗伦萨有关或具有其风格的任何事物。**
