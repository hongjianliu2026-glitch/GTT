## fertilizer

**英音:** _[\'fɜːtɪlaɪzə]_ **美音:** _[\'fɝtəlaɪzɚ]_

**译义:** *n.肥料(尤指化学肥料),[动]受精媒介物*

### 短语

+ **chemical fertilizer:** _化肥_

+ **organic fertilizer:** _有机肥料_

+ **liquid fertilizer:** _液体肥料_

+ **slow-release fertilizer:** _缓释肥料_

+ **nitrogen fertilizer:** _氮肥_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The farmer spread fertilizer on the fields to increase the
yield.**

**中文翻译:** 农民在田里施肥以增加产量。

**单词释义:** 肥料

#### 例句2

> **This new type of fertilizer has shown remarkable results in
improving soil quality.**

**中文翻译:** 这种新型肥料在改善土壤质量方面显示出显着的效果。

**单词释义:** 肥料

#### 例句3

> **The use of excessive fertilizer can cause environmental
pollution.**

**中文翻译:** 过量使用化肥会造成环境污染。

**单词释义:** 肥料

### 背诵小故事

> A farmer was very worried because his crops weren\'t growing well.
Then he discovered the magic of fertilizer. With it, the crops grew
strong and healthy. Fertilizer became his secret weapon for a great
harvest.

**一位农民非常担心，因为他的庄稼长得不好。然后他发现了肥料的神奇之处。有了它，庄稼长得又壮又好。肥料成了他丰收的秘密武器。**
