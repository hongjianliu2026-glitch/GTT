## fluctuation

**英音:** _[,flʌktʃʊ\'eɪʃ(ə)n; -tjʊ-]_ **美音:**
_[,flʌktʃʊ\'eʃən]_

**译义:** *n.波动,起伏*

### 短语

+ **price fluctuation:** _价格波动_

+ **market fluctuation:** _市场波动_

+ **fluctuation range:** _波动范围_

+ **temperature fluctuation:** _温度波动_

+ **exchange rate fluctuation:** _汇率波动_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The price of gold shows significant fluctuations.**

**中文翻译:** 黄金的价格呈现出显著的波动。

**单词释义:** 波动；起伏

#### 例句2

> **There have been fluctuations in the stock market recently.**

**中文翻译:** 最近股市有波动。

**单词释义:** 波动；变动

#### 例句3

> **The fluctuations in temperature can affect your health.**

**中文翻译:** 温度的波动会影响你的健康。

**单词释义:** 波动；变化

### 背诵小故事

> A fish was swimming in a river. The water level had frequent
fluctuations. Sometimes it was deep, and sometimes it was shallow. The
fish had to adapt to these changes, just like we need to understand the
meaning of \'fluctuation\'.

**一条鱼在河里游。水位频繁波动。有时深，有时浅。鱼不得不适应这些变化，就像我们需要理解\'fluctuation\'（波动；起伏）的意思一样。**
