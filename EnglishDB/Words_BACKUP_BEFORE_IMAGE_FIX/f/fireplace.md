## fireplace

**英音:** _[\'faɪəpleɪs]_ **美音:** _[\'faɪɚples]_

**译义:** *n.壁炉*

### 短语

+ **wood-burning fireplace:** _烧木柴的壁炉_

+ **gas fireplace:** _燃气壁炉_

+ **traditional fireplace:** _传统的壁炉_

+ **decorative fireplace:** _装饰性的壁炉_

+ **modern fireplace:** _现代的壁炉_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The old house had a beautiful fireplace in the living room.**

**中文翻译:** 这座老房子的客厅里有一个漂亮的壁炉。

**单词释义:** 壁炉

#### 例句2

> **We sat by the fireplace and enjoyed the warmth of the fire.**

**中文翻译:** 我们坐在壁炉旁享受着火的温暖。

**单词释义:** 壁炉

#### 例句3

> **The fireplace was decorated with Christmas stockings during the
holiday season.**

**中文翻译:** 在节日期间，壁炉上装饰着圣诞袜。

**单词释义:** 壁炉

### 背诵小故事

> One cold winter night, I was shivering. Then I found a warm and cozy
corner with a fireplace. The fire was dancing, giving off heat and
