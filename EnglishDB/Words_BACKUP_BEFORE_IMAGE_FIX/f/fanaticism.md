## fanaticism

**英音:** _[fə\'nætɪsɪz(ə)m]_ **美音:**
_[fə\'nætɪsɪzəm]_

**译义:** *n.狂热,盲信*

### 短语

+ **religious fanaticism:** _宗教狂热_

+ **political fanaticism:** _政治狂热_

+ **fanaticism for sports:** _对体育的狂热_

+ **fanaticism about music:** _对音乐的狂热_

+ **uncontrolled fanaticism:** _失控的狂热_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His fanaticism for football led him to travel across the country
to watch every game.**

**中文翻译:** 他对足球的狂热促使他走遍全国观看每一场比赛。

**单词释义:** 狂热

#### 例句2

> **The fanaticism of the religious group caused concern among the
local community.**

**中文翻译:** 该宗教团体的狂热引起了当地社区的担忧。

**单词释义:** 狂热

#### 例句3

> **She was criticized for her fanaticism in pursuing extreme
diets.**

**中文翻译:** 她因狂热地追求极端饮食而受到批评。

**单词释义:** 狂热

### 背诵小故事

> A group of people were obsessed with finding a lost treasure. Their
extreme enthusiasm and insistence, ignoring all difficulties, was called
fanaticism. They were so fanatical that they forgot everything else.

**一群人痴迷于寻找一件丢失的宝藏。他们那种极度的热情和坚持，不顾所有困难，被称为狂热。他们是如此的狂热，以至于忘记了其他一切。**
