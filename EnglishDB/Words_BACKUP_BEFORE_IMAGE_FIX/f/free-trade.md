### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a land full of merchants, there was no barrier
for trade. Goods could move freely. This situation was called
\'free-trade\', meaning trade without restrictions or excessive
regulations.

**从前，在一个充满商人的国度，贸易没有障碍。货物可以自由流动。这种情况被称为"自由贸易"，意思是没有限制或过多规定的贸易。**
