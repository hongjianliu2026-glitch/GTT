## first-round

**英音:** _[fɜːrst raʊnd]_ **美音:** _[fɜːrst raʊnd]_

**译义:** *n. 第一轮；首轮；初赛*

### 短语

+ **first-round match:** _第一轮比赛_

+ **first-round victory:** _第一轮胜利_

+ **first-round leader:** _第一轮领先者_

+ **first-round loser:** _第一轮失败者_

+ **first-round exit:** _第一轮出局_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The team won the first-round match easily.**

**中文翻译:** 这个团队轻松赢得了第一轮比赛。

**单词释义:** 第一轮的

#### 例句2

> **She was eliminated in the first-round of the competition.**

**中文翻译:** 她在比赛的第一轮就被淘汰了。

**单词释义:** 第一轮的

#### 例句3

> **The first-round interview is just a screening process.**

**中文翻译:** 第一轮面试只是一个筛选过程。

**单词释义:** 第一轮的

### 背诵小故事

> In a sports competition, the first-round was like a gate to the next
stage. Tom was nervous but gave his best. He knew passing the
first-round was crucial for his dream of winning the championship.
