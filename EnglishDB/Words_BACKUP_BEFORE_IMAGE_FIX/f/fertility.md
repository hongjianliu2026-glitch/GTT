## fertility

**英音:** _[fə\'tɪlɪtɪ; fɜː\'tɪlɪtɪ]_ **美音:**
_[fɚ\'tɪləti]_

**译义:** *n.肥沃,丰产,多产,人口生产,生产力*

### 短语

+ **fertility rate:** _生育率_

+ **fertility treatment:** _生育治疗_

+ **soil fertility:** _土壤肥力_

+ **female fertility:** _女性生育能力_

+ **improve fertility:** _提高生育能力；改善肥力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The fertility of the soil in this region is very high, making it
ideal for agriculture.**

**中文翻译:** 该地区的土壤肥力非常高，非常适合农业。

**单词释义:** 肥力

#### 例句2

> **She consulted a specialist to improve her fertility and increase
the chances of having a baby.**

**中文翻译:** 她咨询了专家，以提高她的生育能力并增加生育的机会。

**单词释义:** 生育能力

#### 例句3

> **The study examines the relationship between environmental factors
and fertility rates.**

**中文翻译:** 该研究探讨了环境因素与生育率之间的关系。

**单词释义:** 生育率

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a farmer was worried about the fertility of his
land. But after using special fertilizers, the crops grew well.
Fertility means the ability to produce and support growth, like fertile
land for good harvests.

**从前，有个农民担心他土地的肥力。但使用特殊肥料后，庄稼长得很好。fertility
意思是产生和支持生长的能力，就像肥沃的土地能有好收成。**
