## quantify

**英音:** _[\'kwɒntɪfaɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'kwɑntɪfaɪ]_

**译义:** *vt.确定数量v.量化*

### 短语

+ **quantify the damage:** _量化损害_

+ **quantify the risk:** _量化风险_

+ **quantify the impact:** _量化影响_

+ **quantify the results:** _量化结果_

+ **quantify the value:** _量化价值_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **It\'s difficult to quantify the exact impact of the new
policy.**

**中文翻译:** 很难量化新政策的确切影响。

**单词释义:** 量化

#### 例句2

> **We need to quantify the amount of resources required for the
project.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要量化该项目所需的资源量。

**单词释义:** 量化

#### 例句3

> **Scientists are trying to quantify the damage caused by
pollution.**

**中文翻译:** 科学家们正试图量化污染造成的损害。

**单词释义:** 量化

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was trying to quantify the number of stars in the galaxy.
He used advanced tools and complex math. Finally, he got a specific
number, showing that he successfully quantified the stars.

**一位科学家试图量化星系中的恒星数量。他使用了先进的工具和复杂的数学。最终，他得到了一个具体的数字，表明他成功地量化了恒星。**
