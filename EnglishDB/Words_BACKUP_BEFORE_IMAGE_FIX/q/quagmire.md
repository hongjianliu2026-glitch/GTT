## quagmire

**英音:** _[ˈkwæɡmaɪə(r)]_ **美音:** _[ˈkwæɡmaɪər]_

**译义:** *n.沼泽,湿地*

### 短语

+ **boggy quagmire:** _沼泽般的泥潭_

+ **deep quagmire:** _深深的泥潭_

+ **entrap in a quagmire:** _陷入泥潭_

+ **quagmire of debt:** _债务泥潭_

+ **political quagmire:** _政治泥潭_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company found itself in a quagmire of financial
difficulties.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司发现自己陷入了财务困难的泥潭。

**单词释义:** 困境；沼泽

#### 例句2

> **The project has become a quagmire, with no clear end in
sight.**

**中文翻译:** 该项目已陷入泥潭，看不到明确的结局。

**单词释义:** 难以摆脱的困境

#### 例句3

> **He tried to escape the quagmire of his failed marriage.**

**中文翻译:** 他试图摆脱失败婚姻的泥潭。

**单词释义:** 艰难、棘手的状况

### 背诵小故事

> A traveler was lost in the forest and stepped into a quagmire. He
struggled hard but couldn\'t get out. Luckily, a local guide found and
rescued him. Remember, quagmire means a difficult or dangerous
