## arrange

**英音:** _[əˈreɪndʒ]_ **美音:** _[ə\'reɪn(d)ʒ]_

**译义:** *v.安排,排列,协商*

### 短语

+ **arrange for:** _安排；准备；为...做准备_

+ **arrange with:** _与...商定；与...安排_

+ **arrange sth. in order:** _把某物整理好；将某物按顺序排列_

+ **arrange to do sth.:** _安排做某事_

+ **arrange a meeting:** _安排会议_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I\'ll arrange a meeting with the clients next week.**

**中文翻译:** 我将安排下周与客户会面。

**单词释义:** 安排

#### 例句2

> **She arranged the flowers beautifully in the vase.**

**中文翻译:** 她把花插在花瓶里，布置得很漂亮。

**单词释义:** 布置；排列

#### 例句3

> **Can you arrange for a taxi to pick me up at 6 am?**

**中文翻译:** 你能安排一辆出租车早上 6 点来接我吗？

**单词释义:** 安排；筹备

### 背诵小故事

> A rabbit was trying to arrange a party. It needed to find a nice
place, prepare food, and invite friends. It worked hard to make
everything perfect. Finally, the party was a great success!
