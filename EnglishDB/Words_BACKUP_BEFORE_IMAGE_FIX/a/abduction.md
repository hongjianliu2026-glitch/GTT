## abduction

**英音:** _[əb\'dʌkʃn]_ **美音:** _[æb\'dʌkʃən]_

**译义:** *n.诱拐,诱导*

### 短语

+ **abduction attempt:** _绑架企图_

+ **alien abduction:** _外星人绑架_

+ **abduction case:** _绑架案件_

+ **abduction crime:** _绑架罪行_

+ **prevent abduction:** _防止绑架_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The abduction of the child caused widespread panic in the
community.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子的绑架案在社区引起了广泛的恐慌。

**单词释义:** 绑架

#### 例句2

> **The detective was assigned to investigate the abduction case.**

**中文翻译:** 这位侦探被指派调查这起绑架案。

**单词释义:** 绑架

#### 例句3

> **The movie was about a daring abduction and the subsequent rescue
mission.**

**中文翻译:** 这部电影是关于一次大胆的绑架和随后的营救任务。

**单词释义:** 绑架

### 背诵小故事

> One night, a mysterious figure appeared and performed an abduction. A
girl was taken away from her home. The police later found out that it
was a criminal act of kidnapping. Remember, abduction means kidnapping.

**一天晚上，一个神秘的身影出现并实施了绑架。一个女孩被从家中带走。后来警方发现这是一起绑架的犯罪行为。记住，abduction
意思是绑架。**
