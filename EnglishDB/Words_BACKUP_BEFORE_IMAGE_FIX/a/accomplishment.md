## accomplishment

**英音:** _[ə\'kʌmplɪʃm(ə)nt; ə\'kɒm-]_ **美音:**
_[ə\'kɑmplɪʃmənt]_

**译义:** * ns//n.造诣*

### 短语

+ **great accomplishment:** _巨大的成就_

+ **personal accomplishment:** _个人成就_

+ **sense of accomplishment:** _成就感_

+ **academic accomplishment:** _学术成就_

+ **professional accomplishment:** _职业成就_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The accomplishment of this project required a lot of teamwork and
dedication.**

**中文翻译:** 这个项目的完成需要大量的团队合作和奉献精神。

**单词释义:** 完成；成就

#### 例句2

> **Her greatest accomplishment was winning the championship.**

**中文翻译:** 她最大的成就就是赢得了冠军。

**单词释义:** 成就

#### 例句3

> **The sense of accomplishment after finishing the marathon was
overwhelming.**

**中文翻译:** 完成马拉松后的成就感是巨大的。

**单词释义:** 成就感

### 背诵小故事

> A young artist dreamt of creating a masterpiece. Through months of
hard work and dedication, she finally finished it. This final work was
her great accomplishment. It made her famous and proud.

**一位年轻的艺术家梦想创作一幅杰作。经过数月的努力和奉献，她终于完成了。这幅最终的作品就是她的伟大成就。这让她出名且自豪。**
