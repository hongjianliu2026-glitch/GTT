## absently

**英音:** _[\'æbs(ə)ntlɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'æbsəntli]_

**译义:** *adv.心不在焉地,茫然地*

### 短语

+ **absently minded:** _心不在焉的_

+ **absently stare:** _心不在焉地凝视_

+ **absently listen:** _心不在焉地听_

+ **absently nod:** _心不在焉地点头_

+ **absently wander:** _心不在焉地漫步_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was sitting in the classroom, looking absently out of
the window. His mind was far away, thinking about the exciting adventure
he had during the weekend. He was so lost in his thoughts that he
didn\'t hear the teacher calling his name.

**一天，汤姆坐在教室里，心不在焉地望着窗外。他的思绪飘远，想着周末的那次激动人心的冒险。他如此沉浸在自己的想法中，以至于没听到老师叫他的名字。**
