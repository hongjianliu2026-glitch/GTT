## aristocrat

**英音:** _[\'ærɪstəkræt; ə\'rɪst-]_ **美音:**
_[ə\'rɪstəkræt]_

**译义:** *n.贵族*

### 短语

+ **noble aristocrat:** _高贵的贵族_

+ **ancient aristocrat:** _古代的贵族_

+ **wealthy aristocrat:** _富有的贵族_

+ **powerful aristocrat:** _有权势的贵族_

+ **European aristocrat:** _欧洲贵族_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The aristocrat lived in a grand mansion with servants at his beck
and call.**

**中文翻译:** 这位贵族住在一座宏伟的宅邸里，仆人随叫随到。

**单词释义:** 贵族

#### 例句2

> **She married into an aristocrat family and adapted to their
refined lifestyle.**

**中文翻译:** 她嫁入了一个贵族家庭，适应了他们精致的生活方式。

**单词释义:** 贵族

#### 例句3

> **The aristocrats of the past had great influence over the politics
of the country.**

**中文翻译:** 过去的贵族对国家政治有很大的影响。

**单词释义:** 贵族

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a faraway kingdom, there was a nobleman named
Arthur. He was an aristocrat, always dressed elegantly and attended
grand banquets. His noble status gave him many privileges and
responsibilities.

**从前，在一个遥远的王国里，有一位名叫亚瑟的贵族。他是一位贵族，总是穿着优雅，参加盛大的宴会。他的贵族身份赋予了他许多特权和责任。**
