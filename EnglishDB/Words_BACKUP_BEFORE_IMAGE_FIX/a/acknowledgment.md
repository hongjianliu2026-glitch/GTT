## acknowledgment

**英音:** _[ək\'nɒlɪdʒmənt]_ **美音:**
_[ək\'nɑlɪdʒmənt]_

**译义:** *n.承认,承认书,感谢*

### 短语

+ **acknowledgment letter:** _确认函；致谢信_

+ **acknowledgment of debt:** _债务确认_

+ **acknowledgment slip:** _回执；收条_

+ **express acknowledgment:** _明确承认；表示感谢_

+ **written acknowledgment:** _书面确认；书面承认_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The professor gave an acknowledgment of the student\'s hard
work.**

**中文翻译:** 教授对学生的努力学习表示认可。

**单词释义:** 认可；承认

#### 例句2

> **She received an acknowledgment for her contribution to the
project.**

**中文翻译:** 她因对项目的贡献而收到了致谢。

**单词释义:** 致谢；感谢

#### 例句3

> **His acknowledgment of the mistake was sincere.**

**中文翻译:** 他对错误的承认是真诚的。

**单词释义:** 承认；坦白

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom received a valuable gift. He wrote a letter of
acknowledgment to express his gratitude. The sender felt happy to get
Tom\'s recognition. Acknowledgment means the act of recognizing or
admitting something.
