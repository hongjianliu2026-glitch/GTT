## accompaniment

**英音:** _[ə\'kʌmp(ə)nɪm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[ə\'kʌmpənɪmənt]_

**译义:** *n.陪伴物,伴奏*

### 短语

+ **musical accompaniment:** _音乐伴奏_

+ **vocal accompaniment:** _声乐伴奏_

+ **instrumental accompaniment:** _器乐伴奏_

+ **provide accompaniment:** _提供伴奏_

+ **accompaniment music:** _伴奏音乐_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The piano accompaniment added a beautiful touch to the singer\'s
performance.**

**中文翻译:** 钢琴伴奏为歌手的表演增添了美妙的一笔。

**单词释义:** 伴奏

#### 例句2

> **The salad was served as an accompaniment to the main course.**

**中文翻译:** 沙拉作为主菜的配菜被端上来。

**单词释义:** 配菜；伴随物

#### 例句3

> **The flute accompaniment enhanced the charm of the dance.**

**中文翻译:** 长笛伴奏增强了舞蹈的魅力。

**单词释义:** 伴奏

### 背诵小故事

> A musician was performing on the stage. His wonderful melody had a
great accompaniment, which were the harmonious sounds of various
instruments. The accompaniment made the music more charming and
touching.

**一位音乐家在舞台上表演。他美妙的旋律有很棒的伴奏，那是各种乐器的和谐声音。伴奏让音乐更具魅力和感染力。**
