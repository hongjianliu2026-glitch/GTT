## accompanying

**英音:** _[əˈkʌmpəniɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[əˈkʌmpəniɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.陪伴的,附随的*

### 短语

+ **accompanying document:** _随附文件_

+ **accompanying circumstances:** _伴随情况_

+ **accompanying music:** _伴奏音乐_

+ **accompanying symptoms:** _伴随症状_

+ **accompanying person:** _陪同人员_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The accompanying music added to the charm of the performance.**

**中文翻译:** 伴随的音乐增添了表演的魅力。

**单词释义:** 伴随的

#### 例句2

> **She had all the necessary documents, along with the accompanying
explanations.**

**中文翻译:** 她有所有必要的文件，还有随附的解释。

**单词释义:** 随附的

#### 例句3

> **The doctor provided the patient with the accompanying treatment
plan.**

**中文翻译:** 医生给病人提供了配套的治疗方案。

**单词释义:** 配套的

### 背诵小故事

> A young musician was going to perform on stage. His friend was always
accompanying him during rehearsals, giving support and encouragement.
The friend\'s accompanying presence made the musician feel confident.

**一位年轻的音乐家要上台表演。他的朋友在排练期间总是陪着他，给予支持和鼓励。朋友的陪伴让音乐家感到自信。**
