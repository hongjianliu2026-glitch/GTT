## acupuncture

**英音:** _[\'ækjʊ,pʌŋ(k)tʃə]_ **美音:**
_[\'ækjupʌŋktʃɚ]_

**译义:** *n.针刺疗法v.施行针刺疗法*

### 短语

+ **acupuncture point:** _穴位_

+ **acupuncture therapy:** _针灸疗法_

+ **traditional acupuncture:** _传统针灸_

+ **acupuncture treatment:** _针灸治疗_

+ **modern acupuncture:** _现代针灸_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I decided to try acupuncture to relieve my chronic back pain.**

**中文翻译:** 我决定尝试针灸来缓解我的慢性背痛。

**单词释义:** 针灸

#### 例句2

> **Acupuncture has been practiced in China for thousands of
years.**

**中文翻译:** 针灸在中国已经实践了数千年。

**单词释义:** 针灸

#### 例句3

> **Some people swear by acupuncture as a treatment for
migraines.**

**中文翻译:** 有些人坚信针灸是治疗偏头痛的方法。

**单词释义:** 针灸

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a patient was in great pain. A wise doctor used acupuncture
to solve the problem. The doctor inserted thin needles precisely at
specific points on the patient\'s body, and soon the pain vanished.
Remember, acupuncture is this special treatment!

**有一天，一位病人疼痛难忍。一位睿智的医生使用了针灸来解决问题。医生精准地在病人身体的特定部位插入细针，很快疼痛就消失了。记住，acupuncture
就是这种特殊的治疗方法！**
