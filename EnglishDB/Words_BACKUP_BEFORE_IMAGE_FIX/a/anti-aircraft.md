## anti-aircraft

**英音:** _[ˌæntiˈɛəkræft]_ **美音:** _[ˌæntiˈɛrkræft]_

**译义:** *adj. 防空的，用于防空的；n. 防空武器，高射炮*

### 短语

+ **anti-aircraft gun:** _高射炮_

+ **anti-aircraft missile:** _防空导弹_

+ **anti-aircraft battery:** _防空炮兵连_

+ **anti-aircraft warfare:** _防空作战_

+ **anti-aircraft defense:** _防空防御_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The anti-aircraft guns were positioned around the city to defend
against enemy aircraft.**

**中文翻译:** 防空炮被部署在城市周围以抵御敌机。

**单词释义:** 防空的

#### 例句2

> **The military has developed advanced anti-aircraft missile
systems.**

**中文翻译:** 军方已经开发出先进的防空导弹系统。

**单词释义:** 防空的

#### 例句3

> **The anti-aircraft radar detected the approaching planes.**

**中文翻译:** 防空雷达探测到了接近的飞机。

**单词释义:** 防空的

### 背诵小故事

> In a war, the enemy planes were coming. But our soldiers had the
powerful anti-aircraft guns. They aimed and fired to protect us from the
air attack. Anti-aircraft means designed to stop or fight against
aircraft.

**在一场战争中，敌机来袭。但我们的士兵有强大的防空炮。他们瞄准并开火以保护我们免受空袭。anti-aircraft
意思是防空的；对付飞机的。**
