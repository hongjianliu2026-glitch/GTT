## amplification

_[,æmpləfə\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.扩大*

+ **amplification factor:** _放大系数；放大因数_

+ **power amplification:** _功率放大_

+ **signal amplification:** _信号放大_

+ **amplification stage:** _放大级_
