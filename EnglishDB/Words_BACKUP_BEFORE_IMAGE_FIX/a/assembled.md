## assembled

**英音:** _[əˈsembld]_ **美音:** _[əˈsembld]_

**译义:** *vbl.装配,组合*

### 短语

+ **assembled product:** _装配产品_

+ **assembled line:** _装配线_

+ **assembled components:** _组装部件_

+ **assembled furniture:** _组装家具_

+ **assembled workforce:** _集合劳动力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The workers assembled the machinery in the factory.**

**中文翻译:** 工人们在工厂里组装机器。

**单词释义:** 组装

#### 例句2

> **People assembled in the square to celebrate the festival.**

**中文翻译:** 人们聚集在广场上庆祝节日。

**单词释义:** 聚集

#### 例句3

> **The team assembled for a meeting before the game.**

**中文翻译:** 球队在比赛前集合开会。

**单词释义:** 集合

### 背诵小故事

> A group of robots were commanded to assemble in a room. They moved
quickly and precisely, coming together to form a team. Finally, they
were all assembled successfully.
