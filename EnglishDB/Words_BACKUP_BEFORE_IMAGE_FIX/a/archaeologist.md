## archaeologist

**英音:** _[,ɑːkɪ\'ɒlədʒɪst]_ **美音:**
_[,ɑrkɪ\'ɑlədʒɪst]_

**译义:** *n.考古学家*

### 短语

+ **professional archaeologist:** _专业考古学家_

+ **renowned archaeologist:** _著名考古学家_

+ **experienced archaeologist:** _经验丰富的考古学家_

+ **amateur archaeologist:** _业余考古学家_

+ **prominent archaeologist:** _杰出的考古学家_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The archaeologist carefully examined the ancient ruins.**

**中文翻译:** 考古学家仔细检查了古代遗址。

**单词释义:** 考古学家

#### 例句2

> **She dreams of becoming an archaeologist and uncovering historical
mysteries.**

**中文翻译:** 她梦想成为一名考古学家，揭开历史之谜。

**单词释义:** 考古学家

#### 例句3

> **The renowned archaeologist presented his latest findings at the
conference.**

**中文翻译:** 这位著名考古学家在会议上展示了他的最新发现。

**单词释义:** 考古学家

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was an adventurer named Alice. She was
passionate about old things and spent her days digging and exploring.
Eventually, she became a famous archaeologist, uncovering the secrets of
the past.

**从前，有个叫爱丽丝的冒险家。她热衷于古老的东西，整天挖掘和探索。最终，她成为了著名的考古学家，揭开了过去的秘密。**
