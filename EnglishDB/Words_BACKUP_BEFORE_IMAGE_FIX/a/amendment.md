## amendment

**英音:** _[ə\'men(d)m(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[ə\'mɛndmənt]_

**译义:** *n.改善,改正*

### 短语

+ **constitutional amendment:** _宪法修正案_

+ **proposed amendment:** _提议的修正案_

+ **amendment bill:** _修正案草案_

+ **make an amendment:** _做出修正_

+ **minor amendment:** _微小的修正_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The amendment to the law was passed by a narrow margin.**

**中文翻译:** 该法律修正案以微弱优势获得通过。

**单词释义:** 修正案

#### 例句2

> **She proposed an amendment to the plan to make it more
feasible.**

**中文翻译:** 她提议对该计划进行修改，使其更加可行。

**单词释义:** 修正；修改

#### 例句3

> **The constitution allows for amendments to be made under certain
circumstances.**

**中文翻译:** 宪法允许在某些情况下进行修改。

**单词释义:** 修正；修订

### 背诵小故事

> A king was always making mistakes. So the wise men suggested
\'amendment\'. The king finally changed and made the country better.
Amendment means a change for the better.

**一位国王总是犯错。所以智者们建议"修正"。国王最终改变了，让国家变得更好。Amendment
意思是改进，修正。**
