## autonomy

**英音:** _[ɔː\'tɒnəmɪ]_ **美音:** _[ɔ\'tɑnəmi]_

**译义:** *n.自治*

### 短语

+ **personal autonomy:** _个人自主权_

+ **local autonomy:** _地方自治_

+ **academic autonomy:** _学术自治_

+ **autonomy of will:** _意志自主_

+ **regional autonomy:** _区域自治_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The school is known for giving students a great deal of autonomy
in their studies.**

**中文翻译:** 这所学校以在学生的学习中给予他们很大的自主权而闻名。

**单词释义:** 自主权

#### 例句2

> **The region demands greater autonomy from the central
government.**

**中文翻译:** 该地区要求从中央政府获得更大的自治权。

**单词释义:** 自治权

#### 例句3

> **Employees are granted a certain level of autonomy to make
decisions.**

**中文翻译:** 员工被授予一定程度的自主决定权。

**单词释义:** 自主决定权

### 背诵小故事

> A young entrepreneur desired autonomy. He left his stable job to
start his own business. With hard work and determination, he achieved
the independence and control he craved. Autonomy means independence and
self-government.

**一位年轻的企业家渴望自主。他辞去稳定的工作去创业。通过努力和决心，他获得了渴望的独立和掌控权。Autonomy
意思是独立自主、自治。**
