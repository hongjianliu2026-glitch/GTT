## apprehension

**英音:** _[æprɪ\'henʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,æprɪ\'hɛnʃən]_

**译义:** *n.理解,忧惧,拘捕*

### 短语

+ **apprehension about:** _对\...\...的忧虑_

+ **apprehension of danger:** _对危险的担忧_

+ **feel apprehension:** _感到忧虑_

+ **overcome apprehension:** _克服忧虑_

+ **deep apprehension:** _深深的忧虑_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His apprehension about the exam was evident from his worried
expression.**

**中文翻译:** 他对考试的忧虑从他担忧的表情中显而易见。

**单词释义:** 忧虑；担心

#### 例句2

> **The police officer had an apprehension of the thief as he was
leaving the store.**

**中文翻译:** 警察在小偷离开商店时将其拘捕。

**单词释义:** 拘捕；逮捕

#### 例句3

> **She showed great apprehension when she heard the news of the
accident.**

**中文翻译:** 当她听到事故的消息时，表现出极大的恐惧。

**单词释义:** 恐惧；害怕

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught by the police. His face showed apprehension as he
knew he would face punishment. He was worried and afraid, which is
exactly what \'apprehension\' means - anxiety or fear about something
unpleasant that might happen.

**一个小偷被警察抓住了。他的脸上露出了忧虑，因为他知道自己将面临惩罚。他感到担心和害怕，这正是\'apprehension\'这个词的意思------对可能发生的不愉快的事情感到焦虑或害怕。**
