## appropriately

**英音:** _[əˈprəʊpriətli]_ **美音:** _[ə\'proprɪ,etlɪ]_

**译义:** *adv.适当地*

### 短语

+ **appropriately dressed:** _穿着得体_

+ **appropriately sized:** _尺寸合适_

+ **appropriately timed:** _时机恰当_

+ **appropriately priced:** _价格合理_

+ **appropriately located:** _位置恰当_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The dress was appropriately styled for the formal occasion.**

**中文翻译:** 这件连衣裙的款式适合正式场合。

**单词释义:** 适当地；合适地

#### 例句2

> **He responded appropriately to the difficult question.**

**中文翻译:** 他对这个难题做出了恰当的回应。

**单词释义:** 恰当地；合适地

#### 例句3

> **The funds were appropriately allocated to different projects.**

**中文翻译:** 资金被适当地分配到不同的项目中。

**单词释义:** 适当地；恰当地

### 背诵小故事

> A little monkey wanted to dress appropriately for a party. It chose
the right clothes and shoes and everyone said it looked great.
\'Appropriately\' means in a suitable and proper way, just like the
monkey\'s choice.

**一只小猴子想要为一个聚会穿着得体。它选对了衣服和鞋子，大家都说它看起来很棒。\'appropriately\'意思是以合适恰当的方式，就像猴子的选择。**
