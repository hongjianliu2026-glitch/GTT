## authoritative

**英音:** _[ɔː\'θɒrɪtətɪv; -,teɪtɪv]_ **美音:**
_[ə\'θɔrətetɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.权威的,有权威的,命令的*

### 短语

+ **authoritative source:** _权威来源_

+ **authoritative statement:** _权威声明_

+ **authoritative figure:** _权威人物_

+ **authoritative information:** _权威信息_

+ **authoritative opinion:** _权威意见_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The professor\'s lecture was authoritative and informative.**

**中文翻译:** 这位教授的讲座具有权威性且内容丰富。

**单词释义:** 权威的

#### 例句2

> **The authoritative voice on the radio commanded everyone to stay
calm.**

**中文翻译:** 收音机里那个权威的声音命令大家保持冷静。

**单词释义:** 权威性的

#### 例句3

> **She has an authoritative manner that makes people listen to
her.**

**中文翻译:** 她有一种权威性的态度，让人愿意听她的。

**单词释义:** 有权威的

### 背诵小故事

> A king is always authoritative. He has the power to make decisions
and everyone must follow. Just like the word \'authoritative\', meaning
having the power and being commanding.

**一位国王总是有权威的。他有权做决定，所有人都必须听从。就像单词\'authoritative\'，意思是有权力和权威性的。**
