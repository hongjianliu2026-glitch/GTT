## wine

**英音:** _[waɪn]_ **美音:** _[waɪn]_

**译义:** *n.葡萄酒,酒*

### 短语

+ **red wine:** _红葡萄酒_

+ **white wine:** _白葡萄酒_

+ **wine glass:** _酒杯_

+ **wine cellar:** _酒窖_

+ **wine list:** _酒单_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He opened a bottle of fine wine to celebrate the occasion.**

**中文翻译:** 他打开了一瓶上好的葡萄酒来庆祝这个时刻。

**单词释义:** 葡萄酒

#### 例句2

> **The region is known for its excellent wines.**

**中文翻译:** 这个地区以其优质的葡萄酒而闻名。

**单词释义:** 葡萄酒

#### 例句3

> **She has a refined taste in wine.**

**中文翻译:** 她对葡萄酒有高雅的品味。

**单词释义:** 葡萄酒

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a traveler was very thirsty. He found an old house and
knocked on the door. The owner welcomed him and offered him a glass of
delicious wine. The traveler felt refreshed and happy.
