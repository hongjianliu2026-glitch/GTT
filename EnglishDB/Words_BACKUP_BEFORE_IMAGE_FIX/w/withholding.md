## withholding

**英音:** _[wɪðˈhəʊldɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[wɪðˈhoʊldɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.预扣（税）；v.拒绝给；保留；抑制（withhold
的现在分词）*

### 短语

+ **withholding tax:** _预扣税_

+ **withholding information:** _隐瞒信息_

+ **withholding payment:** _扣付款项_

+ **withholding consent:** _拒绝同意_

+ **withholding support:** _不给予支持_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company was accused of withholding important information from
its shareholders.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司被指控向其股东隐瞒重要信息。

**单词释义:** 隐瞒；扣留

#### 例句2

> **The tax authorities are investigating cases of withholding income
tax.**

**中文翻译:** 税务机关正在调查所得税代扣的案例。

**单词释义:** 代扣；预扣

#### 例句3

> **She was punished for withholding her support at the crucial
moment.**

**中文翻译:** 她因在关键时刻不给予支持而受到惩罚。

**单词释义:** 不给予；保留

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught withholding the stolen jewels. The police were
determined to get the jewels back. Withholding something illegally will
lead to trouble. Remember, don\'t be like that thief!

**一个小偷被抓到藏匿偷来的珠宝。警察决心要拿回珠宝。非法藏匿东西会带来麻烦。记住，别像那个小偷！**
