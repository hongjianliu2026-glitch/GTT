## wastewater

**英音:** _[\'weɪst,wɔːtə]_ **美音:** _[\'westwɔtɚ]_

**译义:** *n.废水*

### 短语

+ **wastewater treatment:** _废水处理_

+ **wastewater discharge:** _废水排放_

+ **industrial wastewater:** _工业废水_

+ **domestic wastewater:** _生活废水_

+ **wastewater pollution:** _废水污染_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The factory was fined for discharging wastewater into the
river.**

**中文翻译:** 这家工厂因向河流排放废水而被罚款。

**单词释义:** 废水

#### 例句2

> **The treatment of wastewater requires advanced technology.**

**中文翻译:** 废水的处理需要先进的技术。

**单词释义:** 废水

#### 例句3

> **We need to find a better way to deal with the increasing amount
of wastewater.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要找到更好的方法来处理日益增多的废水。

**单词释义:** 废水

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a factory produced a lot of dirty water. This dirty
water was called wastewater. It was harmful and needed special
treatment. People worked hard to clean it and protect the environment.

**从前，一家工厂产生了很多脏水。这种脏水被称为废水。它是有害的，需要特殊处理。人们努力去净化它以保护环境。**
