## withdraw

**英音:** _[wɪð\'drɔː]_ **美音:** _[wɪð\'drɔ]_

**译义:** *vt.收回,撤消vi.缩回,退出v.撤退*

### 短语

+ **withdraw money:** _取钱；提款_

+ **withdraw from:** _从\...退出；撤离_

+ **withdraw support:** _撤回支持_

+ **withdraw statement:** _收回声明_

+ **withdraw troops:** _撤军_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He decided to withdraw from the race at the last minute.**

**中文翻译:** 他在最后一刻决定退出比赛。

**单词释义:** 退出

#### 例句2

> **She withdrew all her savings from the bank.**

**中文翻译:** 她从银行取出了她所有的存款。

**单词释义:** 取出

#### 例句3

> **The troops were ordered to withdraw from the area.**

**中文翻译:** 部队奉命从该地区撤离。

**单词释义:** 撤离

### 背诵小故事

> A soldier was in a fierce battle. But the situation was too
dangerous, so he decided to withdraw from the front line. He moved back
carefully, away from the danger. Withdraw means to move back or pull

意思是撤退；撤回**
