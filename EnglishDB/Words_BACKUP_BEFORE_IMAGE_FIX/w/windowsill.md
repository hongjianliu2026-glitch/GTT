## windowsill

**英音:** _[\'wɪndəʊsɪl]_ **美音:** _[\'wɪndo,sɪl]_

**译义:** *n.窗沿,窗台*

### 短语

+ **sit on the windowsill:** _坐在窗台上_

+ **lean against the windowsill:** _靠在窗台上_

+ **decorate the windowsill:** _装饰窗台_

+ **place flowers on the windowsill:** _在窗台上放花_

+ **clean the windowsill:** _清洁窗台_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I placed the flower vase on the windowsill.**

**中文翻译:** 我把花瓶放在了窗台上。

**单词释义:** 窗台

#### 例句2

> **The cat was sitting on the windowsill, enjoying the sunshine.**

**中文翻译:** 猫咪坐在窗台上，享受着阳光。

**单词释义:** 窗台

#### 例句3

> **She wiped the dust off the windowsill carefully.**

**中文翻译:** 她仔细地擦掉了窗台上的灰尘。

**单词释义:** 窗台

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little bird landed on the windowsill. It chirped happily
as if saying hello to the people inside the room. The windowsill became
a special stage for the bird\'s performance.

**有一天，一只小鸟落在了窗台上。它欢快地叽叽喳喳叫着，仿佛在向屋里的人打招呼。窗台成了小鸟表演的特殊舞台。**
