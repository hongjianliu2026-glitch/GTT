## messenger

**英音:** _[\'mesɪn(d)ʒə]_ **美音:** _[\'mɛsndʒɚ]_

**译义:** *n.报信者,使者*

### 短语

+ **messenger RNA:** _信使 RNA_

+ **dispatch a messenger:** _派遣使者_

+ **winged messenger:** _有翼的使者_

+ **courier messenger:** _信使快递员_

+ **divine messenger:** _神圣的使者_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The messenger delivered the important letter to the
recipient.**

**中文翻译:** 信使把重要的信件交给了收件人。

**单词释义:** 信使

#### 例句2

> **A messenger was sent to inform them of the change in plans.**

**中文翻译:** 派了一名使者去通知他们计划有变。

**单词释义:** 使者

#### 例句3

> **The internet has become a modern messenger for spreading
information quickly.**

**中文翻译:** 互联网已成为快速传播信息的现代信使。

**单词释义:** 传递信息的人或物

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little bird was chosen as the messenger to deliver an
important message. It flew through the forest and over the mountains,
finally reaching its destination successfully. The bird was a reliable
messenger.

**有一天，一只小鸟被选为信使去传递一个重要的消息。它飞过森林和高山，最终成功到达目的地。这只鸟是一个可靠的信使。**
