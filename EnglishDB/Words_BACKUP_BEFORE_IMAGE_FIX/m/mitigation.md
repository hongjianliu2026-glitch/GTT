## mitigation

**英音:** _[mɪtɪ\'geɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,mɪtɪ\'ɡeʃən]_

**译义:** *n.缓解,减轻,平静*

### 短语

+ **mitigation measure:** _缓解措施_

+ **mitigation strategy:** _缓解策略_

+ **mitigation plan:** _缓解计划_

+ **mitigation effect:** _缓解效果_

+ **mitigation effort:** _缓解努力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government is implementing measures for the mitigation of
climate change.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在实施应对气候变化的缓解措施。

**单词释义:** 缓解；减轻

#### 例句2

> **Insurance can provide some mitigation against financial
losses.**

**中文翻译:** 保险可以为财务损失提供一些减轻的保障。

**单词释义:** 减轻；缓和

#### 例句3

> **Effective communication is crucial for the mitigation of
conflicts.**

**中文翻译:** 有效的沟通对于冲突的缓解至关重要。

**单词释义:** 缓解；消除

### 背诵小故事

> A city was facing a severe flood. But with the efforts of building
dams and drainage systems, the damage was mitigated. People worked
together to reduce the negative impact, and \'mitigation\' means making
something less harmful or severe.

**一个城市正面临严重的洪水。但通过建造水坝和排水系统的努力，损失得到了减轻。人们共同努力以减少负面影响，'mitigation'意思是使某事的危害性或严重性降低。**
