## misrepresentation

**英音:** _[,mɪsreprɪzen\'teɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[,mɪsrɛprɪzɛn\'teʃən]_

**译义:** * 9mIsreprI\`zen\`teIFEn/n.误传,ntEtIv/adj.不如饰笏*

### 短语

+ **intentional misrepresentation:** _故意的虚假陈述_

+ **fraudulent misrepresentation:** _欺诈性的虚假陈述_

+ **material misrepresentation:** _重大的虚假陈述_

+ **liable for misrepresentation:** _对虚假陈述负责_

+ **prevent misrepresentation:** _防止虚假陈述_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The advertisement was accused of misrepresentation of the
product\'s features.**

**中文翻译:** 这则广告被指控对产品的特征进行了虚假陈述。

**单词释义:** 对\...\...的虚假陈述

#### 例句2

> **His statement was a clear misrepresentation of the facts.**

**中文翻译:** 他的陈述明显是对事实的歪曲。

**单词释义:** 对事实的歪曲

#### 例句3

> **The lawyer exposed the misrepresentation in the contract.**

**中文翻译:** 律师揭露了合同中的不实陈述。

**单词释义:** 合同中的不实陈述

### 背诵小故事

> A businessman was accused of misrepresentation. He claimed his
product was made of pure gold, but it wasn\'t. His false description
caused a lot of trouble. People lost trust in him.

**一位商人被指控歪曲事实。他声称他的产品是纯金制成的，但事实并非如此。他的错误描述带来了很多麻烦。人们不再信任他。**
