## machinery

**英音:** _[mə\'ʃiːn(ə)rɪ]_ **美音:** _[mə\'ʃinəri]_

**译义:** *n.[总称] 机器,机械*

### 短语

+ **industrial machinery:** _工业机械_

+ **farm machinery:** _农业机械_

+ **machinery equipment:** _机械设备_

+ **heavy machinery:** _重型机械_

+ **machinery parts:** _机械零件_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The factory\'s machinery was constantly updated to improve
production efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 工厂的机械不断更新，以提高生产效率。

**单词释义:** 机器设备

#### 例句2

> **The repair of the machinery required skilled technicians.**

**中文翻译:** 机器的修理需要熟练的技术人员。

**单词释义:** 机器

#### 例句3

> **The noise from the machinery in the workshop was deafening.**

**中文翻译:** 车间里机器的噪音震耳欲聋。

**单词释义:** 机器

### 背诵小故事

> A factory owner was very worried because the machinery in his factory
broke down. But a smart engineer fixed it quickly. The machinery started
working smoothly again, and the factory could produce goods as usual.

**一位工厂老板非常担心，因为他厂里的机器坏了。但一位聪明的工程师很快修好了它。机器又开始顺利运转，工厂能够照常生产货物了。**
