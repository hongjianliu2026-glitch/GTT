## mountainside

**英音:** _[\'maʊntɪnsaɪd]_ **美音:** _[\'maʊntənsaɪd]_

**译义:** *n.山腹,山腰*

### 短语

+ **climb the mountainside:** _爬山坡_

+ **scenic mountainside:** _风景优美的山坡_

+ **steep mountainside:** _陡峭的山坡_

+ **green mountainside:** _绿色的山坡_

+ **explore the mountainside:** _探索山坡_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **We climbed up the steep mountainside.**

**中文翻译:** 我们爬上了陡峭的山坡。

**单词释义:** 山坡

#### 例句2

> **The cabin was built on the mountainside.**

**中文翻译:** 小屋建在山坡上。

**单词释义:** 山坡

#### 例句3

> **She found a beautiful flower on the mountainside.**

**中文翻译:** 她在山坡上发现了一朵美丽的花。

**单词释义:** 山坡

### 背诵小故事

> A brave climber was determined to reach the peak. Along the way, he
struggled on the mountainside, but the beautiful view kept him going.
