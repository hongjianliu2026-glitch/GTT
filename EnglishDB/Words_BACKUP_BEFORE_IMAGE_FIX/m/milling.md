## milling

**英音:** _[\'mɪlɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'mɪlɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.磨,制粉,[机]轧齿边*

### 短语

+ **milling machine:** _铣床_

+ **milling cutter:** _铣刀_

+ **milling process:** _铣削过程_

+ **vertical milling:** _立式铣削_

+ **milling operation:** _铣削操作_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The workers are engaged in milling operations in the factory.**

**中文翻译:** 工人们在工厂里从事铣削作业。

**单词释义:** 铣削；研磨

#### 例句2

> **The milling machine is running smoothly.**

**中文翻译:** 铣床运转平稳。

**单词释义:** 铣床

#### 例句3

> **There was a crowd milling around the entrance of the shopping
mall.**

**中文翻译:** 商场门口围满了人群。

**单词释义:** 乱转；徘徊

### 背诵小故事

> A group of workers were busy milling in the factory. They were
grinding grains into fine powder. The sound of the milling machines was
loud, but they kept working hard to produce high-quality products.

**一群工人在工厂里忙着铣削。他们正在把谷物磨成细粉。铣削机器的声音很大，但他们一直努力工作以生产高质量的产品。**
