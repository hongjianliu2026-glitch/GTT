## mediocrity

**英音:** _[ˌmiːdiˈɒkrəti]_ **美音:** _[ˌmiːdiˈɑːkrəti]_

**译义:** *n.平常,平庸之才*

### 短语

+ **fall into mediocrity:** _陷入平庸_

+ **rise above mediocrity:** _超越平庸_

+ **sink to mediocrity:** _堕入平庸_

+ **escape from mediocrity:** _摆脱平庸_

+ **condemn to mediocrity:** _注定平庸_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His work shows a disturbing mediocrity.**

**中文翻译:** 他的作品表现出令人不安的平庸。

**单词释义:** 平庸；平凡；普通

#### 例句2

> **She refused to accept mediocrity in her academic
achievements.**

**中文翻译:** 她拒绝接受自己学业成绩平庸。

**单词释义:** 平庸；平凡

#### 例句3

> **The company is stuck in a state of mediocrity and needs a major
shake-up.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司陷入平庸状态，需要进行重大改组。

**单词释义:** 平庸；普通

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a young artist aimed high but was constantly
surrounded by mediocrity. People with average skills and lackluster
ideas held him back. But he broke free and pursued excellence. Remember,
mediocrity means being average or of moderate quality.

**从前，一位年轻的艺术家志向高远，但却不断被平庸所包围。那些技能一般、想法平淡的人阻碍着他。但他挣脱了束缚，追求卓越。记住，mediocrity
意味着普通或中等质量。**
