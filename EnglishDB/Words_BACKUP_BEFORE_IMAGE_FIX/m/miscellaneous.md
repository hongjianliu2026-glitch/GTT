## miscellaneous

**英音:** _[,mɪsə\'leɪnɪəs]_ **美音:**
_[\'mɪsə\'lenɪəs]_

**译义:** *adj.各色各样混在一起,混杂的,多才多艺的*

### 短语

+ **miscellaneous goods:** _杂货；杂项物品_

+ **miscellaneous expenses:** _杂项费用_

+ **miscellaneous income:** _杂项收入_

+ **miscellaneous tasks:** _杂项任务_

+ **miscellaneous information:** _杂项信息_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The store sells miscellaneous goods, from stationery to home
appliances.**

**中文翻译:** 这家商店出售各种商品，从文具到家用电器。

**单词释义:** 各种各样的

#### 例句2

> **He has a miscellaneous collection of stamps from different
countries.**

**中文翻译:** 他收藏了来自不同国家的各种邮票。

**单词释义:** 各种各样的

#### 例句3

> **The report contains miscellaneous information that needs to be
sorted out.**

**中文翻译:** 该报告包含需要整理的杂项信息。

**单词释义:** 各种各样的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was in a miscellaneous store. There were all kinds of
items, from toys to books. It was so confusing! But he finally found
what he wanted. Remember, miscellaneous means consisting of a mixture of
various kinds.

**一天，汤姆在一家杂货店里。那里有各种各样的物品，从玩具到书籍。太杂乱了！但他最终还是找到了他想要的。记住，miscellaneous
意思是各种各样的混合。**
