## mayonnaise

**英音:** _[meɪə\'neɪz]_ **美音:** _[\'meənez]_

**译义:** *n.蛋黄酱*

### 短语

+ **egg mayonnaise:** _鸡蛋美乃滋_

+ **mayonnaise dressing:** _蛋黄酱调料_

+ **mayonnaise based salad:** _以蛋黄酱为基础的沙拉_

+ **homemade mayonnaise:** _自制蛋黄酱_

+ **spicy mayonnaise:** _辣味蛋黄酱_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I always add a dollop of mayonnaise to my sandwiches.**

**中文翻译:** 我总是在三明治里加一点蛋黄酱。

**单词释义:** 蛋黄酱

#### 例句2

> **The chef used too much mayonnaise in the salad.**

**中文翻译:** 厨师在沙拉中使用了太多蛋黄酱。

**单词释义:** 蛋黄酱

#### 例句3

> **She doesn\'t like the taste of mayonnaise on her burgers.**

**中文翻译:** 她不喜欢汉堡上蛋黄酱的味道。

**单词释义:** 蛋黄酱

### 背诵小故事

> One day, when I was having a picnic, I opened the lunch box and found
a delicious sandwich with thick mayonnaise. It was so creamy and made
the sandwich taste amazing. I\'ll always remember that moment.
