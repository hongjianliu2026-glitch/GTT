## metaphorical

**英音:** _[,metə\'fɒrɪkl]_ **美音:** _[\'mɛtə\'fɔrɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.隐喻性的,比喻性的*

### 短语

+ **metaphorical language:** _隐喻性语言_

+ **metaphorical expression:** _隐喻表达_

+ **metaphorical meaning:** _隐喻意义_

+ **metaphorical image:** _隐喻形象_

+ **metaphorical thinking:** _隐喻思维_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The poet used a metaphorical language to describe love.**

**中文翻译:** 诗人用隐喻的语言来描述爱情。

**单词释义:** 隐喻性的

#### 例句2

> **Her speech was full of metaphorical expressions.**

**中文翻译:** 她的讲话充满了隐喻性的表达。

**单词释义:** 隐喻性的

#### 例句3

> **The novel has a metaphorical undertone that is not easily
grasped.**

**中文翻译:** 小说有一种不易理解的隐喻意味。

**单词释义:** 隐喻性的

### 背诵小故事

> A poet was struggling to describe a beautiful sunset. Instead of
using normal words, he chose metaphorical expressions like \'The sun is
a golden painter.\' This helped him convey the magic more vividly.
Remember, metaphorical means using metaphors.

**一位诗人努力描绘美丽的日落。他没有使用平常的词汇，而是选择了隐喻性的表达，比如'太阳是一位金色的画家。'这帮助他更生动地传达出那种神奇。记住，metaphorical
意思是隐喻性的。**
