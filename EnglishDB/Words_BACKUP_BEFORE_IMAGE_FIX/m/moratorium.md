## moratorium

**英音:** _[,mɒrə\'tɔːrɪəm]_ **美音:** _[,mɔrə\'tɔrɪəm]_

**译义:** *n.延期偿付,延期偿付期间*

### 短语

+ **debt moratorium:** _债务延期_

+ **moratorium on fishing:** _捕鱼禁令_

+ **moratorium on executions:** _死刑暂缓执行_

+ **moratorium on building:** _建筑暂停_

+ **moratorium on imports:** _进口禁令_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government has declared a moratorium on new coal-fired power
plants.**

**中文翻译:** 政府已宣布暂停新建燃煤电厂。

**单词释义:** 暂停；延缓

#### 例句2

> **The bank granted a moratorium on loan repayments for three months
due to the pandemic.**

**中文翻译:** 受疫情影响，该银行暂停偿还贷款三个月。

**单词释义:** 延缓；暂停

#### 例句3

> **There is currently a moratorium on logging in this area to
protect the environment.**

**中文翻译:** 目前，为了保护环境，该地区已暂停伐木。

**单词释义:** 暂停；中止

### 背诵小故事

> A city was facing a serious pollution problem. The government
declared a moratorium on new factories to give the environment a break.
It was like a timeout for development to save nature.

**一个城市正面临严重的污染问题。政府宣布暂停新建工厂，给环境一个喘息的机会。这就像是为了拯救自然，给发展按下了暂停键。**
