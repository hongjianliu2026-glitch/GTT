## temporarily

**英音:** _[\'temp(ə)r(ər)ɪlɪ]_ **美音:**
_[ˈtɛmpəˌrɛrili]_

**译义:** *adv.临时*

### 短语

+ **temporarily unavailable:** _暂时不可用_

+ **temporarily closed:** _暂时关闭_

+ **temporarily out of stock:** _暂时缺货_

+ **temporarily suspend:** _暂时中止_

+ **temporarily occupied:** _暂时占用_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The office is temporarily closed due to renovations.**

**中文翻译:** 该办公室因装修而暂时关闭。

**单词释义:** 暂时地

#### 例句2

> **She was temporarily assigned to the project team.**

**中文翻译:** 她被临时分配到项目组。

**单词释义:** 暂时地

#### 例句3

> **The power supply was temporarily interrupted during the
storm.**

**中文翻译:** 暴风雨期间供电暂时中断。

**单词释义:** 暂时地

### 背诵小故事

> A traveler was lost in a strange town. Fortunately, he was
temporarily helped by a kind local who showed him the way.
\'Temporarily\' here means for a short time, like the traveler\'s brief
assistance.

**一位旅行者在一个陌生的城镇迷路了。幸运的是，他暂时得到了一位善良当地人的帮助，给他指了路。"Temporarily"在这里表示短时间，就像旅行者得到的短暂帮助。**
