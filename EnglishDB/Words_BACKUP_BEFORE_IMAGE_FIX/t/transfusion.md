## transfusion

**英音:** _[træns\'fjuːʒ(ə)n; trɑːns-; -nz-]_ **美音:**
_[trænz\'fjʊʒən]_

**译义:** *n.注入,[医]输血,输液*

### 短语

+ **blood transfusion:** _输血_

+ **platelet transfusion:** _血小板输注_

+ **transfusion reaction:** _输血反应_

+ **transfusion equipment:** _输血设备_

+ **transfusion service:** _输血服务_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The patient needed a blood transfusion after the accident.**

**中文翻译:** 事故发生后，病人需要输血。

**单词释义:** 输血

#### 例句2

> **The doctor ordered a transfusion of plasma to the critically ill
patient.**

**中文翻译:** 医生要求为危重病人输血浆。

**单词释义:** 输注；输血

#### 例句3

> **The transfusion process was carefully monitored by the medical
team.**

**中文翻译:** 输血过程由医疗团队仔细监控。

**单词释义:** 输血过程

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor was in a hurry as a patient needed a transfusion. He quickly
prepared the blood and made the transfusion successfully, saving the
patient\'s life. The transfusion was like a lifeline.

**一位医生很着急，因为有个病人需要输血。他迅速准备好血液并成功进行了输血，挽救了病人的生命。输血就像是一条生命线。**
