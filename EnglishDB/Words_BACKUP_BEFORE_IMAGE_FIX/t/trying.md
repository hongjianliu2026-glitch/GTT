## trying

**英音:** _[\'traɪɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'traɪɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.难受的,费劲的,令人厌烦的*

### 短语

+ **keep trying:** _继续努力；不断尝试_

+ **worth trying:** _值得一试_

+ **stop trying:** _停止尝试_

+ **try hard:** _努力尝试；努力_

+ **try one\'s best:** _尽某人最大努力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I\'m trying my best to finish this project on time.**

**中文翻译:** 我正在尽我最大的努力按时完成这个项目。

**单词释义:** 努力，尝试

#### 例句2

> **She has been trying to lose weight for months.**

**中文翻译:** 她几个月来一直在努力减肥。

**单词释义:** 试图，努力

#### 例句3

> **The baby is trying to stand up by himself.**

**中文翻译:** 这个宝宝正在努力自己站起来。

**单词释义:** 尝试，努力

### 背诵小故事

> A little girl was trying to reach a book on the top shelf. She stood
on tiptoe and stretched her arm, but it was still very difficult.
Finally, with her continuous efforts, she got the book. Trying means
making an effort to do something.
