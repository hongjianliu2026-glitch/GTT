## typing

**英音:** _[\'taɪpɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'taɪpɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.打字,键入*

### 短语

+ **typing speed:** _打字速度_

+ **typing error:** _打字错误_

+ **typing practice:** _打字练习_

+ **typing skill:** _打字技能_

+ **touch typing:** _盲打_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I\'m improving my typing speed by practicing every day.**

**中文翻译:** 我通过每天练习来提高我的打字速度。

**单词释义:** 打字

#### 例句2

> **She is skilled in typing and can finish the work quickly.**

**中文翻译:** 她打字熟练，能很快完成工作。

**单词释义:** 打字

#### 例句3

> **The noise of typing from the next room disturbed me.**

**中文翻译:** 隔壁房间的打字声打扰了我。

**单词释义:** 打字（的声音）

the computer and started typing quickly. His fingers danced on the
keyboard, and the words appeared on the screen one by one. Finally, he
