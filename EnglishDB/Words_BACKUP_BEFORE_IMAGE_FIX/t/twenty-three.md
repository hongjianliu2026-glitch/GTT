## twenty-three

**英音:** _[ˈtwentɪ-ˈθriː]_ **美音:** _[ˈtwɛnti-ˈθriː]_

**译义:** *num.二十三，二十加三的数目*

### 短语

+ **twenty-three years old:** _二十三岁_

+ **twenty-three people:** _二十三个人_

+ **twenty-three books:** _二十三本书_

+ **twenty-three apples:** _二十三个苹果_

+ **twenty-three kilometers:** _二十三公里_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little rabbit was counting. It hopped and said, \'Twenty,
twenty-one, twenty-two, twenty-three!\' It was so happy to reach
twenty-three. It felt like a big number for it.

**有一天，一只小兔子在数数。它一边跳一边说："二十，二十一，二十二，二十三！"
它很高兴数到了二十三。对它来说这像是个大数字。**
