## temptation

**英音:** _[tem(p)\'teɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[tɛmp\'teʃən]_

**译义:** *n.诱惑,诱惑物*

### 短语

+ **resist temptation:** _抵制诱惑_

+ **yield to temptation:** _屈服于诱惑_

+ **fall into temptation:** _陷入诱惑_

+ **overcome temptation:** _克服诱惑_

+ **face temptation:** _面对诱惑_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He resisted the temptation to eat the chocolate cake.**

**中文翻译:** 他抵制住了吃巧克力蛋糕的诱惑。

**单词释义:** 诱惑

#### 例句2

> **The temptation of easy money led him astray.**

**中文翻译:** 轻松赚钱的诱惑使他误入歧途。

**单词释义:** 诱惑

#### 例句3

> **She gave in to the temptation and bought the expensive dress.**

**中文翻译:** 她屈服于诱惑，买了那件昂贵的衣服。

**单词释义:** 诱惑

### 背诵小故事

> A poor boy was constantly facing the temptation of stealing food to
satisfy his hunger. But his strong will helped him resist. Temptation
means something that attracts you to do wrong or unwise things.

**一个贫穷的男孩不断面临偷食物来充饥的诱惑。但他坚强的意志帮助他抵制住了。temptation
指吸引你去做错误或不明智之事的事物。**
