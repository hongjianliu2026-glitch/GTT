## glee

**英音:** _[gliː]_ **美音:** _[ɡli]_

**译义:** *n.欢乐,高兴*

### 短语

+ **be full of glee:** _充满欢乐_

+ **dance with glee:** _欢快地跳舞_

+ **sing with glee:** _高兴地唱歌_

+ **shout for glee:** _因高兴而呼喊_

+ **express glee:** _表达欢乐_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The children shouted with glee when they saw the circus.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子们看到马戏团时高兴得大喊大叫。

**单词释义:** 欢乐；高兴

#### 例句2

> **She sang and danced with glee at the party.**

**中文翻译:** 她在聚会上又唱又跳，十分快乐。

**单词释义:** 欢乐；愉快

#### 例句3

> **His face shone with glee as he opened the present.**

**中文翻译:** 他打开礼物时，脸上洋溢着喜悦。

**单词释义:** 喜悦；欣喜

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a group of friends won a singing competition. The
joy and excitement on their faces was pure glee. They danced and hugged,
feeling the happiness of success. Glee means great happiness and joy.
