## gymnastics

**英音:** _[dʒɪm\'næstɪks]_ **美音:** _[dʒɪm\'næstɪks]_

**译义:** *n.体操,体育*

### 短语

+ **artistic gymnastics:** _艺术体操_

+ **rhythmic gymnastics:** _韵律体操_

+ **competitive gymnastics:** _竞技体操_

+ **gymnastics equipment:** _体操器材_

+ **gymnastics training:** _体操训练_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She is very good at gymnastics and has won many competitions.**

**中文翻译:** 她非常擅长体操并且赢得了许多比赛。

**单词释义:** 体操

#### 例句2

> **The school offers various courses in gymnastics for students to
choose from.**

**中文翻译:** 这所学校为学生提供了各种体操课程以供选择。

**单词释义:** 体操

#### 例句3

> **He spent hours practicing gymnastics every day to improve his
skills.**

**中文翻译:** 他每天花费数小时练习体操以提高他的技能。

**单词释义:** 体操

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved gymnastics. He
practiced every day, doing flips and jumps. Gymnastics made him strong
and flexible. He dreamed of winning medals. Gymnastics became his
passion!

**从前，有一个小男孩热爱体操。他每天练习，做翻转和跳跃动作。体操让他变得强壮和灵活。他梦想着赢得奖牌。体操成为了他的热爱！**
