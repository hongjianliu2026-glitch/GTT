## gasoline

**英音:** _[\'gæsəliːn]_ **美音:** _[\'gæsəlin]_

**译义:** *n.汽油*

### 短语

+ **gasoline engine:** _汽油发动机_

+ **gasoline station:** _加油站_

+ **leaded gasoline:** _含铅汽油_

+ **unleaded gasoline:** _无铅汽油_

+ **gasoline pump:** _汽油泵_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The car needs to be filled with gasoline.**

**中文翻译:** 这辆车需要加汽油。

**单词释义:** 汽油

#### 例句2

> **Gasoline prices have been rising recently.**

**中文翻译:** 最近汽油价格一直在上涨。

**单词释义:** 汽油

#### 例句3

> **He spilled gasoline on the ground by accident.**

**中文翻译:** 他不小心把汽油洒在了地上。

**单词释义:** 汽油

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom\'s car ran out of gasoline in the middle of nowhere. He
was very worried. Luckily, a kind person came and helped him get some
gasoline. After that, Tom always remembered to check the gasoline level.
