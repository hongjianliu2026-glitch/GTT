## unsuspecting

**英音:** _[ʌnsə\'spektɪŋ]_ **美音:**
_[\'ʌnsə\'spɛktɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.不怀疑的,无疑虑的,信任的*

### 短语

+ **unsuspecting victim:** _毫无戒心的受害者_

+ **unsuspecting public:** _毫无戒心的公众_

+ **an unsuspecting traveler:** _一位毫无防备的旅行者_

+ **unsuspecting pedestrians:** _毫无防备的行人_

+ **unsuspecting customers:** _毫无戒心的顾客_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The thief targeted the unsuspecting tourists.**

**中文翻译:** 这个小偷把目标对准了毫无戒心的游客。

**单词释义:** 毫无戒心的

#### 例句2

> **She took advantage of the unsuspecting buyer.**

**中文翻译:** 她占了那个毫无防备的买家的便宜。

**单词释义:** 毫无防备的

#### 例句3

> **The unsuspecting victim was caught off guard.**

**中文翻译:** 那个毫无察觉的受害者猝不及防。

**单词释义:** 毫无察觉的

### 背诵小故事

> A thief targeted an unsuspecting traveler. The traveler was happily
walking, not suspecting anything. Suddenly, the thief snatched the
traveler\'s bag. Poor unsuspecting person!
