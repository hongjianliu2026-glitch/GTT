## universe

**译义:** *n.宇宙,世界,万物,领域*

+ **universe of discourse:** _论域_

+ **parallel universe:** _平行宇宙_

+ **observable universe:** _可观测宇宙_

+ **universe model:** _宇宙模型_

> In a faraway land, a little boy dreamed of exploring the unknown. One
night, he saw a magical star and followed it. It led him to understand
that the entire space, with all its planets and mysteries, is called the
\'universe\'.

**在一个遥远的地方，一个小男孩梦想着探索未知。一天晚上，他看到一颗神奇的星星并跟着它。这让他明白，包含所有行星和奥秘的整个空间被称为"宇宙"。**
