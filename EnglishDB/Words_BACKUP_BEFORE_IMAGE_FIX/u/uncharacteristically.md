## uncharacteristically

**英音:** _[ˌʌnˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪkli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌʌnˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪkli]_

**译义:** *adv. 不典型地；非同寻常地*

### 短语

+ **uncharacteristically quiet:** _异乎寻常地安静_

+ **uncharacteristically generous:** _非同寻常地慷慨_

+ **uncharacteristically slow:** _反常地缓慢_

+ **uncharacteristically friendly:** _出奇地友好_

+ **uncharacteristically optimistic:** _罕见地乐观_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He uncharacteristically lost his temper during the meeting.**

**中文翻译:** 他在会议期间一反常态地发了脾气。

**单词释义:** 一反常态地

#### 例句2

> **She uncharacteristically arrived late for the party.**

**中文翻译:** 她反常地在聚会时迟到了。

**单词释义:** 反常地

#### 例句3

> **The team uncharacteristically performed poorly in the
championship.**

**中文翻译:** 这支队伍在锦标赛中表现得异常糟糕。

**单词释义:** 异常地

### 背诵小故事

> A usually calm and collected person uncharacteristically lost their
temper at the party. Everyone was shocked because it was so unlike their
normal behavior.

**一个通常冷静沉着的人在聚会上一反常态地发了脾气。每个人都很震惊，因为这与他们平常的行为太不一样了。**
