## unconsciously

**英音:** _[ʌnˈkɒnʃəsli]_ **美音:** _[ʌnˈkɑːnʃəsli]_

**译义:** *adv.无意中,不知不觉*

### 短语

+ **act unconsciously:** _无意识地行动_

+ **speak unconsciously:** _不自觉地说话_

+ **behave unconsciously:** _无意识地表现_

+ **react unconsciously:** _无意识地反应_

+ **do something unconsciously:** _无意识地做某事_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He unconsciously tapped his fingers on the table.**

**中文翻译:** 他不知不觉地用手指轻敲着桌子。

**单词释义:** 不知不觉地

#### 例句2

> **She unconsciously imitated her sister\'s behavior.**

**中文翻译:** 她下意识地模仿了她姐姐的行为。

**单词释义:** 下意识地

#### 例句3

> **He spoke unconsciously, revealing his true thoughts.**

**中文翻译:** 他无意识地说了出来，暴露了他的真实想法。

**单词释义:** 无意识地

### 背诵小故事

crossed the road unconsciously and almost got hit by a car. Luckily, the
driver stopped in time. Unconsciously means without being aware or
conscious of what you\'re doing.
