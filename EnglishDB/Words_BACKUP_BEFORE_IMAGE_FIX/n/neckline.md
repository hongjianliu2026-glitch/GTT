## neckline

**英音:** _[\'neklaɪn]_ **美音:** _[\'nɛklaɪn]_

**译义:** *n.领口,领圈*

### 短语

+ **low neckline:** _低领口_

+ **high neckline:** _高领口_

+ **scoop neckline:** _汤匙领_

+ **square neckline:** _方领口_

+ **off-the-shoulder neckline:** _露肩领口_
