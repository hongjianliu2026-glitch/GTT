## nationalize

**英音:** _[ˈnæʃnəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[ˈnæʃnəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.国有化*

### 短语

+ **nationalize industry:** _将工业国有化_

+ **nationalize assets:** _将资产国有化_

+ **nationalize bank:** _将银行国有化_

+ **nationalize enterprise:** _将企业国有化_

+ **nationalize resource:** _将资源国有化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government decided to nationalize the failing banks to
stabilize the financial system.**

**中文翻译:** 政府决定将那些经营不善的银行国有化以稳定金融体系。

**单词释义:** 使国有化

#### 例句2

> **Some industries were nationalized for the greater good of the
country.**

**中文翻译:** 一些产业为了国家的更大利益被国有化了。

**单词释义:** 使国有化

#### 例句3

> **The new regime nationalized all foreign-owned companies.**

**中文翻译:** 新政权将所有外资公司国有化。

**单词释义:** 使国有化

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a small country\'s important industries were taken
over by the government. This action was called \'nationalize\'. It means
to bring something under the control or ownership of the nation.

**从前，有个小国家，其重要产业被政府接管。这种行为被称为'nationalize'。它的意思是使某物归国家控制或所有。**
