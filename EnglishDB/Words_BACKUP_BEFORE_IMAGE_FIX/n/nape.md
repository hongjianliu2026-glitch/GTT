## nape

**英音:** _[neɪp]_ **美音:** _[nep]_

**译义:** *n.项,颈背*

### 短语

+ **the nape of the neck:** _颈背；后颈_

+ **nape hair:** _颈背的头发_

+ **nape pain:** _颈背疼痛_

+ **nape massage:** _颈背按摩_

+ **nape injury:** _颈背损伤_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She wore her hair up, exposing the nape of her neck.**

**中文翻译:** 她把头发盘起来，露出颈背。

**单词释义:** 脖子后面

#### 例句2

> **He gently kissed the nape of her neck.**

**中文翻译:** 他轻轻吻着她的颈背。

**单词释义:** 颈背

#### 例句3

> **The cool breeze blew on the nape, giving a refreshing
feeling.**

**中文翻译:** 凉风吹在颈背上，给人一种清爽的感觉。

**单词释义:** 颈后

### 背诵小故事

its nape. The monkey felt itchy and tried to catch the bug. It was funny
and made all the other animals laugh.
