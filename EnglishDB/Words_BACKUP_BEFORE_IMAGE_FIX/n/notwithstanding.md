## notwithstanding

**英音:** _[nɒtwɪð\'stændɪŋ; -wɪθ-]_ **美音:**
_[,nɑtwɪθ\'stændɪŋ]_

**译义:** * prep.虽然,尽管adv.尽管,还是 conj.虽然,尽管*

### 短语

+ **notwithstanding anything to the contrary:**
_尽管有任何相反的情况_

+ **notwithstanding that:** _尽管；虽然_

+ **notwithstanding the fact that:** _尽管事实是......_

+ **notwithstanding difficulties:** _尽管有困难_

+ **notwithstanding obstacles:** _尽管有障碍_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Notwithstanding the bad weather, they still went for a hike.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管天气恶劣，他们还是去徒步旅行了。

**单词释义:** 尽管；虽然

#### 例句2

> **He remained calm notwithstanding the danger.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管面临危险，他仍然保持冷静。

**单词释义:** 尽管；虽然

#### 例句3

> **Notwithstanding her protests, he went ahead with his plan.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管她提出抗议，他还是继续执行他的计划。

**单词释义:** 尽管；虽然

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was determined to rescue the princess notwithstanding
the dangerous monsters and traps. He overcame all difficulties, showing
that \'notwithstanding\' means in spite of or despite.

**一位勇敢的骑士决心营救公主，尽管有危险的怪物和陷阱。他克服了所有困难，这表明'notwithstanding'意思是尽管、不管。**
