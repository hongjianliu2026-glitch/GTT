## northwestern

**英音:** _[\'nɔ:θ\'westən]_ **美音:** _[\'nɔθ\'westən]_

**译义:** *adj.西北方的,来自西北的*

### 短语

+ **northwestern region:** _西北部地区_

+ **northwestern university:** _西北大学_

+ **northwestern direction:** _西北方向_

+ **northwestern wind:** _西北风_

+ **northwestern part:** _西北部_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The northwestern part of the country is known for its beautiful
mountains.**

**中文翻译:** 该国西北部以其美丽的山脉而闻名。

**单词释义:** 位于西北的；来自西北的

#### 例句2

> **He moved to a northwestern city to start a new life.**

**中文翻译:** 他搬到了西北的一座城市，开始了新的生活。

**单词释义:** 西北的

#### 例句3

> **The northwestern wind is blowing strongly today.**

**中文翻译:** 今天西北风刮得很大。

**单词释义:** 西北方向的

### 背诵小故事

> A brave adventurer was lost in a forest. He decided to follow the
northwestern path as he believed it would lead him to safety. Finally,
he found a village and was saved. Remember, northwestern means in the
