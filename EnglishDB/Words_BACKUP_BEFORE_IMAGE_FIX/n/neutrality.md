## neutrality

**英音:** _[njuː\'trælɪtɪ]_ **美音:** _[nu\'træləti]_

**译义:** *n.中立,中性*

### 短语

+ **armed neutrality:** _武装中立_

+ **passive neutrality:** _消极中立_

+ **strict neutrality:** _严格中立_

+ **neutrality agreement:** _中立协议_

+ **neutrality policy:** _中立政策_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The country has always maintained a policy of neutrality in
international conflicts.**

**中文翻译:** 这个国家在国际冲突中一直保持中立政策。

**单词释义:** 中立；中立状态

#### 例句2

> **His expression showed neutrality when asked about the
controversial issue.**

**中文翻译:** 当被问及这个有争议的问题时，他的表情显示出中立。

**单词释义:** 中立；不偏不倚

#### 例句3

> **The judge is expected to maintain neutrality throughout the
trial.**

**中文翻译:** 预计法官在整个审判过程中保持中立。

**单词释义:** 中立；公正

### 背诵小故事

> A king was faced with a war. But he chose neutrality, not joining
either side. His decision was to keep his kingdom peaceful and avoid
getting involved in the chaos. Thus, neutrality means not taking sides
or being impartial.

**一位国王面临一场战争。但他选择了中立，不加入任何一方。他的决定是为了让他的王国保持和平，避免卷入混乱。因此，neutrality
意思是中立；中立地位；不偏不倚。**
