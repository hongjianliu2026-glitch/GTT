## limousine

**英音:** _[\'lɪməziːn; ,lɪmə\'ziːn]_ **美音:**
_[\'lɪməzin]_

**译义:** *n.豪华轿车*

### 短语

+ **limousine service:** _豪华轿车服务_

+ **limousine driver:** _豪华轿车司机_

+ **hire a limousine:** _租用一辆豪华轿车_

+ **limousine fleet:** _豪华轿车车队_

+ **limousine rental:** _豪华轿车租赁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The celebrity arrived at the event in a luxurious limousine.**

**中文翻译:** 这位名人乘坐豪华轿车抵达活动现场。

**单词释义:** 豪华轿车

#### 例句2

> **The newlyweds hired a limousine for their wedding day.**

**中文翻译:** 新婚夫妇为婚礼当天租了一辆豪华轿车。

**单词释义:** 豪华轿车

#### 例句3

> **The limousine driver was very professional and polite.**

**中文翻译:** 豪华轿车司机非常专业而且有礼貌。

**单词释义:** 豪华轿车

### 背诵小故事

> A rich man was late for a party. He rushed out and got into the
limousine waiting for him. The long and shiny car quickly took him to
the destination.
