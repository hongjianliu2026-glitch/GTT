## laundering

**英音:** _[ˈlɔːndərɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[ˈlɔːndərɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.洗烫*

### 短语

+ **money laundering:** _洗钱_

+ **laundering machine:** _洗衣机_

+ **laundering service:** _洗衣服务_

+ **laundering facility:** _洗衣设施_

+ **laundering process:** _洗涤过程_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The police suspected him of money laundering.**

**中文翻译:** 警方怀疑他洗钱。

**单词释义:** 洗钱

#### 例句2

> **She was arrested for drug laundering.**

**中文翻译:** 她因洗毒被捕。

**单词释义:** 洗钱

#### 例句3

> **The company was accused of laundering illegal funds through its
accounts.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司被指控通过其账户洗钱非法资金。

**单词释义:** 清洗（非法资金）

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught for laundering money. He thought he was smart, but
the police were smarter. They followed the money trail and exposed his
illegal act. Remember, laundering means cleaning dirty money to make it
seem legal.

**一个小偷因洗钱被抓。他以为自己聪明，但警察更聪明。他们顺着资金线索揭露了他的非法行为。记住，laundering
意思是把非法获得的钱通过各种手段变为合法的。**
