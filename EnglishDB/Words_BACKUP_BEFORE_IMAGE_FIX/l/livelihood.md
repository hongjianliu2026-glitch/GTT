## livelihood

**英音:** _[\'laɪvlɪhʊd]_ **美音:** _[\'laɪvlɪhʊd]_

**译义:** *n.生计,谋生*

### 短语

+ **earn a livelihood:** _谋生_

+ **livelihood security:** _生计保障_

+ **livelihood difficulties:** _生活困难_

+ **livelihood strategy:** _生计策略_

+ **livelihood source:** _生活来源_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Fishing is the main livelihood of the villagers here.**

**中文翻译:** 捕鱼是这里村民的主要生计。

**单词释义:** 生计；营生

#### 例句2

> **He lost his livelihood when the factory closed.**

**中文翻译:** 工厂关闭后，他失去了生计。

**单词释义:** 生计

#### 例句3

> **The new policy threatens the livelihood of many small
businesses.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策威胁到了许多小企业的营生。

**单词释义:** 营生

### 背诵小故事

> A poor man struggled to find a way to earn money for his livelihood.
One day, he got a job in a factory and finally had a stable income to
support his life. Livelihood means the way you earn money to live.

**一个贫穷的人努力寻找赚钱的方法来维持生计。有一天，他在一家工厂得到了一份工作，终于有了稳定的收入来支撑生活。livelihood
意思是谋生的方式。**
