## literal

**英音:** _[\'lɪt(ə)r(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[\'lɪtərəl]_

**译义:** *adj.文字的,照字面上的,无夸张的*

### 短语

+ **literal meaning:** _字面意义_

+ **literal translation:** _直译_

+ **literal interpretation:** _字面解释_

+ **be literal about:** _对\...\...拘泥于字面_

+ **literal description:** _如实的描述_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The literal meaning of the word \'home\' is a place where one
lives.**

**中文翻译:** "家"这个词的字面意思就是一个人居住的地方。

**单词释义:** 字面的

#### 例句2

> **He took my words in a literal sense and got offended.**

**中文翻译:** 他从字面意义上理解了我的话，并感到被冒犯了。

**单词释义:** 字面的；逐字的

#### 例句3

> **The translation was too literal and failed to convey the true
meaning.**

**中文翻译:** 翻译过于字面意思，没有传达出真正的意思。

**单词释义:** 拘泥于字面的

### 背诵小故事

> A student was reading a book and came across the word \'literal\'. He
thought of a literal lock on a treasure chest, which could only be
opened with the exact key. It helped him remember that \'literal\' means
exactly as stated, without exaggeration or interpretation.

**一名学生在读书时遇到了单词\' literal
