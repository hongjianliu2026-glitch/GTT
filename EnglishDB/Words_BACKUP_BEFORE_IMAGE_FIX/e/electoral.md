## electoral

**英音:** _[ɪ\'lekt(ə)r(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[ɪ\'lɛktərəl]_

**译义:** *adj.选举人的,选举的*

### 短语

+ **electoral college:** _选举团_

+ **electoral district:** _选区_

+ **electoral system:** _选举制度_

+ **electoral campaign:** _竞选活动_

+ **electoral fraud:** _选举舞弊_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The electoral process was closely monitored by international
observers.**

**中文翻译:** 国际观察员密切关注选举进程。

**单词释义:** 选举的

#### 例句2

> **The electoral college determines the outcome of the presidential
election in some countries.**

**中文翻译:** 在一些国家，选举团决定总统选举的结果。

**单词释义:** 选举的

#### 例句3

> **The government is reforming the electoral system to increase
participation.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在改革选举制度以提高参与度。

**单词释义:** 选举的

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, there was a big electoral event. People were excited
to vote. The electoral process was fair and everyone hoped for a good
result. Finally, a new leader was chosen.

**在一个小镇上，有一场重大的选举活动。人们兴奋地投票。选举过程很公平，每个人都期待有好的结果。最终，选出了一位新领导。**
