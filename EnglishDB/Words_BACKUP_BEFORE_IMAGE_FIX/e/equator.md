## equator

**英音:** _[ɪ\'kweɪtə]_ **美音:** _[ɪ\'kwetɚ]_

**译义:** *n.赤道,赤道似的圈.圆*

### 短语

+ **celestial equator:** _天球赤道_

+ **equator line:** _赤道线_

+ **near the equator:** _在赤道附近_

+ **pass the equator:** _穿过赤道_

+ **climate near the equator:** _赤道附近的气候_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The equator divides the Earth into the Northern Hemisphere and
the Southern Hemisphere.**

**中文翻译:** 赤道将地球分为北半球和南半球。

**单词释义:** 赤道

#### 例句2

> **The climate near the equator is usually hot and humid.**

**中文翻译:** 赤道附近的气候通常炎热潮湿。

**单词释义:** 赤道

#### 例句3

> **Some countries lie close to the equator and enjoy abundant
sunlight throughout the year.**

**中文翻译:** 有些国家靠近赤道，全年阳光充足。

**单词释义:** 赤道

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a brave adventurer traveling along a line on the Earth. This
line is always warm and has equal days and nights. It\'s the equator!
The adventurer marvels at the unique climate and realizes this is the
dividing line of the Earth\'s halves.

**想象一位勇敢的探险家沿着地球上的一条线旅行。这条线总是很温暖，昼夜时长相等。这就是赤道！探险家惊叹于独特的气候，意识到这是地球两半的分界线。**
