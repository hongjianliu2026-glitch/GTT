## extramarital

**英音:** _[,ekstrə\'mærɪt(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,ɛkstrə\'mærɪtl]_

**译义:** *adj.私通的*

### 短语

+ **extramarital affair:** _婚外情_

+ **extramarital relationship:** _婚外关系_

+ **extramarital sex:** _婚外性行为_

+ **extramarital liaison:** _婚外私通_

+ **extramarital encounter:** _婚外邂逅_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He was involved in an extramarital affair.**

**中文翻译:** 他有一段婚外情。

**单词释义:** 婚外的

#### 例句2

> **Extramarital relationships often lead to family breakdown.**

**中文翻译:** 婚外关系常常导致家庭破裂。

**单词释义:** 婚外的

#### 例句3

> **She discovered her husband\'s extramarital activities.**

**中文翻译:** 她发现了她丈夫的婚外活动。

**单词释义:** 婚外的

### 背诵小故事

> A man was always secretive about his activities. One day, his wife
discovered he had extramarital affairs. It was a shock. \'Extramarital\'
means related to sex or a relationship outside marriage.

**一个男人总是对自己的活动遮遮掩掩。有一天，他的妻子发现他有婚外情。这令人震惊。\'extramarital\'意思是与婚外的性或关系有关。**
