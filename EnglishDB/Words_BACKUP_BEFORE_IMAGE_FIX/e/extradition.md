## extradition

**英音:** _[ekstrə\'dɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,ɛkstrə\'dɪʃən]_

**译义:** *n.(根据条约或法令对逃犯等的)引渡*

### 短语

+ **extradition treaty:** _引渡条约_

+ **extradition request:** _引渡请求_

+ **extradition procedure:** _引渡程序_

+ **extradition law:** _引渡法_

+ **extradition case:** _引渡案件_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The extradition process can be lengthy and complex.**

**中文翻译:** 引渡过程可能漫长而复杂。

**单词释义:** 引渡

#### 例句2

> **The government is considering the extradition request from the
other country.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在考虑对方的引渡请求。

**单词释义:** 引渡

#### 例句3

> **He was fighting against his extradition to face trial abroad.**

**中文翻译:** 他反对引渡到国外接受审判。

**单词释义:** 引渡

### 背诵小故事

> A criminal tried to escape justice by fleeing to another country. But
the legal process of extradition was initiated. Eventually, he was
brought back to face the law. Remember, extradition means the act of
sending someone accused or convicted of a crime to another jurisdiction.

**一名罪犯试图逃往他国逃避法律制裁。但引渡程序启动了。最终，他被带回接受法律审判。记住，extradition
指的是将被指控或被判有罪的人送回另一司法管辖区的行为。**
