## electrode

**英音:** _[ɪ\'lektrəʊd]_ **美音:** _[ɪ\'lɛktrod]_

**译义:** *n.电极*

### 短语

+ **graphite electrode:** _石墨电极_

+ **welding electrode:** _焊条；焊接电极_

+ **reference electrode:** _参比电极_

+ **metal electrode:** _金属电极_

+ **platinum electrode:** _铂电极_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The electrode in the battery needs to be replaced.**

**中文翻译:** 电池中的电极需要更换。

**单词释义:** 电极

#### 例句2

> **The scientist carefully examined the electrode during the
experiment.**

**中文翻译:** 这位科学家在实验中仔细检查了电极。

**单词释义:** 电极

#### 例句3

> **The new type of electrode improves the performance of the
device.**

**中文翻译:** 这种新型电极提高了设备的性能。

**单词释义:** 电极

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a scientist in a lab. He\'s holding a special stick that
conducts electricity. This stick is called an electrode. It helps him
conduct experiments and control the flow of electricity.

**想象一位科学家在实验室里。他拿着一根传导电流的特殊棍子。这根棍子就叫电极。它帮助他进行实验和控制电流的流动。**
