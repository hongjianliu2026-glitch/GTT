## experimenter

**英音:** _[ɪk\'sperɪməntə(r)]_ **美音:**
_[ɪk\'sperɪməntə(r)]_

**译义:** *n.实验者*

### 短语

+ **skilled experimenter:** _熟练的实验者_

+ **experienced experimenter:** _经验丰富的实验者_

+ **novice experimenter:** _新手实验者_

+ **professional experimenter:** _专业的实验者_

+ **prominent experimenter:** _杰出的实验者_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The experimenter carefully recorded the data during the
scientific experiment.**

**中文翻译:** 在科学实验期间，实验员仔细地记录数据。

**单词释义:** 实验员

#### 例句2

> **The experienced experimenter guided the new team members.**

**中文翻译:** 经验丰富的实验员指导新的团队成员。

**单词释义:** 实验员

#### 例句3

> **The experimenter made several adjustments to the equipment.**

**中文翻译:** 实验员对设备做了几次调整。

**单词释义:** 实验员

### 背诵小故事

> A brave experimenter was in a laboratory. He wore a white coat and
held test tubes. He was passionate about trying new experiments to
discover unknown truths. His efforts made great contributions to
science.

**一位勇敢的实验员在实验室里。他穿着白大褂，手持试管。他热衷于尝试新实验以发现未知的真相。他的努力为科学做出了巨大贡献。**
