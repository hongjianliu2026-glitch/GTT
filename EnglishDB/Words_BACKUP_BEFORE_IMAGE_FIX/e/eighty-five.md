## eighty-five

**英音:** _[ˈeɪti faɪv]_ **美音:** _[ˈeɪti faɪv]_

**译义:** *num.八十五*

### 短语

+ **eighty-five percent:** _百分之八十五_

+ **eighty-five years old:** _八十五岁_

+ **eighty-five dollars:** _八十五美元_

+ **eighty-five minutes:** _八十五分钟_

+ **eighty-five students:** _八十五个学生_
