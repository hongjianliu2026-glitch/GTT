## earthquake

**英音:** _[\'ɜːθkweɪk]_ **美音:** _[\'ɝθ\'kwek]_

**译义:** *n.地震,[喻]在震荡,在变动*

### 短语

+ **major earthquake:** _大地震_

+ **earthquake prediction:** _地震预测_

+ **earthquake zone:** _地震带_

+ **aftershock of earthquake:** _地震余震_

+ **earthquake damage:** _地震破坏_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The earthquake caused widespread damage to buildings.**

**中文翻译:** 这次地震对建筑物造成了广泛的破坏。

**单词释义:** 地震

#### 例句2

> **People were terrified by the powerful earthquake.**

**中文翻译:** 人们被这场强烈的地震吓坏了。

**单词释义:** 地震

#### 例句3

> **After the earthquake, rescue teams were quickly dispatched.**

**中文翻译:** 地震过后，救援队伍迅速被派遣出去。

**单词释义:** 地震

### 背诵小故事

> One day, when people were going about their normal lives on the
earth, suddenly the ground started shaking violently. Buildings were
collapsing and there was chaos everywhere. It was an earthquake, a
terrifying natural disaster.

**有一天，当人们在地球上过着正常生活时，突然地面开始剧烈摇晃。建筑物倒塌，到处一片混乱。这就是地震，一种可怕的自然灾害。**
