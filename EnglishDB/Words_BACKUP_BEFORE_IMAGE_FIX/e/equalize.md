## equalize

**英音:** _[\'iːkwəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[\'ikwəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *vt.使相等,补偿*

### 短语

+ **equalize the pressure:** _使压力均衡_

+ **equalize opportunities:** _使机会均等_

+ **equalize scores:** _使分数相等_

+ **equalize income:** _使收入平等_

+ **equalize resources:** _使资源均衡_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new policy aims to equalize educational opportunities for all
students.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策旨在为所有学生提供平等的教育机会。

**单词释义:** 使平等

#### 例句2

> **We need to equalize the pressure on both sides of the
container.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要平衡容器两侧的压力。

**单词释义:** 使相等

#### 例句3

> **The coach tried to equalize the strength of the two teams by
making some adjustments.**

**中文翻译:** 教练试图通过一些调整来平衡两队的实力。

**单词释义:** 使均衡

### 背诵小故事

> A football team was losing badly. But in the second half, they made
strategic changes to equalize the score. Finally, they had the same
number of goals as the opponent. Now you know \'equalize\' means to make
equal.

**一支足球队一开始输得很惨。但在下半场，他们做出战略调整扳平了比分。最终，他们和对手的进球数相同。现在你知道\'equalize\'意思是使平等、使相等。**
