## electrical

**英音:** _[ɪ\'lektrɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[ɪ\'lɛktrɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.电的,有关电的*

### 短语

+ **electrical engineer:** _电气工程师_

+ **electrical equipment:** _电气设备_

+ **electrical energy:** _电能_

+ **electrical system:** _电气系统_

+ **electrical circuit:** _电路_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The electrical engineer is working on a new project.**

**中文翻译:** 电气工程师正在研究一个新项目。

**单词释义:** 电气的；与电有关的

#### 例句2

> **There was a problem with the electrical system in the
building.**

**中文翻译:** 大楼内的电力系统出现问题。

**单词释义:** 电气的；用电的

#### 例句3

> **She studied electrical engineering at university.**

**中文翻译:** 她在大学学习电气工程。

**单词释义:** 电气（工程）的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was in a room full of electrical devices. The lights,
fans, and computers were all working thanks to the electrical power. He
realized that \'electrical\' means related to electricity and its uses.

**有一天，汤姆在一个满是电器的房间里。灯、风扇和电脑都靠电力在运行。他意识到'electrical'意思是与电及其用途有关的。**
