## precedence

**英音:** _[\'presɪd(ə)ns; prɪ\'siːd(ə)ns]_ **美音:**
_[\'presidəns]_

**译义:** *n.优先,居先*

### 短语

+ **give precedence to:** _优先考虑；给予优先权_

+ **take precedence over:** _优先于；比\...\...更重要_

+ **precedence rule:** _优先规则_

+ **precedence relation:** _优先关系_

+ **precedence diagram:** _优先图_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **In this company, safety always takes precedence over
productivity.**

**中文翻译:** 在这家公司，安全始终优先于生产力。

**单词释义:** 优先；优先权

#### 例句2

> **The needs of the elderly should have precedence in our
society.**

**中文翻译:** 在我们的社会中，老年人的需求应该优先考虑。

**单词释义:** 优先；优先权

#### 例句3

> **When there\'s a conflict, legal obligations take precedence.**

**中文翻译:** 当发生冲突时，法律义务优先。

**单词释义:** 优先；居先

### 背诵小故事

> A king was deciding which of his two advisors had precedence in
giving advice. The one with more wisdom and experience was given
precedence. Remember, \'precedence\' means the condition of being
considered more important than someone or something else.

**一位国王要决定他的两位顾问中谁在提建议方面具有优先权。那位更有智慧和经验的顾问被赋予了优先权。记住，\'precedence\'意思是比其他人或事物更重要的地位。**
