## preventative

**英音:** _[prɪ\'ventətɪv]_ **美音:** _[prɪ\'vɛntətɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.&n.=preventive*

### 短语

+ **preventative measure:** _预防措施_

+ **preventative medicine:** _预防医学_

+ **preventative action:** _预防行动_

+ **preventative maintenance:** _预防性维护_

+ **preventative approach:** _预防方法_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Regular exercise is a preventative measure against many
diseases.**

**中文翻译:** 经常锻炼是预防许多疾病的一种措施。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

#### 例句2

> **The new law aims to implement preventative actions to reduce
crime.**

**中文翻译:** 新法律旨在实施预防行动以减少犯罪。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

#### 例句3

> **Taking preventative steps can save a lot of trouble in the
future.**

**中文翻译:** 采取预防步骤可以在未来省去很多麻烦。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor was explaining to a patient that taking regular exercise and
having a balanced diet are preventative measures. They can stop diseases
before they happen. So, being proactive is key.

**一位医生向病人解释，定期锻炼和均衡饮食是预防措施。它们能在疾病发生之前就加以阻止。所以，积极主动是关键。**
