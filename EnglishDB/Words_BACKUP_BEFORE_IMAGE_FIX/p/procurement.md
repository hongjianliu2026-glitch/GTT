## procurement

**英音:** _[prə\'kjʊəmənt]_ **美音:** _[prə\'kjʊrmənt]_

**译义:** *n.获得,取得*

### 短语

+ **procurement process:** _采购流程_

+ **procurement department:** _采购部门_

+ **public procurement:** _公共采购_

+ **procurement contract:** _采购合同_

+ **procurement strategy:** _采购策略_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The procurement of raw materials is a crucial step in the
production process.**

**中文翻译:** 原材料的采购是生产过程中的关键步骤。

**单词释义:** 采购

#### 例句2

> **The company has a dedicated team for procurement to ensure
quality and cost-effectiveness.**

**中文翻译:** 公司有一个专门的采购团队以确保质量和成本效益。

**单词释义:** 采购

#### 例句3

> **The government\'s procurement policies aim to promote fair
competition.**

**中文翻译:** 政府的采购政策旨在促进公平竞争。

**单词释义:** 采购

### 背诵小故事

> A company was in trouble as its procurement department failed to get
essential materials. But a smart employee fixed it, ensuring smooth
procurement. Now the company thrives. PROCUREMENT means the act of
obtaining goods or services.

**一家公司陷入困境，因为其采购部门未能获得关键材料。但一位聪明的员工解决了问题，确保了顺利采购。现在公司蓬勃发展。PROCUREMENT
意思是采购；获得的行为。**
