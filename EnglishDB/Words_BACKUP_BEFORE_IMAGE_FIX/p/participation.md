## participation

**英音:** _[pɑː,tɪsɪ\'peɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[pɑr,tɪsɪ\'peʃən]_

**译义:** *n.分享,参与*

### 短语

+ **active participation:** _积极参与_

+ **voluntary participation:** _自愿参与_

+ **widespread participation:** _广泛参与_

+ **public participation:** _公众参与_

+ **student participation:** _学生参与_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His participation in the project was highly valuable.**

**中文翻译:** 他对这个项目的参与非常有价值。

**单词释义:** 参与；参加

#### 例句2

> **The participation rate in the event was surprisingly low.**

**中文翻译:** 此次活动的参与率出奇的低。

**单词释义:** 参与率；参加率

#### 例句3

> **We need more active participation from all of you.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要大家更加积极的参与。

**单词释义:** 参与；参加

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to organize a charity event. Everyone was
excited about their participation. Tom actively helped with the
planning, Mary contributed by collecting donations, and they all felt
proud of their involvement.

**一群朋友决定组织一场慈善活动。每个人都对他们的参与感到兴奋。汤姆积极帮忙策划，玛丽通过募集捐款做出贡献，他们都为自己的参与感到自豪。**
