## perspiration

**英音:** _[pɜːspɪ\'reɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,pɝspə\'reʃən]_

**译义:** *n.排汗*

### 短语

+ **excessive perspiration:** _过度出汗_

+ **perspiration odor:** _汗味_

+ **reduce perspiration:** _减少出汗_

+ **perspiration glands:** _汗腺_

+ **night perspiration:** _盗汗_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The athlete was covered in perspiration after the intense
workout.**

**中文翻译:** 这位运动员在剧烈锻炼后浑身是汗。

**单词释义:** 汗水

#### 例句2

> **Perspiration dripped from his forehead as he carried the heavy
box.**

**中文翻译:** 他搬着那个重箱子时，汗水从额头滴下来。

**单词释义:** 汗水

#### 例句3

> **Her face was shiny with perspiration in the hot summer sun.**

**中文翻译:** 在炎热的夏日阳光下，她的脸上满是汗水，闪闪发亮。

**单词释义:** 汗水

### 背诵小故事

> One hot summer day, Tom was running a marathon. His face was covered
in perspiration as he pushed himself hard. But he didn\'t give up and
finally crossed the finish line, all thanks to his determination and the
perspiration that showed his effort.

**一个炎热的夏日，汤姆正在跑马拉松。他的脸上满是汗水，因为他在努力坚持。但他没有放弃，最终冲过了终点线，这都归功于他的决心和那代表他努力的汗水。**
