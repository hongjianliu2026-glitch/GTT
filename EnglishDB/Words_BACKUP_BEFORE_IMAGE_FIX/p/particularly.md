## particularly

**英音:** _[pə\'tɪkjʊləlɪ]_ **美音:** _[pɚ\'tɪkjəlɚlɪ]_

**译义:** *adv.独特地,显著地*

### 短语

+ **particularly important:** _特别重要_

+ **particularly difficult:** _特别困难_

+ **particularly sensitive:** _特别敏感_

+ **particularly useful:** _特别有用_

+ **particularly interesting:** _特别有趣_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I particularly like the blue dress.**

**中文翻译:** 我特别喜欢蓝色的衣服。

**单词释义:** 特别；尤其

#### 例句2

> **The weather has been particularly bad this week.**

**中文翻译:** 这周的天气特别糟糕。

**单词释义:** 特别；尤其

#### 例句3

> **She was particularly proud of her achievements.**

**中文翻译:** 她对自己的成就感到特别自豪。

**单词释义:** 特别；尤其

### 背诵小故事

> A traveler was exploring many places. Everywhere was nice, but one
town was particularly charming. It had beautiful gardens and friendly
people. The traveler remembered this town especially. So, particularly
means especially.

**一位旅行者在探索许多地方。每个地方都不错，但有一个小镇特别迷人。它有美丽的花园和友好的人们。旅行者尤其记住了这个小镇。所以，particularly
意思是尤其。**
