## provenance

**英音:** _[\'prɒv(ə)nəns]_ **美音:** _[\'prɑvənəns]_

**译义:** *n.起源,出处*

### 短语

+ **trace the provenance:** _追溯来源_

+ **unknown provenance:** _未知的出处_

+ **documented provenance:** _有记录的来源_

+ **questionable provenance:** _可疑的出处_

+ **establish provenance:** _确立来源_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The provenance of this antique vase is unknown.**

**中文翻译:** 这个古董花瓶的出处不明。

**单词释义:** 出处；起源

#### 例句2

> **We need to verify the provenance of these documents before
accepting them as evidence.**

**中文翻译:** 在接受这些文件作为证据之前，我们需要验证它们的出处。

**单词释义:** 来源

#### 例句3

> **The provenance of the painting was traced back to a famous
artist\'s studio.**

**中文翻译:** 这幅画的出处可以追溯到一位著名艺术家的工作室。

**单词释义:** 出处

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught with stolen jewels. When asked about the
provenance of the jewels, he stuttered and couldn\'t answer. Finally, he
confessed that they were stolen. Remember, \'provenance\' is about the
source or origin.

**一个小偷被抓到带着偷来的珠宝。当被问到这些珠宝的出处时，他结结巴巴答不上来。最后，他承认是偷来的。记住，'provenance'就是指来源或出处。**
