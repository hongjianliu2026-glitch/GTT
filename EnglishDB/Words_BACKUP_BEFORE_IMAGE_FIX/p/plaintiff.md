## plaintiff

**英音:** _[\'pleɪntɪf]_ **美音:** _[\'plentɪf]_

**译义:** *n.[律]起诉人,原告*

### 短语

+ **plaintiff in chief:** _主诉原告_

+ **plaintiff witness:** _原告证人_

+ **original plaintiff:** _原诉原告_

+ **the plaintiff\'s case:** _原告的案件_

+ **prosecuting plaintiff:** _起诉原告_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The plaintiff alleged that the defendant had committed fraud.**

**中文翻译:** 原告声称被告犯了欺诈罪。

**单词释义:** 原告

#### 例句2

> **The plaintiff presented strong evidence in court.**

**中文翻译:** 原告在法庭上出示了有力的证据。

**单词释义:** 原告

#### 例句3

> **The plaintiff\'s lawyer made a compelling argument.**

**中文翻译:** 原告的律师提出了令人信服的论点。

**单词释义:** 原告

### 背诵小故事

> In a courtroom drama, a poor man was harmed by a big company. He
decided to fight for justice and became the plaintiff. With courage, he
presented his case, hoping for a fair outcome.
