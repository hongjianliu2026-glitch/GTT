## participatory

**英音:** _[pɑ:tɪsɪ\'peɪtərɪ]_ **美音:**
_[pɑr\'tɪsəpətɔri]_

**译义:** *adj.供人分享的*

### 短语

+ **participatory democracy:** _参与式民主_

+ **participatory approach:** _参与式方法_

+ **participatory process:** _参与过程_

+ **participatory budgeting:** _参与式预算_

+ **participatory research:** _参与式研究_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The project emphasizes a participatory approach to community
development.**

**中文翻译:** 该项目强调社区发展的参与性方法。

**单词释义:** 参与式的

#### 例句2

> **We need to create a more participatory environment in the
workplace.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要在工作场所创造一个更具参与性的环境。

**单词释义:** 参与性的

#### 例句3

> **The participatory nature of the event made it very popular.**

**中文翻译:** 该活动的参与性使其非常受欢迎。

**单词释义:** 参与的

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to organize a participatory party.
Everyone was involved in planning, decorating, and preparing food. It
was so much fun because of the active participation of all. Remember,
\'participatory\' means involving the participation of all.

**一群朋友决定组织一个参与式的聚会。每个人都参与到策划、装饰和准备食物中。因为所有人的积极参与，聚会非常有趣。记住，\'participatory\'意思是涉及所有人的参与。**
