## peptide

**英音:** _[\'peptaɪd]_ **美音:** _[\'pɛptaɪd]_

**译义:** *n.缩氨酸*

### 短语

+ **peptide bond:** _肽键_

+ **peptide hormone:** _肽类激素_

+ **bioactive peptide:** _生物活性肽_

+ **cyclic peptide:** _环肽_

+ **peptide sequence:** _肽序列_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **This peptide has significant biological activity.**

**中文翻译:** 这种肽具有显著的生物活性。

**单词释义:** 肽

#### 例句2

> **The scientist is studying the structure of the peptide.**

**中文翻译:** 这位科学家正在研究肽的结构。

**单词释义:** 肽

#### 例句3

> **The new drug contains a special peptide that targets cancer
cells.**

**中文翻译:** 这种新药含有一种针对癌细胞的特殊肽。

**单词释义:** 肽

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working in the lab, trying to understand a special
substance. He called it \'peptide\'. It was like a key that unlocked the
secrets of cells. With peptides, he could solve many mysteries of life.

**一位科学家在实验室工作，试图了解一种特殊物质。他称其为"肽"。它就像一把钥匙，解开了细胞的秘密。有了肽，他就能解开许多生命的谜团。**
