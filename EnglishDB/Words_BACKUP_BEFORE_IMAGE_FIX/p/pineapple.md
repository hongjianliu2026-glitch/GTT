## pineapple

**英音:** _[\'paɪnæp(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[\'paɪn\'æpl]_

**译义:** *n.凤梨,菠萝*

### 短语

+ **pineapple juice:** _菠萝汁_

+ **fresh pineapple:** _新鲜菠萝_

+ **canned pineapple:** _罐装菠萝_

+ **pineapple chunks:** _菠萝块_

+ **sweet pineapple:** _甜菠萝_

### 背诵小故事

> A long time ago, people found a fruit that looked like a pine cone
and tasted sweet. They named it \'pineapple\' because it combined the
features of a pine cone and an apple. So, whenever you see pineapple,
remember this unique mix.

**很久以前，人们发现一种水果，看起来像松果，尝起来很甜。他们把它命名为"pineapple"，因为它结合了松果和苹果的特点。所以，每当你看到菠萝，就记住这种独特的组合。**
