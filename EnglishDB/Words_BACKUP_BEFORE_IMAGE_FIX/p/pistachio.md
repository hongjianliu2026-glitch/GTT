## pistachio

**英音:** _[pɪ\'stɑːʃɪəʊ; pɪ\'stætʃəʊ]_ **美音:**
_[pɪ\'stæʃɪo]_

**译义:** *n.[植]阿月浑子树,阿月浑子果实,淡黄绿色*

### 短语

+ **pistachio nut:** _开心果_

+ **pistachio ice cream:** _开心果冰淇淋_

+ **pistachio green:** _开心果绿（一种颜色）_

+ **roasted pistachio:** _烤开心果_

+ **salted pistachio:** _咸开心果_

\'pistachio\'. It cracked the hard shell and enjoyed the delicious green
kernel inside. From then on, it always remembered the taste of
pistachio.
