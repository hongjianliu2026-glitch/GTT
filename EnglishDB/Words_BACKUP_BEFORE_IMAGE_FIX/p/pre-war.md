## pre-war

**英音:** _[priː wɔː]_ **美音:** _[pri wɔr]_

**译义:** *adj.战前的；n.战前时期*

### 短语

+ **pre-war era:** _战前时期_

+ **pre-war economy:** _战前经济_

+ **pre-war conditions:** _战前状况_

+ **pre-war society:** _战前社会_

+ **pre-war buildings:** _战前的建筑_

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a time before a big war. There were peaceful streets and
happy people. But as the pre-war days passed, tensions grew. \'Pre-war\'
means the period before the war began.

**想象一个在大战之前的时期。有宁静的街道和快乐的人们。但随着战前的日子过去，紧张局势加剧。\'pre-war\'
意思是战争开始之前的时期。**
