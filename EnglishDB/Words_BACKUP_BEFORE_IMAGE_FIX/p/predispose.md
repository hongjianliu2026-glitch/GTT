## predispose

**英音:** _[,priːdɪ\'spəʊz]_ **美音:** _[\'pridɪ\'spoz]_

**译义:** *vt.预先安排,使偏向于*

### 短语

+ **predispose to:** _使倾向于；使易患_

+ **predispose for:** _为\...\...预先准备；使适合于_

+ **predispose towards:** _使偏向于_

+ **be predisposed to:** _有\...\...的倾向；容易\...\..._

+ **genetic predispose:** _遗传易感性_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **A family history of heart disease may predispose you to having
the same condition.**

**中文翻译:** 心脏病家族史可能使您容易患上同样的疾病。

**单词释义:** 使倾向于；使易受影响

#### 例句2

> **Certain genes can predispose individuals to certain types of
cancer.**

**中文翻译:** 某些基因会使个体易患某些类型的癌症。

**单词释义:** 使倾向于；使易患

#### 例句3

> **Early childhood experiences can predispose a person to a
particular personality trait.**

**中文翻译:** 童年时期的经历可以使一个人倾向于某种特定的人格特质。

**单词释义:** 使倾向于；使易形成

### 背诵小故事

> A little girl was often exposed to sweet treats, which predisposed
her to have a toothache. Later, she learned that such habits could cause
problems. Predispose means to make someone likely to have a particular
condition or behave in a particular way.
