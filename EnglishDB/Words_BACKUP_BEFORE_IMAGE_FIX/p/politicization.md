## politicization

**英音:** _[ˌpɒlɪtɪsaɪˈzeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[ˌpɑːlɪtɪsaɪˈzeɪʃən]_

**译义:** *n.政治化；使政治化*

### 短语

+ **avoid politicization:** _避免政治化_

+ **the politicization of issue:** _问题的政治化_

+ **resist politicization:** _抵制政治化_

+ **reduce politicization:** _减少政治化_

+ **prevent politicization:** _防止政治化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The politicization of the issue has made it more difficult to
find a practical solution.**

**中文翻译:**
这个问题的政治化使得找到一个实际的解决方案变得更加困难。

**单词释义:** 政治化

#### 例句2

> **We must avoid the politicization of economic policies.**

**中文翻译:** 我们必须避免经济政策的政治化。

**单词释义:** 政治化

#### 例句3

> **The politicization of environmental problems has led to
ineffective measures being taken.**

**中文翻译:** 环境问题的政治化导致了无效措施的采取。

**单词释义:** 政治化

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends were discussing a simple event, but one person
kept trying to turn it into a political issue. This act of making
something ordinary overly political was called politicization.

**一群朋友在讨论一件简单的事，但有一个人一直试图把它变成政治问题。这种让普通事物变得过度政治化的行为就叫做politicization。**
