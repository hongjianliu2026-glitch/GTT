## physics

**英音:** _[\'fɪzɪks]_ **美音:** _[\'fɪzɪks]_

**译义:** *n.物理学*

### 短语

+ **quantum physics:** _量子物理学_

+ **nuclear physics:** _核物理学_

+ **experimental physics:** _实验物理学_

+ **theoretical physics:** _理论物理学_

+ **particle physics:** _粒子物理学_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little boy was very curious about why things fall to the
ground. He started to explore and study. This process was like opening
the door to the world of \'physics\', which is all about understanding
how nature works.

**有一天，一个小男孩对为什么东西会掉到地上非常好奇。他开始探索和研究。这个过程就像是打开了'物理学'的大门，物理学就是关于理解自然如何运作的。**
