## paradoxical

**英音:** _[,pærə\'dɒksɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,pærə\'dɑksɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.荒谬的*

### 短语

+ **paradoxical situation:** _自相矛盾的情况_

+ **paradoxical effect:** _矛盾的效果_

+ **paradoxical statement:** _悖论性陈述_

+ **paradoxical phenomenon:** _矛盾的现象_

+ **paradoxical behavior:** _矛盾的行为_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The situation was paradoxical: the more she tried to please him,
the less he seemed to care.**

**中文翻译:** 这种情况很矛盾：她越是试图取悦他，他似乎就越不在乎。

**单词释义:** 矛盾的

#### 例句2

> **It is paradoxical that some of the poorest people are the
happiest.**

**中文翻译:** 有些最贫穷的人却是最快乐的，这很矛盾。

**单词释义:** 矛盾的

#### 例句3

> **His behavior was paradoxical; he was friendly one moment and
hostile the next.**

**中文翻译:** 他的行为很矛盾；一会儿友好，一会儿充满敌意。

**单词释义:** 矛盾的

### 背诵小故事

> A man claimed he hated parties but always showed up at every one.
This was paradoxical. His words and actions didn\'t match. It\'s like
saying you love rain but always carry an umbrella.

**一个男人声称他讨厌聚会，但每次聚会都出现。这很矛盾。他的言语和行动不相符。就像说你喜欢雨却总是带着伞。**
