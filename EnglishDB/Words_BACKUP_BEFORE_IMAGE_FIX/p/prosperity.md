## prosperity

**英音:** _[prɒ\'sperɪtɪ]_ **美音:** _[prɑ\'spɛrətɪ]_

**译义:** *n.繁荣*

### 短语

+ **economic prosperity:** _经济繁荣_

+ **prosperity and growth:** _繁荣与增长_

+ **period of prosperity:** _繁荣时期_

+ **achieve prosperity:** _实现繁荣_

+ **enjoy prosperity:** _享受繁荣_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The country is enjoying a period of peace and prosperity.**

**中文翻译:** 国家正处于和平繁荣时期。

**单词释义:** 繁荣

#### 例句2

> **We wish you health, happiness and prosperity in the new year.**

**中文翻译:** 我们祝您在新的一年里健康、幸福、繁荣。

**单词释义:** 繁荣；兴旺

#### 例句3

> **The new policies have brought prosperity to the region.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策给该地区带来了繁荣。

**单词释义:** 繁荣；昌盛

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a small village was in poverty. But with the
villagers\' hard work and unity, they achieved prosperity. Crops grew
well, businesses flourished, and everyone lived a happy life. Remember,
prosperity means success and wealth.

**从前，一个小村庄处于贫困之中。但通过村民们的辛勤工作和团结，他们实现了繁荣。庄稼长得好，生意兴隆，每个人都过上了幸福的生活。记住，prosperity
意味着成功和富有。**
