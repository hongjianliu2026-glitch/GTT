## pimple

**英音:** _[\'pɪmp(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[\'pɪmpl]_

**译义:** *n.[医]丘疹,面泡,疙瘩*

### 短语

+ **get pimples:** _长痘痘_

+ **pop a pimple:** _挤痘痘_

+ **pimple cream:** _祛痘霜_

+ **pimple problem:** _痘痘问题_

+ **treat pimples:** _治疗痘痘_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I have a pimple on my nose.**

**中文翻译:** 我鼻子上长了一个痘痘。

**单词释义:** 痘痘

#### 例句2

> **The pimple on her chin made her feel self-conscious.**

**中文翻译:** 下巴上的痘痘让她感觉不自在。

**单词释义:** 痘痘

#### 例句3

> **He tried to pop the pimple, but it just got worse.**

**中文翻译:** 他试图挤掉痘痘，但情况变得更糟。

**单词释义:** 痘痘

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom found a pimple on his face. It was red and annoying. He
was so worried and tried every way to get rid of it. Finally, with
proper care, the pimple disappeared.
