## publicize

**英音:** _[ˈpʌblɪsaɪz]_ **美音:** _[ˈpʌblɪsaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.宣扬*

### 短语

+ **publicize widely:** _广泛宣传_

+ **publicize actively:** _积极宣传_

+ **publicize thoroughly:** _彻底宣传_

+ **publicize effectively:** _有效宣传_

+ **publicize vigorously:** _大力宣传_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company decided to publicize its new product through a series
of advertisements.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司决定通过一系列广告来宣传其新产品。

**单词释义:** 宣传

#### 例句2

> **The media often publicize the achievements of famous people.**

**中文翻译:** 媒体经常宣传名人的成就。

**单词释义:** 宣扬

#### 例句3

> **It\'s important to publicize the dangers of smoking to prevent
more people from getting addicted.**

**中文翻译:** 宣传吸烟的危害以防止更多人上瘾非常重要。

**单词释义:** 公布

### 背诵小故事

> A famous singer decided to publicize her new song. She held a big
concert and invited many media. Through this event, her song was known
to everyone.

**一位著名歌手决定宣传她的新歌。她举办了一场大型演唱会并邀请了众多媒体。通过这次活动，她的歌被所有人知晓。**
