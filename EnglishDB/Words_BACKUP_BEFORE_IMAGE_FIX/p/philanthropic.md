## philanthropic

**英音:** _[,fɪlən\'θrɒpɪk]_ **美音:**
_[,fɪlən\'θrɑpɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.博爱的*

### 短语

+ **philanthropic act:** _慈善行为_

+ **philanthropic organization:** _慈善组织_

+ **philanthropic donation:** _慈善捐赠_

+ **philanthropic foundation:** _慈善基金会_

+ **philanthropic spirit:** _慈善精神_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The philanthropic organization donated millions of dollars to
support education in underprivileged areas.**

**中文翻译:** 这个慈善组织捐赠了数百万美元来支持贫困地区的教育。

**单词释义:** 慈善的

#### 例句2

> **She has always been involved in philanthropic activities to help
those in need.**

**中文翻译:** 她一直参与慈善活动来帮助那些需要帮助的人。

**单词释义:** 慈善的

#### 例句3

> **His philanthropic efforts have made a significant difference in
the lives of many.**

**中文翻译:** 他的慈善努力对许多人的生活产生了重大影响。

**单词释义:** 慈善的

### 背诵小故事

> A rich man named Phil was always kind-hearted. He spent a lot of
money to help poor children go to school. His actions were called
philanthropic. People praised him for his love and care for others.

**有个叫菲尔的富人，心地一直很善良。他花很多钱帮助贫困孩子上学。他的这种行为被称为慈善的。人们称赞他对他人的关爱。**
