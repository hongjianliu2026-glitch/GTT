## precipitously

**英音:** _[prɪ\'sɪpɪtəsli]_ **美音:**
_[prɪ\'sɪpɪtəsli]_

**译义:** *adv. 陡峭地；急速地；仓促地*

### 短语

+ **fall precipitously:** _急剧下降_

+ **drop precipitously:** _陡然下跌_

+ **increase precipitously:** _急剧增加_

+ **decline precipitously:** _急剧衰退_

+ **rise precipitously:** _急剧上升_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The stock market declined precipitously last week.**

**中文翻译:** 上周股市急剧下跌。

**单词释义:** 急剧地

#### 例句2

> **The cliff dropped precipitously to the sea below.**

**中文翻译:** 悬崖陡峭地直落到下面的大海。

**单词释义:** 陡峭地

#### 例句3

> **Her health deteriorated precipitously after the accident.**

**中文翻译:** 事故发生后，她的健康状况急剧恶化。

**单词释义:** 急剧地

### 背诵小故事

> A brave climber was ascending a mountain. Suddenly, he lost his
footing and fell precipitously. It was a terrifying moment. Luckily, he
was saved by a rope. \'Precipitously\' here means very quickly and
steeply.

**一位勇敢的登山者正在登山。突然，他失足陡峭快速地跌落。这是可怕的一刻。幸运的是，一根绳子救了他。这里\'precipitously\'意思是快速陡峭地。**
