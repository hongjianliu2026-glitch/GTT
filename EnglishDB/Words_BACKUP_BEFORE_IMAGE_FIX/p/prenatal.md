## prenatal

**英音:** _[priː\'neɪt(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[,pri\'netl]_

**译义:** *adj.出生以前的*

### 短语

+ **prenatal care:** _产前护理_

+ **prenatal diagnosis:** _产前诊断_

+ **prenatal education:** _产前教育_

+ **prenatal period:** _产前时期_

+ **prenatal test:** _产前检查_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She underwent prenatal check-ups regularly during her
pregnancy.**

**中文翻译:** 她在怀孕期间定期进行产前检查。

**单词释义:** 产前的

#### 例句2

> **Prenatal care is crucial for the health of the mother and the
baby.**

**中文翻译:** 产前护理对母亲和婴儿的健康至关重要。

**单词释义:** 产前的

#### 例句3

> **The doctor recommended some prenatal vitamins for her.**

**中文翻译:** 医生为她推荐了一些产前维生素。

**单词释义:** 产前的

### 背诵小故事

> A pregnant woman was very careful during her prenatal period. She ate
healthy food, took vitamins, and went for regular check-ups to ensure
the baby\'s health. Remember, prenatal means before birth.

**一位孕妇在产前阶段非常小心。她吃健康的食物，服用维生素，并定期去做检查以确保宝宝的健康。记住，prenatal
意思是出生前的。**
