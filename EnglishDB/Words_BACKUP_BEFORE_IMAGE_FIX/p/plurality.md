## plurality

**英音:** _[plʊə\'rælɪtɪ]_ **美音:** _[plʊ\'ræləti]_

**译义:** *n.复数,较大数,多数,兼职*

### 短语

+ **plurality of voices:** _多种声音_

+ **plurality of opinions:** _多种意见_

+ **plurality of choices:** _多种选择_

+ **plurality of cultures:** _多元文化_

+ **plurality of candidates:** _多个候选人_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The plurality of votes went to the independent candidate.**

**中文翻译:** 多数票投给了独立候选人。

**单词释义:** 多数

#### 例句2

> **In a plurality system, the candidate with the most votes
wins.**

**中文翻译:** 在多数制中，得票最多的候选人获胜。

**单词释义:** 多数

#### 例句3

> **The plurality of opinions made it difficult to reach a
consensus.**

**中文翻译:** 意见较多，难以达成共识。

**单词释义:** 多元化

### 背诵小故事

> A king wanted to choose a successor. Many candidates competed. In the
end, there was no clear winner. It was a situation of plurality, with
several having almost equal chances.

**一位国王想要挑选继承人。许多候选人竞争。最后，没有明确的获胜者。这是一种多元化的情况，有几个人机会几乎均等。**
