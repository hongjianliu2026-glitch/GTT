## retardation

**英音:** _[,riːtɑː\'deɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[,ritɑr\'deʃən]_

**译义:** *n.延迟*

### 短语

+ **mental retardation:** _智力迟钝；智力障碍_

+ **growth retardation:** _生长迟缓_

+ **developmental retardation:** _发育迟缓_

+ **retardation factor:** _延迟因子_

+ **thermal retardation:** _热延迟_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The retardation of the economic growth was a cause for
concern.**

**中文翻译:** 经济增长的迟缓是一个令人担忧的原因。

**单词释义:** 迟缓；延迟

#### 例句2

> **Mental retardation can present significant challenges for
individuals and their families.**

**中文翻译:** 智力迟钝会给个人和他们的家庭带来重大挑战。

**单词释义:** 智力迟钝

#### 例句3

> **The retardation in the project\'s progress was due to unforeseen
circumstances.**

**中文翻译:** 项目进展的迟缓是由于不可预见的情况。

**单词释义:** 迟缓；阻碍

### 背诵小故事

> A little rabbit was running in a race. But it got hurt and its speed
showed retardation. It was sad but still tried its best. Finally,
everyone understood that retardation doesn\'t mean giving up.

**一只小兔子在赛跑。但它受伤了，速度显示出迟缓。它很伤心，但仍尽力而为。最后，大家都明白迟缓并不意味着放弃。**
