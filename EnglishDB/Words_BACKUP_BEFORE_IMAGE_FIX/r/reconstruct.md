## reconstruct

**英音:** _[riːkən\'strʌkt]_ **美音:**
_[,rikən\'strʌkt]_

**译义:** *v.重建,改造,推想*

### 短语

+ **reconstruct history:** _重现历史_

+ **reconstruct a building:** _重建一座建筑_

+ **reconstruct an event:** _重构一个事件_

+ **reconstruct a scene:** _重现一个场景_

+ **reconstruct a face:** _重塑一张脸_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The city is trying to reconstruct the old historical
buildings.**

**中文翻译:** 该市正在努力重建古老的历史建筑。

**单词释义:** 重建

#### 例句2

> **Scientists are working hard to reconstruct the dinosaur\'s
skeleton.**

**中文翻译:** 科学家们正在努力重建恐龙的骨骼。

**单词释义:** 重构

#### 例句3

> **After the earthquake, they had to reconstruct their lives from
scratch.**

**中文翻译:** 地震发生后，他们不得不从头开始重建生活。

**单词释义:** 重建

### 背诵小故事

> A city was destroyed by a disaster. But people were determined to
reconstruct it. They worked hard, using new materials and plans.
Finally, the city was reborn, beautiful and strong.

**一座城市被灾难摧毁。但人们决心重建它。他们努力工作，使用新的材料和规划。最终，城市获得新生，美丽且坚固。**
