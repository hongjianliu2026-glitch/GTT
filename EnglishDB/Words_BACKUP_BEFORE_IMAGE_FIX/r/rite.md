## rite

**英音:** _[raɪt]_ **美音:** _[raɪt]_

**译义:** *n.仪式,典礼,习俗,惯例,礼拜式,教派*

### 短语

+ **religious rite:** _宗教仪式_

+ **initiation rite:** _入会仪式_

+ **funeral rite:** _葬礼仪式_

+ **marriage rite:** _婚礼仪式_

+ **coming-of-age rite:** _成人仪式_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The wedding rite was beautiful and touching.**

**中文翻译:** 婚礼仪式美丽而感人。

**单词释义:** 仪式

#### 例句2

> **The religious rite is held every year.**

**中文翻译:** 每年都会举行宗教仪式。

**单词释义:** 仪式

#### 例句3

> **They performed the ancient rite with great solemnity.**

**中文翻译:** 他们非常庄严地举行了古老的仪式。

**单词释义:** 仪式

### 背诵小故事

> A little girl was lost in a forest. When she found a path, she felt
it was a rite of passage to get back home safely. Finally, she succeeded
and was very happy.
