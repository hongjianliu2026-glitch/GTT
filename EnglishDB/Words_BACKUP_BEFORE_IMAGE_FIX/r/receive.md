## receive

**英音:** _[rɪ\'siːv]_ **美音:** _[rɪ\'siv]_

**译义:** *vt.收到,接到,接收,遭到,受到,接待,接见*

### 短语

+ **receive guests:** _接待客人_

+ **receive a letter:** _收到一封信_

+ **receive treatment:** _接受治疗_

+ **receive an award:** _获得奖项_

+ **receive education:** _接受教育_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I received a letter from my friend yesterday.**

**中文翻译:** 昨天我收到朋友的来信。

**单词释义:** 收到

#### 例句2

> **The company received many complaints about its products.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司收到了许多有关其产品的投诉。

**单词释义:** 收到

#### 例句3

> **She received a warm welcome when she arrived.**

**中文翻译:** 她抵达时受到热烈欢迎。

**单词释义:** 受到

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was at home waiting excitedly. A courier came and
knocked on the door. Tom opened it and happily received a big gift box.
It was a surprise from his friend.
