## reasonably

**英音:** _[\'riːznəblɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'riznəbli]_

**译义:** *adv.适度地,相当地*

### 短语

+ **reasonably priced:** _价格合理_

+ **reasonably sure:** _相当肯定_

+ **reasonably accurate:** _相当准确_

+ **reasonably expected:** _合理预期_

+ **reasonably possible:** _相当可能_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The hotel was reasonably priced and clean.**

**中文翻译:** 这家酒店价格合理而且干净。

**单词释义:** 合理地

#### 例句2

> **She performed reasonably well in the exam.**

**中文翻译:** 她在考试中表现相当不错。

**单词释义:** 相当地

#### 例句3

> **You should be reasonably careful when crossing the road.**

**中文翻译:** 过马路时您应该相当小心。

**单词释义:** 适度地

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a detective was solving a case. He thought
reasonably and analyzed every clue carefully. Finally, he found the
truth. \'reasonably\' here means in a sensible and fair way.

**从前，有个侦探在破案。他思考得合情合理，仔细分析每一条线索。最后，他找到了真相。'reasonably'在这里指以明智和公平的方式。**
