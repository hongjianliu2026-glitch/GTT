## reconcile

**英音:** _[\'rek(ə)nsaɪl]_ **美音:** _[\'rɛkənsaɪl]_

**译义:** *vt.使和解,使和谐,使顺从*

### 短语

+ **reconcile with:** _与\...和解；与\...和好_

+ **reconcile oneself to:** _使自己顺从于；使自己甘心于_

+ **be reconciled to:** _甘心于；接受_

+ **reconcile differences:** _调和分歧；消除分歧_

+ **reconcile accounts:** _核对账目；使账目相符_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **It took a long time for him to reconcile with his father after
the argument.**

**中文翻译:** 争吵后，他花了很长时间才与父亲和解。

**单词释义:** 和解；调和

#### 例句2

> **She tried to reconcile her career and family life.**

**中文翻译:** 她试图协调事业和家庭生活。

**单词释义:** 协调；使一致

#### 例句3

> **The accountant had to reconcile the accounts at the end of the
month.**

**中文翻译:** 会计必须在月底核对账目。

**单词释义:** 核对；使相符

### 背诵小故事

> A couple had a big fight. But they thought of the happy times and
decided to make up. They finally reconciled and their love became
