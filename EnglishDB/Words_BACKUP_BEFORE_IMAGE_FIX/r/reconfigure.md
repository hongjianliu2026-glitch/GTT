## reconfigure

**英音:** _[riːkən\'fɪgə]_ **美音:** _[,rikən\'fɪɡjɚ]_

**译义:** *v.重新装配,改装*

### 短语

+ **reconfigure system:** _重新配置系统_

+ **reconfigure settings:** _重新配置设置_

+ **reconfigure network:** _重新配置网络_

+ **reconfigure equipment:** _重新配置设备_

+ **reconfigure layout:** _重新配置布局_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The IT team had to reconfigure the server to improve its
performance.**

**中文翻译:** IT 团队必须重新配置服务器以提高其性能。

**单词释义:** 重新配置

#### 例句2

> **We need to reconfigure the furniture layout in the living room to
make more space.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要重新配置客厅的家具布局，以腾出更多空间。

**单词释义:** 重新配置

#### 例句3

> **The engineer was tasked with reconfiguring the manufacturing
process to increase efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 工程师的任务是重新配置制造流程以提高效率。

**单词释义:** 重新配置

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a spaceship that needs to be reconfigured for a new mission.
Its systems and layouts are changed to adapt. Just like that,
\'reconfigure\' means to change the arrangement or setup of something
again.

**想象一艘太空飞船需要为新的任务重新配置。它的系统和布局都被改变以适应。就像这样，'reconfigure'意思是再次改变某物的排列或设置。**
