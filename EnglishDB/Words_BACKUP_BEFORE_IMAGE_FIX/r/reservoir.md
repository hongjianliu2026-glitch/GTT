## reservoir

**英音:** _[\'rezəvwɑ: (r)]_ **美音:** _[\'rɛzɚ,vɔr]_

**译义:** *n.水库,蓄水池*

### 短语

+ **reservoir capacity:** _水库容量_

+ **oil reservoir:** _储油层_

+ **reservoir engineer:** _油藏工程师_

+ **reservoir pressure:** _油层压力_

+ **water reservoir:** _蓄水池_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The reservoir supplies water to the nearby town.**

**中文翻译:** 这个水库为附近的城镇供水。

**单词释义:** 水库

#### 例句2

> **He has a great reservoir of knowledge about history.**

**中文翻译:** 他有丰富的历史知识储备。

**单词释义:** 储备；蓄积

#### 例句3

> **The human body is a reservoir of energy.**

**中文翻译:** 人体是能量的储存库。

**单词释义:** 储存库

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a village was suffering from a drought. But they
found a hidden RESERVOIR in the mountains. It was like a treasure chest
of water, saving the village. Remember, a reservoir is a large storage
place for water.

**从前，有个村庄遭遇干旱。但他们在山里发现了一个隐藏的水库。它就像一个装满水的宝箱，拯救了村庄。记住，reservoir
就是大型的储水之地。**
