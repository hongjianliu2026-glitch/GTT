## recreational

**英音:** _[rekrɪ\'eɪʃənl]_ **美音:** _[,rɛkri\'eʃənəl]_

**译义:** *adj.休养的,娱乐的*

### 短语

+ **recreational activities:** _娱乐活动_

+ **recreational facilities:** _娱乐设施_

+ **recreational vehicle:** _休闲车；房车_

+ **recreational area:** _娱乐区；休闲区_

+ **recreational fishing:** _休闲钓鱼_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Recreational activities like hiking and cycling are very
popular.**

**中文翻译:** 远足和骑自行车等休闲活动非常受欢迎。

**单词释义:** 娱乐的；消遣的

#### 例句2

> **The park provides recreational facilities for children.**

**中文翻译:** 公园为儿童提供游乐设施。

**单词释义:** 娱乐的；消遣的

#### 例句3

> **He spends his weekends on recreational pursuits.**

**中文翻译:** 他利用周末进行休闲活动。

**单词释义:** 娱乐的；消遣的

### 背诵小故事

camping, played games, and enjoyed the beautiful nature. It was a time
for relaxation and fun. Recreational means related to enjoyment and
