## recrimination

**英音:** _[rɪ,krɪmɪ\'neɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[rɪ,krɪmɪ\'neʃən]_

**译义:** *n.揭丑,反责*

### 短语

+ **mutual recrimination:** _相互指责_

+ **recrimination and accusation:** _指责和控告_

+ **avoid recrimination:** _避免指责_

+ **endless recrimination:** _无休止的指责_

+ **recrimination among colleagues:** _同事之间的指责_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The argument descended into a cycle of recrimination.**

**中文翻译:** 争论陷入了互相指责的循环。

**单词释义:** 相互指责

#### 例句2

> **There was a lot of recrimination between the two teams after the
match.**

**中文翻译:** 赛后，两队之间发生了很多争吵。

**单词释义:** 相互指责

#### 例句3

> **The divorce proceedings were filled with recrimination.**

**中文翻译:** 离婚诉讼充满了相互指责。

**单词释义:** 相互指责

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught and started recrimination, blaming the police for
not giving him a chance. But his words were just an attempt to shift the
blame. Remember, recrimination is about blaming others in return.

**一个小偷被抓住后开始反责，指责警察不给他机会。但他的话只是试图推卸责任。记住，recrimination
就是反过来指责他人。**
