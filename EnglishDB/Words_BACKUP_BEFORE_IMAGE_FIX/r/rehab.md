## rehab

**英音:** _[ˈriːhæb]_ **美音:** _[ˈriːhæb]_

**译义:** * (=rehabilitation)n.修复,复兴,恢复名誉,复职*

### 短语

+ **rehab center:** _康复中心_

+ **rehab facility:** _康复设施_

+ **drug rehab:** _戒毒康复_

+ **alcohol rehab:** _戒酒康复_

+ **rehab program:** _康复计划_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He went to rehab to overcome his drug addiction.**

**中文翻译:** 他去了康复中心以克服毒瘾。

**单词释义:** 康复中心；修复

#### 例句2

> **After the accident, she needed rehab to regain her mobility.**

**中文翻译:** 事故发生后，她需要康复治疗来恢复行动能力。

**单词释义:** 康复治疗

#### 例句3

> **The athlete is in rehab for his knee injury.**

**中文翻译:** 这位运动员因膝伤正在接受康复治疗。

**单词释义:** 康复；恢复

### 背诵小故事

> A young athlete got injured and needed rehab. He faced many
challenges but was determined. Through hard work and perseverance in
rehab, he finally recovered and returned to the field. Rehab means a
process of restoring health and ability.
