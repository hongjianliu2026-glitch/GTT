## responsibility

**英音:** _[rɪˌspɒnsəˈbɪlətɪ]_ **美音:**
_[rɪˌspɑːnsəˈbɪləti]_

**译义:** *n.责任,职责*

### 短语

+ **take responsibility:** _承担责任_

+ **assume responsibility:** _承担责任；负责_

+ **bear responsibility:** _承担责任_

+ **shift responsibility:** _推卸责任_

+ **sense of responsibility:** _责任感_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **It\'s your responsibility to lock the door before leaving.**

**中文翻译:** 在离开之前锁门是你的责任。

**单词释义:** 责任

#### 例句2

> **She took on the responsibility of caring for her elderly
parents.**

**中文翻译:** 她承担起照顾年迈父母的责任。

**单词释义:** 责任

#### 例句3

> **The company has a responsibility to protect the environment.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司有保护环境的责任。

**单词释义:** 责任

### 背诵小故事

> A young student was chosen to be the class monitor. With this role
came the responsibility to keep the class organized and help classmates.
He took it seriously and learned the meaning of responsibility.

**一个年轻的学生被选为班长。这个角色带来了保持班级秩序和帮助同学的责任。他认真对待，明白了责任的含义。**
