## replant

**英音:** _[\'riː\'plɑːnt]_ **美音:** _[riː\'plɑːnt]_

**译义:** *v.再植,改种,移(民),使复位*

### 短语

+ **replant trees:** _重新植树_

+ **replant crops:** _重新种植农作物_

+ **replant a garden:** _重新布置花园_

+ **replant the field:** _重新耕种田地_

+ **replant damaged areas:** _重新在受损区域种植_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The farmers had to replant the damaged crops after the flood.**

**中文翻译:** 洪水过后，农民们不得不重新种植受损的庄稼。

**单词释义:** 重新种植

#### 例句2

> **They decided to replant the garden with different flowers.**

**中文翻译:** 他们决定在花园里重新种植不同的花。

**单词释义:** 重新种植

#### 例句3

> **The forestry department is working on a project to replant the
deforested area.**

**中文翻译:** 林业部门正在开展一个项目，重新在被砍伐的地区种植树木。

**单词释义:** 重新种植

### 背诵小故事

> A farmer\'s crops were destroyed by a flood. But he didn\'t give up.
He replanted new seeds, hoping for a better harvest. With hard work, his
fields were green again. Replant means to plant again.
