## scaffolding

**英音:** _[\'skæfəʊldɪŋ; -f(ə)ld-]_ **美音:**
_[\'skæfəldɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.脚手架*

### 短语

+ **scaffolding equipment:** _脚手架设备_

+ **scaffolding platform:** _脚手架平台_

+ **scaffolding system:** _脚手架系统_

+ **metal scaffolding:** _金属脚手架_

+ **temporary scaffolding:** _临时脚手架_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The construction workers erected the scaffolding around the
building.**

**中文翻译:** 建筑工人在建筑物周围搭起了脚手架。

**单词释义:** 脚手架

#### 例句2

> **The painter climbed up the scaffolding to reach the high part of
the wall.**

**中文翻译:** 画家爬上脚手架，到达墙的高处。

**单词释义:** 脚手架

#### 例句3

> **The scaffolding was removed after the construction was
completed.**

**中文翻译:** 施工完毕后，脚手架被拆除。

**单词释义:** 脚手架

### 背诵小故事

> A worker named Tom was building a tall building. He relied on the
scaffolding to climb up and down safely. The scaffolding was like a
strong helper for him.

**一个叫汤姆的工人正在建造一座高楼。他依靠脚手架安全地上下攀爬。脚手架对他来说就像一个强大的帮手。**
