## stockbroker

**英音:** _[\'stɒkbrəʊkə]_ **美音:** _[\'stɑkbrokɚ]_

**译义:** *n.股票经纪人*

### 短语

+ **stockbroker firm:** _证券经纪公司_

+ **experienced stockbroker:** _经验丰富的股票经纪人_

+ **stockbroker license:** _股票经纪人执照_

+ **independent stockbroker:** _独立股票经纪人_

+ **stockbroker service:** _股票经纪服务_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **My father is a successful stockbroker who always makes wise
investment decisions.**

**中文翻译:**
我的父亲是一位成功的股票经纪人，他总是做出明智的投资决策。

**单词释义:** 股票经纪人

#### 例句2

> **The stockbroker advised me to buy some shares of that promising
company.**

**中文翻译:** 这位股票经纪人建议我购买那家有前途的公司的一些股票。

**单词释义:** 股票经纪人

#### 例句3

> **She became a stockbroker after graduating from college and has
been doing well in the financial industry.**

**中文翻译:**
她大学毕业后成为了一名股票经纪人，并且在金融行业表现出色。

**单词释义:** 股票经纪人

### 背诵小故事

day, he got a chance to work in a big firm. With hard work and smart
decisions, he became a successful stockbroker, helping people manage
