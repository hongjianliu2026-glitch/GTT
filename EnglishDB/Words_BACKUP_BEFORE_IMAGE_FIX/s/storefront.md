## storefront

**英音:** _[\'stɔːfrʌnt]_ **美音:** _[\'stɔr\'frʌnt]_

**译义:** *n.店头,店面*

### 短语

+ **storefront window:** _店面窗户_

+ **storefront design:** _店面设计_

+ **storefront renovation:** _店面翻新_

+ **attractive storefront:** _吸引人的店面_

+ **busy storefront:** _繁忙的店面_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new storefront attracted a lot of customers.**

**中文翻译:** 新的店面吸引了很多顾客。

**单词释义:** 店面

#### 例句2

> **They are renovating the old storefront to give it a modern
look.**

**中文翻译:** 他们正在翻新旧店面，使其具有现代外观。

**单词释义:** 店面

#### 例句3

> **The storefront was decorated with colorful lights for the holiday
season.**

**中文翻译:** 店面为假日季节装饰了彩灯。

**单词释义:** 店面

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a little boy was lost. He wandered along the street
and saw a beautiful storefront. It had colorful toys and candies. The
storefront became his landmark to find his way home.
