## sharpness

**英音:** _[ˈʃɑːpnəs]_ **美音:** _[ˈʃɑrpnəs]_

**译义:** *n.锐利*

### 短语

+ **edge sharpness:** _边缘锐度_

+ **image sharpness:** _图像清晰度_

+ **lens sharpness:** _镜头清晰度_

+ **sharpness of mind:** _思维敏捷_

+ **sharpness of vision:** _视力敏锐_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The sharpness of the knife allowed for precise cutting.**

**中文翻译:** 这把刀的锋利度使得切割变得精准。

**单词释义:** 锋利；锐利

#### 例句2

> **The sharpness of her wit made her stand out in the debate.**

**中文翻译:** 她的机智敏锐使她在辩论中脱颖而出。

**单词释义:** 敏锐；灵敏

#### 例句3

> **The sharpness of the image on the new TV is impressive.**

**中文翻译:** 新电视上图像的清晰度令人印象深刻。

**单词释义:** 清晰；鲜明

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little mouse was looking for food. Suddenly, it saw a
sharp knife. The sharpness of the knife scared the mouse. It ran away
quickly. The mouse learned that sharpness can be dangerous.
