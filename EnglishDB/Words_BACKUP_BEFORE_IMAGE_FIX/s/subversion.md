## subversion

**英音:** _[səb\'vɜːʃən]_ **美音:** _[səb\'vɝʒən]_

**译义:** *n.颠覆*

### 短语

+ **subversion attempt:** _颠覆企图_

+ **subversion activity:** _颠覆活动_

+ **prevent subversion:** _防止颠覆_

+ **subversion plot:** _颠覆阴谋_

+ **guard against subversion:** _警惕颠覆_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government was vigilant against any acts of subversion.**

**中文翻译:** 政府对任何颠覆行为保持警惕。

**单词释义:** 颠覆；破坏

#### 例句2

> **He was accused of subversion of the state.**

**中文翻译:** 他被指控颠覆国家罪。

**单词释义:** 颠覆；破坏

#### 例句3

> **The book was banned for its potential to cause subversion.**

**中文翻译:** 这本书因其潜在的颠覆性而被禁。

**单词释义:** 颠覆；破坏

### 背诵小故事

> In a kingdom, a group of spies were secretly planning subversion.
They wanted to overthrow the king and change the rule. But their evil
plan was finally discovered and stopped. Remember, subversion means the
act of trying to overthrow a government or system.

**在一个王国里，一群间谍秘密策划颠覆活动。他们想推翻国王并改变统治。但他们的邪恶计划最终被发现并阻止了。记住，subversion
指的是试图推翻政府或制度的行为。**
