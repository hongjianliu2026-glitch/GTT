## sapling

**英音:** _[\'sæplɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'sæplɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.树苗,小树*

### 短语

+ **young sapling:** _幼树_

+ **sapling stage:** _树苗阶段_

+ **plant saplings:** _种植树苗_

+ **sapling growth:** _树苗生长_

+ **nurture saplings:** _培育树苗_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The gardener planted several saplings in the park.**

**中文翻译:** 园丁在公园里种了几棵树苗。

**单词释义:** 树苗

#### 例句2

> **A strong wind damaged some of the saplings in the forest.**

**中文翻译:** 一阵强风损坏了森林里的一些树苗。

**单词释义:** 树苗

#### 例句3

> **The saplings need regular watering to grow well.**

**中文翻译:** 这些树苗需要定期浇水才能长得好。

**单词释义:** 树苗

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a magical forest, a little fairy found a weak
sapling. She used her magic to protect and nurture it. As time passed,
the sapling grew strong and tall. Remember, a sapling is a young tree!

就是幼树！**
