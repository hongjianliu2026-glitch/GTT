## superstar

**英音:** _[ˈsупəstɑː(r)]_ **美音:** _[ˈsuːpərˌstɑr]_

**译义:** *n.超级明星，巨星；adj.超级的，一流的*

### 短语

+ **pop superstar:** _流行巨星_

+ **international superstar:** _国际巨星_

+ **music superstar:** _音乐巨星_

+ **movie superstar:** _电影巨星_

+ **sports superstar:** _体育巨星_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Taylor Swift is a superstar in the music industry.**

**中文翻译:** 泰勒·斯威夫特是音乐界的超级巨星。

**单词释义:** 超级巨星

#### 例句2

> **The basketball game attracted many superstars.**

**中文翻译:** 这场篮球赛吸引了很多超级明星。

**单词释义:** 超级明星

#### 例句3

> **He dreams of becoming a superstar one day.**

**中文翻译:** 他梦想有一天能成为超级巨星。

**单词释义:** 超级巨星

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was walking on the street and saw a huge crowd. I pushed
through and found a SUPERSTAR singing on the stage. Everyone was crazy
about him. His charm and talent made him stand out and become a
SUPERSTAR.
