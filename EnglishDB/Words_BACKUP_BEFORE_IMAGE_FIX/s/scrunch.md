## scrunch

**英音:** _[skrʌn(t)ʃ]_ **美音:** _[skrʌntʃ]_

**译义:** *vt.碾碎,使弯曲vi.嘎吱嘎吱地作响,蜷缩n.嘎吱嘎吱的响声*

### 短语

+ **scrunch up:** _揉皱；使起皱_

+ **scrunch down:** _蹲伏；蜷缩_

+ **scrunch into:** _挤进；塞入_

+ **scrunch something together:** _把某物揉在一起_

+ **scrunch one\'s face:** _皱起脸_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She scrunched up the piece of paper and threw it in the bin.**

**中文翻译:** 她把那张纸揉成一团，扔进了垃圾桶。

**单词释义:** 揉皱；挤压

#### 例句2

> **The child scrunched his face in anger.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子愤怒地皱起了脸。

**单词释义:** 皱起（脸）

#### 例句3

> **The sand scrunched beneath our feet as we walked along the
beach.**

**中文翻译:** 当我们沿着海滩行走时，脚下的沙子嘎吱作响。

**单词释义:** 发出嘎吱声

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was walking on the beach and saw a crab scrunching up into
a ball to protect itself. The way it scrunched made me remember this
word meaning to crush or squeeze something together.
