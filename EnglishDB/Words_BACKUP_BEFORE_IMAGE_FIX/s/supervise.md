## supervise

**英音:** _[\'suːpəvaɪz; \'sjuː-]_ **美音:**
_[\'sʊpɚ\'vaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.监督,管理,指导*

### 短语

+ **supervise closely:** _密切监督_

+ **supervise effectively:** _有效监督_

+ **supervise strictly:** _严格监督_

+ **supervise personally:** _亲自监督_

+ **supervise constantly:** _持续监督_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The manager supervises the work of the employees.**

**中文翻译:** 经理监督员工的工作。

**单词释义:** 监督

#### 例句2

> **She was appointed to supervise the project.**

**中文翻译:** 她被任命监督这个项目。

**单词释义:** 监督；管理

#### 例句3

> **The teacher supervises the students during the exam.**

**中文翻译:** 老师在考试期间监督学生。

**单词释义:** 监督；监考

### 背诵小故事

> A young manager was assigned to supervise a team. He had to watch
over every detail, making sure everything was done correctly. It was a
big responsibility, but he did it well. \'Supervise\' means to watch
over and direct.

**一位年轻的经理被指派监督一个团队。他必须关注每一个细节，确保一切都正确完成。这是一项重大责任，但他做得很好。\'supervise\'意思是监督、管理。**
