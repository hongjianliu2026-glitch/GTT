## sweltering

**英音:** _[\'swelt(ə)rɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'swɛltərɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.闷热的,中暑的,酷热的*

### 短语

+ **sweltering heat:** _酷热_

+ **sweltering summer:** _闷热的夏天_

+ **sweltering day:** _炎热的一天_

+ **sweltering climate:** _闷热的气候_

+ **sweltering room:** _闷热的房间_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The sweltering heat made it difficult to sleep at night.**

**中文翻译:** 酷热使得晚上难以入睡。

**单词释义:** 酷热的

#### 例句2

> **We were working in sweltering conditions in the factory.**

**中文翻译:** 我们在工厂里的闷热环境中工作。

**单词释义:** 闷热的

#### 例句3

> **The sweltering summer days were unbearable for many people.**

**中文翻译:** 炎热的夏日对许多人来说难以忍受。

**单词释义:** 炎热的

### 背诵小故事

> In a sweltering summer, Tom was on a hiking trip. The sun was fierce,
and the heat made him feel like he was in an oven. He was sweating
profusely and struggling to breathe. \'Sweltering\' indeed describes
such a hot and uncomfortable situation.

**在一个酷热的夏天，汤姆正在徒步旅行。太阳很毒，炎热让他感觉像在烤箱里。他大汗淋漓，呼吸困难。"sweltering"确实描述了这样炎热又难受的情形。**
