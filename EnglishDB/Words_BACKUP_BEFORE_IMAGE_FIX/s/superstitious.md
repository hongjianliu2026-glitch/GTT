## superstitious

**英音:** _[suːpə\'stɪʃəs; sjuː-]_ **美音:**
_[\'sʊpɚ\'stɪʃəs]_

**译义:** *adj.迷信的*

### 短语

+ **superstitious belief:** _迷信的信仰_

+ **superstitious custom:** _迷信的习俗_

+ **superstitious fear:** _迷信的恐惧_

+ **superstitious notion:** _迷信的观念_

+ **superstitious practice:** _迷信的做法_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She has always been superstitious and believes in lucky
charms.**

**中文翻译:** 她一直很迷信，并且相信幸运符。

**单词释义:** 迷信的

#### 例句2

> **His superstitious fears prevented him from going on the trip on
Friday the 13th.**

**中文翻译:** 他迷信的恐惧使他在 13 号星期五那天没有去旅行。

**单词释义:** 迷信的

#### 例句3

> **The old man\'s superstitious beliefs led him to perform strange
rituals.**

**中文翻译:** 这位老人的迷信信仰导致他进行奇怪的仪式。

**单词释义:** 迷信的

### 背诵小故事

> One night, Tom was walking in a dark forest. He heard strange noises
and felt scared. Later, he realized it was just his superstitious mind
playing tricks. Superstitious means having or showing a belief in
superstitions.

**一天晚上，汤姆在黑暗的森林里散步。他听到奇怪的声音，感到害怕。后来，他意识到这只是他迷信的头脑在作祟。Superstitious
意思是有迷信或表现出迷信。**
