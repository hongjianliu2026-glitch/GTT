## self-confidence

**英音:** _[selˈf.kɒnfɪd(ə)ns]_ **美音:**
_[ˈsɛlfˈkɑnd.fɪd.əns]_

**译义:** *n.自信；自信心*

### 短语

+ **build self-confidence:** _建立自信_

+ **lack of self-confidence:** _缺乏自信_

+ **boost self-confidence:** _增强自信_

+ **have self-confidence:** _有自信_

+ **gain self-confidence:** _获得自信_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She has a lot of self-confidence and is not afraid to speak in
public.**

**中文翻译:** 她非常自信，不害怕在公众面前发言。

**单词释义:** 自信

#### 例句2

> **Losing the game didn\'t shake his self-confidence.**

**中文翻译:** 输掉比赛并没有动摇他的自信。

**单词释义:** 自信

#### 例句3

> **Building self-confidence takes time and effort.**

**中文翻译:** 建立自信需要时间和努力。

**单词释义:** 自信

### 背诵小故事

> Once there was a shy boy. But he decided to change. He faced
challenges alone, learned new skills. Gradually, he found himself
believing in himself. That\'s how he gained self-confidence and became
brave.

**曾经有一个害羞的男孩。但他决定改变。他独自面对挑战，学习新技能。渐渐地，他发现自己相信自己了。就这样他获得了自信，变得勇敢。**
