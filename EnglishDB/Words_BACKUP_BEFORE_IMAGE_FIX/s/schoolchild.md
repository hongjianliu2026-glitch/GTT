## schoolchild

**英音:** _[\'skuːltʃaɪld]_ **美音:** _[\'skultʃaɪld]_

**译义:** *n.学童*

### 短语

+ **primary schoolchild:** _小学生_

+ **junior schoolchild:** _初中学生_

+ **schoolchild\'s uniform:** _学童制服_

+ **naughty schoolchild:** _调皮的学童_

+ **intelligent schoolchild:** _聪明的学童_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The schoolchild was excited to start the new term.**

**中文翻译:** 这名小学生对新学期的开始感到很兴奋。

**单词释义:** 学童

#### 例句2

> **The schoolchild carried a heavy backpack to school.**

**中文翻译:** 那个小学生背着沉重的书包去学校。

**单词释义:** 学童

#### 例句3

> **Many schoolchildren participated in the art competition.**

**中文翻译:** 许多小学生参加了艺术比赛。

**单词释义:** 学童

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little schoolchild named Lily was excited to go to school.
She carried her colorful backpack and met her friends. They learned and
played together, having a wonderful time at school.
