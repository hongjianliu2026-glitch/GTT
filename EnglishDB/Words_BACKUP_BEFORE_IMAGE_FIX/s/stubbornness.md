## stubbornness

**英音:** _[ˈstʌbənnɪs]_ **美音:** _[ˈstʌbənnɪs]_

**译义:** *n.倔强,顽强*

### 短语

+ **show stubbornness:** _表现出固执_

+ **unreasonable stubbornness:** _不合理的固执_

+ **stubbornness of character:** _性格的固执_

+ **overcome stubbornness:** _克服固执_

+ **display stubbornness:** _展示固执_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His stubbornness often leads to arguments with his friends.**

**中文翻译:** 他的固执常常导致他和朋友们发生争吵。

**单词释义:** 固执

#### 例句2

> **Stubbornness can prevent you from accepting helpful advice.**

**中文翻译:** 固执会阻止你接受有用的建议。

**单词释义:** 固执；倔强

#### 例句3

> **Despite her stubbornness, she eventually realized her
mistake.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管她很固执，但最终她还是意识到了自己的错误。

**单词释义:** 固执

### 背诵小故事

> Once there was a stubborn donkey named Stub. It refused to move no
matter how hard its owner pushed. This donkey\'s unyielding attitude
showed the meaning of stubbornness - the quality of being difficult to
change or persuade.

**从前有一只叫Stub的倔强的驴子。无论主人怎么推，它都不肯动。这只驴子坚决的态度展现了stubbornness（倔强）这个词的意思------难以改变或说服的特质。**
