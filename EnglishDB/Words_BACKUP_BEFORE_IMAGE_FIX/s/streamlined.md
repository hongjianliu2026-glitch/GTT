## streamlined

**英音:** _[\'striːmlaɪnd]_ **美音:** _[\'strim,laɪnd]_

**译义:** *adj.最新型的,改进的*

### 短语

+ **streamlined design:** _流线型设计_

+ **streamlined process:** _简化的流程_

+ **streamlined body:** _流线型车身_

+ **streamlined organization:** _精简的组织_

+ **streamlined operation:** _流线型操作_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new car has a streamlined design that reduces wind
resistance.**

**中文翻译:** 这辆新车具有流线型的设计，能够减少风阻。

**单词释义:** 流线型的

#### 例句2

> **The company streamlined its production process to increase
efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 公司精简了生产流程以提高效率。

**单词释义:** 精简；使有效率

#### 例句3

> **The athlete\'s streamlined body allowed for faster movement in
the water.**

**中文翻译:** 这位运动员流线型的身体使得在水中移动速度更快。

**单词释义:** 流线型的

### 背诵小故事

> A fish was swimming in the river. It had a streamlined body, which
helped it move smoothly through the water without any resistance. The
shape made it very efficient and fast. Just like a well-designed object
with a streamlined form.

**一条鱼在河里游。它有一个流线型的身体，这帮助它在水中毫无阻力地顺畅游动。这种形状使它高效且快速。就像一个设计良好的流线型物体。**
