## subjectivity

**英音:** _[ˌsʌbdʒɛkˈtɪvɪti]_ **美音:**
_[səbdʒɛkˈtɪvɪti]_

**译义:** *n.主观性,主观*

### 短语

+ **subjectivity bias:** _主观性偏差_

+ **subjectivity judgment:** _主观性判断_

+ **degree of subjectivity:** _主观性程度_

+ **reduce subjectivity:** _减少主观性_

+ **subjectivity factor:** _主观性因素_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The study of literature often involves analyzing the subjectivity
of the author\'s perspective.**

**中文翻译:** 文学研究经常涉及分析作者观点的主观性。

**单词释义:** 主观性

#### 例句2

> **We should be aware of the subjectivity in our judgments and try
to be more objective.**

**中文翻译:** 我们应该意识到我们判断中的主观性，并努力更加客观。

**单词释义:** 主观性

#### 例句3

> **The artist\'s work reflects a high degree of subjectivity and
personal expression.**

**中文翻译:** 这位艺术家的作品反映了高度的主观性和个人表达。

**单词释义:** 主观性

### 背诵小故事

> A philosopher was always discussing the concept of subjectivity. One
day, in a deep thought, he realized that subjectivity means the quality
of being based on or influenced by personal feelings, tastes, or
opinions. It\'s like seeing the world through your own unique lens.

**一位哲学家总是在讨论主观性的概念。有一天，在深入思考中，他意识到主观性指的是基于或受个人感情、品味或意见影响的特质。这就像通过你自己独特的视角看世界。**
