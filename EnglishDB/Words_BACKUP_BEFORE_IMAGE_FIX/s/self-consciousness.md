## self-consciousness

**英音:** _[ˌsɛlf ˈkɒnʃəsnəs]_ **美音:** _[ˌsɛlf
ˈkɑːnʃəsnəs]_

**译义:** *n.自我意识，自觉；害羞，不自然*

### 短语

+ **heighten self-consciousness:** _增强自我意识_

+ **lack of self-consciousness:** _缺乏自我意识_

+ **develop self-consciousness:** _培养自我意识_

+ **excessive self-consciousness:** _过度的自我意识_

+ **awaken self-consciousness:** _唤醒自我意识_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She felt a wave of self-consciousness when she entered the room
full of strangers.**

**中文翻译:** 当她走进满是陌生人的房间时，她感到一阵难为情。

**单词释义:** 难为情；自我意识

#### 例句2

> **His self-consciousness prevented him from speaking freely in
public.**

**中文翻译:** 他的自我意识使他无法在公众场合畅所欲言。

**单词释义:** 自我意识；拘束

#### 例句3

> **Overcoming self-consciousness is an important step in building
confidence.**

**中文翻译:** 克服自我意识是建立自信的重要一步。

**单词释义:** 自我意识

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Lily was at a party. She kept worrying about how she looked
and acted, feeling self-conscious. She was too focused on herself and
how others might perceive her. This is what self-consciousness means -
being overly aware of oneself.

**有一天，莉莉在一个聚会上。她一直担心自己的外表和行为，感到难为情。她过度关注自己以及别人可能如何看待她。这就是
self-consciousness 的意思------过度自我意识。**
