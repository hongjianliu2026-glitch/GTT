## sporadically

**英音:** _[spə\'rædɪkli]_ **美音:** _[spə\'rædɪkli]_

**译义:** *adv.偶发地,零星地*

### 短语

+ **occur sporadically:** _偶尔发生_

+ **appear sporadically:** _偶尔出现_

+ **distribute sporadically:** _零星分布_

+ **fluctuate sporadically:** _偶尔波动_

+ **report sporadically:** _偶尔报告_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The disease occurs sporadically in this region.**

**中文翻译:** 这种疾病在这个地区偶尔发生。

**单词释义:** 偶尔地

#### 例句2

> **He sporadically visits his grandparents.**

**中文翻译:** 他偶尔去看望他的祖父母。

**单词释义:** 偶尔

#### 例句3

> **Rain fell sporadically throughout the day.**

**中文翻译:** 一整天雨断断续续地下着。

**单词释义:** 断断续续地

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was walking in the forest and saw fireflies shining
sporadically. It was not a continuous light but appeared randomly and
intermittently. Just like this, I remembered the word \'sporadically\'
means occurring at irregular intervals.

**有一天，我在森林里散步，看到萤火虫零星地闪烁。不是持续的光亮，而是随机、断断续续地出现。就像这样，我记住了'sporadically'这个词意思是不定期地发生。**
