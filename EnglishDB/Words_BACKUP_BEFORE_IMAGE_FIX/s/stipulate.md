## stipulate

**英音:** _[\'stɪpjʊleɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'stɪpjulet]_

**译义:** *v.规定,保证*

### 短语

+ **stipulate for:** _规定；要求_

+ **stipulate clearly:** _明确规定_

+ **stipulate conditions:** _规定条件_

+ **stipulate terms:** _规定条款_

+ **stipulate a time limit:** _规定时限_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The contract stipulates that the rent must be paid by the 5th of
each month.**

**中文翻译:** 合同规定租金必须在每月 5 号支付。

**单词释义:** 规定；明确要求

#### 例句2

> **The law stipulates certain penalties for this crime.**

**中文翻译:** 法律对这种罪行规定了某些处罚。

**单词释义:** 规定

#### 例句3

> **The company stipulates that employees must arrive at work before
9 a.m.**

**中文翻译:** 公司规定员工必须在上午 9 点之前上班。

**单词释义:** 规定

### 背诵小故事

> A king stipulated that all knights must follow certain rules. If they
didn\'t, they would be punished. \'Stipulate\' means to state or demand
as a condition or requirement.

**一位国王规定所有骑士都必须遵守某些规则。如果他们不遵守，就会受到惩罚。\'stipulate\'意思是作为条件或要求陈述或要求。**
