## secretion

**英音:** _[sɪ\'kriːʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[sɪ\'kriʃən]_

**译义:** *n.分泌,分泌物(液)*

### 短语

+ **hormone secretion:** _激素分泌_

+ **secretion system:** _分泌系统_

+ **glandular secretion:** _腺分泌_

+ **secretion disorder:** _分泌紊乱_

+ **exocrine secretion:** _外分泌_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The secretion of hormones regulates various bodily functions.**

**中文翻译:** 激素的分泌调节着各种身体机能。

**单词释义:** 分泌（物）

#### 例句2

> **The doctor studied the secretion from the patient\'s wound.**

**中文翻译:** 医生研究了病人伤口的分泌物。

**单词释义:** 分泌物

#### 例句3

> **The gland is responsible for the secretion of digestive
enzymes.**

**中文翻译:** 该腺体负责分泌消化酶。

**单词释义:** 分泌

### 背诵小故事

> The little bee was working hard in the hive. It produced a special
liquid, which was its secretion. This secretion was very important for
making honey. Secretion means the process of producing and releasing a
substance.

**小蜜蜂在蜂巢里努力工作。它产生了一种特殊的液体，这就是它的分泌物。这种分泌物对酿蜜非常重要。Secretion
意思是产生和释放某种物质的过程。**
