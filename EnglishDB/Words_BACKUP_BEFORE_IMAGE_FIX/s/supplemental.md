## supplemental

**英音:** _[,sʌplɪ\'mentəl]_ **美音:** _[,sʌplə\'mɛntl]_

**译义:** *adj.补足的,追加的*

### 短语

+ **supplemental income:** _额外收入_

+ **supplemental education:** _补充教育_

+ **supplemental material:** _补充材料_

+ **supplemental insurance:** _补充保险_

+ **supplemental feeding:** _补充喂养_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company provided supplemental training for new employees.**

**中文翻译:** 公司为新员工提供了补充培训。

**单词释义:** 补充的

#### 例句2

> **We need to review the supplemental materials before the exam.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要在考试前复习补充材料。

**单词释义:** 补充的

#### 例句3

> **The supplemental insurance covered additional medical
expenses.**

**中文翻译:** 补充保险涵盖了额外的医疗费用。

**单词释义:** 补充的

### 背诵小故事

> A traveler was lost in a strange city. Just when he was worried, he
found a supplemental map that guided him to his destination. The map was
an extra help, just like the meaning of\'supplemental\'.

**一位旅行者在一个陌生的城市迷路了。就在他忧心忡忡时，他发现了一张补充地图，引导他到达了目的地。这张地图是额外的帮助，就像\'supplemental\'这个词的意思。**
