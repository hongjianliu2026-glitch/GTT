## volleyball

**英音:** _[\'vɒlɪbɔːl]_ **美音:** _[\'vɑlɪbɔl]_

**译义:** *n.排球*

### 短语

+ **play volleyball:** _打排球_

+ **volleyball game:** _排球比赛_

+ **volleyball team:** _排球队_

+ **volleyball court:** _排球场_

+ **volleyball player:** _排球运动员_
