## vertically

**英音:** _[ˈvɜːtɪkəli]_ **美音:** _[ˈvɜrtɪkli]_

**译义:** *adv.垂直地*

### 短语

+ **vertically integrated:** _垂直整合的_

+ **vertically polarized:** _垂直极化的_

+ **vertically challenged:** _个子矮的（幽默说法）_

+ **vertically opposite angles:** _对顶角_

+ **vertically aligned:** _垂直对齐的_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The building is vertically symmetrical.**

**中文翻译:** 这座建筑是垂直对称的。

**单词释义:** 垂直地

#### 例句2

> **The flagpole stands vertically in the middle of the square.**

**中文翻译:** 旗杆笔直地立在广场中央。

**单词释义:** 垂直地

#### 例句3

> **The rock layers are vertically aligned.**

**中文翻译:** 岩层是垂直排列的。

**单词释义:** 垂直地

### 背诵小故事

> A little monkey wanted to climb to the top of a tall tree vertically.
But it was very difficult. Finally, with great effort, it reached the
top and saw the beautiful view. \'Vertically\' means in a straight up
and down direction.

**一只小猴子想垂直爬上一棵高大的树顶。但这非常困难。最终，费了很大的劲，它到达了树顶并看到了美丽的景色。\'vertically\'
意思是垂直地，直立地。**
