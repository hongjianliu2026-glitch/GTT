## incarcerate

**英音:** _[ɪn\'kɑːsəreɪt]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'kɑrsəret]_

**译义:** *vt.把\...关进监狱,监禁,幽闭*

### 短语

+ **incarcerate someone for life:** _终身监禁某人_

+ **be incarcerated without trial:** _未经审判被监禁_

+ **incarcerate illegally:** _非法监禁_

+ **incarcerate in a high-security prison:**
_关押在高度戒备的监狱_

+ **incarcerate for a crime:** _因犯罪被监禁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The criminal was incarcerated for life.**

**中文翻译:** 该罪犯被终身监禁。

**单词释义:** 监禁

#### 例句2

> **He was falsely incarcerated and spent years fighting for his
freedom.**

**中文翻译:** 他被错误地监禁，并花了数年时间争取自由。

**单词释义:** 监禁

#### 例句3

> **The government has the power to incarcerate those who break the
law.**

**中文翻译:** 政府有权监禁违法者。

**单词释义:** 监禁

### 背诵小故事

> A criminal was caught stealing. The police decided to incarcerate
him. They took him to a prison and locked him up to stop him from
causing more harm.

**一个罪犯偷东西被抓了。警察决定监禁他。他们把他带到监狱关起来，以阻止他造成更多危害。**
