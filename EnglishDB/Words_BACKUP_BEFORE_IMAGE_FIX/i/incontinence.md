## incontinence

**英音:** _[ɪnˈkɒntɪnəns]_ **美音:** _[ɪnˈkɑːntɪnəns]_

**译义:** *n.不能自制,无节制,[医]失禁*

### 短语

+ **urinary incontinence:** _尿失禁_

+ **fecal incontinence:** _大便失禁_

+ **stress incontinence:** _压力性尿失禁_

+ **incontinence pad:** _失禁护垫_

+ **incontinence care:** _失禁护理_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The patient suffered from urinary incontinence after the
surgery.**

**中文翻译:** 手术后，患者出现尿失禁。

**单词释义:** 尿失禁；大小便失禁

#### 例句2

> **Incontinence can have a significant impact on a person\'s quality
of life.**

**中文翻译:** 失禁会对一个人的生活质量产生重大影响。

**单词释义:** 失禁

#### 例句3

> **She was embarrassed by her sudden incontinence.**

**中文翻译:** 她因突然失禁而感到尴尬。

**单词释义:** 失禁

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was an old man who had trouble controlling
his bladder, showing incontinence. His family was very worried and
sought help. Finally, with proper treatment, his condition improved.
Remember, \'incontinence\' means the inability to control bodily
functions.

**从前，有个老人难以控制自己的膀胱，出现了失禁的情况。他的家人非常担心并寻求帮助。最终，通过适当的治疗，他的状况有所改善。记住，\'incontinence\'意思是身体机能失控。**
