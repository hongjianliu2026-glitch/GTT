## isolation

**英音:** _[aɪsə\'leɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,aɪsə\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.隔绝,孤立,隔离,绝缘,离析*

### 短语

+ **in isolation:** _孤立地；单独地_

+ **social isolation:** _社会孤立_

+ **isolation ward:** _隔离病房_

+ **sense of isolation:** _孤独感；孤立感_

+ **thermal isolation:** _热绝缘；绝热_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The patient was placed in isolation to prevent the spread of the
infection.**

**中文翻译:** 该患者已被隔离，以防止感染传播。

**单词释义:** 隔离（为了防止疾病传播）

#### 例句2

> **He felt a sense of isolation living alone in a foreign
country.**

**中文翻译:** 独自生活在异国他乡，他感到一种孤独感。

**单词释义:** 孤独；孤立

#### 例句3

> **The island\'s isolation has preserved its unique ecosystem.**

**中文翻译:** 该岛的孤立性保留了其独特的生态系统。

**单词释义:** 与世隔绝

### 背诵小故事

> A brave scientist was in isolation in a secret lab to study a
dangerous virus. He was alone, cut off from the outside world. Isolation
means being alone and separated.

意思是孤立、隔离。**
