## instantaneously

**英音:** _[ˌɪnstənˈteɪniəsli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɪnstənˈteɪniəsli]_

**译义:** *立即地，即刻地；瞬时地，马上*

### 短语

+ **act instantaneously:** _立即行动_

+ **react instantaneously:** _即刻反应_

+ **respond instantaneously:** _瞬间响应_

+ **happen instantaneously:** _瞬间发生_

+ **change instantaneously:** _瞬间改变_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The machine responded instantaneously to the command.**

**中文翻译:** 这台机器立即对指令做出了响应。

**单词释义:** 立即；即刻

#### 例句2

> **She understood the problem instantaneously and came up with a
solution.**

**中文翻译:** 她瞬间就理解了这个问题，并想出了一个解决方案。

**单词释义:** 瞬间；马上

#### 例句3

> **The light turned off instantaneously when he pressed the
switch.**

**中文翻译:** 当他按下开关时，灯立即熄灭了。

**单词释义:** 即刻；瞬时

### 背诵小故事

> A time traveler was able to move instantaneously from one era to
another. One moment he was in the past, and in the next, he was in the
future. It happened so fast, just like the meaning of
\'instantaneously\'.

**一位时间旅行者能够瞬间从一个时代移动到另一个时代。前一刻他还在过去，下一刻就到了未来。发生得如此之快，就像\'instantaneously\'这个词的意思。**
