## incarceration

**英音:** _[ɪn,kɑːsə\'reɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[ɪn,kɑrsə\'reʃən]_

**译义:** *n.钳闭*

### 短语

+ **prevent incarceration:** _防止监禁_

+ **risk of incarceration:** _监禁风险_

+ **long-term incarceration:** _长期监禁_

+ **unjust incarceration:** _不公正的监禁_

+ **avoid incarceration:** _避免监禁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The judge sentenced him to a long period of incarceration for the
crime.**

**中文翻译:** 法官因犯罪判处他长期监禁。

**单词释义:** 监禁

#### 例句2

> **Incarceration often has a negative impact on an individual\'s
mental health.**

**中文翻译:** 监禁通常会对个人的心理健康产生负面影响。

**单词释义:** 监禁；坐牢

#### 例句3

> **She was terrified at the prospect of incarceration.**

**中文翻译:** 她对被监禁的前景感到恐惧。

**单词释义:** 监禁；拘留

### 背诵小故事

> A brave detective was trying to solve a mystery. He found that the
villain\'s evil plan involved incarcerating innocent people. Finally,
the detective stopped the villain and saved the imprisoned ones.

**一位勇敢的侦探试图解开一个谜团。他发现反派的邪恶计划包括监禁无辜的人。最终，侦探阻止了反派并解救了被监禁的人。**
