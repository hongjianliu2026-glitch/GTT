## Israeli-Palestinian

**英音:** _[ˌɪzreɪˈliː pæləˈstiniən]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɪzreɪˈliː pæləˈstiniən]_

**译义:** *adj. 以色列-巴勒斯坦的；n. 以色列-巴勒斯坦人*

+ **Israeli-Palestinian relations:** _以巴关系_

+ **Israeli-Palestinian negotiations:** _以巴谈判_

+ **Israeli-Palestinian ceasefire:** _以巴停火_

> In a distant land, there was a constant struggle between the Israeli
and Palestinian people. They fought for land and peace. This word,
Israeli-Palestinian, represents the complex relationship and conflicts
between them.

**在一片遥远的土地上，以色列人和巴勒斯坦人之间存在着持续不断的争斗。他们为土地和和平而战。这个词【Israeli-Palestinian】代表了他们之间复杂的关系和冲突。**
