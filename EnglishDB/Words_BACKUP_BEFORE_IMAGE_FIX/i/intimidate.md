## intimidate

**英音:** _[ɪn\'tɪmɪdeɪt]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'tɪmɪdet]_

**译义:** *v.胁迫*

### 短语

+ **intimidate someone into doing something:** _胁迫某人做某事_

+ **be intimidated by:** _被\...\...吓住；害怕\...\..._

+ **intimidate with threats:** _用威胁恐吓_

+ **intimidate out of:** _恐吓（某人）放弃\...\..._

+ **intimidate the witness:** _恐吓证人_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The bully tried to intimidate the smaller child.**

**中文翻译:** 那个恶霸试图恐吓那个较小的孩子。

**单词释义:** 恐吓

#### 例句2

> **She was intimidated by the complexity of the task.**

**中文翻译:** 她被这项任务的复杂性吓到了。

**单词释义:** 使害怕；使胆怯

#### 例句3

> **The criminal\'s threats intimidated the witnesses into
silence.**

**中文翻译:** 罪犯的威胁吓得证人不敢说话。

**单词释义:** 威胁；胁迫

### 背诵小故事

> A small mouse was intimidated by a big cat. The cat showed its sharp
teeth and fierce look, making the mouse tremble with fear.
\'Intimidate\' means to make someone feel frightened or less confident.

**一只小老鼠被一只大猫恐吓。猫露出锋利的牙齿和凶猛的表情，让老鼠害怕得发抖。\'intimidate\'意思是使某人感到害怕或不自信。**
