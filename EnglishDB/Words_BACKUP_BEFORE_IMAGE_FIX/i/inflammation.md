## inflammation

**英音:** _[ɪnflə\'meɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[\'ɪnflə\'meʃən]_

**译义:** *n.怒火,发火,燃烧,[医]炎症,发炎*

### 短语

+ **chronic inflammation:** _慢性炎症_

+ **inflammation response:** _炎症反应_

+ **reduce inflammation:** _减轻炎症_

+ **inflammation of the joints:** _关节炎症_

+ **inflammation marker:** _炎症标志物_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The inflammation in his knee made it difficult for him to
walk.**

**中文翻译:** 他膝盖的炎症使他走路很困难。

**单词释义:** 炎症

#### 例句2

> **Chronic inflammation can lead to serious health problems.**

**中文翻译:** 慢性炎症可能会导致严重的健康问题。

**单词释义:** 炎症

#### 例句3

> **The doctor prescribed some medicine to reduce the
inflammation.**

**中文翻译:** 医生开了一些药来减轻炎症。

**单词释义:** 炎症

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a battle inside your body. The soldiers (white blood cells)
are fighting against invaders. This intense fight causes heat, redness,
and swelling. This chaotic scene is called \'inflammation\'.

**想象一下身体内部的一场战斗。士兵（白细胞）正在对抗入侵者。这场激烈的战斗导致发热、发红和肿胀。这种混乱的场景就被称为"炎症"。**
