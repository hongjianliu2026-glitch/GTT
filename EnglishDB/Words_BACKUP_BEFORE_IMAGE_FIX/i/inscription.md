## inscription

**英音:** _[ɪn\'skrɪpʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'skrɪpʃən]_

**译义:** *n.题字,碑铭*

### 短语

+ **stone inscription:** _石刻铭文_

+ **inscription on a tombstone:** _墓碑上的铭文_

+ **ancient inscription:** _古代铭文_

+ **religious inscription:** _宗教铭文_

+ **engraved inscription:** _雕刻的铭文_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The inscription on the tombstone was difficult to read.**

**中文翻译:** 墓碑上的铭文很难辨认。

**单词释义:** 铭文；碑文

#### 例句2

> **The ancient pot had an inscription around its rim.**

**中文翻译:** 这个古老的罐子的边缘有一个铭文。

**单词释义:** 题字；铭刻

#### 例句3

> **The inscription on the medal commemorated the victory.**

**中文翻译:** 奖牌上的铭文纪念了这次胜利。

**单词释义:** 铭文；铭记

### 背诵小故事

> A long time ago, a king wanted to leave a message for future
generations. So, he ordered his men to carve words on a big stone. These
carved words were called \'inscription\', meaning the act of writing or
engraving something.

**很久以前，一位国王想给后代留下一则信息。于是，他命令手下在一块大石头上刻字。这些刻上去的字就被称为"inscription"，意思是书写或雕刻某物的行为。**
