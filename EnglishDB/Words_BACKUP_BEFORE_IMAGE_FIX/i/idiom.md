## idiom

**英音:** _[\'ɪdɪəm]_ **美音:** _[\'ɪdɪəm]_

**译义:** *n.成语,方言,土语,习惯用语*

### 短语

+ **idiomatic expression:** _习惯用语；习语_

+ **common idiom:** _常见习语_

+ **figurative idiom:** _比喻性习语_

+ **colloquial idiom:** _口语习语_

+ **archaic idiom:** _古旧习语_

### 例句

**单词释义:** 习语；成语；惯用语

### 背诵小故事

> A foreigner was confused when he heard people say \'It\'s raining
cats and dogs\'. But later he learned that this is an idiom meaning
heavy rain. He was so happy to understand this special way of
expression.

**一个外国人听到人们说'It\'s raining cats and
dogs'时很困惑。但后来他知道了这是个习语，意思是下大雨。他很高兴理解了这种特殊的表达方式。**
