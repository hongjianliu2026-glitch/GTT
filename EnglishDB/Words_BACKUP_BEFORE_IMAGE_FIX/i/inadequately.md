## inadequately

**英音:** _[ɪn\'ædɪkwətli]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'ædɪkwətli]_

**译义:** *adv.不适当地*

### 短语

+ **inadequately funded:** _资金不足_

+ **inadequately trained:** _训练不足_

+ **inadequately equipped:** _装备不足_

+ **inadequately protected:** _保护不足_

+ **inadequately prepared:** _准备不足_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The project was inadequately funded, resulting in delays.**

**中文翻译:** 这个项目资金不足，导致了延误。

**单词释义:** 不足地

#### 例句2

> **He performed inadequately in the exam and failed.**

**中文翻译:** 他在考试中表现不佳，不及格。

**单词释义:** 不充分地；不足地

#### 例句3

> **The equipment was inadequately maintained and broke down
frequently.**

**中文翻译:** 设备维护不当，经常出故障。

**单词释义:** 不恰当；不充分

### 背诵小故事

> A young artist was criticized for inadequately preparing for an
important exhibition. He had rushed through his works and didn\'t pay
attention to details. This made him realize he needed to put in more
effort and be more thorough next time.

**一位年轻的艺术家因准备重要展览不充分而受到批评。他匆忙完成作品，不注重细节。这使他意识到下次需要投入更多努力，做得更周全。**
