## imperfection

**英音:** _[ɪmpə\'fekʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,ɪmpɚ\'fɛkʃən]_

**译义:** *n.不完整性,非理想性*

### 短语

+ **minor imperfection:** _小瑕疵_

+ **surface imperfection:** _表面缺陷_

+ **structural imperfection:** _结构缺陷_

+ **visual imperfection:** _视觉瑕疵_

+ **imperfection in character:** _性格上的缺陷_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The imperfection of the product led to many customer
complaints.**

**中文翻译:** 产品的不完美导致了许多客户的投诉。

**单词释义:** 不完美；缺点

#### 例句2

> **She accepted her own imperfection and strived to improve.**

**中文翻译:** 她接受了自己的不完美并努力改进。

**单词释义:** 不完美之处

#### 例句3

> **The artist saw the imperfection in his painting as an opportunity
for growth.**

**中文翻译:** 这位艺术家把他画作中的不完美视为成长的机会。

**单词释义:** 缺陷

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a potter was frustrated by the imperfection of a
vase he made. It had a small crack. But he realized that imperfection
made it unique. Imperfection means something not perfect, having flaws
or faults.
