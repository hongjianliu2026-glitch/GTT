## improvement

**英音:** _[ɪm\'pruːvm(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[ɪm\'prʊvmənt]_

**译义:** *n.改进,进步*

### 短语

+ **continuous improvement:** _持续改进_

+ **quality improvement:** _质量改进_

+ **performance improvement:** _性能改进_

+ **significant improvement:** _显著改进_

+ **process improvement:** _流程改进_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The improvement in her grades was remarkable this semester.**

**中文翻译:** 她这学期的成绩进步非常显著。

**单词释义:** 进步；改善

#### 例句2

> **The company is constantly seeking improvement in its products and
services.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司一直在寻求其产品和服务的改进。

**单词释义:** 改进；改善

#### 例句3

> **There has been a significant improvement in the local
economy.**

**中文翻译:** 当地经济有了显著的改善。

**单词释义:** 改善；好转

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a little boy\'s grades were not good. But he worked
hard every day and made many changes. Finally, his progress was obvious.
This was an improvement that made his parents proud.

**从前，有个小男孩成绩不好。但他每天努力学习，做出了很多改变。最终，他的进步很明显。这就是一种提升，让他的父母感到骄傲。**
