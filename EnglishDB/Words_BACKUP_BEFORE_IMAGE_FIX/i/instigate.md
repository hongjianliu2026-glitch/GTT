## instigate

**英音:** _[\'ɪnstɪgeɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'ɪnstə\'get]_

**译义:** *v.鼓动*

### 短语

+ **instigate violence:** _煽动暴力_

+ **instigate a rebellion:** _煽动叛乱_

+ **instigate an investigation:** _发起一项调查_

+ **instigate a change:** _挑起变革_

+ **instigate a conflict:** _挑起冲突_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He was accused of instigating the violence.**

**中文翻译:** 他被指控煽动暴力。

**单词释义:** 煽动

#### 例句2

> **The leader instigated the workers to go on strike.**

**中文翻译:** 领导煽动工人罢工。

**单词释义:** 鼓动

#### 例句3

> **She instigated a rebellion among the students.**

**中文翻译:** 她煽动学生造反。

**单词释义:** 挑起

### 背诵小故事

> A bad guy wanted to cause trouble. So, he instigated his friends to
fight with others. He whispered bad ideas to them, making them do
something wrong.

**一个坏人想要惹麻烦。所以，他煽动他的朋友和别人打架。他向他们低声说坏话，让他们做错事。**
