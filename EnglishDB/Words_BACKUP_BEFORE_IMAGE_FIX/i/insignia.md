## insignia

**英音:** _[ɪn\'sɪgnɪə]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'sɪɡnɪə]_

**译义:** *n.勋章,徽章*

### 短语

+ **military insignia:** _军事徽章_

+ **corporate insignia:** _公司标志_

+ **distinctive insignia:** _独特的徽章_

+ **official insignia:** _官方徽章_

+ **ceremonial insignia:** _礼仪徽章_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The soldier wore the insignia of his rank on his uniform.**

**中文翻译:** 这名士兵的制服上佩戴着他的军衔徽章。

**单词释义:** 徽章；标志

#### 例句2

> **The club\'s insignia is easily recognizable.**

**中文翻译:** 俱乐部的徽章很容易辨认。

**单词释义:** 标志；徽章

#### 例句3

> **The car had special insignia to indicate its limited edition
status.**

**中文翻译:** 该车有特殊的徽章来表明其限量版状态。

**单词释义:** 徽章；标记

the battle. The insignia, a shiny emblem on his chest, marked his honor
and courage. It was a symbol everyone recognized and respected.
