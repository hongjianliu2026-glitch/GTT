## interchangeable

**英音:** _[ɪntə\'tʃeɪn(d)ʒəbl]_ **美音:**
_[,ɪntɚ\'tʃendʒəbl]_

**译义:** * /n.可交换性*

### 短语

+ **interchangeable parts:** _可互换的零件_

+ **interchangeable items:** _可互换的物品_

+ **interchangeable roles:** _可互换的角色_

+ **interchangeable skills:** _可互换的技能_

+ **interchangeable elements:** _可互换的元素_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The two parts are interchangeable.**

**中文翻译:** 这两个部分是可以互换的。

**单词释义:** 可互换的

#### 例句2

> **These shoes are interchangeable with each other.**

**中文翻译:** 这些鞋子彼此可以互换。

**单词释义:** 可互换的

#### 例句3

> **The components of this machine are not interchangeable.**

**中文翻译:** 这台机器的组件不可互换。

**单词释义:** 可互换的

### 背诵小故事

> A factory had two machines. They were so similar that their parts
were interchangeable. Workers could swap the parts easily to keep the
production going. This shows what \'interchangeable\' means - able to be
exchanged.

**一家工厂有两台机器。它们非常相似，零件可以相互替换。工人们可以轻松地交换零件以保持生产继续。这就展示了\'interchangeable\'的意思------可互换的。**
