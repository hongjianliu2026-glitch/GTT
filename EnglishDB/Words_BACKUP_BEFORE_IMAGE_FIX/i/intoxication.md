## intoxication

**英音:** _[ɪn,tɒksɪ\'keɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[ɪn,tɑksə\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.陶醉*

### 短语

+ **alcohol intoxication:** _酒精中毒_

+ **drug intoxication:** _药物中毒_

+ **intoxication by fumes:** _烟雾中毒_

+ **intoxication level:** _中毒程度_

+ **acute intoxication:** _急性中毒_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His intoxication made him unable to walk straight.**

**中文翻译:** 他的醉酒状态使他无法直走。

**单词释义:** 醉酒；中毒

#### 例句2

> **The intoxication of success can blind us to potential
problems.**

**中文翻译:** 成功的陶醉可能会让我们对潜在的问题视而不见。

**单词释义:** 陶醉；极度兴奋

#### 例句3

> **She was overcome by intoxication after taking the wrong
medicine.**

**中文翻译:** 她吃错药后陷入了中毒状态。

**单词释义:** 中毒

### 背诵小故事

> One night, Tom drank too much wine and felt a strange feeling. He
realized it was intoxication. His mind was fuzzy and his steps were
unsteady. This experience made him understand the meaning of
intoxication.

**一天晚上，汤姆喝了太多酒，感觉到一种奇怪的感觉。他意识到这是醉酒。他头脑迷糊，脚步不稳。这次经历让他明白了intoxication（醉酒；中毒）的意思。**
