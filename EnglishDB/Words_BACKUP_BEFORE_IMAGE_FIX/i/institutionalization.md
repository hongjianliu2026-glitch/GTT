## institutionalization

**英音:** _[ˌɪnstɪˌtjuːʃənəlaɪˈzeɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɪnstɪˌtuːʃənələˈzeɪʃn]_

**译义:** *n.制度化；体制化*

### 短语

+ **institutionalization process:** _制度化过程_

+ **degree of institutionalization:** _制度化程度_

+ **institutionalization theory:** _制度化理论_

+ **prevent institutionalization:** _防止制度化_

+ **accelerate institutionalization:** _加速制度化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The institutionalization of mental health care has improved over
the years.**

**中文翻译:** 多年来，精神卫生保健的制度化已经有所改善。

**单词释义:** 制度化

#### 例句2

> **The institutionalization of education systems varies from country
to country.**

**中文翻译:** 各国教育系统的制度化情况各不相同。

**单词释义:** 制度化

#### 例句3

> **The process of institutionalization often leads to standardized
procedures.**

**中文翻译:** 制度化的过程往往会导致标准化的程序。

**单词释义:** 制度化

### 背诵小故事

> A small village was in chaos. But with the institutionalization of
rules and systems, order was restored. Schools, hospitals, and markets
were properly organized. Now, the village thrived and everyone lived
happily.

**一个小村庄曾处于混乱之中。但随着规则和制度的制度化，秩序得以恢复。学校、医院和市场都被妥善组织起来。现在，村庄繁荣起来，每个人都幸福地生活着。**
