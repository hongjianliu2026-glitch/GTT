## individualistic

**英音:** _[,ɪndɪvɪdjʊə\'lɪstɪk]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɪndɪˌvɪdʒʊr\'lɪstɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.个人主义的*

### 短语

+ **individualistic approach:** _个人主义的方法_

+ **individualistic behavior:** _个人主义的行为_

+ **highly individualistic:** _高度个人主义的_

+ **individualistic style:** _个人主义的风格_

+ **individualistic thinking:** _个人主义的思维_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She has an individualistic style in her artworks.**

**中文翻译:** 她的作品具有个人主义风格。

**单词释义:** 个人主义的

#### 例句2

> **The company encourages individualistic thinking among its
employees.**

**中文翻译:** 公司鼓励员工的个人主义思维。

**单词释义:** 个人主义的

#### 例句3

> **His individualistic approach often sets him apart from the
crowd.**

**中文翻译:** 他的个人主义风格常常使他与众不同。

**单词释义:** 个人主义的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a special artist. He refused to follow
the crowd and always pursued his own unique style. His works were full
of individualistic charm. This artist showed us what it means to be
individualistic - being true to oneself.

**从前，有一位特别的艺术家。他拒绝随大流，总是追求自己独特的风格。他的作品充满了个人主义的魅力。这位艺术家向我们展示了
individualistic 的含义------做真实的自己。**
