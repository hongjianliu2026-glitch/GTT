## internment

**英音:** _[ɪn\'tɜːnmənt]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'tɝnmənt]_

**译义:** *n.扣留,收容*

### 短语

+ **internment camp:** _拘留营；集中营_

+ **mass internment:** _大规模拘禁_

+ **preventive internment:** _预防性拘留_

+ **internment order:** _拘留令_

+ **political internment:** _政治拘禁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The internment of Japanese Americans during World War II was a
controversial event.**

**中文翻译:** 二战期间日裔美国人被拘禁是一个有争议的事件。

**单词释义:** 拘禁；拘留

#### 例句2

> **He was sent to an internment camp for political prisoners.**

**中文翻译:** 他被送到了一个政治犯拘留营。

**单词释义:** 拘留营

#### 例句3

> **The internment of refugees has raised humanitarian concerns.**

**中文翻译:** 对难民的拘禁引起了人道主义关切。

**单词释义:** 拘禁

### 背诵小故事

> During the war, many people were sent to a special place for
internment. They had to stay there and couldn\'t leave freely. Imagine
being trapped like that. Now you know what \'internment\' means - the
confinement or imprisonment of people.

**在战争期间，许多人被送到一个特殊的地方被拘禁。他们不得不待在那里，不能自由离开。想象一下像那样被困住。现在你知道'internment'的意思了 -
对人的拘禁或监禁。**
