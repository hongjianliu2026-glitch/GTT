## inhalation

**英音:** _[ɪnhə\'leɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,ɪnhə\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.吸入*

### 短语

+ **inhalation therapy:** _吸入疗法_

+ **inhalation device:** _吸入装置_

+ **inhalation exposure:** _吸入暴露_

+ **inhalation hazard:** _吸入危害_

+ **inhalation route:** _吸入途径_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The inhalation of smoke can cause serious health problems.**

**中文翻译:** 吸入烟雾会导致严重的健康问题。

**单词释义:** 吸入

#### 例句2

> **He was treated for inhalation of toxic fumes.**

**中文翻译:** 他因吸入有毒烟雾而接受治疗。

**单词释义:** 吸入

#### 例句3

> **Proper inhalation techniques are important when using an
inhaler.**

**中文翻译:** 使用吸入器时，正确的吸入技术很重要。

**单词释义:** 吸入

### 背诵小故事

> A brave astronaut was on a space mission. When a sudden gas leak
occurred, he had to perform an emergency inhalation of oxygen to
survive. The act of breathing in the oxygen was called inhalation.

**一位勇敢的宇航员正在执行太空任务。当突然发生气体泄漏时，他不得不紧急吸入氧气以生存。这种吸气的行为就叫做
inhalation。**
