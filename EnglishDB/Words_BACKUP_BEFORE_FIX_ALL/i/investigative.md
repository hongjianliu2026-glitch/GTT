## investigative

**英音:** _[ɪn\'vestɪgətɪv; -geɪtɪv]_ **美音:**
_[ɪn\'vɛstɪɡetɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.研究的,好研究的*

### 短语

+ **investigative journalism:** _调查性新闻报道_

+ **investigative report:** _调查报告_

+ **investigative team:** _调查团队_

+ **investigative process:** _调查过程_

+ **investigative work:** _调查工作_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The investigative journalist spent months uncovering the
corruption scandal.**

**中文翻译:** 这位调查记者花了数月时间揭露了这起腐败丑闻。

**单词释义:** 调查的；侦查的

#### 例句2

> **The police have launched an investigative operation to find the
missing person.**

**中文翻译:** 警方已经展开了一项调查行动来寻找失踪人员。

**单词释义:** 调查性质的

#### 例句3

> **The documentary takes an investigative look at the environmental
issues in the region.**

**中文翻译:** 这部纪录片对该地区的环境问题进行了调查性的审视。

**单词释义:** 探究的；调研的

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was on an investigative mission. He had to solve a
mysterious case. He collected clues, interviewed witnesses, and did not
stop until he found the truth. The word \'investigative\' describes his
actions.

**一位侦探正在执行一项调查任务。他必须解决一个神秘的案件。他收集线索、询问证人，不停歇直到找到真相。单词\'investigative\'描述了他的这些行动。**
