## industrialization

**英音:** _[ɪnˌdʌstrɪəlaɪˈzeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[ɪnˌdʌstriəlɪˈzeɪʃən]_

**译义:** *n.工业化,产业化*

### 短语

+ **rapid industrialization:** _快速工业化_

+ **late industrialization:** _后期工业化_

+ **industrialization process:** _工业化进程_

+ **advanced industrialization:** _先进工业化_

+ **early industrialization:** _早期工业化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The rapid industrialization has brought both benefits and
challenges to the country.**

**中文翻译:** 快速的工业化给这个国家带来了好处和挑战。

**单词释义:** 工业化

#### 例句2

> **Industrialization led to a significant increase in urban
population.**

**中文翻译:** 工业化导致城市人口显著增加。

**单词释义:** 工业化

#### 例句3

> **The process of industrialization has transformed the economic
structure of the region.**

**中文翻译:** 工业化的进程改变了这个地区的经济结构。

**单词释义:** 工业化

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a small town, there were only simple workshops.
But as time went by, more and more factories were built. This process of
turning the town into a place full of industries was called
industrialization.

**从前，在一个小镇上，只有简单的作坊。但随着时间推移，越来越多的工厂被建立。这个将小镇变成充满工业的地方的过程被称为工业化。**
