## investigate

**英音:** _[ɪn\'vestɪgeɪt]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'vɛstɪɡet]_

**译义:** *v.调查,研究*

### 短语

+ **investigate into:** _调查；深入研究_

+ **investigate thoroughly:** _彻底调查_

+ **investigate the case:** _调查案件_

+ **investigate the matter:** _调查此事_

+ **begin to investigate:** _开始调查_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The police are investigating the crime scene.**

**中文翻译:** 警察正在调查犯罪现场。

**单词释义:** 调查

#### 例句2

> **Scientists are investigating the effects of climate change.**

**中文翻译:** 科学家们正在研究气候变化的影响。

**单词释义:** 研究

#### 例句3

> **The company will investigate the complaint from the customer.**

**中文翻译:** 公司将调查来自客户的投诉。

**单词释义:** 调查

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was assigned to investigate a mysterious theft. He
carefully collected clues, questioned witnesses, and analyzed evidence.
Finally, his efforts paid off and he solved the case.

**一位侦探被指派去调查一起神秘的盗窃案。他仔细收集线索，询问证人，并分析证据。最终，他的努力得到了回报，他破了案。**
