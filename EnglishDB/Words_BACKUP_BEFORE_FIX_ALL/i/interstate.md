## interstate

**英音:** _[ɪntə\'steɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'ɪntɚstet]_

**译义:** *adj.州际的*

### 短语

+ **interstate commerce:** _州际贸易_

+ **interstate highway:** _州际公路_

+ **interstate conflict:** _州际冲突_

+ **interstate relations:** _州际关系_

+ **interstate travel:** _州际旅行_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The interstate highway was crowded with traffic during the
holiday.**

**中文翻译:** 在假期期间，州际公路上交通拥堵。

**单词释义:** 州际的

#### 例句2

> **Interstate commerce is regulated by the federal government.**

**中文翻译:** 州际商业由联邦政府监管。

**单词释义:** 州际的

#### 例句3

> **They are planning to build an interstate railway to improve
transportation.**

**中文翻译:** 他们正计划修建一条州际铁路以改善交通。

**单词释义:** 州际的

### 背诵小故事

> A man was driving on the interstate highway. He saw beautiful scenery
and met friendly people. Interstate means connecting states. So this
highway connects different states smoothly.

**一个男人正在州际公路上开车。他看到了美丽的风景，遇到了友好的人。interstate
意思是州与州之间的。所以这条公路顺畅地连接着不同的州。**
