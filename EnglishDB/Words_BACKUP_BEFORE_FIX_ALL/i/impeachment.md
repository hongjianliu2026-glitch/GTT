## impeachment

**英音:** _[ɪm\'pi:tʃmənt]_ **美音:** _[ɪm\'pitʃmənt]_

**译义:** *n.弹劾,指摘*

### 短语

+ **impeachment proceedings:** _弹劾程序_

+ **impeachment trial:** _弹劾审判_

+ **face impeachment:** _面临弹劾_

+ **threat of impeachment:** _弹劾的威胁_

+ **impeachment process:** _弹劾过程_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The impeachment process against the president began last
month.**

**中文翻译:** 针对总统的弹劾程序于上个月开始。

**单词释义:** 弹劾

#### 例句2

> **The threat of impeachment hung over him like a dark cloud.**

**中文翻译:** 弹劾的威胁像乌云一样笼罩在他的头上。

**单词释义:** 弹劾

#### 例句3

> **The impeachment proceedings have drawn widespread attention.**

**中文翻译:** 弹劾程序引起广泛关注。

**单词释义:** 弹劾程序

### 背诵小故事

> A king was accused of serious crimes, and the process of holding him
accountable was called impeachment. The people wanted justice, and
impeachment was the way to seek it.

**一位国王被指控犯了严重的罪行，对他进行问责的过程被称为弹劾。民众想要正义，弹劾就是寻求正义的方式。**
