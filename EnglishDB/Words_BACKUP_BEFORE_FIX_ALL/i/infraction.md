## infraction

**英音:** _[ɪn\'frækʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[ɪn\'frækʃən]_

**译义:** *n.违反,侵害*

### 短语

+ **minor infraction:** _轻微违规_

+ **traffic infraction:** _交通违规_

+ **code infraction:** _违反法规_

+ **serious infraction:** _严重违规_

+ **infraction of rules:** _违反规则_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The driver was fined for an infraction of traffic rules.**

**中文翻译:** 这位司机因违反交通规则而被罚款。

**单词释义:** 违反，违规

#### 例句2

> **His infraction of the company\'s code of conduct led to his
dismissal.**

**中文翻译:** 他违反公司的行为准则导致被解雇。

**单词释义:** 违反

#### 例句3

> **The athlete received a penalty for an infraction during the
competition.**

**中文翻译:** 这名运动员在比赛中因违规而受到处罚。

**单词释义:** 违规

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught for an infraction. He broke into a house, but was
quickly found by the police. This illegal act was a clear infraction of
the law. Remember, an infraction is a violation or a breach.

**一个小偷因违规行为被抓。他闯入一所房子，但很快被警察发现。这种非法行为显然是对法律的违反。记住，infraction
就是违规、违法。**
