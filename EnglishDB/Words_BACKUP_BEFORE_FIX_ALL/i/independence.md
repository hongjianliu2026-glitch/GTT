## independence

**英音:** _[ɪndɪ\'pend(ə)ns]_ **美音:**
_[,ɪndɪ\'pɛndəns]_

**译义:** *n.独立,自主*

### 短语

+ **political independence:** _政治独立_

+ **economic independence:** _经济独立_

+ **national independence:** _国家独立_

+ **gain independence:** _获得独立_

+ **declare independence:** _宣布独立_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The country gained independence after a long struggle.**

**中文翻译:** 经过长期斗争，该国获得了独立。

**单词释义:** 独立；自主

#### 例句2

> **She values her independence and likes to make her own
decisions.**

**中文翻译:** 她重视自己的独立性，喜欢自己做决定。

**单词释义:** 独立；自主

#### 例句3

> **Independence Day is celebrated with great enthusiasm every
year.**

**中文翻译:** 每年人们都会以极大的热情庆祝独立日。

**单词释义:** 独立（日）

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a young bird struggled to leave its nest and fly
freely. It finally achieved independence, meaning it could live and act
on its own without relying on others. Just like this bird, independence
is about being self-reliant.

**从前，一只小鸟努力离开它的巢自由飞翔。它最终获得了独立，这意味着它可以不依赖他人独自生活和行动。就像这只鸟一样，独立就是自力更生。**
