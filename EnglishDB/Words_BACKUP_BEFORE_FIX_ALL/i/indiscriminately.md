## indiscriminately

**英音:** _[ˌɪndɪˈskrɪmɪnətli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɪndɪˈskrɪmɪnətli]_

**译义:** *adv.不加选择地,不分青红皂白地*

### 短语

+ **act indiscriminately:** _肆意行动_

+ **distribute indiscriminately:** _胡乱分配_

+ **use indiscriminately:** _滥用_

+ **spend money indiscriminately:** _乱花钱_

+ **criticize indiscriminately:** _乱批评_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The soldiers fired indiscriminately into the crowd.**

**中文翻译:** 士兵们向人群胡乱开枪。

**单词释义:** 胡乱地；不加选择地

#### 例句2

> **He spent money indiscriminately on unnecessary things.**

**中文翻译:** 他胡乱地把钱花在不必要的东西上。

**单词释义:** 不加区分地；随意地

#### 例句3

> **The company was accused of indiscriminately laying off
workers.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司被指责随意裁员。

**单词释义:** 任意地；不加甄别地

### 背诵小故事

> A king was distributing rewards indiscriminately. He didn\'t consider
the efforts and merits of his subjects. As a result, his kingdom was in
chaos. This shows that doing things indiscriminately leads to bad
consequences.

**一位国王在不加区分地分发奖赏。他不考虑臣民的努力和功绩。结果，他的王国陷入了混乱。这表明不加区分地做事会导致糟糕的后果。**
