## instrumentation

**英音:** _[,ɪnstrʊmen\'teɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,ɪnstrəmɛn\'teʃən]_

**译义:** *n.使用仪器*

### 短语

+ **instrumentation equipment:** _仪器设备_

+ **instrumentation panel:** _仪表盘；仪表板_

+ **instrumentation system:** _仪表系统；检测系统_

+ **scientific instrumentation:** _科学仪器_

+ **process instrumentation:** _过程仪表；过程检测仪表_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The instrumentation in the new laboratory is
state-of-the-art.**

**中文翻译:** 新实验室的仪器是最先进的。

**单词释义:** 仪器设备

#### 例句2

> **The orchestra\'s instrumentation included violins, cellos, and
flutes.**

**中文翻译:** 乐团的乐器包括小提琴、大提琴和长笛。

**单词释义:** 乐器编制

#### 例句3

> **The instrumentation of the aircraft was damaged during the
flight.**

**中文翻译:** 飞机的仪表在飞行过程中被损坏。

**单词释义:** 仪表

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working in the lab. He needed to measure some data
precisely. The INSTRUMENTATION on the table, like advanced meters and
sensors, helped him get accurate results. Without this instrumentation,
his research would be in trouble.

**一位科学家在实验室工作。他需要精确测量一些数据。桌上的仪器设备，比如先进的仪表和传感器，帮助他获得了准确的结果。没有这些仪器设备，他的研究就会陷入困境。**
