## inflammatory

**英音:** _[ɪn\'flæmət(ə)rɪ]_ **美音:**
_[ɪn\'flæmə\'tɔri]_

**译义:** *adj.煽动性的*

### 短语

+ **inflammatory remarks:** _煽动性言论_

+ **inflammatory disease:** _炎症性疾病_

+ **inflammatory response:** _炎症反应_

+ **inflammatory process:** _炎症过程_

+ **inflammatory bowel disease:** _炎性肠病_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctor prescribed some medicine to reduce the inflammatory
response in my body.**

**中文翻译:** 医生开了一些药来减轻我身体里的炎症反应。

**单词释义:** 炎症的；炎性的

#### 例句2

> **His inflammatory remarks caused a lot of controversy.**

**中文翻译:** 他的煽动性言论引起了很多争议。

**单词释义:** 煽动性的；使人激怒的

#### 例句3

> **The inflammatory article was quickly taken down from the
website.**

**中文翻译:** 这篇煽动性的文章很快就从网站上撤下来了。

**单词释义:** 煽动性的；刺激性的

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor was explaining to a patient that certain foods can cause
inflammatory reactions in the body. For example, too much fried chicken
can trigger inflammation. So, remember \'inflammatory\' means causing
inflammation.

**一位医生向病人解释某些食物会在体内引起炎症反应。比如，吃太多炸鸡会引发炎症。所以，记住'inflammatory'意思是引起炎症的。**
