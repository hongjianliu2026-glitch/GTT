## intimately

**英音:** _[ˈɪntɪmɪtli]_ **美音:** _[ˈɪntəmətli]_

**译义:** *adv.密切地*

### 短语

+ **intimately connected:** _紧密相连_

+ **intimately involved:** _密切参与_

+ **intimately acquainted:** _非常熟悉_

+ **intimately related:** _密切相关_

+ **know intimately:** _深知；非常了解_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **They have been intimately involved in the project from the
start.**

**中文翻译:** 他们从一开始就密切参与了这个项目。

**单词释义:** 密切地

#### 例句2

> **She intimately understands his problems.**

**中文翻译:** 她非常了解他的问题。

**单词释义:** 非常；深深地

#### 例句3

> **The two friends are intimately acquainted with each other\'s
families.**

**中文翻译:** 这两个朋友对彼此的家庭都很熟悉。

**单词释义:** 熟悉地

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a couple who knew each other intimately.
They shared every secret and feeling, understanding each other deeply.
Intimately means having a close and deep knowledge or connection.

**从前，有一对夫妻彼此非常亲密。他们分享每一个秘密和感受，深深地理解对方。intimately
意思是亲密地；密切地；深入地**
