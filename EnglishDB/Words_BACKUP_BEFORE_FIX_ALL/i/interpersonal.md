## interpersonal

**英音:** _[ɪntə\'pɜːs(ə)n(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[\'ɪntɚ\'pɝsnl]_

**译义:** *adj.人与人之间的,关于人与人之间关系的*

### 短语

+ **interpersonal relationship:** _人际关系_

+ **interpersonal communication:** _人际沟通_

+ **interpersonal skill:** _人际交往技巧_

+ **interpersonal conflict:** _人际冲突_

+ **interpersonal attraction:** _人际吸引_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Good interpersonal skills are essential in the workplace.**

**中文翻译:** 良好的人际交往技能在工作场所是必不可少的。

**单词释义:** 人际的；人与人之间的

#### 例句2

> **She has excellent interpersonal relationships with her
colleagues.**

**中文翻译:** 她和同事们有着极好的人际关系。

**单词释义:** 人际的；人与人之间的

#### 例句3

> **Interpersonal communication is very important in building
friendships.**

**中文翻译:** 人际交流在建立友谊方面非常重要。

**单词释义:** 人际的；人与人之间的

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends were on an adventure. They had to solve problems
together, which tested their interpersonal skills. They learned to
communicate, understand, and support each other. Through this, they
became closer. \'Interpersonal\' means relating to relationships between
people.

**一群朋友在冒险。他们必须一起解决问题，这考验了他们的人际交往技能。他们学会了沟通、理解和互相支持。通过这个，他们变得更亲密。\'interpersonal\'意思是人与人之间关系的。**
