## intentionally

**英音:** _[ɪnˈtɛnʃənli]_ **美音:** _[ɪnˈtɛnʃənəli]_

**译义:** *adv.有意地,故意地*

### 短语

+ **intentionally deceive:** _故意欺骗_

+ **intentionally avoid:** _有意避开_

+ **intentionally harm:** _故意伤害_

+ **intentionally delay:** _故意拖延_

+ **intentionally ignore:** _故意忽视_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He intentionally avoided answering my question.**

**中文翻译:** 他故意回避回答我的问题。

**单词释义:** 故意地

#### 例句2

> **She intentionally broke the vase to get attention.**

**中文翻译:** 她故意打碎花瓶以引起注意。

**单词释义:** 故意地

#### 例句3

> **The company intentionally misled consumers with false
advertising.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司故意用虚假广告误导消费者。

**单词释义:** 故意地

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was trying to solve a case. The suspect intentionally
left clues to mislead the investigation. But the detective was smart and
saw through the intentionally placed false leads.

**一位侦探正在努力侦破一个案件。嫌疑人故意留下线索来误导调查。但侦探很聪明，看穿了这些故意设置的假线索。**
