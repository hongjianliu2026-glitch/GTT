## greatly

**英音:** _[\'greɪtlɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'ɡretli]_

**译义:** *adv.很,非常*

### 短语

+ **greatly appreciated:** _非常感激_

+ **greatly influenced:** _极大地影响_

+ **greatly improved:** _大大改善_

+ **greatly reduced:** _大幅减少_

+ **greatly enhanced:** _大大增强_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new policy has greatly improved the living conditions of the
people.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策极大改善了人民群众的生活条件。

**单词释义:** 极大地

#### 例句2

> **Her performance was greatly appreciated by the audience.**

**中文翻译:** 她的表演受到了观众的高度赞赏。

**单词释义:** 极大地

#### 例句3

> **The price of the product has greatly decreased in recent
months.**

**中文翻译:** 近几个月来，该产品的价格大幅下降。

**单词释义:** 大幅

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a little bird wanted to fly greatly high. It
practiced every day and finally achieved its dream. \'Greatly\' here
means to a large extent or degree, just like the bird\'s amazing
progress.

**从前，一只小鸟想要飞得非常高。它每天练习，最终实现了梦想。"greatly"在这里指在很大程度上，就像小鸟惊人的进步。**
