## guerrilla

**英音:** _[gə\'rɪlə]_ **美音:** _[gə\'rɪlə]_

**译义:** *n.[古]游击战,游击队*

### 短语

+ **guerrilla warfare:** _游击战_

+ **guerrilla fighter:** _游击队员_

+ **guerrilla attack:** _游击式袭击_

+ **urban guerrilla:** _城市游击队_

+ **guerrilla movement:** _游击运动_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The guerrilla fighters launched a surprise attack on the enemy
camp.**

**中文翻译:** 游击战士向敌营发起奇袭。

**单词释义:** 游击队员

#### 例句2

> **The government is struggling to deal with the guerrilla
activities in the region.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在努力应对该地区的游击活动。

**单词释义:** 游击活动

#### 例句3

> **He was captured by the guerrilla forces during the war.**

**中文翻译:** 战争期间，他被游击队俘虏。

**单词释义:** 游击队

### 背诵小故事

> A brave soldier was alone in the forest. He used smart tactics to
fight against the enemy like a guerrilla. He hid, attacked suddenly, and
then disappeared quickly. The enemy was always confused and couldn\'t
catch him.

**一位勇敢的士兵独自在森林里。他像游击队员一样运用聪明的战术对抗敌人。他隐藏起来，突然袭击，然后迅速消失。敌人总是困惑不已，抓不住他。**
