## gelatin

**英音:** _[ˈdʒelətɪn]_ **美音:** _[ˈdʒelətɪn]_

**译义:** *n.凝胶,白明胶*

### 短语

+ **gelatin dessert:** _明胶甜点_

+ **gelatin capsule:** _明胶胶囊_

+ **food-grade gelatin:** _食品级明胶_

+ **gelatin powder:** _明胶粉_

+ **gelatin sheet:** _明胶片_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The dessert is made with gelatin and fresh fruit.**

**中文翻译:** 这个甜点是用明胶和新鲜水果制成的。

**单词释义:** 明胶

#### 例句2

> **Gelatin is often used in the production of gummy candies.**

**中文翻译:** 明胶常用于生产软糖。

**单词释义:** 明胶

#### 例句3

> **She added some gelatin to thicken the soup.**

**中文翻译:** 她加了一些明胶来使汤变稠。

**单词释义:** 明胶

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a chef was making a special dessert. He needed
gelatin to make it solid and shiny. The gelatin was like magic powder
that turned the dessert into a delicious treat.
