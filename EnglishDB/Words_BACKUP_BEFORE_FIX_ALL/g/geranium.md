## geranium

**译义:** *n.天竺葵*

### 短语

+ **geranium oil:** _天竺葵油_

+ **geranium extract:** _天竺葵提取物_

+ **scented geranium:** _香天竺葵_

+ **geranium plant:** _天竺葵植物_

+ **geranium flower:** _天竺葵花_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was walking in the garden and saw a beautiful flower. I
asked the gardener what it was. He told me it was a geranium. The bright
color and sweet smell of the geranium left a deep impression on me.

**有一天，我在花园里散步，看到一朵漂亮的花。我问园丁这是什么。他告诉我这是天竺葵。天竺葵鲜艳的颜色和香甜的气味给我留下了深刻的印象。**
