## grammatical

**英音:** _[grə\'mætɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[grə\'mætɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.文法的,合乎文法的*

### 短语

+ **grammatical error:** _语法错误_

+ **grammatical structure:** _语法结构_

+ **grammatical form:** _语法形式_

+ **grammatical feature:** _语法特征_

+ **grammatical analysis:** _语法分析_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little student was confused about grammatical rules. But
with the help of a wise owl, he learned that \'grammatical\' means
following the rules of language. Now he can write correctly and speak
beautifully.

**有一天，一个小学生对语法规则感到困惑。但在一只聪明的猫头鹰的帮助下，他了解到'grammatical'意味着遵循语言的规则。现在他能正确书写和漂亮地说话了。**
