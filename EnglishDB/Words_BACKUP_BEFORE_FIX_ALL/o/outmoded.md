## outmoded

**英音:** _[aʊt\'məʊdɪd]_ **美音:** _[,aʊt\'modɪd]_

**译义:** *adj.过时的*

### 短语

+ **outmoded ideas:** _过时的想法_

+ **outmoded methods:** _过时的方法_

+ **outmoded equipment:** _陈旧的设备_

+ **outmoded fashion:** _过时的时尚_

+ **outmoded technology:** _过时的技术_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The outmoded furniture was replaced with new ones.**

**中文翻译:** 过时的家具换成了新的。

**单词释义:** 过时的

#### 例句2

> **His outmoded ideas were no longer accepted by the team.**

**中文翻译:** 他过时的想法不再被团队接受。

**单词释义:** 过时的；陈旧的

#### 例句3

> **The company\'s outmoded management system needed a complete
overhaul.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司过时的管理体系需要彻底改革。

**单词释义:** 过时的；陈旧的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a fashion designer had a collection of clothes that
were outmoded. No one wanted to buy them. It made the designer realize
that staying updated is crucial. Outmoded means old-fashioned and no
longer fashionable.

**从前，一位时装设计师有一系列过时的衣服。没人想买。这让设计师意识到与时俱进至关重要。outmoded
意思是过时的、不再流行的。**
