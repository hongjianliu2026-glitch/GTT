## onward

**英音:** _[\'ɒnwəd]_ **美音:** _[\'ɑnwɚd]_

**译义:** *adj.向前的adv.向前,在前面*

### 短语

+ **from this day onward:** _从今天起_

+ **move onward:** _向前移动_

+ **onward journey:** _前行的旅程_

+ **onward march:** _向前行进_

+ **onward progress:** _前进的进展_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The train moved onward towards its destination.**

**中文翻译:** 火车朝着目的地继续前行。

**单词释义:** 向前；前进

#### 例句2

> **From this day onward, I will work harder.**

**中文翻译:** 从今天起，我会更努力工作。

**单词释义:** 从（某时）起

#### 例句3

> **The company\'s profits have been increasing onward for the past
few years.**

**中文翻译:** 在过去的几年里，公司的利润一直在持续增长。

**单词释义:** 持续地；不断地

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was on a journey. He kept moving onward, facing many
difficulties. He never stopped or turned back. His determination led him
to reach the destination. Onward means forward or advancing.
