## overstate

**英音:** _[əʊvə\'steɪt]_ **美音:** _[,ovɚ\'stet]_

**译义:** *vt.夸大的叙述,夸张*

### 短语

+ **overstate the case:** _夸大情况_

+ **overstate one\'s achievements:** _夸大某人的成就_

+ **overstate the problem:** _夸大问题_

+ **overstate the importance:** _夸大重要性_

+ **overstate the difficulty:** _夸大困难_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He tends to overstate his achievements.**

**中文翻译:** 他倾向于夸大自己的成就。

**单词释义:** 夸大

#### 例句2

> **Don\'t overstate the problem; it\'s not as serious as you
think.**

**中文翻译:** 不要夸大问题；事情没有你想象的那么严重。

**单词释义:** 夸大

#### 例句3

> **The media often overstate the impact of certain events.**

**中文翻译:** 媒体经常夸大某些事件的影响。

**单词释义:** 夸大

### 背诵小故事

> A man was trying to sell his old car. He kept saying it was in
perfect condition and had amazing features. But in fact, he was
overstating. The car had many problems. So, overstate means to
exaggerate or represent something as being better or more than it really
is.

**一个男人试图卖掉他的旧车。他一直说车况完美还有很棒的功能。但实际上，他言过其实了。这辆车有很多问题。所以，overstate
意思是夸大或把某事说得比实际更好或更多。**
