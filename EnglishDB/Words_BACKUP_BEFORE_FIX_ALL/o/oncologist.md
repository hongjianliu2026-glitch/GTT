## oncologist

**英音:** _[ˌɒŋkəˈlɒdʒɪst]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɑːŋkəˈlɑːdʒɪst]_

**译义:** *n.肿瘤学家*

### 短语

+ **Medical Oncologist:** _医学肿瘤学家_

+ **Radiation Oncologist:** _放射肿瘤科医生_

+ **Pediatric Oncologist:** _儿科肿瘤学家_

+ **Oncologist Team:** _肿瘤学家团队_

+ **Leading Oncologist:** _首席肿瘤学家_

### 背诵小故事

> Once there was a doctor named Tom. He specialized in treating cancer
patients. People called him an oncologist. He was very dedicated and
always tried his best to help patients fight cancer.

**从前有个叫汤姆的医生。他专门治疗癌症患者。人们称他为肿瘤学家。他非常敬业，总是尽力帮助患者对抗癌症。**
