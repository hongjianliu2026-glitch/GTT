## ordnance

**英音:** _[\'ɔːdnəns]_ **美音:** _[\'ɔrdnəns]_

**译义:** *n.军火*

### 短语

+ **ordnance depot:** _军火库_

+ **ordnance survey:** _（英国）全国地形测量_

+ **ordnance factory:** _兵工厂_

+ **ordnance equipment:** _军械装备_

+ **ordnance officer:** _军械军官_

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a battle, a soldier was in charge of handling
ordnance. He knew the power of these weapons and was very careful. The
ordnance included cannons and bullets. With them, they protected their
land.

**从前，在一场战斗中，一名士兵负责处理军火。他知道这些武器的威力并且非常小心。军火包括大炮和子弹。凭借它们，他们保卫了自己的土地。**
