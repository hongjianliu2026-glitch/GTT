## witchcraft

**英音:** _[\'wɪtʃkrɑːft]_ **美音:** _[\'wɪtʃkræft]_

**译义:** *n.魔法,魔力*

### 短语

+ **black witchcraft:** _黑魔法_

+ **practice witchcraft:** _施行巫术_

+ **witchcraft belief:** _巫术信仰_

+ **ancient witchcraft:** _古代巫术_

+ **witchcraft ritual:** _巫术仪式_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She was accused of practicing witchcraft and was brought to
trial.**

**中文翻译:** 她被指控施行巫术并被送上法庭。

**单词释义:** 巫术；魔法

#### 例句2

> **The villagers believed that the strange illness was caused by
witchcraft.**

**中文翻译:** 村民们认为这种奇怪的疾病是由巫术引起的。

**单词释义:** 巫术；魔法

#### 例句3

> **He was fascinated by the history and mystery of witchcraft.**

**中文翻译:** 他对巫术的历史和神秘性着迷。

**单词释义:** 巫术；魔法

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there was a mysterious woman
practicing witchcraft. She could make things disappear and control the
elements. But her power was discovered, and people were afraid.
Remember, witchcraft is the magic of witches.

**从前，在一片黑暗的森林里，有一个神秘的女人在施行巫术。她能让东西消失并控制元素。但她的力量被发现了，人们很害怕。记住，witchcraft
就是女巫的魔法。**
