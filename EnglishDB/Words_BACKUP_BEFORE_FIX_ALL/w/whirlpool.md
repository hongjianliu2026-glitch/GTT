## whirlpool

**英音:** _[\'wɜːlpuːl]_ **美音:** _[\'wɝlpul]_

**译义:** *n.漩涡,涡流,混乱,纷乱*

### 短语

+ **whirlpool bath:** _漩涡浴_

+ **whirlpool galaxy:** _漩涡星系_

+ **create a whirlpool:** _制造一个漩涡_

+ **fall into a whirlpool:** _落入漩涡_

+ **navigate a whirlpool:** _驾驭漩涡_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The boat was sucked into the whirlpool and vanished.**

**中文翻译:** 那艘船被漩涡吸进去然后消失了。

**单词释义:** 漩涡

#### 例句2

> **He narrowly escaped being drowned in the whirlpool.**

**中文翻译:** 他险些在漩涡中溺水。

**单词释义:** 漩涡

#### 例句3

> **The powerful whirlpool pulled everything around it
underwater.**

**中文翻译:** 强大的漩涡把周围的一切都卷入水下。

**单词释义:** 漩涡

### 背诵小故事

> A brave adventurer was rowing a boat. Suddenly, the boat was caught
in a powerful whirlpool. It spun around and around. The adventurer
struggled but finally managed to escape. Remember, whirlpool means a
powerful circular movement of water.

**一位勇敢的探险家正在划船。突然，船被卷入了一个强大的漩涡。它不停地旋转。探险家奋力挣扎，最终成功逃脱。记住，whirlpool
意思是强大的水流漩涡。**
