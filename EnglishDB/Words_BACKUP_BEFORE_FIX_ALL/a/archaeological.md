## archaeological

**英音:** _[ˌɑːkɪəˈlɒdʒɪkəl]_ **美音:**
_[ˌɑrkiəˈlɑdʒɪkəl]_

**译义:** *adj.考古学的,考古学上的*

### 短语

+ **archaeological site:** _考古遗址_

+ **archaeological discovery:** _考古发现_

+ **archaeological research:** _考古研究_

+ **archaeological evidence:** _考古证据_

+ **archaeological excavation:** _考古发掘_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The archaeological discovery shed new light on ancient
civilizations.**

**中文翻译:** 这一考古发现为了解古代文明提供了新的线索。

**单词释义:** 考古学的

#### 例句2

> **She is engaged in archaeological research in the desert.**

**中文翻译:** 她在沙漠里从事考古研究。

**单词释义:** 考古学的

#### 例句3

> **The museum has a rich collection of archaeological artifacts.**

**中文翻译:** 博物馆藏有丰富的考古文物。

**单词释义:** 考古学的

### 背诵小故事

> A group of brave adventurers went on an archaeological expedition.
They dug deep into the ground, looking for ancient treasures and clues.
Their efforts were all for understanding the past, which is what
\'archaeological\' is about - related to the study of ancient things.

**一群勇敢的冒险家进行了一次考古探险。他们深挖土地，寻找古老的宝藏和线索。他们的努力都是为了了解过去，这就是"archaeological"的含义 -
与古代事物的研究有关。**
