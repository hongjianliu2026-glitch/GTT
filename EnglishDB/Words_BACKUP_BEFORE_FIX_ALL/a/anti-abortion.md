## anti-abortion

**英音:** _[ˌænti əˈbɔːʃn]_ **美音:** _[ˌænti əˈbɔːrʃn]_

**译义:** *adj. 反堕胎的*

+ **anti-abortion movement:** _反堕胎运动_

+ **anti-abortion law:** _反堕胎法_

+ **anti-abortion activist:** _反堕胎活动家_

+ **anti-abortion stance:** _反堕胎立场_

+ **anti-abortion campaign:** _反堕胎运动_

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, a group of passionate people held signs with the
word \'anti-abortion\' on them. They firmly believed that every life
should have a chance to be born. They fought to protect the unborn
babies, showing their strong stance.

**在一个小镇上，一群充满激情的人举着写有'anti-abortion（反堕胎）'字样的牌子。他们坚信每一个生命都应该有出生的机会。他们为保护未出生的婴儿而斗争，表明了他们坚定的立场。**
