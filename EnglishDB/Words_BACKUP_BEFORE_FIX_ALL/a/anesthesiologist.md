## anesthesiologist

**英音:** _[,ænəs,θiːzɪ\'ɒlədʒɪst]_ **美音:**
_[,ænəs,θizɪ\'ɑlədʒɪst]_

**译义:** *n.麻醉学者*

### 短语

+ **board-certified anesthesiologist:** _获得委员会认证的麻醉师_

+ **experienced anesthesiologist:** _经验丰富的麻醉师_

+ **anesthesiologist in charge:** _主管麻醉师_

+ **qualified anesthesiologist:** _合格的麻醉师_

+ **renowned anesthesiologist:** _著名的麻醉师_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The anesthesiologist carefully monitored the patient\'s vital
signs during the surgery.**

**中文翻译:** 在手术期间，麻醉师仔细监测病人的生命体征。

**单词释义:** 麻醉师

#### 例句2

> **She decided to become an anesthesiologist to help patients
through difficult surgeries.**

**中文翻译:** 她决定成为一名麻醉师，以帮助病人度过艰难的手术。

**单词释义:** 麻醉师

#### 例句3

> **The anesthesiologist explained the risks and benefits of the
anesthesia to the patient.**

**中文翻译:** 麻醉师向病人解释了麻醉的风险和益处。

**单词释义:** 麻醉师

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a patient was very nervous before the surgery. But the
anesthesiologist came and reassured him, saying, \'Don\'t worry, I\'ll
make you feel no pain.\' The patient trusted the anesthesiologist and
the operation went smoothly.

**有一天，一位病人在手术前非常紧张。但是麻醉师来了并安慰他说：'别担心，我会让你感觉不到疼痛。'病人信任了麻醉师，手术顺利进行。**
