## antagonistic

**英音:** _[æn,tæg(ə)\'nɪstɪk]_ **美音:**
_[æn,tæɡə\'nɪstɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.反对的,敌对的*

### 短语

+ **antagonistic attitude:** _敌对态度_

+ **antagonistic relationship:** _对抗关系_

+ **antagonistic forces:** _敌对势力_

+ **antagonistic behavior:** _对抗行为_

+ **antagonistic reaction:** _对抗反应_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The two dogs had an antagonistic relationship and always fought
when they met.**

**中文翻译:** 这两只狗关系敌对，每次见面都会打架。

**单词释义:** 敌对的，对抗性的

#### 例句2

> **His antagonistic attitude made it difficult to work with him.**

**中文翻译:** 他的对抗态度使得和他一起工作很困难。

**单词释义:** 对抗的，对立的

#### 例句3

> **The new policy received antagonistic responses from the
public.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策遭到了公众的反对回应。

**单词释义:** 反对的，对抗性的

### 背诵小故事

> A king had two advisers. One was always friendly, but the other was
antagonistic. He always opposed the king\'s decisions and created
conflicts. So, \'antagonistic\' means being opposed and causing
conflicts.

**一位国王有两个顾问。一个总是很友好，但另一个则是敌对的。他总是反对国王的决定并制造冲突。所以，'antagonistic'意思是对立的、引起冲突的。**
