## ascertain

**英音:** _[,æsə\'teɪn]_ **美音:** _[\'æsɚ\'ten]_

**译义:** *vt.确定,探知*

### 短语

+ **ascertain the truth:** _查明真相_

+ **ascertain the cause:** _确定原因_

+ **ascertain the facts:** _查明事实_

+ **ascertain one\'s position:** _确定某人的位置_

+ **ascertain the details:** _弄清楚细节_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The detective was determined to ascertain the truth of the
crime.**

**中文翻译:** 这位侦探决心查明犯罪的真相。

**单词释义:** 查明；确定

#### 例句2

> **It took several weeks to ascertain the cause of the problem.**

**中文翻译:** 花了好几周才确定问题的原因。

**单词释义:** 确定；弄清楚

#### 例句3

> **We need to ascertain whether he is qualified for the
position.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要确定他是否胜任这个职位。

**单词释义:** 确定；查明

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was trying to ascertain the secret of a mysterious plant.
He did many experiments and finally found the answer. \'Ascertain\'
means to find out something for sure, just like the scientist did.

**一位科学家试图确定一种神秘植物的秘密。他做了许多实验，最终找到了答案。\'ascertain\'意思是确定，弄清楚某事，就像这位科学家所做的那样。**
