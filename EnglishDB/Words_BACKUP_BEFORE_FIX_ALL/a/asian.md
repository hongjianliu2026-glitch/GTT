## Asian

**英音:** _[ˈeɪʃn]_ **美音:** _[ˈeɪʒən]_

**译义:** *adj.亚洲的；亚洲人的 n.亚洲人*

### 短语

+ **Asian cuisine:** _亚洲美食_

+ **Asian elephant:** _亚洲象_

+ **Asian Games:** _亚运会_

+ **South Asian:** _南亚的；南亚人_

+ **East Asian:** _东亚的；东亚人_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She is an Asian girl with beautiful black hair.**

**中文翻译:** 她是一个有着漂亮黑发的亚洲女孩。

**单词释义:** 亚洲的；亚洲人的

#### 例句2

> **The Asian market has great potential for our products.**

**中文翻译:** 亚洲市场对我们的产品有很大的潜力。

**单词释义:** 亚洲的

#### 例句3

> **Many Asian countries have a long history and rich culture.**

**中文翻译:** 许多亚洲国家有着悠久的历史和丰富的文化。

**单词释义:** 亚洲的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. He met a friendly
Asian girl during his travel. She had beautiful black hair and wore a
traditional dress. Tom was deeply impressed by her kindness and charm.
