## arbitration

**英音:** _[ɑːbɪ\'treɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,ɑrbɪ\'treʃən]_

**译义:** *n.仲裁,公断*

### 短语

+ **arbitration award:** _仲裁裁决_

+ **arbitration clause:** _仲裁条款_

+ **arbitration process:** _仲裁程序_

+ **commercial arbitration:** _商业仲裁_

+ **international arbitration:** _国际仲裁_

### 背诵小故事

> A king needed to solve a dispute between two knights. So, he chose a
wise old man for arbitration. The old man listened carefully and made a
fair decision, ending the conflict.

**一位国王需要解决两位骑士之间的争端。于是，他选择了一位睿智的老人进行仲裁。老人仔细聆听后做出了公平的决定，结束了这场冲突。**
