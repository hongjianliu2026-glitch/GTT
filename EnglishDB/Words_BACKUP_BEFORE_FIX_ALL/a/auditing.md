## auditing

**英音:** _[\'ɔːdɪtɪŋ 　]_ **美音:** _[\'ɔdɪtɪŋ]_

**译义:** *v.审核*

### 短语

+ **internal auditing:** _内部审计_

+ **auditing standards:** _审计标准_

+ **financial auditing:** _财务审计_

+ **audit trail:** _审计跟踪；审查跟踪_

+ **auditing committee:** _审计委员会_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company\'s financial statements are subject to regular
auditing.**

**中文翻译:** 公司的财务报表需要定期接受审计。

**单词释义:** 审计

#### 例句2

> **Auditing helps to ensure the accuracy and transparency of
financial records.**

**中文翻译:** 审计有助于确保财务记录的准确性和透明度。

**单词释义:** 审计

#### 例句3

> **She is an expert in the field of auditing and has many years of
experience.**

**中文翻译:** 她是审计领域的专家，有多年的经验。

**单词释义:** 审计

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a company was in chaos. But then came an expert in auditing.
He checked every detail carefully, like a detective. Finally, he found
the problems and saved the company. Auditing means the process of
checking and verifying financial records.

**有一天，一家公司陷入混乱。但随后来了一位审计专家。他仔细检查每一个细节，像个侦探。最后，他发现了问题并拯救了公司。Auditing
意思是检查和核实财务记录的过程。**
