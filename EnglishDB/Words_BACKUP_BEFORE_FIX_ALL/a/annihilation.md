## annihilation

**英音:** _[ənaɪɪ\'leɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[ə,naɪə\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.歼灭,灭绝*

### 短语

+ **total annihilation:** _彻底毁灭_

+ **nuclear annihilation:** _核毁灭_

+ **risk of annihilation:** _毁灭的风险_

+ **prevent annihilation:** _防止毁灭_

+ **threat of annihilation:** _毁灭的威胁_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The enemy forces suffered total annihilation in the battle.**

**中文翻译:** 敌军在战斗中遭受了全军覆没。

**单词释义:** 毁灭；消灭

#### 例句2

> **The threat of nuclear annihilation hangs over the world.**

**中文翻译:** 核毁灭的威胁笼罩着世界。

**单词释义:** 灭绝；歼灭

#### 例句3

> **The annihilation of the ancient civilization remains a
mystery.**

**中文翻译:** 古代文明的消亡仍然是个谜。

**单词释义:** 消亡；湮灭

### 背诵小故事

> A brave warrior was facing a powerful enemy. The enemy\'s aim was
total annihilation of the warrior\'s village. But the warrior fought
hard and prevented this disaster. Remember, annihilation means total
destruction.

**一位勇敢的战士面对着强大的敌人。敌人的目的是将战士的村庄彻底毁灭。但战士奋力抵抗，阻止了这场灾难。记住，annihilation
意思是彻底毁灭。**
