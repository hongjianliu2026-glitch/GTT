## apparatus

**英音:** _[ˌæpəˈreɪtəs]_ **美音:** _[ˌæpəˈrætəs]_

**译义:** *n.器械,设备,仪器*

### 短语

+ **experimental apparatus:** _实验仪器_

+ **fire-fighting apparatus:** _消防设备_

+ **medical apparatus:** _医疗设备_

+ **measuring apparatus:** _测量仪器_

+ **gymnastic apparatus:** _体操器械_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The firemen needed special apparatus to rescue the people from
the burning building.**

**中文翻译:** 消防员需要特殊的设备来将人们从着火的建筑物中救出。

**单词释义:** 设备；仪器；装置

#### 例句2

> **The scientific apparatus in the laboratory is very advanced.**

**中文翻译:** 实验室的科学仪器非常先进。

**单词释义:** 仪器；设备

#### 例句3

> **The breathing apparatus helped the diver stay underwater for a
long time.**

**中文翻译:** 呼吸器帮助潜水员在水下长时间停留。

**单词释义:** 装置；设备

### 背诵小故事

> A group of scientists was in a rush to complete an important
experiment. But they were missing the key apparatus. Just in time, a
brave assistant brought it, and the experiment succeeded. The apparatus
was the hero!

**一群科学家急于完成一项重要实验。但他们缺少关键仪器。就在这时，一位勇敢的助手带来了它，实验成功了。仪器是英雄！**
