## appraise

**英音:** _[ə\'preɪz]_ **美音:** _[ə\'prez]_

**译义:** *v.评价*

### 短语

+ **appraise value:** _评估价值_

+ **appraise performance:** _评估绩效_

+ **appraise quality:** _评估质量_

+ **appraise situation:** _评估情况_

+ **appraise asset:** _评估资产_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The expert was hired to appraise the value of the antique
vase.**

**中文翻译:** 专家被雇来评估这个古董花瓶的价值。

**单词释义:** 评估；估价

#### 例句2

> **The manager will appraise the performance of each employee at the
end of the year.**

**中文翻译:** 经理将在年底评估每位员工的表现。

**单词释义:** 评价；评定

#### 例句3

> **It is necessary to appraise the situation carefully before making
a decision.**

**中文翻译:** 在做决定之前有必要仔细评估形势。

**单词释义:** 估量；鉴定

### 背诵小故事

> A king needed to appraise the value of a precious gem. His wise
advisor carefully examined it and gave an accurate assessment. The king
was happy with the appraise and rewarded the advisor.

**一位国王需要评估一颗珍贵宝石的价值。他聪明的顾问仔细检查后给出了准确的评价。国王对这次评估很满意，并奖励了顾问。**
