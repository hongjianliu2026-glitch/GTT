## accustomed

**英音:** _[ə\'kʌstəmd]_ **美音:** _[ə\'kʌstəmd]_

**译义:** *adj.通常的,习惯的,按照风俗习惯的*

### 短语

+ **be accustomed to:** _习惯于_

+ **become accustomed to:** _变得习惯于_

+ **get accustomed to:** _适应；习惯_

+ **accustomed habits:** _习惯的习性_

+ **accustomed way:** _习惯的方式_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I am accustomed to getting up early.**

**中文翻译:** 我习惯早起。

**单词释义:** 习惯

#### 例句2

> **She is accustomed to hard work.**

**中文翻译:** 她习惯艰苦工作。

**单词释义:** 习惯

#### 例句3

> **He has become accustomed to the new environment.**

**中文翻译:** 他已经习惯了新环境。

**单词释义:** 习惯

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a boy who was accustomed to getting up
early to read. Every day, he did the same thing. Eventually, this habit
made him very knowledgeable. \'Accustomed\' means being used to
something.

**从前，有个男孩习惯早起读书。每天他都如此。最终，这个习惯让他知识渊博。\'accustomed\'意思是习惯于某事。**
