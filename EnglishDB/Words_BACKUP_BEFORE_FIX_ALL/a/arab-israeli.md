## Arab-Israeli

**英音:** _[ˌærəb ɪzˈreɪli]_ **美音:** _[ˌærəb
ɪzˈreɪli]_

**译义:** *adj. 阿拉伯-以色列的；n. 阿拉伯-以色列人（或事物）*

### 短语

+ **Arab-Israeli conflict:** _阿以冲突_

+ **Arab-Israeli peace process:** _阿以和平进程_

+ **Arab-Israeli relations:** _阿以关系_

+ **Arab-Israeli war:** _阿以战争_

+ **Arab-Israeli negotiations:** _阿以谈判_

#### 例句1

> **The Arab-Israeli conflict has a long and complex history.**

**单词释义:** 指阿拉伯国家和以色列之间的

### 背诵小故事

> In a small village, there was a conflict between the Arab and Israeli
people. They fought over land and resources. But in the end, they
decided to talk and find a peaceful solution. This shows the complexity
of the Arab-Israeli relationship.

**在一个小村庄里，阿拉伯人和以色列人之间存在冲突。他们为土地和资源而争斗。但最后，他们决定交谈并找到和平的解决方案。这展现了阿以关系的复杂性。**
