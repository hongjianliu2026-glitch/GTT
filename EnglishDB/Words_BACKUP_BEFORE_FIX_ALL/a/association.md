## association

**英音:** _[əˌsəʊʃiˈeɪʃn]_ **美音:** _[əˌsosɪˈeʃən]_

**译义:** *n.协会,联合,结交,联想*

### 短语

+ **trade association:** _贸易协会_

+ **professional association:** _专业协会_

+ **charitable association:** _慈善协会_

+ **student association:** _学生协会_

+ **sports association:** _体育协会_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The association between smoking and lung cancer is well
established.**

**中文翻译:** 吸烟和肺癌之间的关联已被充分证实。

**单词释义:** 关联；联系

#### 例句2

> **He is the president of the local football association.**

**中文翻译:** 他是当地足球协会的主席。

**单词释义:** 协会；社团

#### 例句3

> **I have a pleasant association with that place.**

**中文翻译:** 我对那个地方有愉快的联想。

**单词释义:** 联想；记忆

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to form an association to protect the
local park. They worked together, associated their efforts, and finally
made the park more beautiful. The word \'association\' means a group or
organization formed for a particular purpose.

**一群朋友决定成立一个协会来保护当地的公园。他们共同努力，联合他们的力量，最终让公园变得更漂亮。单词\'association\'意思是为特定目的而形成的团体或组织。**
