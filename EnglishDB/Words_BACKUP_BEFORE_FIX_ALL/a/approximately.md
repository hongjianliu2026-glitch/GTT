## approximately

**英音:** _[ə\'prɒksɪmətlɪ]_ **美音:**
_[ə\'prɑksɪmətli]_

**译义:** *adv.近似地,大约*

### 短语

+ **approximately equal:** _大约相等_

+ **approximately correct:** _大致正确_

+ **approximately the same:** _大致相同_

+ **approximately estimated:** _大约估计_

+ **approximately calculated:** _大约计算_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The journey will take approximately two hours.**

**中文翻译:** 旅程大约需要两个小时。

**单词释义:** 大约

#### 例句2

> **The cost is approximately \$50.**

**中文翻译:** 费用约为 50 美元。

**单词释义:** 大概

#### 例句3

> **There are approximately 300 people in the room.**

**中文翻译:** 房间里大约有300人。

**单词释义:** 大约

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Alice was planning a party. She needed to buy approximately
20 cupcakes. But she wasn\'t sure exactly. So, she guessed and bought a
number close to 20. That\'s what approximately means - not exact but
close.

**有一天，爱丽丝在计划一个聚会。她需要买大约 20
个纸杯蛋糕。但她不确定具体数量。所以，她猜测并买了一个接近 20
的数量。这就是 approximately 的意思 - 不精确但接近。**
