## urinate

**英音:** _[\'jʊərɪneɪt]_ **美音:** _[ˈjʊərɪˌneɪt]_

**译义:** *vi.小便,撒尿*

### 短语

+ **urinate frequently:** _频繁排尿_

+ **urinate involuntarily:** _不由自主地排尿_

+ **urinate normally:** _正常排尿_

+ **difficulty urinating:** _排尿困难_

+ **refrain from urinating:** _忍住不排尿_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I need to urinate urgently.**

**中文翻译:** 我急需小便。

**单词释义:** 小便

#### 例句2

> **The doctor asked him if he had difficulty urinating.**

**中文翻译:** 医生问他排尿是否有困难。

**单词释义:** 排尿

#### 例句3

> **She went to the bathroom to urinate.**

**中文翻译:** 她去洗手间小便。

**单词释义:** 小便

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was in a hurry and needed to urinate. He looked
everywhere for a toilet. Finally, he found one and felt relieved.
