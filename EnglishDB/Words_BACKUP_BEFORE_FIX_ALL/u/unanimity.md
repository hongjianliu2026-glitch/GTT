## unanimity

**英音:** _[ˌju:nəˈnɪmətɪ]_ **美音:** _[ˌjunəˈnɪmətɪ]_

**译义:** *n.全体一致*

### 短语

+ **reach unanimity:** _达成一致_

+ **unanimity of opinion:** _意见一致_

+ **unanimity among:** _在\...\...之中一致_

+ **unanimity agreement:** _一致同意_

+ **unanimity decision:** _一致决定_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The committee reached unanimity on the important decision.**

**中文翻译:** 委员会在这个重要决定上达成了一致意见。

**单词释义:** 一致意见

#### 例句2

> **There was unanimity among the shareholders regarding the new
business strategy.**

**中文翻译:** 股东们对新的商业策略意见一致。

**单词释义:** 一致

#### 例句3

> **The unanimity of the team was crucial for the success of the
project.**

**中文翻译:** 团队的一致是项目成功的关键。

**单词释义:** 一致；一致同意

### 背诵小故事

> In a village meeting, everyone had different opinions at first. But
after thorough discussion, they reached unanimity and agreed on a plan
to build a new school. Unanimity means complete agreement among all.

**在一次村庄会议上，起初大家意见不一。但经过深入讨论，他们达成了一致，同意修建一所新学校。unanimity
意思是全体一致。**
