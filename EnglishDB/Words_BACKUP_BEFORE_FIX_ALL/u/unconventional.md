## unconventional

**英音:** _[ʌnkən\'venʃ(ə)n(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,ʌnkən\'vɛnʃənl]_

**译义:** * /n.非常规,异常ly//adv.不依惯例l/adj.非传统的*

### 短语

+ **unconventional methods:** _非传统的方法_

+ **unconventional thinking:** _非传统的思维_

+ **unconventional approach:** _非传统的途径_

+ **unconventional style:** _非传统的风格_

+ **unconventional design:** _非传统的设计_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Her unconventional approach to problem-solving often leads to
innovative solutions.**

**中文翻译:** 她解决问题的非传统方法常常带来创新的解决方案。

**单词释义:** 非传统的

#### 例句2

> **The artist\'s unconventional style sets him apart from others in
the field.**

**中文翻译:** 这位艺术家非传统的风格使他在这个领域与众不同。

**单词释义:** 非传统的

#### 例句3

> **The unconventional design of the building attracted a lot of
attention.**

**中文翻译:** 这座建筑非传统的设计吸引了很多关注。

**单词释义:** 非传统的

### 背诵小故事

> Once there was a painter who always used strange colors and wild
styles. Everyone thought he was unconventional. But his art became
famous because it was so unique. Unconventional means not following the
usual or traditional way.

**曾经有一位画家，总是使用奇特的色彩和狂野的风格。每个人都认为他与众不同。但他的艺术因其独特而出名。unconventional
意思是不遵循通常或传统的方式。**
