## horseshoe

**英音:** _[\'hɔːsʃuː; -ʃʃ-]_ **美音:** _[\'hɔrʃʃu]_

**译义:** *n.马蹄铁vt.装蹄铁于*

### 短语

+ **horseshoe crab:** _鲎_

+ **horseshoe magnet:** _马蹄形磁铁_

+ **throw a horseshoe:** _扔马蹄铁_

+ **horseshoe bend:** _马蹄形弯道_

+ **horseshoe kidney:** _马蹄肾_

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a horse lost its shoe. The blacksmith worked hard
to make a new horseshoe for it. With the new horseshoe, the horse could
run freely again. Remember, a horseshoe is the metal thing protecting a
horse\'s hoof.

**从前，一匹马丢了它的蹄铁。铁匠努力为它打造了一个新的马蹄铁。有了新的马蹄铁，马又能自由奔跑了。记住，horseshoe
就是保护马蹄的金属物件。**
