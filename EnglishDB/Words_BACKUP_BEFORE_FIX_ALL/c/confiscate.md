## confiscate

**英音:** _[\'kɒnfɪskeɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'kɑnfɪsket]_

**译义:** *vt.没收,充公,查抄,征用adj.被没收的*

### 短语

+ **confiscate property:** _没收财产_

+ **confiscate goods:** _没收货物_

+ **confiscate weapons:** _没收武器_

+ **confiscate illegal gains:** _没收非法所得_

+ **be confiscated:** _被没收_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The customs officials confiscated the illegal drugs.**

**中文翻译:** 海关官员没收了这些非法毒品。

**单词释义:** 没收

#### 例句2

> **The teacher confiscated the mobile phone from the student who was
using it in class.**

**中文翻译:** 老师没收了在课堂上使用手机的学生。

**单词释义:** 没收

#### 例句3

> **The police confiscated the stolen goods.**

**中文翻译:** 警方没收了赃物。

**单词释义:** 没收

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was caught stealing valuable jewels. The police confiscated
all the stolen items from him. This action showed that confiscate means
to take away someone\'s property officially, especially as a punishment.

**一个小偷被抓到偷了贵重的珠宝。警察没收了他所有偷来的东西。这个行为表明confiscate意思是官方地拿走某人的财产，尤其是作为一种惩罚。**
