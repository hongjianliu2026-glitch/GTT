## crackdown

**英音:** _[\'krækdaʊn]_ **美音:** _[\'krækdaʊn]_

**译义:** *n.压迫,镇压,打击*

### 短语

+ **crackdown on crime:** _打击犯罪_

+ **severe crackdown:** _严厉打击_

+ **government crackdown:** _政府的打击行动_

+ **crackdown measures:** _打击措施_

+ **enforce a crackdown:** _实施打击_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government has launched a crackdown on illegal gambling.**

**中文翻译:** 政府已对非法赌博展开打击。

**单词释义:** 打击行动；镇压

#### 例句2

> **There was a major crackdown on drug trafficking in the city.**

**中文翻译:** 该市对贩毒活动进行了严厉打击。

**单词释义:** 严厉打击；制裁

#### 例句3

> **The police\'s crackdown on crime has led to a decrease in the
crime rate.**

**中文翻译:** 警方打击犯罪导致犯罪率下降。

**单词释义:** 镇压；打击

### 背诵小故事

> A group of criminals were causing chaos in the city. The police
decided to take a strong action, a crackdown. They worked hard to catch
the criminals and restore peace. Finally, the crackdown was successful
and the city became safe again.

**一群罪犯在城市里制造混乱。警方决定采取强硬行动，即镇压。他们努力抓捕罪犯，恢复和平。最终，镇压行动成功了，城市再次变得安全。**
