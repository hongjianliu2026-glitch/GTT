## cancellation

**英音:** _[,kænsə\'leɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,kænsə\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.取消*

### 短语

+ **order cancellation:** _订单取消_

+ **flight cancellation:** _航班取消_

+ **cancellation fee:** _取消费用_

+ **cancellation policy:** _取消政策_

+ **cancellation notice:** _取消通知_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The cancellation of the flight caused a lot of inconvenience for
the passengers.**

**中文翻译:** 航班的取消给乘客带来了很多不便。

**单词释义:** 取消

#### 例句2

> **Due to bad weather, the cancellation of the outdoor concert was
announced.**

**中文翻译:** 由于恶劣天气，户外音乐会的取消被宣布了。

**单词释义:** 取消

#### 例句3

> **The hotel accepted the cancellation of our reservation without
any charge.**

**中文翻译:** 酒店接受了我们预订的取消，没有收取任何费用。

**单词释义:** 取消

### 背诵小故事

> A tourist was very excited about a trip but received a cancellation
notice. He was sad because all his plans were ruined. The word
\'cancellation\' means the act of cancelling something.

**一位游客对一次旅行非常兴奋，但收到了取消通知。他很伤心，因为他所有的计划都泡汤了。单词\'cancellation\'意思是取消某物的行为。**
