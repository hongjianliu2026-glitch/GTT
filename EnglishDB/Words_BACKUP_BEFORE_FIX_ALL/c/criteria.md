## criteria

**英音:** _[kraɪ\'tɪərɪə]_ **美音:** _[kraɪ\'tɪrɪə]_

**译义:** *n.pl.标准*

### 短语

+ **evaluation criteria:** _评估标准_

+ **selection criteria:** _选择标准_

+ **design criteria:** _设计标准_

+ **quality criteria:** _质量标准_

+ **success criteria:** _成功标准_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The criteria for this scholarship are very strict.**

**中文翻译:** 该奖学金的标准非常严格。

**单词释义:** 标准；准则

#### 例句2

> **We need to establish clear criteria for evaluating the success of
the project.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要建立明确的标准来评估项目的成功。

**单词释义:** 标准

#### 例句3

> **The criteria used to select candidates were based on their skills
and experience.**

**中文翻译:** 用于选择候选人的标准是基于他们的技能和经验。

**单词释义:** 标准；条件

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was solving a case. The criteria for finding the criminal
were fingerprints and witnesses. But he needed to be very careful to
meet all these criteria and catch the right person.

**一位侦探正在破案。找到罪犯的标准是指纹和证人。但他需要非常小心，满足所有这些标准并抓住正确的人。**
