## cadmium

**英音:** _[\'kædmɪəm]_ **美音:** _[\'kædmɪəm]_

**译义:** *n.[化]镉*

### 短语

+ **cadmium sulfide:** _硫化镉_

+ **cadmium plating:** _镀镉_

+ **cadmium red:** _镉红_

+ **cadmium yellow:** _镉黄_

+ **cadmium poisoning:** _镉中毒_
