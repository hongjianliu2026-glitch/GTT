## collegiate

**英音:** _[kə\'liːdʒ(ɪ)ət]_ **美音:** _[kə\'lidʒɪət]_

**译义:** *adj.学院的*

### 短语

+ **collegiate system:** _大学制度_

+ **collegiate education:** _大学教育_

+ **collegiate atmosphere:** _大学氛围_

+ **collegiate sports:** _大学体育_

+ **collegiate life:** _大学生活_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She participated in various collegiate sports events during her
university years.**

**中文翻译:** 她在大学期间参加了各种大学体育赛事。

**单词释义:** 大学的

#### 例句2

> **The collegiate atmosphere in this campus is very vibrant.**

**中文翻译:** 这个校园的学院氛围非常活跃。

**单词释义:** 大学的

#### 例句3

> **His collegiate life was filled with academic achievements and
social activities.**

**中文翻译:** 他的大学生活充满了学术成就和社会活动。

**单词释义:** 大学的

### 背诵小故事

> A student named Lily was excited to enter the collegiate world. She
found it full of challenges and opportunities. She joined various
collegiate activities and made many friends. \'Collegiate\' means
related to or typical of college.

**一个叫莉莉的学生很兴奋地进入了大学世界。她发现那里充满了挑战和机会。她参加了各种大学活动并交了很多朋友。'collegiate'意思是与大学有关的或具有大学特点的。**
