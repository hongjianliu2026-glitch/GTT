## concentrated

**英音:** _[\'kɒnsntreɪtɪd]_ **美音:**
_[\'kɑnsn\'tretɪd]_

**译义:** *adj.集中的,浓缩的*

### 短语

+ **concentrated effort:** _集中的努力_

+ **concentrated solution:** _浓缩溶液_

+ **concentrated study:** _专心的研究_

+ **concentrated power:** _集中的权力_

+ **concentrated attention:** _集中的注意力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She concentrated on her studies and got excellent grades.**

**中文翻译:** 她专心于学习并取得了优异的成绩。

**单词释义:** 专心于

#### 例句2

> **The concentrated juice needs to be diluted before drinking.**

**中文翻译:** 浓缩果汁在饮用前需要稀释。

**单词释义:** 浓缩的

#### 例句3

> **He concentrated his efforts on solving the difficult problem.**

**中文翻译:** 他集中精力解决这个难题。

**单词释义:** 集中

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was doing an experiment. He needed to get a concentrated
solution. So he focused all his attention and effort, removing
impurities to make the liquid highly concentrated. Finally, he
succeeded!

**一位科学家正在做实验。他需要得到一种浓缩溶液。所以他集中了所有的注意力和精力，去除杂质以制成高浓度的液体。最终，他成功了！**
