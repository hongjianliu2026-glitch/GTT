## chimpanzee

**英音:** _[tʃɪmpæn\'ziː]_ **美音:** _[,tʃɪmpæn\'zi]_

**译义:** *n.[动]非洲的小人猿,黑猩猩*

### 短语

+ **chimpanzee habitat:** _黑猩猩栖息地_

+ **chimpanzee behavior:** _黑猩猩行为_

+ **chimpanzee intelligence:** _黑猩猩智力_

+ **chimpanzee communication:** _黑猩猩交流_

+ **chimpanzee conservation:** _黑猩猩保护_

### 背诵小故事

> A chimpanzee named Chico was very smart. It could use sticks to get
fruits. One day, it showed us how funny it was by making faces.
Remember, chimpanzee is a kind of smart ape.

**一只叫奇科的黑猩猩非常聪明。它能用棍子获取水果。有一天，它向我们做鬼脸展示它有多有趣。记住，chimpanzee
是一种聪明的猿类。**
