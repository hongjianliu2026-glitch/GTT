## contribution

**英音:** _[kɒntrɪ\'bjuːʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,kɑntrɪ\'bjuʃən]_

**译义:** *n.捐献,贡献,投稿*

### 短语

+ **make a contribution:** _做出贡献_

+ **significant contribution:** _重大贡献_

+ **valuable contribution:** _有价值的贡献_

+ **contribution to:** _对\...\...的贡献_

+ **individual contribution:** _个人贡献_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His contribution to the project was highly valued.**

**中文翻译:** 他对这个项目的贡献受到了高度重视。

**单词释义:** 贡献

#### 例句2

> **The company relies on the contributions of its employees to
succeed.**

**中文翻译:** 公司依靠员工的贡献来取得成功。

**单词释义:** 贡献

#### 例句3

> **She made a significant contribution to the charity event.**

**中文翻译:** 她为慈善活动做出了重大贡献。

**单词释义:** 贡献

### 背诵小故事

> A poor student wanted to make a contribution to his school. He worked
hard and saved money to buy books for the library. His act was praised
by all. Remember, contribution means something given to help.

**一个贫困学生想为他的学校做出贡献。他努力工作并攒钱为图书馆买书。他的行为受到了所有人的称赞。记住，contribution
意思是做出的有助于某事的东西。**
