## comprehend

**英音:** _[kɒmprɪ\'hend]_ **美音:** _[,kɑmprɪ\'hɛnd]_

**译义:** *vt.领会,理解,包括(包含),由\...组成*

### 短语

+ **fail to comprehend:** _未能理解_

+ **begin to comprehend:** _开始理解_

+ **fully comprehend:** _完全理解_

+ **struggle to comprehend:** _努力理解_

+ **easily comprehend:** _容易理解_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I find it hard to comprehend his motives.**

**中文翻译:** 我很难理解他的动机。

**单词释义:** 理解

#### 例句2

> **She could not comprehend the extent of the damage.**

**中文翻译:** 她无法理解损坏的程度。

**单词释义:** 理解；领会

#### 例句3

> **It is difficult for children to comprehend adult humor.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子们很难理解成人的幽默。

**单词释义:** 理解；明白

### 背诵小故事

> A little monkey wanted to comprehend the mystery of the stars. It
read many books and observed the sky every night. Finally, it began to
understand and could explain the movement of the stars. Just like that,
the monkey comprehended the unknown.

**一只小猴子想要理解星星的奥秘。它读了很多书，每晚都观察天空。最终，它开始明白并且能够解释星星的运动。就像这样，猴子理解了未知的事物。**
