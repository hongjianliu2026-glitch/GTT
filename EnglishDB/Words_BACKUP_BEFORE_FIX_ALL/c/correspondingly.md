## correspondingly

**英音:** _[ˌkɒrəˈspɒndɪŋli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndɪŋli]_

**译义:** *adv.相对地,比照地*

### 短语

+ **correspondingly high:** _相应地高_

+ **correspondingly low:** _相应地低_

+ **correspondingly increased:** _相应地增加_

+ **correspondingly decreased:** _相应地减少_

+ **correspondingly modified:** _相应地修改_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The price of the product increased, and correspondingly, the
demand decreased.**

**中文翻译:** 产品的价格上涨，相应地，需求减少。

**单词释义:** 相应地

#### 例句2

> **The quality of the service improved, and correspondingly,
customer satisfaction rose.**

**中文翻译:** 服务质量提高了，客户满意度相应提高。

**单词释义:** 相应地

#### 例句3

> **The company expanded its business globally, and correspondingly,
its workforce grew.**

**中文翻译:**
该公司在全球范围内拓展业务，相应地，其员工人数也随之增加。

**单词释义:** 相应地

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was solving a mystery. When he found one clue,
correspondingly, another important piece of information emerged. This
helped him solve the case step by step.

**一位侦探正在破解一个谜团。当他发现一条线索时，相应地，另一条重要的信息也出现了。这帮助他一步步地解决了案件。**
