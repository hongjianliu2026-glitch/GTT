## circulation

**英音:** _[sɜːkjʊ\'leɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,sɝkjə\'leʃən]_

**译义:** *n.循环,流通,发行额*

### 短语

+ **blood circulation:** _血液循环_

+ **in circulation:** _在流通；在传播_

+ **circulation system:** _循环系统_

+ **circulation of air:** _空气流通_

+ **improve circulation:** _改善循环_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The circulation of the newspaper has increased significantly this
year.**

**中文翻译:** 这份报纸的发行量今年大幅增加。

**单词释义:** 发行量

#### 例句2

> **Poor circulation can cause cold hands and feet.**

**中文翻译:** 血液循环不良会导致手脚冰凉。

**单词释义:** 血液循环

#### 例句3

> **The book is in wide circulation.**

**中文翻译:** 这本书广泛流传。

**单词释义:** 流传；传播

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, newspapers were distributed everywhere. People
passed them around, ensuring the news had good circulation. This way,
information reached every corner. The word \'circulation\' means the
movement or spread of something, like news or blood.

**在一个小镇上，报纸到处分发。人们相互传阅，确保了新闻有良好的流通。这样，信息传到了每个角落。单词\'circulation\'意思是某物的移动或传播，比如新闻或血液。**
