## continued

**英音:** _[kən\'tɪnjuːd]_ **美音:** _[kən\'tɪnjud]_

**译义:** *adj.继续的,延续的*

### 短语

+ **continued growth:** _持续增长_

+ **continued support:** _持续支持_

+ **continued education:** _继续教育_

+ **continued improvement:** _持续改进_

+ **continued existence:** _持续存在_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The rain continued throughout the night.**

**中文翻译:** 雨整夜下个不停。

**单词释义:** 持续；继续

#### 例句2

> **She continued working despite feeling ill.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管感觉不舒服，她还是继续工作。

**单词释义:** 继续

#### 例句3

> **The story continued in the next chapter.**

**中文翻译:** 这个故事在下一章继续。

**单词释义:** 继续

### 背诵小故事

> A little bird wanted to fly to a distant mountain. It faced many
difficulties but continued its journey without giving up. Finally, it
reached the destination. \'Continued\' means went on without stopping.

**一只小鸟想要飞到一座遥远的山。它面临许多困难，但继续前行没有放弃。最终，它到达了目的地。\'continued\'意思是不停歇地持续下去。**
