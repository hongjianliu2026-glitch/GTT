## conserve

**英音:** _[kən\'sɜːv]_ **美音:** _[kən\'sɝv]_

**译义:** *vt.保存,保藏*

### 短语

+ **conserve energy:** _节约能源_

+ **conserve resources:** _保护资源_

+ **conserve water:** _节约用水_

+ **conserve nature:** _保护大自然_

+ **conserve wildlife:** _保护野生动物_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **We should conserve energy to protect the environment.**

**中文翻译:** 我们应该节约能源以保护环境。

**单词释义:** 节约

#### 例句2

> **The national park is designed to conserve rare species.**

**中文翻译:** 这个国家公园旨在保护珍稀物种。

**单词释义:** 保护

#### 例句3

> **She tried to conserve her strength for the final stretch of the
race.**

**中文翻译:** 她试图为比赛的最后一段路程保存体力。

**单词释义:** 保存

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight named Con was determined to conserve the precious
water in the kingdom. He fought against waste and protected every drop.
With his efforts, the water was saved. Remember, Con + serve = conserve,
to keep and protect.

**一位名叫康的勇敢骑士决心保护王国里珍贵的水资源。他与浪费作斗争，保护每一滴水。通过他的努力，水被保存了下来。记住，Con（康）+
serve（服务）= conserve（保护；保存）**
