## compartment

**英音:** _[kəm\'pɑːtm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[kəm\'pɑrtmənt]_

**译义:** *n.间隔间,车厢*

### 短语

+ **luggage compartment:** _行李舱_

+ **storage compartment:** _存储隔间_

+ **engine compartment:** _发动机舱_

+ **passenger compartment:** _乘客舱_

+ **compartment syndrome:** _筋膜间隔综合征_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The train has compartments for first-class and second-class
passengers.**

**中文翻译:** 火车设有一等座和二等座乘客车厢。

**单词释义:** 车厢

#### 例句2

> **The toolbox has several compartments to organize different
tools.**

**中文翻译:** 工具箱有几个隔间，可以整理不同的工具。

**单词释义:** 隔层

#### 例句3

> **The body is divided into different compartments for various
organs.**

**中文翻译:** 身体被分为不同的室，用于不同的器官。

**单词释义:** 区域

### 背诵小故事

> A train has many compartments. One day, a little girl lost her toy in
a compartment. She searched everywhere and finally found it under the
seat. Remember, compartment means a separate section or room.

**火车有很多隔间。有一天，一个小女孩在一个隔间里弄丢了她的玩具。她到处找，最后在座位下找到了。记住，compartment
意思是单独的空间或隔间。**
