## chip

**英音:** _[tʃɪp]_ **美音:** _[tʃɪp]_

**译义:** *n.碎片,筹码v.削成碎片,碎裂 芯片*

### 短语

+ **chip in:** _to contribute money to a common fund or effort_

+ **chip away:** _to gradually reduce or weaken something_

+ **chip off the old block:** _a child who is very similar to one
of their parents_

+ **chip on one\'s shoulder:** _a feeling of anger or resentment,
often from past experiences_

+ **computer chip:** _a small piece of silicon containing
electronic circuits, used in computers and other devices_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I had fish and chips for lunch.**

**中文翻译:** 我午餐吃了炸鱼薯条。

**单词释义:** 薯条

#### 例句2

> **The cup has a chip on the rim.**

**中文翻译:** 这个杯子边缘有个缺口。

**单词释义:** 缺口；碎片

#### 例句3

> **He chipped a piece of wood off the table.**

**中文翻译:** 他从桌子上削下了一块木头。

**单词释义:** 削；凿；剥落
