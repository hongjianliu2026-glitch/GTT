## cappuccino

**英音:** _[,kæpʊ\'tʃiːnəʊ]_ **美音:** _[,kæpu\'tʃino]_

**译义:** *n.热牛奶咖啡*

### 短语

+ **iced cappuccino:** _冰卡布奇诺_

+ **vanilla cappuccino:** _香草卡布奇诺_

+ **chocolate cappuccino:** _巧克力卡布奇诺_

+ **cappuccino coffee:** _卡布奇诺咖啡_

+ **frothy cappuccino:** _泡沫丰富的卡布奇诺_

### 例句

ordered a cappuccino. The rich and foamy cappuccino gave him great
