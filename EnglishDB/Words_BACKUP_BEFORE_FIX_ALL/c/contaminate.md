## contaminate

**英音:** _[kən\'tæmɪneɪt]_ **美音:** _[kən\'tæmɪnet]_

**译义:** *v.污染*

### 短语

+ **contaminate the environment:** _污染环境_

+ **contaminate food:** _污染食物_

+ **contaminate water:** _污染水_

+ **be contaminated with:** _被\...\...污染_

+ **contaminate the soil:** _污染土壤_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The river was contaminated by industrial waste.**

**中文翻译:** 这条河被工业废料污染了。

**单词释义:** 污染

#### 例句2

> **The food was contaminated during transportation.**

**中文翻译:** 这些食物在运输过程中受到了污染。

**单词释义:** 使受污染

#### 例句3

> **The soil has been contaminated with toxic chemicals.**

**中文翻译:** 这片土壤被有毒化学物质污染了。

**单词释义:** 弄脏；玷污

### 背诵小故事

> A group of scientists were conducting experiments in a laboratory.
One day, a careless assistant spilled a toxic liquid, which contaminated
all the samples and equipment. This incident taught them the importance
of caution.

**一群科学家在实验室里做实验。一天，一个粗心的助手洒出了有毒液体，污染了所有的样本和设备。这个事件让他们明白了谨慎的重要性。**
