## commentator

**英音:** _[\'kɒmənteɪtə]_ **美音:** _[\'kɑmən\'tetɚ]_

**译义:** * 评论员,讲解员*

### 短语

+ **political commentator:** _政治评论员_

+ **sports commentator:** _体育评论员_

+ **television commentator:** _电视评论员_

+ **news commentator:** _新闻评论员_

+ **radio commentator:** _电台评论员_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The sports commentator provided insightful analysis during the
game.**

**中文翻译:** 体育解说员在比赛中提供了精辟的分析。

**单词释义:** 评论员

#### 例句2

> **The political commentator was invited to the talk show to discuss
the latest events.**

**中文翻译:** 政治评论员受邀参加脱口秀节目，讨论最新事件。

**单词释义:** 评论员

#### 例句3

> **The fashion commentator criticized the new collection for its
lack of innovation.**

**中文翻译:** 时尚评论员批评新系列缺乏创新。

**单词释义:** 评论员

### 背诵小故事

> A commentator was at a big sports game. Everyone was excited, but the
commentator had to stay calm and clearly describe what was happening.
His words guided the viewers and made the game more understandable.

**一位评论员在一场大型体育比赛现场。所有人都很兴奋，但评论员必须保持冷静，清晰地描述正在发生的事情。他的话语引导着观众，让比赛更易于理解。**
