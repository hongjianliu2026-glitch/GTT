## collaboration

**英音:** _[kəlæbə\'reɪʃn]_ **美音:** _[kə,læbə\'reʃən]_

**译义:** *n.协作,通敌*

### 短语

+ **in collaboration with:** _与......合作；与......勾结_

+ **collaboration agreement:** _合作协议_

+ **research collaboration:** _研究合作_

+ **international collaboration:** _国际合作_

+ **close collaboration:** _密切合作_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The collaboration between the two companies was highly
successful.**

**中文翻译:** 两家公司之间的合作非常成功。

**单词释义:** 合作

#### 例句2

> **We need more collaboration in our team to finish this project on
time.**

**中文翻译:** 我们的团队需要更多的协作才能按时完成这个项目。

**单词释义:** 协作

#### 例句3

> **Their collaboration in the scientific research led to a
breakthrough.**

**中文翻译:** 他们在科学研究方面的合作取得了突破。

**单词释义:** 合作

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist and an engineer were working on a project. They shared
ideas, helped each other, and this joint effort was called
collaboration. It means working together to achieve a common goal.

**一位科学家和一位工程师正在做一个项目。他们分享想法，互相帮助，这种共同的努力被称为合作。意思是一起工作以实现共同的目标。**
