## claw

**英音:** _[klɔː]_ **美音:** _[klɔ]_

**译义:** *n.爪,脚爪v.抓*

### 短语

+ ** claw back something means to get it back, especially with
difficulty or by force.:** _null_

+ **To scratch or grasp at something with claws.:** _null_

+ **To progress or move forward with difficulty and effort, using
one\'s claws or metaphorically.:** _null_

+ **Claws that are pointed and able to cause injury easily.:**
_null_

+ **The claw of a lion.:** _null_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The cat used its sharp claws to catch the mouse.**

**中文翻译:** 这只猫用它锋利的爪子去抓老鼠。

**单词释义:** 爪子

#### 例句2

> **The eagle clawed at its prey.**

**中文翻译:** 这只鹰用爪子抓它的猎物。

**单词释义:** 用爪子抓

#### 例句3

> **She clawed her way to the top of the corporate ladder.**

**中文翻译:** 她奋力爬上了公司的高层。

**单词释义:** 奋力攀爬

### 背诵小故事

> A brave mouse was cornered by a fierce cat. The mouse saw the cat\'s
sharp claws and was very scared. But it bravely fought back and bit the
cat\'s claw. Finally, it escaped. Remember, \'claw\' means the sharp
part of an animal\'s foot.

**一只勇敢的老鼠被一只凶猛的猫逼到了角落。老鼠看到了猫锋利的爪子，非常害怕。但它勇敢地反击并咬了猫的爪子。最后，它逃走了。记住，\'claw\'是指动物脚部的锋利部分。**
