## commander-in-chief

**英音:** _[ˌkɒməndə ɪn ˈtʃiːf]_ **美音:** _[ˌkɑːməndər ɪn
ˈtʃiːf]_

**译义:** *n.总司令；统帅*

### 短语

+ **military commander-in-chief:** _军事总司令_

+ **naval commander-in-chief:** _海军总司令_

+ **air force commander-in-chief:** _空军总司令_

+ **commander-in-chief of the army:** _陆军总司令_

+ **commander-in-chief of the armed forces:** _武装部队总司令_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The commander-in-chief made a strategic decision during the
war.**

**中文翻译:** 总司令在战争期间做出了一个战略决策。

**单词释义:** 总司令

#### 例句2

> **The president is also the commander-in-chief of the armed
forces.**

**中文翻译:** 总统也是武装部队的总司令。

**单词释义:** 总司令

#### 例句3

> **The commander-in-chief inspected the troops before the
battle.**

**中文翻译:** 总司令在战斗前检阅了部队。

**单词释义:** 总司令

### 背诵小故事

> A brave general was chosen as the commander-in-chief. In a crucial
battle, he led the troops with wisdom and courage, making decisive
decisions that led to victory. His leadership was the key to success.

**一位勇敢的将军被选为总司令。在一场关键的战斗中，他凭借智慧和勇气领导部队，做出果断决定并取得胜利。他的领导是成功的关键。**
