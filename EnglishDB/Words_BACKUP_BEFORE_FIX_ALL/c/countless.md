## countless

**英音:** _[\'kaʊntlɪs]_ **美音:** _[\'kaʊntləs]_

**译义:** *adj.无数的,数不尽的,*

### 短语

+ **countless times:** _无数次_

+ **countless opportunities:** _无数的机会_

+ **countless stars:** _无数的星星_

+ **countless problems:** _无数的问题_

+ **countless people:** _无数的人_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The universe contains countless stars and galaxies.**

**中文翻译:** 宇宙包含无数的恒星和星系。

**单词释义:** 无数的

#### 例句2

> **She has overcome countless difficulties in her life.**

**中文翻译:** 她一生克服了无数困难。

**单词释义:** 无数的

#### 例句3

> **There are countless opportunities waiting for you in the
future.**

**中文翻译:** 未来还有无数的机会在等着你。

**单词释义:** 无数的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a poor boy had countless dreams. He wanted to
travel the world and see countless beautiful sceneries. But he had no
money. However, his determination was countless and he finally made it.

**从前，一个贫穷的男孩有无数的梦想。他想环游世界，看无数的美丽风景。但他没钱。然而，他的决心是无数的，最终他成功了。**
