## constitution

**英音:** _[kɒnstɪ\'tjuːʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[\'kɑnstə\'tʊʃən]_

**译义:** *n.宪法,构造,体质,体格,国体,章程,惯例*

### 短语

+ **written constitution:** _成文宪法_

+ **amend the constitution:** _修改宪法_

+ **constitutional rights:** _宪法权利_

+ **constitutional amendment:** _宪法修正案_

+ **constitutional monarchy:** _君主立宪制_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The constitution guarantees basic rights and freedoms to all
citizens.**

**中文翻译:** 宪法保障所有公民的基本权利和自由。

**单词释义:** 宪法

#### 例句2

> **He has a weak constitution and often gets sick.**

**中文翻译:** 他体质虚弱，经常生病。

**单词释义:** 体质

#### 例句3

> **The new law is contrary to the constitution of the country.**

**中文翻译:** 新法律与该国的宪法相违背。

**单词释义:** 宪法

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to create a club. They wrote down a set of
rules and principles, which formed the constitution of their club. This
constitution guided how they would operate and interact. It was like a
map for their fun journey together.

**一群朋友决定创建一个俱乐部。他们写下了一套规则和原则，这就形成了他们俱乐部的章程。这个章程指导着他们如何运作和互动。它就像他们一起快乐旅程的地图。**
