## cleanliness

**英音:** _[\'klenlɪnəs]_ **美音:** _[\'klɛnlɪnəs]_

**译义:** *n.洁癖,清洁*

### 短语

+ **personal cleanliness:** _个人卫生_

+ **environmental cleanliness:** _环境清洁_

+ **hygienic cleanliness:** _卫生清洁_

+ **food cleanliness:** _食品清洁_

+ **cleanliness standard:** _清洁标准_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Good personal cleanliness is essential for good health.**

**中文翻译:** 良好的个人清洁对于身体健康至关重要。

**单词释义:** 清洁；干净

#### 例句2

> **The cleanliness of the hotel room left much to be desired.**

**中文翻译:** 这个酒店房间的清洁程度不尽人意。

**单词释义:** 清洁；干净

#### 例句3

> **We should maintain the cleanliness of public places.**

**中文翻译:** 我们应该保持公共场所的清洁。

**单词释义:** 清洁；干净

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a princess was very sad because her castle was dirty. Then a
fairy appeared and taught her the importance of cleanliness. With hard
work, the castle became shiny and clean again. The princess learned that
cleanliness brings happiness.

**有一天，一位公主非常伤心，因为她的城堡很脏。然后一位仙女出现了，教导她清洁的重要性。通过努力，城堡再次变得闪亮干净。公主明白了清洁会带来快乐。**
