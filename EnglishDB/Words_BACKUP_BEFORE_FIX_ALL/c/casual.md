## casual

**英音:** _[\'kæʒjʊəl; -zj-]_ **美音:** _[\'kæʒʊəl]_

**译义:** *adj.偶然的,不经意的,临时的*

### 短语

+ **casual clothes:** _休闲服_

+ **casual worker:** _临时工_

+ **casual meeting:** _非正式会议_

+ **casual glance:** _不经意的一瞥_

+ **casual relationship:** _随便的关系_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He was dressed in casual clothes for the weekend.**

**中文翻译:** 周末他穿着休闲服。

**单词释义:** 休闲的；非正式的

#### 例句2

> **Their relationship seemed very casual.**

**中文翻译:** 他们的关系看起来很随意。

**单词释义:** 随便的；漫不经心的

#### 例句3

> **She has a casual attitude towards work.**

**中文翻译:** 她对待工作的态度很随意。

**单词释义:** 随意的；不在乎的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I went to a party. Everyone was dressed casually. There were
no formal suits or gowns. Just simple T-shirts and jeans. It was a very
relaxed and casual atmosphere.
