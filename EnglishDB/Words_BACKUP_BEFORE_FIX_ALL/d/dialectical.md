## dialectical

**英音:** _[daɪə\'lektɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,daɪə\'lɛktɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.方言的,辩证的*

### 短语

+ **dialectical materialism:** _辩证唯物主义_

+ **dialectical logic:** _辩证逻辑_

+ **dialectical relationship:** _辩证关系_

+ **dialectical method:** _辩证方法_

+ **dialectical process:** _辩证过程_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His thinking is highly dialectical, able to analyze problems from
multiple perspectives.**

**中文翻译:** 他的思维具有很强的辩证性，能够从多个角度分析问题。

**单词释义:** 辩证的

#### 例句2

> **The dialectical relationship between theory and practice is
crucial in academic research.**

**中文翻译:** 理论与实践的辩证关系对于学术研究至关重要。

**单词释义:** 辩证的

#### 例句3

> **We need to adopt a dialectical approach to solve this complex
issue.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要采取辩证的方法来解决这个复杂的问题。

**单词释义:** 辩证的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a philosopher was explaining the concept of \'dialectical\'
to his students. He used the example of a debate where two sides
presented opposing views, but through reasoning and discussion, a deeper
truth emerged. This showed the dynamic and interactive nature of
dialectical thinking.

**有一天，一位哲学家向他的学生解释"dialectical（辩证的）"这个概念。他以一场辩论为例，双方提出相反的观点，但通过推理和讨论，一个更深刻的真理出现了。这展示了辩证思维的动态和互动性质。**
