## dissuade

**英音:** _[dɪ\'sweɪd]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'swed]_

**译义:** *vt.劝阻*

### 短语

+ **dissuade from:** _劝阻（某人）不要（做某事）_

+ **dissuade someone of something:** _使某人打消某事的念头_

+ **try to dissuade:** _试图劝阻_

+ **be dissuaded:** _被劝阻_

+ **successfully dissuade:** _成功劝阻_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I tried to dissuade her from leaving.**

**中文翻译:** 我试图劝阻她离开。

**单词释义:** 劝阻

#### 例句2

> **He couldn\'t be dissuaded from his dangerous plan.**

**中文翻译:** 他无法被劝阻放弃他那危险的计划。

**单词释义:** 劝止

#### 例句3

> **Friends dissuaded him from investing all his money in that
business.**

**中文翻译:** 朋友们劝他不要把所有的钱都投资到那项生意中。

**单词释义:** 劝诫

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom wanted to quit school to start a business. But his
friend Jack dissuaded him, saying that education was crucial. Tom
listened and stayed in school. Eventually, he graduated and had a
successful career. So, to dissuade means to persuade someone not to do
something.

**一天，汤姆想辍学去创业。但他的朋友杰克劝他不要这样做，说教育至关重要。汤姆听了并留在了学校。最终，他毕业了，事业有成。所以，dissuade
意思是劝某人不要做某事。**
