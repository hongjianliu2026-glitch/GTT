## dissolution

**英音:** _[dɪsə\'luːʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[,dɪsə\'luʃən]_

**译义:** *n.分解,解散*

### 短语

+ **company dissolution:** _公司解散_

+ **dissolution of marriage:** _解除婚姻_

+ **dissolution of partnership:** _合伙关系的解除_

+ **chemical dissolution:** _化学溶解_

+ **rapid dissolution:** _快速溶解_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The dissolution of the company was a shock to all the
employees.**

**中文翻译:** 公司的解散让所有员工都感到震惊。

**单词释义:** 解散；解体

#### 例句2

> **The dissolution of the marriage was a painful process for both of
them.**

**中文翻译:** 婚姻的解体对他们双方来说都是一个痛苦的过程。

**单词释义:** 解体；破裂

#### 例句3

> **The dissolution of the parliament was announced by the
president.**

**中文翻译:** 总统宣布议会解散。

**单词释义:** 解散

### 背诵小故事

> A king\'s bad decisions led to the dissolution of his kingdom. People
were angry and revolted. Finally, the once great kingdom fell apart.
Remember, \'dissolution\' means the breaking up or ending of something.

**一位国王的糟糕决策导致了他王国的解体。民众愤怒并反抗。最终，这个曾经伟大的王国分崩离析。记住，'dissolution'意思是某物的分解或结束。**
