## dictatorial

**英音:** _[dɪktə\'tɔːrɪəl]_ **美音:**
_[\'dɪktə\'tɔrɪəl]_

**译义:** *adj.独裁的*

### 短语

+ **dictatorial regime:** _独裁政权_

+ **dictatorial rule:** _独裁统治_

+ **dictatorial leader:** _独裁领导人_

+ **dictatorial style:** _独裁作风_

+ **dictatorial power:** _独裁权力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The boss has a dictatorial management style, which makes the
employees very stressed.**

**中文翻译:** 老板独裁的管理风格，让员工压力很大。

**单词释义:** 独裁的；专横的

#### 例句2

> **The country was ruled by a dictatorial regime for many years.**

**中文翻译:** 该国多年来一直受到独裁政权的统治。

**单词释义:** 独裁的；专制的

#### 例句3

> **His dictatorial attitude towards his children caused them to
rebel.**

**中文翻译:** 他对孩子们的独裁态度导致了他们的反抗。

**单词释义:** 专横的；霸道的

### 背诵小故事

> A king was very dictatorial. He made all the decisions alone and
never listened to others. People were unhappy. But in the end, they
stood up and overthrew him. Remember, dictatorial means ruling in an
autocratic way.

**一位国王非常独裁。他独自做所有决定，从不听取他人意见。人们很不开心。但最后，他们站起来推翻了他。记住，dictatorial
意思是独裁专制的。**
