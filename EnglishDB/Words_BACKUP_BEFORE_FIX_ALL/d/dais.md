> In a grand hall, a beautiful princess stood on a special platform
called a dais. Everyone looked up to her as she gracefully addressed the
crowd. The dais made her seem even more noble and important.

**在一个宏伟的大厅里，一位美丽的公主站在一个被称为讲台的特殊平台上。当她优雅地向人群讲话时，每个人都抬头看着她。这个讲台使她看起来更加高贵和重要。**
