## distinguishing

**英音:** _[dɪ\'stɪŋgwɪʃɪŋ]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ\'stɪŋgwɪʃɪŋ]_

**译义:** *adj.有区别的*

### 短语

+ **distinguishing feature:** _显著特征_

+ **distinguishing mark:** _区别标记_

+ **distinguishing characteristic:** _显著特点_

+ **distinguishing quality:** _独特品质_

+ **distinguishing ability:** _辨别能力_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The distinguishing feature of this car is its fuel
efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 这辆车的显著特点是燃油效率高。

**单词释义:** 显著的；有区别的

#### 例句2

> **She has a distinguishing style in her paintings.**

**中文翻译:** 她的画作有一种独特的风格。

**单词释义:** 独特的；与众不同的

#### 例句3

> **The scientist is known for his distinguishing research in the
field of genetics.**

**中文翻译:** 这位科学家因其在遗传学领域的杰出研究而闻名。

**单词释义:** 杰出的；出色的

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was solving a case. He needed to find the distinguishing
feature of the criminal. It was a small scar on the left cheek. With
this, he caught the criminal. Remember, \'distinguishing\' means marking
as different.

**一位侦探在破案。他需要找到罪犯的显著特征。那是左脸颊上的一个小伤疤。凭借这个，他抓住了罪犯。记住，\'distinguishing\'意思是使有别于其他的。**
