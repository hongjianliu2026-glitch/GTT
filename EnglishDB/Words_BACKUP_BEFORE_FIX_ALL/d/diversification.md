## diversification

**英音:** _[daɪ,vɜːsɪfɪ\'keɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ,vɝsɪfɪ\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.变化,多样化*

### 短语

+ **economic diversification:** _经济多元化_

+ **industry diversification:** _产业多元化_

+ **portfolio diversification:** _投资组合多元化_

+ **risk diversification:** _风险分散_

+ **diversification strategy:** _多元化战略_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company\'s diversification strategy has helped it weather
economic downturns.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司的多元化战略帮助其度过了经济低迷时期。

**单词释义:** 多元化

#### 例句2

> **Diversification of investment is essential for reducing
risks.**

**中文翻译:** 投资多元化对于降低风险至关重要。

**单词释义:** 多样化

#### 例句3

> **The diversification of agricultural products has increased
farmers\' income.**

**中文翻译:** 农产品多样化增加了农民收入。

**单词释义:** 多样化

### 背诵小故事

> A small company was in trouble. But with diversification, it started
new projects like making toys and clothes. This made it successful.
Diversification means having many different types of things or
activities.

**一家小公司陷入困境。但通过多元化，它开展了制作玩具和服装等新项目。这使它获得成功。多元化意味着拥有许多不同类型的事物或活动。**
