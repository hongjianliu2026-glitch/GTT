## disruption

**英音:** _[dɪs\'rʌpʃn]_ **美音:** _[dɪs\'rʌpʃən]_

**译义:** *n.中断,分裂,瓦解,破坏*

### 短语

+ **market disruption:** _市场扰乱_

+ **disruptive innovation:** _颠覆性创新_

+ **supply disruption:** _供应中断_

+ **environmental disruption:** _环境破坏_

+ **technological disruption:** _技术颠覆_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The disruption of the internet connection caused chaos in the
office.**

**中文翻译:** 互联网连接的中断在办公室造成了混乱。

**单词释义:** 中断；扰乱

#### 例句2

> **The new technology brought about a major disruption in the
transportation industry.**

**中文翻译:** 这项新技术给交通运输业带来了重大的变革。

**单词释义:** 变革；破坏

#### 例句3

> **The company managed to overcome the disruption in the supply
chain.**

**中文翻译:** 公司设法克服了供应链的中断。

**单词释义:** 中断；干扰

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working on a new invention. But a sudden power
disruption made all the machines stop. It caused chaos in the lab. The
scientist had to start over. Disruption means a disturbance that
interrupts something.

**一位科学家正在研究一项新发明。但突然的电力中断使所有机器停止了运转。这在实验室里造成了混乱。科学家不得不重新开始。Disruption
意思是中断某事的干扰。**
