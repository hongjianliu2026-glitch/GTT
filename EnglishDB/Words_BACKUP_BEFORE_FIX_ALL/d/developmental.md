## developmental

**英音:** _[dɪ,veləp\'ment(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ,vɛləp\'mɛntl]_

**译义:** *adj.发展的*

### 短语

+ **developmental stage:** _发育阶段；发展阶段_

+ **developmental disorder:** _发育障碍；发展性失常_

+ **developmental psychology:** _发展心理学_

+ **developmental delay:** _发育迟缓；发展延迟_

+ **developmental process:** _发展过程；发育过程_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The developmental stage of a child is crucial for their future
growth.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子的发展阶段对他们未来的成长至关重要。

**单词释义:** 发展的；发育的

#### 例句2

> **This research focuses on the developmental processes of
plants.**

**中文翻译:** 这项研究专注于植物的发育过程。

**单词释义:** 发展的；发育的

#### 例句3

> **The company is investing heavily in developmental projects.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在大力投资发展项目。

**单词释义:** 发展的；开发的

### 背诵小故事

> A little scientist was working on a Developmental project. He faced
many challenges but never gave up. Finally, his efforts led to a great
discovery. The word Developmental means related to the process of
developing.
