## disappointment

**英音:** _[dɪsə\'pɒɪntm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[,dɪsə\'pɔɪntmənt]_

**译义:** *n.失望*

### 短语

+ **to one\'s disappointment:** _令某人失望的是_

+ **big disappointment:** _重大的失望_

+ **sense of disappointment:** _失望感_

+ **express disappointment:** _表达失望_

+ **disappointment at/with sth.:** _对某事感到失望_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The result was a great disappointment to him.**

**中文翻译:** 结果让他非常失望。

**单词释义:** 失望

#### 例句2

> **She tried to hide her disappointment when she didn\'t get the
promotion.**

**中文翻译:** 当她没有升职时，她试图掩饰自己的失望。

**单词释义:** 失望

#### 例句3

> **To my disappointment, the movie was not as good as I
expected.**

**中文翻译:** 令我失望的是，这部电影没有我想象的那么好。

**单词释义:** 失望

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom expected to win the game but failed. The feeling of not
achieving what he hoped for filled him with disappointment. It was like
a dark cloud over his heart.

**有一天，汤姆期望赢得比赛但失败了。未达成所望的这种感觉让他满心失望。就像一片乌云笼罩在他心头。**
