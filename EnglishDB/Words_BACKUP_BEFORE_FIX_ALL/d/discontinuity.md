## discontinuity

**英音:** _[dɪs,kɒntɪ\'njuːɪtɪ]_ **美音:**
_[,dɪs,kɑntə\'nuəti]_

**译义:** *n.断绝,不连续,中断*

### 短语

+ **structural discontinuity:** _结构不连续性_

+ **thermal discontinuity:** _热不连续性_

+ **discontinuity point:** _不连续点_

+ **discontinuity in supply:** _供应中断_

+ **discontinuity of service:** _服务中断_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The discontinuity in the supply chain caused significant delays
in production.**

**中文翻译:** 供应链中的不连续性导致了生产的严重延误。

**单词释义:** 不连续性；中断

#### 例句2

> **There was a discontinuity in his career when he took a
sabbatical.**

**中文翻译:** 当他休假时，他的职业生涯出现了中断。

**单词释义:** 中断；间断

#### 例句3

> **The discontinuity between the two theories makes it difficult to
form a unified framework.**

**中文翻译:** 这两种理论之间的不连续性使得很难形成一个统一的框架。

**单词释义:** 不连续；不一致

### 背诵小故事

> A brave explorer was climbing a mountain. Suddenly, he faced a
discontinuity in the path - a huge gap. He had to find another way. This
gap represented the meaning of discontinuity - a break or lack of
continuity.

**一位勇敢的探险家正在爬山。突然，他在道路上遇到了不连续性------一个巨大的缺口。他不得不另寻他路。这个缺口代表了discontinuity的意思------中断或缺乏连续性。**
