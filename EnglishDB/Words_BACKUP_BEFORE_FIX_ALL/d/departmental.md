## departmental

**英音:** _[diːpɑːt\'ment(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,dipɑrt\'mɛntl]_

**译义:** *adj.部门的*

### 短语

+ **departmental meeting:** _部门会议_

+ **departmental budget:** _部门预算_

+ **departmental policy:** _部门政策_

+ **departmental structure:** _部门结构_

+ **departmental staff:** _部门员工_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The departmental meeting was held in the conference room.**

**中文翻译:** 部门会议在会议室举行。

**单词释义:** 部门的

#### 例句2

> **There are some departmental regulations that need to be
followed.**

**中文翻译:** 有一些部门的规定需要遵守。

**单词释义:** 部门的

#### 例句3

> **The departmental budget has been approved.**

**中文翻译:** 部门预算已经获得批准。

**单词释义:** 部门的

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a big company. Each department has its own special tasks and
rules. The word \'departmental\' describes things related to these
specific departments. Like departmental meetings or departmental
policies.

**想象一家大公司。每个部门都有自己的特殊任务和规则。"departmental"这个词描述的就是与这些特定部门有关的事物。比如部门会议或部门政策。**
