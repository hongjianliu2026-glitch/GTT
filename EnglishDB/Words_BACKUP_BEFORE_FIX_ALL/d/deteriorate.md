## deteriorate

**英音:** _[dɪ\'tɪərɪəreɪt]_ **美音:** _[dɪ\'tɪrɪəret]_

**译义:** *v.(使)恶化*

### 短语

+ **deteriorate rapidly:** _迅速恶化_

+ **deteriorate gradually:** _逐渐恶化_

+ **deteriorate seriously:** _严重恶化_

+ **deteriorate condition:** _恶化状况_

+ **deteriorate health:** _健康恶化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The patient\'s condition deteriorated rapidly.**

**中文翻译:** 病人的病情迅速恶化。

**单词释义:** 恶化

#### 例句2

> **The relationship between the two countries has deteriorated in
recent years.**

**中文翻译:** 近年来两国关系恶化了。

**单词释义:** 恶化；变坏

#### 例句3

> **The quality of the product deteriorated after the change in
manufacturing process.**

**中文翻译:** 在制造工艺改变后，产品质量恶化了。

**单词释义:** 变差；退化

### 背诵小故事

> A beautiful flower was growing happily in the garden. But as time
passed and it was exposed to bad weather and no care, it began to
deteriorate. Its petals withered and its color faded. Poor flower!

**一朵美丽的花在花园里快乐地生长着。但随着时间的推移，它暴露在恶劣的天气中且无人照料，开始变差。它的花瓣枯萎，颜色褪去。可怜的花！**
