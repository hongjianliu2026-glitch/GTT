## dissatisfaction

**英音:** _[dɪssætɪs\'fækʃn]_ **美音:**
_[dɪs\'sætɪs\'fækʃən]_

**译义:** *n.不满,不平,令人不满的事物*

### 短语

+ **express dissatisfaction:** _表达不满_

+ **feel dissatisfaction:** _感到不满_

+ **cause dissatisfaction:** _引起不满_

+ **deep dissatisfaction:** _深深的不满_

+ **general dissatisfaction:** _普遍的不满_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **There is growing dissatisfaction among the employees about the
working conditions.**

**中文翻译:** 员工对工作条件的不满日益增加。

**单词释义:** 不满

#### 例句2

> **Her dissatisfaction with the result was obvious.**

**中文翻译:** 她对结果的不满很明显。

**单词释义:** 不满

#### 例句3

> **The customer expressed his dissatisfaction to the manager.**

**中文翻译:** 这位顾客向经理表达了他的不满。

**单词释义:** 不满

### 背诵小故事

> A customer always had dissatisfaction when visiting a certain
restaurant. The food was bad, the service was slow. This made the
customer decide never to come back. The feeling of not being satisfied
is \'dissatisfaction\'.

**一位顾客每次去某家餐厅都感到不满。食物难吃，服务又慢。这使得顾客决定再也不来了。这种不满足的感觉就是\'dissatisfaction\'（不满；不满意）。**
