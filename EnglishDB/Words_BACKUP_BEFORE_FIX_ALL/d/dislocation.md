## dislocation

**英音:** _[,dɪslə(ʊ)\'keɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,dɪslo\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.混乱,断层,脱臼*

### 短语

+ **crystal dislocation:** _晶体位错_

+ **dislocation density:** _位错密度_

+ **edge dislocation:** _刃型位错_

+ **screw dislocation:** _螺型位错_

+ **dislocation pile-up:** _位错塞积_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The earthquake caused a dislocation of the railway tracks.**

**中文翻译:** 地震导致了铁路轨道的错位。

**单词释义:** 错位；脱位

#### 例句2

> **There was a dislocation in the supply chain due to the factory
fire.**

**中文翻译:** 由于工厂火灾，供应链出现了混乱。

**单词释义:** 混乱；失调

#### 例句3

> **The rapid technological change led to a dislocation of many
workers\' jobs.**

**中文翻译:** 快速的技术变革导致许多工人的工作岗位变动。

**单词释义:** 变动；脱臼

### 背诵小故事

> A worker was in a hurry and ran too fast, causing a dislocation of
his ankle. His colleagues quickly sent him to the hospital. Remember,
\'dislocation\' means the displacement of a part of the body from its
normal position.

**一名工人很着急，跑得太快，导致脚踝脱位。他的同事迅速把他送到了医院。记住，\'dislocation\'意思是身体某部位偏离正常位置。**
