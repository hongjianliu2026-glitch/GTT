## deterioration

**英音:** _[dɪ,tɪərɪə\'reɪʃn]_ **美音:**
_[dɪ,tɪrɪə\'reʃən]_

**译义:** *n.变坏,退化,堕落*

### 短语

+ **environmental deterioration:** _环境恶化_

+ **deterioration of health:** _健康状况恶化_

+ **quality deterioration:** _质量下降_

+ **economic deterioration:** _经济衰退_

+ **condition deterioration:** _状况恶化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The deterioration of the environment is a serious problem.**

**中文翻译:** 环境的恶化是一个严重的问题。

**单词释义:** 恶化；变坏；退化

#### 例句2

> **His health showed a marked deterioration after the accident.**

**中文翻译:** 事故发生后，他的健康状况明显恶化。

**单词释义:** 恶化；变坏；退化

#### 例句3

> **The deterioration of the building\'s structure made it unsafe to
inhabit.**

**中文翻译:** 这座建筑结构的恶化使其不宜居住。

**单词释义:** 恶化；变坏；退化

### 背诵小故事

> A beautiful garden was once full of colorful flowers. But due to lack
of care and bad weather, it faced deterioration. The flowers withered,
and the grass turned yellow. This is what deterioration means - a
process of becoming worse.

**一个美丽的花园曾经满是五颜六色的花。但由于缺乏照料和恶劣的天气，它面临恶化。花儿枯萎了，草儿变黄了。这就是'deterioration'的意思------变得更糟的过程。**
