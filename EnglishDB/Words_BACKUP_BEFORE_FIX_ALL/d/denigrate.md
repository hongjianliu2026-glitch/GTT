## denigrate

**英音:** _[\'denɪgreɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'dɛnɪɡret]_

**译义:** *v.毁誉*

### 短语

+ **denigrate someone\'s character:** _诋毁某人的性格_

+ **denigrate the value:** _贬低价值_

+ **denigrate one\'s efforts:** _诋毁某人的努力_

+ **denigrate the importance:** _贬低重要性_

+ **denigrate the achievement:** _诋毁成就_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He constantly denigrated her achievements in front of others.**

**中文翻译:** 他经常在别人面前贬低她的成就。

**单词释义:** 贬低；诋毁

#### 例句2

> **The media\'s tendency to denigrate public figures is
unethical.**

**中文翻译:** 媒体贬低公众人物的倾向是不道德的。

**单词释义:** 诋毁；中伤

#### 例句3

> **Don\'t denigrate the efforts of those who have tried their
best.**

**中文翻译:** 不要贬低那些已经尽力的人的努力。

**单词释义:** 贬低；轻视

### 背诵小故事

> A painter was angry when someone denigrated his masterpiece. He
shouted, \'You can\'t just badmouth my work like this!\' Remember, when
someone denigrates, they speak ill or belittle.

**一位画家很生气有人诋毁他的杰作。他大喊："你不能这样贬低我的作品！"记住，当有人denigrate，就是说坏话或贬低。**
