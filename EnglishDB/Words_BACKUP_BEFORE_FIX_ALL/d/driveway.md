## driveway

**英音:** _[\'draɪvweɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'draɪvwe]_

**译义:** *n.车道*

### 短语

+ **long driveway:** _长的车道_

+ **private driveway:** _私人车道_

+ **narrow driveway:** _狭窄的车道_

+ **curved driveway:** _弯曲的车道_

+ **gravel driveway:** _砾石车道_

find my way home. It was a long path leading straight to my house. The
