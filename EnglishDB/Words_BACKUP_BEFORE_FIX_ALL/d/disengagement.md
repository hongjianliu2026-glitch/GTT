## disengagement

**英音:** _[dɪsɪn\'geɪdʒm(ə)nt; dɪsen-]_ **美音:**
_[,dɪsɪn\'gedʒmənt]_

**译义:** *n.脱离*

### 短语

+ **military disengagement:** _军事脱离接触_

+ **disengagement policy:** _脱离政策_

+ **disengagement process:** _脱离过程_

+ **emotional disengagement:** _情感上的脱离_

+ **strategic disengagement:** _战略脱离_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The disengagement of troops from the conflict zone was a complex
operation.**

**中文翻译:** 从冲突地区撤军是一项复杂的行动。

**单词释义:** 脱离；撤离

#### 例句2

> **His disengagement from the project came as a surprise to
everyone.**

**中文翻译:** 他退出该项目令每个人都感到惊讶。

**单词释义:** 退出；脱离

#### 例句3

> **The disengagement of the gears caused the machine to stop
working.**

**中文翻译:** 齿轮脱开导致机器停止工作。

**单词释义:** 脱开；分离

### 背诵小故事

> A soldier was in a war, feeling tired and stressed. But when he got
the order of disengagement, it was like a huge relief. He could finally
leave the battlefield and return to peace.

**一名士兵身处战争中，感到疲惫和压力巨大。但当他接到撤离的命令（disengagement）时，仿佛如释重负。他终于可以离开战场，回归和平。**
