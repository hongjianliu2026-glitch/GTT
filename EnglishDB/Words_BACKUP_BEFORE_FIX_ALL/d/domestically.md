## domestically

**英音:** _[də\'mestɪkli]_ **美音:** _[də\'mestɪkli]_

**译义:** *adv.家庭式地,合乎国内地*

### 短语

+ **domestically produced:** _国内生产的_

+ **domestically oriented:** _面向国内的_

+ **domestically available:** _国内可获得的_

+ **domestically consumed:** _国内消费的_

+ **domestically developed:** _国内开发的_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company is performing well domestically but faces challenges
internationally.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司在国内表现良好，但在国际上面临挑战。

**单词释义:** 在国内

#### 例句2

> **Domestically produced goods are often preferred by consumers.**

**中文翻译:** 国内生产的商品往往受到消费者的青睐。

**单词释义:** 国内地

#### 例句3

> **The policy has had a significant impact domestically.**

**中文翻译:** 该政策在国内产生了重大影响。

**单词释义:** 在国内

### 背诵小故事

> A young entrepreneur wanted to succeed domestically. He started a
local business, providing high-quality products. With hard work and
smart strategies, he became famous domestically for his excellent
services.

**一位年轻的企业家想要在国内取得成功。他创办了一家本地企业，提供高质量的产品。通过努力工作和明智的策略，他因出色的服务在国内声名远扬。**
