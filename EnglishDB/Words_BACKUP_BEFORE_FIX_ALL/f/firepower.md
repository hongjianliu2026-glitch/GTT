## firepower

**英音:** _[\'faɪəpəʊə]_ **美音:** _[\'faɪɚpaʊɚ]_

**译义:** *n.火力*

### 短语

+ **military firepower:** _军事火力_

+ **enhance firepower:** _增强火力_

+ **heavy firepower:** _重火力_

+ **concentrated firepower:** _集中火力_

+ **firepower superiority:** _火力优势_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The enemy\'s firepower was too intense for us to advance.**

**中文翻译:** 敌人的火力太猛，我们无法前进。

**单词释义:** 火力

#### 例句2

> **The military operation requires a strong firepower support.**

**中文翻译:** 这次军事行动需要强大的火力支援。

**单词释义:** 火力

#### 例句3

> **They are assessing the firepower of the opposing team.**

**中文翻译:** 他们正在评估对方团队的火力。

**单词释义:** 火力

### 背诵小故事

> In a fierce battle, the army was in a difficult situation. But when
their reinforcement arrived with strong firepower, including advanced
cannons and machine guns, they quickly turned the tide and won the
battle.

**在一场激烈的战斗中，军队陷入困境。但当他们的增援带着强大的火力到来，包括先进的大炮和机枪，他们迅速扭转了局势并赢得了战斗。**
