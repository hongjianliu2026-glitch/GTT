## formation

**英音:** _[fɔː\'meɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[fɔr\'meʃən]_

**译义:** *n.形成,构成,编队*

### 短语

+ **rock formation:** _岩层_

+ **formation flying:** _编队飞行_

+ **cloud formation:** _云的形成_

+ **formation dance:** _队列舞_

+ **formation of character:** _性格的形成_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The formation of the new government is expected to bring positive
changes.**

**中文翻译:** 新政府的组建有望带来积极变化。

**单词释义:** 组建；形成

#### 例句2

> **The rock formation is millions of years old.**

**中文翻译:** 岩层已有数百万年的历史。

**单词释义:** 岩层；地层

#### 例句3

> **The team practiced hard to perfect their formation for the
competition.**

**中文翻译:** 队员们刻苦训练，完善比赛阵型。

**单词释义:** 队形；排列

### 背诵小故事

> A group of little birds was flying in the sky. They formed a
beautiful formation, like a heart. This special arrangement was called a
formation. It means the way things are arranged or organized.

**一群小鸟在天空中飞翔。它们组成了一个美丽的队形，像一颗心。这种特殊的排列被称为formation。它的意思是事物的排列或组织方式。**
