## falsify

**英音:** _[\'fɔːlsɪfaɪ; \'fɒls-]_ **美音:**
_[\'fɔlsɪfaɪ]_

**译义:** *v.伪造*

### 短语

+ **falsify records:** _伪造记录_

+ **falsify evidence:** _篡改证据_

+ **falsify accounts:** _做假账_

+ **falsify history:** _歪曲历史_

+ **falsify reports:** _谎报_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He was accused of falsifying the company\'s financial
records.**

**中文翻译:** 他被指控伪造公司财务记录。

**单词释义:** 伪造

#### 例句2

> **The scientist was found to have falsified the research data.**

**中文翻译:** 该科学家被发现伪造了研究数据。

**单词释义:** 篡改

#### 例句3

> **She tried to falsify her age on the application form.**

**中文翻译:** 她试图在申请表上伪造自己的年龄。

**单词释义:** 谎报

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was accused of falsify the research data. He tried to
make the results look better than they really were, but was eventually
found out. This shows that falsify means to change something to be
false.

**一位科学家被指控篡改研究数据。他试图让结果看起来比实际更好，但最终被发现。这表明falsify意思是伪造、篡改。**
