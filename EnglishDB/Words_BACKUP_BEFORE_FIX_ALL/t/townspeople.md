## townspeople

**英音:** _[\'taʊnzpiːp(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[\'taʊnz\'pipl]_

**译义:** *n.市民,镇民*

### 短语

+ **the townspeople of a place:** _一个地方的镇民_

+ **ordinary townspeople:** _普通镇民_

+ **local townspeople:** _当地镇民_

+ **friendly townspeople:** _友好的镇民_

+ **wealthy townspeople:** _富裕的镇民_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The townspeople gathered in the square to celebrate the
festival.**

**中文翻译:** 镇民们聚集在广场上庆祝节日。

**单词释义:** 镇民

#### 例句2

> **The new policy will benefit the townspeople greatly.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策将极大地惠及镇民。

**单词释义:** 镇民

#### 例句3

> **The townspeople are proud of their local traditions.**

**中文翻译:** 镇民们为他们当地的传统感到骄傲。

**单词释义:** 镇民

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, there was a big party. All the townspeople came.
They included the baker, the teacher, and the shopkeeper. They had fun
together and showed the unity of the community.

**在一个小镇上，有一场盛大的聚会。所有镇民都来了。他们包括面包师、教师和店主。他们一起玩得很开心，展现了社区的团结。**
