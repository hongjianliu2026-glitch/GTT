## twenty-one

**英音:** _[ˈtwenti wʌn]_ **美音:** _[ˈtwenti wʌn]_

**译义:** *num.二十一；二十一点（纸牌游戏）*

### 短语

+ **twenty-one years old:** _二十一岁_

+ **twenty-one days:** _二十一天_

+ **twenty-one people:** _二十一人_

+ **twenty-one things:** _二十一件东西_

+ **twenty-one dollars:** _二十一美元_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little monkey wanted to buy 21 bananas. He counted them
carefully, twenty and one more, that\'s twenty-one. He was so happy with
his big bunch of bananas.

**有一天，一只小猴子想买 21
