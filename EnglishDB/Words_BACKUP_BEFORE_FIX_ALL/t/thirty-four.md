## thirty-four

**英音:** _[ˈθɜːti fɔː]_ **美音:** _[ˈθɜrti fɔr]_

**译义:** *num.三十四*

### 短语

+ **thirty-four people:** _三十四个人_

+ **thirty-four books:** _三十四本书_

+ **thirty-four apples:** _三十四个苹果_

+ **thirty-four days:** _三十四天_

+ **thirty-four kilometers:** _三十四公里_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little monkey was counting apples. He counted thirty-four
apples in total. He was so happy because it was enough for all his
friends.

**一天，一只小猴子在数苹果。他总共数了三十四个苹果。他非常高兴，因为这足够他所有的朋友吃了。**
