## trailhead

**英音:** _[\'treɪlhed]_ **美音:** _[\'treɪlˌhed]_

**译义:** *n.小道的起点*

### 短语

+ **reach the trailhead:** _到达小径起点_

+ **start from the trailhead:** _从小径起点出发_

+ **locate the trailhead:** _找到小径起点_

+ **mark the trailhead:** _标记小径起点_

+ **approach the trailhead:** _接近小径起点_
