## narcissistic

**英音:** _[,nɑːsɪ\'sɪstɪk]_ **美音:**
_[,nɑrsɪ\'sɪstɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.自恋的,自我陶醉的*

### 短语

+ **narcissistic personality:** _自恋型人格_

+ **narcissistic disorder:** _自恋障碍_

+ **narcissistic trait:** _自恋特质_

+ **narcissistic behavior:** _自恋行为_

+ **narcissistic tendency:** _自恋倾向_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He has a narcissistic personality and always talks about
himself.**

**中文翻译:** 他有自恋的性格，总是谈论自己。

**单词释义:** 形容某人具有自恋的特性

#### 例句2

> **Her narcissistic behavior made her unpopular among her
colleagues.**

**中文翻译:** 她自恋的行为使她在同事中不受欢迎。

**单词释义:** 描述行为是自恋的

#### 例句3

> **The narcissistic actor was only concerned with his
appearance.**

**中文翻译:** 这位自恋的演员只关心自己的外表。

**单词释义:** 用于修饰演员，表明其自恋

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a guy named Narcissus who was extremely
narcissistic. He spent all day looking at his reflection in the water,
thinking he was the most beautiful. This behavior made the word
\'narcissistic\' represent excessive self-love.

**从前，有个叫纳西索斯的人极度自恋。他整天看着水中自己的倒影，认为自己是最美的。这种行为使得'narcissistic'这个词代表过度自爱。**
