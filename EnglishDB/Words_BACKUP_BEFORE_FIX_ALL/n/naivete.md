## naivete

**英音:** _[nɑː\'iːvteɪ]_ **美音:** _[nɑ\'iːvteɪ]_

**译义:** *n.天真烂漫,纯真无邪*

### 短语

+ **childish naivete:** _幼稚的天真_

+ **innocent naivete:** _无辜的天真_

+ **blind naivete:** _盲目的天真_

+ **unworldly naivete:** _不谙世事的天真_

+ **artless naivete:** _朴实无华的天真_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His naivete led him to believe everything the salesman said.**

**中文翻译:** 他的天真让他相信了推销员所说的一切。

**单词释义:** 天真，幼稚

#### 例句2

> **Despite her naivete, she managed to handle the difficult
situation with grace.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管她很天真，但她还是优雅地处理了困难的情况。

**单词释义:** 天真，单纯

#### 例句3

> **The naivete of the young boy was charming but also worrying at
times.**

**中文翻译:** 小男孩的天真令人着迷，但有时也令人担忧。

**单词释义:** 天真，质朴

### 背诵小故事

> A little girl believed that unicorns could be her friends and would
bring her candies. Her naivete was charming. But as she grew up, she
learned the reality. Naivete means the quality of being naive or showing
a lack of experience and wisdom.
