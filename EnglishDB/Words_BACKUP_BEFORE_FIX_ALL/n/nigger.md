## nigger

**英音:** _[\'nɪgə]_ **美音:** _[\'nɪɡɚ]_

**译义:** *n.\<美>被歧视者,黑鬼(对黑人的蔑称)*

### 短语

+ **Nigger in the woodpile:** _A hidden or unspoken factor that
could explain or complicate a situation._

+ **Nigger lover:** _A derogatory term used to describe someone
who is perceived as being overly sympathetic or supportive of African
Americans._

+ **Nigger-rigged:** _A term used to describe a makeshift or
improvised solution to a problem, often involving the use of available
materials in an unorthodox way._

+ **Niggerhead:** _A term that has historically been used as a
racial slur, often referring to a rock or a geographical feature, but
also used as a derogatory term for African Americans._

+ **Niggerati:** _A term used to describe a group of African
American intellectuals or artists, often associated with the Harlem
Renaissance._
