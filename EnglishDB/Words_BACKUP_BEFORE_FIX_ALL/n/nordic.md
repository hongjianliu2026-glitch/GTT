## Nordic

**英音:** _[ˈnɔːdɪk]_ **美音:** _[ˈnɔːrdɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.北欧的;北欧人的;北欧语的n.北欧人;北欧语*

### 短语

+ **Nordic countries:** _北欧国家_

+ **Nordic skiing:** _北欧式滑雪_

+ **Nordic style:** _北欧风格_

+ **Nordic mythology:** _北欧神话_

+ **Nordic Council:** _北欧理事会_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The Nordic countries are known for their high standard of
living.**

**中文翻译:** 北欧国家以其高生活水平而闻名。

**单词释义:** 北欧的

**单词释义:** 北欧人的

**单词释义:** 北欧（式）的

people with unique customs. They called themselves Nordic. Jack was
impressed by their strength and warmth in the harsh environment.
