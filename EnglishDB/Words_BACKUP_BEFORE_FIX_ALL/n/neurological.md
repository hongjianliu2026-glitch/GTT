## neurological

**英音:** _[,njʊərə\'lɒdʒɪkl]_ **美音:**
_[,nʊrə\'lɑdʒɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.神经学上的*

### 短语

+ **neurological disorder:** _神经障碍；神经系统疾病_

+ **neurological examination:** _神经学检查_

+ **neurological symptoms:** _神经症状_

+ **neurological research:** _神经学研究_

+ **neurological damage:** _神经损伤_

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was studying the neurological system. He found that
nerves send signals like messengers. Neurological problems can make
these messengers confused. So, we need to take care of our brain and
nerves.

**一位科学家正在研究神经系统。他发现神经像信使一样发送信号。神经系统的问题会使这些信使混乱。所以，我们需要照顾好我们的大脑和神经。**
