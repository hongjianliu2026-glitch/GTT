### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom and Jerry were playing in the garden. Tom said he liked
the flowers. Jerry likewise expressed his fondness for them. It means in
the same way. So, \'likewise\' means in a similar way or manner.

**一天，汤姆和杰瑞在花园里玩耍。汤姆说他喜欢那些花。杰瑞同样表示喜欢。这意味着以相同的方式。所以，\'likewise\'意思是以相似的方式或态度。**
