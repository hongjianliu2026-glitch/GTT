## livestock

**英音:** _[\'laɪvstɒk]_ **美音:** _[\'laɪvstɑk]_

**译义:** *n.家畜,牲畜*

### 短语

+ **livestock farming:** _畜牧业_

+ **livestock market:** _牲畜市场_

+ **livestock feed:** _牲畜饲料_

+ **livestock disease:** _牲畜疾病_

+ **livestock breeding:** _牲畜繁殖_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The farmer takes good care of his livestock.**

**中文翻译:** 这位农民悉心照料他的家畜。

**单词释义:** 家畜

#### 例句2

> **Livestock breeding is an important part of agriculture.**

**中文翻译:** 家畜养殖是农业的一个重要部分。

**单词释义:** 家畜

#### 例句3

> **The disease affected a large number of livestock.**

**中文翻译:** 这场疾病影响了大量的家畜。

**单词释义:** 家畜

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a farmer was counting his livestock. He had cows, sheep and
pigs. These animals are all called livestock. They are important for his
farm and life. Remember, livestock means farm animals.

**有一天，一位农民在数他的家畜。他有牛、羊和猪。这些动物都被称为家畜。它们对他的农场和生活很重要。记住，livestock
意思是家畜。**
