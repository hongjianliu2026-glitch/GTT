## reconciliation

**英音:** _[,rek(ə)nsɪlɪ\'eɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[,rɛkənsɪlɪ\'eʃən]_

**译义:** *n.和解,调和,顺从*

### 短语

+ **reconciliation process:** _和解过程_

+ **reconciliation attempt:** _和解尝试_

+ **reconciliation meeting:** _和解会议_

+ **reconciliation agreement:** _和解协议_

+ **family reconciliation:** _家庭和解_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The reconciliation between the two feuding families was a joyous
occasion.**

**中文翻译:** 两个不和的家庭之间的和解是一件令人高兴的事情。

**单词释义:** 和解；调和

#### 例句2

> **Their marriage went through a period of difficulty but eventually
found reconciliation.**

**中文翻译:** 他们的婚姻经历了一段困难时期，但最终找到了和解。

**单词释义:** 和解；和好

#### 例句3

> **The process of reconciliation after the war was long and
arduous.**

**中文翻译:** 战后和解的过程是漫长而艰难的。

**单词释义:** 和解；协调

### 背诵小故事

> A couple had a big fight and were on the verge of divorce. But with
sincere efforts and understanding, they achieved reconciliation. They
remembered the good times and decided to give love another chance.

**一对夫妻大吵了一架，差点离婚。但通过真诚的努力和相互理解，他们达成了和解。他们回忆起美好的时光，决定再给爱一次机会。**
