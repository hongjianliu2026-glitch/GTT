## restructure

**英音:** _[riː\'strʌktʃə]_ **美音:** _[ri\'strʌktʃɚ]_

**译义:** *vt.更改结构,重建构造,调整,改组*

### 短语

+ **restructure the company:** _重组公司_

+ **restructure the economy:** _调整经济结构_

+ **restructure the system:** _重构系统_

+ **restructure the organization:** _改组组织_

+ **restructure the plan:** _重新规划_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company decided to restructure its management team to improve
efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 公司决定重组其管理团队以提高效率。

**单词释义:** 重组

#### 例句2

> **The government is planning to restructure the education
system.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正计划对教育系统进行改组。

**单词释义:** 改组

#### 例句3

> **We need to restructure our business model to adapt to the new
market conditions.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要重新构建我们的商业模式以适应新的市场条件。

**单词释义:** 重新构建

### 背诵小故事

> A company was in trouble. But the smart boss decided to restructure
it. They changed the departments and the way of working. Finally, the
company became successful again.

**一家公司陷入困境。但聪明的老板决定对其进行重组。他们改变了部门和工作方式。最终，公司再次取得成功。**
