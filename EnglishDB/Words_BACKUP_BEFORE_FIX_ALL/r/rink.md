## rink

**英音:** _[rɪŋk]_ **美音:** _[rɪŋk]_

**译义:** *n.溜冰场,冰球场,室内溜冰场vi.溜冰*

### 短语

+ **ice rink:** _溜冰场；冰场_

+ **skating rink:** _溜冰场；滑冰场_

+ **roller rink:** _旱冰场_

+ **rink side:** _溜冰场边_

+ **rink attendant:** _溜冰场管理员_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The kids were having fun skating at the rink.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子们在溜冰场玩得很开心。

**单词释义:** 溜冰场

#### 例句2

> **The hockey team practiced at the rink every day.**

**中文翻译:** 曲棍球队每天都在溜冰场训练。

**单词释义:** 溜冰场

#### 例句3

> **We went to the rink to watch the figure skating competition.**

**中文翻译:** 我们去溜冰场观看花样滑冰比赛。

**单词释义:** 溜冰场

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to a big rink. She
put on her skates and had a wonderful time skating around the rink. The
rink was full of joy and laughter.
