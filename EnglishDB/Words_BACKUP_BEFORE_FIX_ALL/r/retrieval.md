## retrieval

**英音:** _[rɪ\'triːvl]_ **美音:** _[rɪ\'trivl]_

**译义:** *n.取回,恢复,修补,重获,挽救,拯救*

### 短语

+ **information retrieval:** _信息检索_

+ **data retrieval:** _数据检索_

+ **retrieval system:** _检索系统_

+ **retrieval process:** _检索过程_

+ **retrieval efficiency:** _检索效率_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The retrieval of lost data was a complex process.**

**中文翻译:** 丢失数据的检索是一个复杂的过程。

**单词释义:** 检索；取回

#### 例句2

> **She was responsible for the retrieval of important documents from
the archives.**

**中文翻译:** 她负责从档案中检索重要文件。

**单词释义:** 检索；获取

#### 例句3

> **The retrieval system needs to be updated for more efficient
search results.**

**中文翻译:** 检索系统需要更新以获得更高效的搜索结果。

**单词释义:** 检索

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was on a case. He needed retrieval of important evidence.
He searched everywhere and finally found it, solving the mystery.
Retrieval means the act of getting something back.

**一位侦探正在办案。他需要找回重要证据。他到处搜寻，最终找到了，解开了谜团。retrieval
意思是取回，找回的行为。**
