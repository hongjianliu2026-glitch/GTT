## regress

**英音:** _[rɪ\'gres]_ **美音:** _[rɪ\'ɡrɛs]_

**译义:** *v.复原,逆行,使倒退n.退回,回归,归复权*

### 短语

+ **linear regress:** _线性回归_

+ **regress analysis:** _回归分析_

+ **regress to the mean:** _回归均值_

+ **multiple regress:** _多元回归_

+ **logistic regress:** _逻辑回归_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **After the surgery, his condition began to regress.**

**中文翻译:** 手术后，他的病情开始恶化。

**单词释义:** 恶化；倒退

#### 例句2

> **If you don\'t keep practicing, your skills will regress.**

**中文翻译:** 如果你不持续练习，你的技能将会退步。

**单词释义:** 退步；衰退

#### 例句3

> **The economy seems to be regressing instead of progressing.**

**中文翻译:** 经济似乎在倒退而不是进步。

**单词释义:** 倒退；衰退

### 背诵小故事

his way back but seemed to regress deeper and deeper. Finally, with the
help of a map, he managed to return to the starting point.
