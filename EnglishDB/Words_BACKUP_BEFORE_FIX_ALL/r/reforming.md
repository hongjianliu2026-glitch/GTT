## reforming

**英音:** _[rɪˈfɔːmɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[rɪˈfɔːrmɪŋ]_

**译义:** *v.重整*

### 短语

+ **reforming process:** _改革过程_

+ **economic reforming:** _经济改革_

+ **reforming measures:** _改革措施_

+ **political reforming:** _政治改革_

+ **reforming initiative:** _改革倡议_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government is committed to reforming the education
system.**

**中文翻译:** 政府致力于改革教育体系。

**单词释义:** 改革

#### 例句2

> **She spent years reforming her bad habits.**

**中文翻译:** 她花了很多年的时间来改变自己的坏习惯。

**单词释义:** 改正

#### 例句3

> **The company is in the process of reforming its management
structure.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在进行管理架构改革。

**单词释义:** 改革

### 背诵小故事

> A factory was in trouble. But the boss decided to start reforming.
They changed the old machines and methods. Finally, the factory became
successful. Reforming means to make changes to improve something.

**一家工厂陷入困境。但老板决定开始改革。他们更换了旧机器和方法。最终，工厂取得了成功。Reforming
意思是进行变革以改进某物。**
