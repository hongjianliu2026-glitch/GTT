## reconstructive

**英音:** _[ˌriːkənˈstrʌktɪv]_ **美音:**
_[ˌriːkənˈstrʌktɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.重建的,改造的*

### 短语

+ **reconstructive surgery:** _重建手术；整形手术_

+ **reconstructive therapy:** _重建疗法_

+ **reconstructive project:** _重建项目_

+ **reconstructive process:** _重建过程_

+ **reconstructive approach:** _重建方法；重构途径_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctor performed a reconstructive surgery on the patient\'s
damaged face.**

**中文翻译:** 医生对病人受损的脸部进行了重建手术。

**单词释义:** 重建的，整形的

#### 例句2

> **Reconstructive measures were taken to restore the historical
building to its original state.**

**中文翻译:** 采取了重建措施，将历史建筑恢复到原来的状态。

**单词释义:** 重建的，修复的

#### 例句3

> **The company is investing in reconstructive projects to improve
the local infrastructure.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司正在投资重建项目，以改善当地基础设施。

**单词释义:** 重建的，改造的

### 背诵小故事

> A city was destroyed by a disaster. But with the efforts of many
people and advanced technology, it began a reconstructive process.
Buildings were rebuilt, and hope was restored. \'Reconstructive\' means
related to the action of building or restoring something again.

**一个城市被灾难摧毁。但在许多人的努力和先进技术下，它开始了重建的过程。建筑物被重新建造，希望得以恢复。'reconstructive'意思是与再次建造或恢复某物的行动有关。**
