## respectful

**英音:** _[rɪ\'spek(t)fʊl; -f(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[rɪ\'spɛktfəl]_

**译义:** *adj.恭敬的,尊敬的,尊重人的,有礼貌的*

### 短语

+ **be respectful to:** _对\...\...尊敬_

+ **respectful attitude:** _尊敬的态度_

+ **respectful greeting:** _尊敬的问候_

+ **respectful behavior:** _尊敬的行为_

+ **remain respectful:** _保持尊敬_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The children were respectful to their elders.**

**中文翻译:** 孩子们对长辈很尊敬。

**单词释义:** 尊敬的

#### 例句2

> **She is always respectful in her interactions with others.**

**中文翻译:** 她在与他人交往时总是很有礼貌。

**单词释义:** 有礼貌的

#### 例句3

> **The employees showed a respectful attitude towards the boss\'s
decision.**

**中文翻译:** 员工们对老板的决定表现出尊重的态度。

**单词释义:** 尊重的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little boy was very respectful to his elders. He always
listened carefully when they spoke and helped them with tasks. His
behavior made everyone like him. Remember, being respectful means
showing politeness and admiration.

**有一天，一个小男孩对长辈非常尊敬。他在长辈讲话时总是认真倾听，并帮助他们做事。他的行为让每个人都喜欢他。记住，respectful
意思是有礼貌和表示钦佩。**
