## roadblock

**英音:** _[\'rəʊdblɒk]_ **美音:** _[\'rodblɑk]_

**译义:** *n.障碍,障碍物*

### 短语

+ **remove a roadblock:** _移除路障_

+ **encounter a roadblock:** _遭遇路障_

+ **put up a roadblock:** _设置路障_

+ **clear the roadblock:** _清除路障_

+ **hit a roadblock:** _撞到路障_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The construction work on the road created a major roadblock for
traffic.**

**中文翻译:** 道路上的施工工作给交通造成了主要障碍。

**单词释义:** 障碍；路障

#### 例句2

> **The lack of funds has become a roadblock to the project\'s
progress.**

**中文翻译:** 资金匮乏成为项目进展的障碍。

**单词释义:** 阻碍；障碍

#### 例句3

> **They encountered a roadblock on their way to success.**

**中文翻译:** 他们在成功的路上遇到了障碍。

**单词释义:** 阻碍；障碍

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was driving on a road. Suddenly, a big tree fell and
became a roadblock. I had to stop and find another way. It made me
understand the meaning of \'roadblock\' clearly - something that blocks
the road.

**有一天，我在路上开车。突然，一棵大树倒了，变成了路障。我不得不停下来另寻他路。这让我清楚地明白了'roadblock'的意思------挡路的东西。**
