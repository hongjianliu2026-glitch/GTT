## reorganization

**英音:** _[\'riː,ɔːɡənaɪ\'zeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[\'ri,ɔɡənɪ\'zeʃən]_

**译义:** *n.改组,改编,整编,重新组织*

### 短语

+ **business reorganization:** _企业重组_

+ **structural reorganization:** _结构重组_

+ **corporate reorganization:** _公司重组_

+ **major reorganization:** _重大重组_

+ **system reorganization:** _系统重组_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company is undergoing a major reorganization to improve
efficiency.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司正在进行一次重大的重组以提高效率。

**单词释义:** 重组

#### 例句2

> **The reorganization of the department led to better communication
among the staff.**

**中文翻译:** 部门的重组使得员工之间的交流更好了。

**单词释义:** 重组

#### 例句3

> **The school\'s reorganization of the curriculum has received
positive feedback from parents.**

**中文翻译:** 学校对课程的重新组织收到了家长们的积极反馈。

**单词释义:** 重新组织

### 背诵小故事

> A company was in chaos. But with the magic of reorganization, teams
were reshuffled, processes were improved. It was like giving the company
a new life. Now it\'s thriving and successful!

**一家公司陷入混乱。但通过重组的魔力，团队重新洗牌，流程得到改进。这就像是给公司赋予了新生命。现在它蓬勃发展，取得成功！**
