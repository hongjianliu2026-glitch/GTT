## repatriation

**英音:** _[ˌriːpætrɪˈeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[riˌpeɪtriˈeɪʃən]_

**译义:** *n.遣送回国,归国*

### 短语

+ **voluntary repatriation:** _自愿遣返_

+ **forced repatriation:** _强制遣返_

+ **repatriation process:** _遣返过程_

+ **repatriation of funds:** _资金汇回_

+ **repatriation agreement:** _遣返协议_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The repatriation of refugees is a complex issue.**

**中文翻译:** 难民的遣返是一个复杂的问题。

**单词释义:** 遣返

#### 例句2

> **The company is responsible for the repatriation of its employees
after the project ends.**

**中文翻译:** 项目结束后，公司负责员工的遣返。

**单词释义:** 遣返

#### 例句3

> **The repatriation of cultural relics requires international
cooperation.**

**中文翻译:** 文物的遣返需要国际合作。

**单词释义:** 遣返

### 背诵小故事

> A man was forced to leave his home country. But after many years,
there was a policy of repatriation. He was finally able to return and
reunite with his family. It was a happy ending.

**一个男人被迫离开了自己的祖国。但多年后，出台了遣返政策。他终于能够回国与家人团聚。这是个美好的结局。**
