## reprimand

**英音:** _[\'reprɪmɑːnd]_ **美音:** _[\'rɛprɪmænd]_

**译义:** *n.申斥*

### 短语

+ **issue a reprimand:** _发出谴责_

+ **severe reprimand:** _严厉斥责_

+ **deserve reprimand:** _应受谴责_

+ **face reprimand:** _面临斥责_

+ **warranted reprimand:** _正当的谴责_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The teacher reprimanded the student for not doing his
homework.**

**中文翻译:** 老师因为学生没做作业而训斥了他。

**单词释义:** 训斥；谴责

#### 例句2

> **The boss reprimanded the employee for being late again.**

**中文翻译:** 老板因员工再次迟到而斥责了他。

**单词释义:** 斥责；批评

#### 例句3

> **The judge reprimanded the lawyer for unethical behavior.**

**中文翻译:** 法官因律师的不道德行为而申斥了他。

**单词释义:** 申斥；训诫

### 背诵小故事

> A naughty student was constantly making trouble in class. The teacher
decided to reprimand him seriously. The student felt ashamed and
promised to behave better. Remember, \'reprimand\' means a severe
scolding or rebuke.

**一个调皮的学生在课堂上不断捣乱。老师决定严厉地斥责他。这个学生感到羞愧并承诺会表现得更好。记住，\'reprimand\'意思是严厉的训斥或指责。**
