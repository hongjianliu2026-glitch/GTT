## repression

**英音:** _[rɪ\'preʃən]_ **美音:** _[rɪ\'prɛʃən]_

**译义:** *n.镇压,抑制,抑压*

### 短语

+ **political repression:** _政治镇压_

+ **emotional repression:** _情绪压抑_

+ **sexual repression:** _性压抑_

+ **repressive measures:** _镇压措施_

+ **repressive regime:** _专制政权_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government\'s repression of political dissidents was widely
condemned.**

**中文翻译:** 政府对政治异见人士的镇压受到了广泛谴责。

**单词释义:** 镇压；压制

#### 例句2

> **She suffered from emotional repression and found it hard to
express her feelings.**

**中文翻译:** 她遭受着情感压抑，发现很难表达自己的感受。

**单词释义:** 压抑；抑制

#### 例句3

> **The repression of his memories caused him a lot of psychological
problems.**

**中文翻译:** 对他记忆的压抑给他造成了很多心理问题。

**单词释义:** 抑制；压抑

### 背诵小故事

> A brave knight was constantly fighting against repression. The evil
king tried to repress the knight\'s freedom and rights. But the knight
never gave up, and finally overcame the repression.

**一位勇敢的骑士一直在与镇压作斗争。邪恶的国王试图镇压骑士的自由和权利。但骑士从未放弃，最终战胜了镇压。**
