## reinforcement

**英音:** _[riːɪn\'fɔːsm(ə)nt]_ **美音:**
_[,riɪn\'fɔrsmənt]_

**译义:** *n.增援,加强,加固,援军*

### 短语

+ **positive reinforcement:** _正强化；积极强化_

+ **reinforcement learning:** _强化学习_

+ **structural reinforcement:** _结构加固_

+ **reinforcement bar:** _钢筋；加强筋_

+ **behavioral reinforcement:** _行为强化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The reinforcement of the army was necessary to defend the
country.**

**中文翻译:** 加强军队对于保卫国家是必要的。

**单词释义:** 加强；增援

#### 例句2

> **Positive feedback serves as a reinforcement for good
behavior.**

**中文翻译:** 积极的反馈是对良好行为的一种强化。

**单词释义:** 强化；巩固

#### 例句3

> **The bridge needed additional reinforcement to withstand heavy
traffic.**

**中文翻译:** 这座桥需要额外的加固才能承受繁忙的交通。

**单词释义:** 加固；增强

### 背诵小故事

> A soldier was alone in the battlefield, feeling weak. Suddenly, a
group of comrades arrived as reinforcement. They brought hope and
strength. With their support, the soldier became brave again.
Reinforcement means the act of strengthening or supporting.

**一名士兵独自在战场，感到虚弱。突然，一群战友作为增援抵达。他们带来了希望和力量。在他们的支持下，士兵再次变得勇敢。reinforcement
意思是加强或支持的行为。**
