## surreptitiously

**英音:** _[ˌsʌrəp\'tɪʃəsli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌsʌrəp\'tɪʃəsli]_

**译义:** *adv.秘密地*

### 短语

+ **act surreptitiously:** _秘密地行动_

+ **move surreptitiously:** _偷偷摸摸地移动_

+ **speak surreptitiously:** _悄悄地说话_

+ **observe surreptitiously:** _暗中观察_

+ **approach surreptitiously:** _鬼鬼祟祟地靠近_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He surreptitiously glanced at his watch during the meeting.**

**中文翻译:** 他在会议期间偷偷地瞥了一眼手表。

**单词释义:** 偷偷地

#### 例句2

> **She surreptitiously took a cookie from the jar.**

**中文翻译:** 她偷偷地从罐子里拿了一块饼干。

**单词释义:** 偷偷地

#### 例句3

> **The thief surreptitiously entered the house when no one was
looking.**

**中文翻译:** 小偷趁没人注意偷偷地进了房子。

**单词释义:** 偷偷地

### 背诵小故事

> A thief was moving surreptitiously in the dark. He tried to steal
jewels without being noticed. But the police caught him because of a
small noise he made. \'Surreptitiously\' means secretly and stealthily.

**一个小偷在黑暗中偷偷摸摸地移动。他试图偷珠宝而不被发现。但警察因为他发出的一点小声响抓住了他。\'surreptitiously\'意思是秘密地、偷偷摸摸地。**
