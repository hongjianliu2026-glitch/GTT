## shortfall

**英音:** _[\'ʃɔːtfɔːl]_ **美音:** _[\'ʃɔrtfɔl]_

**译义:** *n.不足,不足量*

### 短语

+ **budget shortfall:** _预算缺口_

+ **supply shortfall:** _供应短缺_

+ **energy shortfall:** _能源短缺_

+ **production shortfall:** _生产不足_

+ **funding shortfall:** _资金短缺_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company is facing a shortfall in profits this year.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司今年面临利润缺口。

**单词释义:** 短缺

#### 例句2

> **There is a shortfall of skilled workers in the manufacturing
industry.**

**中文翻译:** 制造业技术工人短缺。

**单词释义:** 不足；短缺

#### 例句3

> **The budget shortfall has forced the government to cut
spending.**

**中文翻译:** 预算短缺迫使政府削减开支。

**单词释义:** 差额；短缺

### 背诵小故事

> A company was facing a shortfall in funds. They needed to find a way
to solve it. The boss worked hard and finally got enough investment to
cover the shortfall.

**一家公司面临资金短缺。他们需要想办法解决。老板努力工作，最终获得了足够的投资来弥补缺口。**
