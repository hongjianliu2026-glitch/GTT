## solvent

**英音:** _[\'sɒlv(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[\'sɑlvənt]_

**译义:** *adj.溶解的,有偿付能力的,有溶解力n.溶媒,溶剂,解决方法*

### 短语

+ **solvent extraction:** _溶剂萃取_

+ **organic solvent:** _有机溶剂_

+ **solvent resistance:** _耐溶剂性_

+ **aqueous solvent:** _水溶剂_

+ **polar solvent:** _极性溶剂_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company remains solvent despite the economic downturn.**

**中文翻译:** 尽管经济衰退，这家公司仍有偿付能力。

**单词释义:** 有偿付能力的

#### 例句2

> **Water is a good solvent for many substances.**

**中文翻译:** 水对许多物质来说是一种良好的溶剂。

**单词释义:** 溶剂

#### 例句3

> **He found a solvent to remove the stain.**

**中文翻译:** 他找到了一种溶剂来去除污渍。

**单词释义:** 溶剂

### 背诵小故事

> A poor man was in debt and facing financial trouble. But then he
found a magic potion called\'solvent\' that could solve all his money
problems. With it, he paid off his debts and became solvent again.
