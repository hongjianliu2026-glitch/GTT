## selfishness

**英音:** _[\'selfɪʃnɪs]_ **美音:** _[\'sɛlfɪʃnɪs]_

**译义:** *n.自私自利*

### 短语

+ **excessive selfishness:** _过度自私_

+ **utter selfishness:** _极度自私_

+ **show selfishness:** _表现出自私_

+ **overcome selfishness:** _克服自私_

+ **indulge in selfishness:** _沉溺于自私_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His selfishness prevented him from sharing his toys with
others.**

**中文翻译:** 他的自私使他无法和别人分享他的玩具。

**单词释义:** 自私

#### 例句2

> **I can\'t stand her selfishness; she always thinks only of
herself.**

**中文翻译:** 我受不了她的自私，她总是只考虑自己。

**单词释义:** 自私

#### 例句3

> **The selfishness of some people is astonishing in times of
crisis.**

**中文翻译:** 在危机时刻，一些人的自私令人震惊。

**单词释义:** 自私

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a man who always thought only about
himself. He never cared about others\' feelings or needs. This behavior
was called selfishness. His selfishness made him lonely in the end.

**从前，有一个人总是只考虑自己。他从不关心别人的感受或需求。这种行为被称为自私。他的自私最终使他变得孤独。**
