## summarize

**英音:** _[ˈsʌməraɪz]_ **美音:** _[ˈsʌməraɪz]_

**译义:** *v.概述,总结,摘要而言*

### 短语

+ **summarize briefly:** _简要总结_

+ **summarize accurately:** _准确概括_

+ **summarize comprehensively:** _全面总结_

+ **summarize the main points:** _总结要点_

+ **summarize in a few words:** _用几句话概括_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The teacher asked the students to summarize the main points of
the article.**

**中文翻译:** 老师让学生总结文章的要点。

**单词释义:** 总结

#### 例句2

> **Can you summarize your presentation in a few sentences?**

**中文翻译:** 您能用几句话总结一下您的演讲吗？

**单词释义:** 概括

#### 例句3

> **The report summarizes the achievements and challenges of the past
year.**

**中文翻译:** 报告总结了过去一年的成就和挑战。

**单词释义:** 总结

### 背诵小故事

> A student was asked to summarize a long book. He read carefully and
picked out the key points. Finally, he wrote a short summary that made
everyone understand the main ideas easily.

**一名学生被要求总结一本很长的书。他认真阅读并挑出了关键点。最后，他写了一个简短的总结，让每个人都能轻松理解主要思想。**
