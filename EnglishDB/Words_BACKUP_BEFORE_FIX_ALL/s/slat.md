## slat

**英音:** _[slæt]_ **美音:** _[slæt]_

**译义:** *n.板条,狭板vt.用板条做或装备,打,猛投*

### 短语

+ **wooden slat:** _木制板条_

+ **metal slat:** _金属板条_

+ **slat blind:** _板条百叶窗_

+ **slat bed:** _板条床_

+ **slat conveyor:** _板条输送机_

slat on the floor and thought it was a perfect place to hide. But when
