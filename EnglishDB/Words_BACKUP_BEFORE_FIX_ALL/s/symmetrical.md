## symmetrical

**英音:** _[sɪ\'metrɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[sɪ\'mɛtrɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.对称的,均匀的*

### 短语

+ **symmetrical design:** _对称设计_

+ **symmetrical figure:** _对称图形_

+ **symmetrical arrangement:** _对称排列_

+ **symmetrical pattern:** _对称模式_

+ **symmetrical structure:** _对称结构_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The building has a symmetrical design, with two wings of equal
size.**

**中文翻译:** 这座建筑有一个对称的设计，有两个大小相等的侧翼。

**单词释义:** 对称的

#### 例句2

> **Her face is beautifully symmetrical, with evenly spaced
features.**

**中文翻译:** 她的脸很美很对称，五官分布均匀。

**单词释义:** 对称的

#### 例句3

> **The pattern on the fabric is symmetrical, creating a visually
pleasing effect.**

**中文翻译:** 织物上的图案是对称的，产生了一种视觉上令人愉悦的效果。

**单词释义:** 对称的

### 背诵小故事

> A king had a beautiful garden. The layout was symmetrical, with
equal-sized flower beds on both sides. The paths were perfectly
balanced. This made the garden look orderly and pleasing. Remember,
symmetrical means having balanced and matching parts.

**一位国王有一个美丽的花园。布局是对称的，两边都有大小相等的花坛。小路也完美平衡。这使得花园看起来整齐又令人愉悦。记住，symmetrical
意思是具有平衡和匹配的部分。**
