## stronghold

**英音:** _[ˈstrɒŋhəʊld]_ **美音:** _[ˈstrɔːŋhoʊld]_

**译义:** *n.要塞,据点*

### 短语

+ **military stronghold:** _军事要塞_

+ **fortified stronghold:** _设防的据点_

+ **enemy stronghold:** _敌人的据点_

+ **strategic stronghold:** _战略要地_

+ **last stronghold:** _最后的据点_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The castle was once a stronghold of the enemy.**

**中文翻译:** 这座城堡曾经是敌人的据点。

**单词释义:** 据点；要塞

#### 例句2

> **The company\'s stronghold in the market is its innovative
products.**

**中文翻译:** 这家公司在市场上的优势在于其创新产品。

**单词释义:** 优势；牢固地位

#### 例句3

> **The mountain village was a stronghold of traditional culture.**

**中文翻译:** 这个山村是传统文化的堡垒。

**单词释义:** 堡垒；根据地

### 背诵小故事

> In a medieval kingdom, there was a powerful fortress called
\'Stronghold\'. It had thick walls and was protected by brave soldiers.
It was so secure that no enemy could break in. The word \'stronghold\'
means a place that is strongly defended and difficult to attack.

**在一个中世纪的王国里，有一座强大的堡垒叫做"Stronghold"。它有厚厚的城墙，由勇敢的士兵守卫。它非常安全，没有敌人能够攻入。"stronghold"这个词的意思是防御坚固、难以攻破的地方。**
