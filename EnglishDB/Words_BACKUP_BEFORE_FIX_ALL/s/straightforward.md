## straightforward

**英音:** _[streɪt\'fɔːwəd]_ **美音:**
_[,stret\'fɔrwɚd]_

**译义:** * dly//adv.正直地*

### 短语

+ **straightforward manner:** _坦率的态度_

+ **straightforward process:** _简单的过程_

+ **straightforward answer:** _直截了当的回答_

+ **straightforward task:** _简单的任务_

+ **straightforward explanation:** _清晰明了的解释_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The instructions were straightforward and easy to follow.**

**中文翻译:** 这些说明简单明了，易于遵循。

**单词释义:** 简单明了的

#### 例句2

> **He has a straightforward personality and never beats around the
bush.**

**中文翻译:** 他性格直爽，从不拐弯抹角。

**单词释义:** 直爽的

#### 例句3

> **The solution to this problem is quite straightforward.**

**中文翻译:** 这个问题的解决办法相当直接。

**单词释义:** 直接的

### 背诵小故事

> A young detective was solving a case. The clues were straightforward.
It was like a clear path without any twists or turns. He easily followed
them and found the culprit. So, straightforward means simple and easy to
understand.

**一位年轻的侦探在破案。线索很直接明了。就像一条没有任何曲折的清晰道路。他轻松地顺着线索就找到了罪犯。所以，straightforward
意思是简单易懂的。**
