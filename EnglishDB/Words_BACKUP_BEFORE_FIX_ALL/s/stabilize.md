## stabilize

**英音:** _[\'steɪbəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[\'stebəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.稳定*

### 短语

+ **stabilize prices:** _稳定物价_

+ **stabilize the economy:** _稳定经济_

+ **stabilize the situation:** _稳定局势_

+ **stabilize one\'s emotions:** _稳定某人的情绪_

+ **stabilize the market:** _稳定市场_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government is taking measures to stabilize the economy.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在采取措施稳定经济。

**单词释义:** 使稳定；使稳固

#### 例句2

> **The patient\'s condition has stabilized after the operation.**

**中文翻译:** 手术后病人的病情已经稳定下来。

**单词释义:** （使）稳定；（使）安定

#### 例句3

> **This new policy is aimed at stabilizing prices.**

**中文翻译:** 这项新政策旨在稳定物价。

**单词释义:** 使稳定；使保持不变

### 背诵小故事

> A little boat was rocking wildly in the stormy sea. But when the
sailors used special tools and techniques to stabilize it, the boat
finally stopped swaying and sailed smoothly. Remember, \'stabilize\'
means to make something stable.

**一艘小船在狂风巨浪的大海中剧烈摇晃。但当水手们使用特殊的工具和技术使其稳定下来后，小船终于不再晃动，平稳航行了。记住，\'stabilize\'意思是使某物稳定。**
