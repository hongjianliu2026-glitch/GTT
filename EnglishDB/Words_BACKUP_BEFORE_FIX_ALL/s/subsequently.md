## subsequently

**英音:** _[\'sʌbsɪkwəntlɪ]_ **美音:**
_[\'sʌbsɪkwəntli]_

**译义:** *adv.后来,随后*

### 短语

+ **subsequently discovered:** _随后被发现_

+ **subsequently confirmed:** _随后被证实_

+ **subsequently modified:** _随后被修改_

+ **subsequently implemented:** _随后被实施_

+ **subsequently abandoned:** _随后被放弃_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He failed in the exam and subsequently lost his scholarship.**

**中文翻译:** 他考试不及格，随后失去了奖学金。

**单词释义:** 随后；接着

#### 例句2

> **The company released a new product, and subsequently saw a
significant increase in sales.**

**中文翻译:** 公司发布了一款新产品，随后销售额大幅增长。

**单词释义:** 后来；其后

#### 例句3

> **She got married and subsequently moved to a new city.**

**中文翻译:** 她结婚了，随后搬到了一个新的城市。

**单词释义:** 紧接着；其后

### 背诵小故事

> A brave adventurer set out on a journey. Firstly, he faced many
challenges. Subsequently, he found a hidden treasure chest and became
rich. The word \'subsequently\' shows what happened next.

**一位勇敢的冒险家踏上了旅程。首先，他面临诸多挑战。随后，他找到了一个隐藏的宝箱并变得富有。单词\'subsequently\'
表示接下来发生的事情。**
