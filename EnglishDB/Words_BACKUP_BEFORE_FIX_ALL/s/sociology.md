## sociology

_[,sosɪ\'ɑlədʒi]_

**译义:** *n.社会学*

+ **urban sociology:** _城市社会学_

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to study how people interact in society.
They called this study \'sociology\'. They observed, asked questions,
and learned a lot about social behaviors and groups. It was an exciting
adventure to understand the complexity of human society.

**一群朋友决定研究人们在社会中的互动方式。他们把这项研究称为"社会学"。他们观察、提问，并了解了很多关于社会行为和群体的知识。这是一次理解人类社会复杂性的激动人心的冒险。**
