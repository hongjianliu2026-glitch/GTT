## schoolhouse

**英音:** _[\'skuːlhaʊs]_ **美音:** _[\'skʊl\'haʊs]_

**译义:** *n.校舍*

### 短语

+ **old schoolhouse:** _旧校舍_

+ **small schoolhouse:** _小型校舍_

+ **abandoned schoolhouse:** _废弃的校舍_

+ **newly built schoolhouse:** _新建的校舍_

+ **country schoolhouse:** _乡村校舍_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The old schoolhouse was renovated into a community center.**

**中文翻译:** 旧校舍被改造成社区中心。

**单词释义:** 校舍

#### 例句2

> **We played hide-and-seek around the schoolhouse when we were
kids.**

**中文翻译:** 小时候，我们在校舍周围玩捉迷藏。

**单词释义:** 学校的房子

#### 例句3

> **The schoolhouse stood alone at the end of the road.**

**中文翻译:** 校舍孤零零地矗立在路的尽头。

**单词释义:** 校舍

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little boy got lost on his way home. He saw a building and
asked a passerby, \'What is this place?\' The passerby replied, \'This
is a schoolhouse where students study.\' The boy finally understood.

**一天，一个小男孩在回家的路上迷路了。他看到一座建筑并问一个路人："这是什么地方？"路人回答："这是一所学生学习的校舍。"男孩终于明白了。**
