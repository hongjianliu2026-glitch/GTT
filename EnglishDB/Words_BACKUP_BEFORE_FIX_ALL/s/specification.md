## specification

**英音:** _[,spesɪfɪ\'keɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[\'spɛsəfə\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.详述,[常 pl.] 规格,说明书,规范*

### 短语

+ **technical specification:** _技术规格_

+ **product specification:** _产品规格_

+ **design specification:** _设计规范_

+ **job specification:** _工作规范；职位说明_

+ **specification sheet:** _规格表_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The specification of this new car model includes advanced safety
features.**

**中文翻译:** 这款新车的规格包括先进的安全功能。

**单词释义:** 规格；明细单

#### 例句2

> **Please read the job specification carefully before applying.**

**中文翻译:** 申请前请仔细阅读职位说明书。

**单词释义:** 规范；说明书

#### 例句3

> **The product failed to meet the specification and was
recalled.**

**中文翻译:** 该产品不符合规格并被召回。

**单词释义:** 规格；标准

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working hard on a new project. The specification of
the project was very detailed. It included all the requirements and
standards needed to make the invention successful. Finally, with the
clear specification, the project was completed perfectly.

**一位科学家正在努力研究一个新项目。这个项目的规格非常详细。它包含了使发明成功所需的所有要求和标准。最终，凭借清晰的规格说明，项目完美完成。**
