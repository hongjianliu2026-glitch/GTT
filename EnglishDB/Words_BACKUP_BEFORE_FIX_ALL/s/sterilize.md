## sterilize

**英音:** _[ˈsterəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[ˈsterəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *vt.杀菌,消毒,使成不毛*

### 短语

+ **sterilize equipment:** _给设备消毒_

+ **sterilize the wound:** _给伤口消毒_

+ **sterilize surgical instruments:** _给外科器械消毒_

+ **sterilize the environment:** _对环境进行消毒_

+ **sterilize the soil:** _给土壤消毒_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The surgeon sterilized the surgical instruments before the
operation.**

**中文翻译:** 外科医生在手术前对手术器械进行了消毒。

**单词释义:** 消毒

#### 例句2

> **They decided to sterilize the cat to prevent it from having
kittens.**

**中文翻译:** 他们决定给猫做绝育手术，防止它生小猫。

**单词释义:** 使绝育

#### 例句3

> **The laboratory equipment was sterilized to ensure the accuracy of
the experiments.**

**中文翻译:** 实验室设备经过了灭菌处理，以确保实验的准确性。

**单词释义:** 灭菌

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor needed to sterilize the surgical tools. But the sterilizing
machine broke. So, he had to find a quick fix to make sure everything
was clean and free of germs. This is how we remember \'sterilize\' -
making something germ-free!

**一位医生需要给手术工具消毒。但消毒机坏了。所以，他不得不迅速找到解决办法以确保所有东西都干净且无菌。这就是我们记住'sterilize'------使某物无菌的方法！**
