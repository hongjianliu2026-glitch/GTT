## shorts

**译义:** *n.短裤*

### 短语

+ **a pair of shorts:** _一条短裤_

+ **be in shorts:** _穿着短裤_

+ **sports shorts:** _运动短裤_

+ **cotton shorts:** _棉质短裤_

+ **denim shorts:** _牛仔短裤_
