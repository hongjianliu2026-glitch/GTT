## submissive

**英音:** _[səb\'mɪsɪv]_ **美音:** _[səb\'mɪsɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.顺从的*

### 短语

+ **submissive attitude:** _顺从的态度_

+ **submissive behavior:** _顺从的行为_

+ **be submissive to:** _对\...\...顺从_

+ **submissive personality:** _顺从的性格_

+ **remain submissive:** _保持顺从_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She has always been a submissive wife, obeying her husband\'s
every command.**

**中文翻译:** 她一直是个顺从的妻子，听从丈夫的每一个命令。

**单词释义:** 顺从的

#### 例句2

> **The submissive child rarely expressed his own opinions.**

**中文翻译:** 这个温顺的孩子很少表达自己的意见。

**单词释义:** 温顺的

#### 例句3

> **In the workplace, he was seen as too submissive and was often
overlooked for promotions.**

**中文翻译:** 在工作场所，他被认为太顺从，经常被忽视而得不到晋升。

**单词释义:** 顺从的；服从的

### 背诵小故事

> A little rabbit was always submissive in the forest. When facing the
big bad wolf\'s threats, it never resisted but obeyed quietly. This
shows that\'submissive\' means being obedient and yielding without
resistance.

**一只小兔子在森林里总是很顺从。当面对大灰狼的威胁时，它从不反抗，只是安静地服从。这表明'submissive'意思是顺从的，屈服的，不抵抗的。**
