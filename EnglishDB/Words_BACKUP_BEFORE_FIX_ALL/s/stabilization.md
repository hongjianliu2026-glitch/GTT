## stabilization

**英音:** _[,steɪbɪlaɪ\'zeɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[,stebɪlɪ\'zeʃən]_

**译义:** *n.稳定性*

### 短语

+ **economic stabilization:** _经济稳定_

+ **price stabilization:** _价格稳定_

+ **stabilization policy:** _稳定政策_

+ **stabilization fund:** _稳定基金_

+ **structural stabilization:** _结构稳定_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government is working on the stabilization of the
economy.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在致力于经济的稳定。

**单词释义:** 稳定；稳固

#### 例句2

> **Stabilization of the patient\'s condition is our top
priority.**

**中文翻译:** 稳定病人的病情是我们的首要任务。

**单词释义:** （病情、局势等的）稳定

#### 例句3

> **The new policy aims at the stabilization of housing prices.**

**中文翻译:** 新政策旨在稳定房价。

**单词释义:** 稳定；使稳定

### 背诵小故事

> A big storm was coming. The town was in chaos. But with the efforts
of everyone, they built strong walls and fixed the houses. Finally,
stabilization was achieved and the town was safe again.

**一场大风暴即将来临。小镇陷入混乱。但在大家的努力下，他们筑起坚固的围墙，修缮房屋。最终，实现了稳定，小镇再次安全。**
