## structurally

**英音:** _[ˈstrʌktʃərəli]_ **美音:** _[ˈstrʌktʃərəli]_

**译义:** *adv.在结构上*

### 短语

+ **structurally sound:** _结构坚固_

+ **structurally deficient:** _结构有缺陷_

+ **structurally complex:** _结构复杂_

+ **structurally simple:** _结构简单_

+ **structurally related:** _结构相关_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The building is structurally sound.**

**中文翻译:** 这座建筑结构坚固。

**单词释义:** 在结构上

#### 例句2

> **Structurally, the bridge needs some reinforcement.**

**中文翻译:** 从结构上讲，这座桥需要一些加固。

**单词释义:** 在结构方面

#### 例句3

> **The company is structurally organized to handle complex
projects.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司在结构上进行了组织，以处理复杂的项目。

**单词释义:** 从结构上

### 背诵小故事

> A little ant was exploring a building. It noticed that everything was
arranged structurally. The columns, beams, and floors were all in
perfect order. This helped the ant understand the meaning
of\'structurally\' - related to the structure or way things are
organized.

**一只小蚂蚁在探索一座建筑。它注意到一切都结构合理地排列着。柱子、横梁和地板都井井有条。这帮助蚂蚁理解了\'structurally\'这个词------与结构或事物的组织方式有关。**
