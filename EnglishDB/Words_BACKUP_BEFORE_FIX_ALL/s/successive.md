## successive

**英音:** _[sək\'sesɪv]_ **美音:** _[sək\'sɛsɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.继承的,连续的*

### 短语

+ **successive generations:** _连续的世代_

+ **successive years:** _连年_

+ **successive attempts:** _连续的尝试_

+ **successive victories:** _接连的胜利_

+ **successive steps:** _连续的步骤_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The team has achieved successive victories in the tournament.**

**中文翻译:** 球队在比赛中取得了连胜。

**单词释义:** 连续的

#### 例句2

> **He has held successive positions in the company.**

**中文翻译:** 曾在该公司历任职务。

**单词释义:** 连续的

#### 例句3

> **The successive governments have tried to solve the economic
problems.**

**中文翻译:** 历届政府都试图解决经济问题。

**单词释义:** 接连的；相继的

### 背诵小故事

> A king wanted to build successive castles to protect his kingdom. But
each time, there were new challenges. Finally, with perseverance, he
achieved successive victories and his kingdom was safe.
Remember,\'successive\' means following one after another.

**一位国王想要建造一个接一个的城堡来保护他的王国。但每次都有新的挑战。最终，凭借毅力，他取得了接连不断的胜利，他的王国安全了。记住，'successive'意思是接连的，连续的。**
