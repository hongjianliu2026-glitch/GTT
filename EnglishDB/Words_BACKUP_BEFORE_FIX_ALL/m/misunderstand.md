## misunderstand

**英音:** _[,mɪsʌndə\'stænd]_ **美音:**
_[,mɪsʌndɚ\'stænd]_

**译义:** *vt.误解,误会*

### 短语

+ **misunderstand something:** _误解某事_

+ **misunderstand someone:** _误解某人_

+ **be misunderstood:** _被误解_

+ **misunderstand the meaning:** _误解意思_

+ **misunderstand the situation:** _误解情况_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I think you misunderstand me.**

**中文翻译:** 我认为你误解我了。

**单词释义:** 误解

#### 例句2

> **They misunderstood each other\'s intentions.**

**中文翻译:** 他们误解了彼此的意图。

**单词释义:** 误解

#### 例句3

> **Don\'t misunderstand what I\'m saying.**

**中文翻译:** 别误解我正在说的话。

**单词释义:** 误解

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom said something casually. But Mary misunderstood him and
got very angry. Tom felt helpless and tried to explain that it was just
a misunderstanding. Finally, Mary understood and they made up.

**有一天，汤姆随口说了些什么。但玛丽误解了他，非常生气。汤姆感到很无奈，试图解释这只是个误会。最后，玛丽明白了，他们和好了。**
