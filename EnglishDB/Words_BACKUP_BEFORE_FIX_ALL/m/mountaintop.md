## mountaintop

**英音:** _[ˈmaʊntəntɒp]_ **美音:** _[ˈmaʊntntɑːp]_

**译义:** *n.山顶*

### 短语

+ **reach the mountaintop:** _到达山顶_

+ **stand on the mountaintop:** _站在山顶_

+ **climb to the mountaintop:** _爬到山顶_

+ **view from the mountaintop:** _从山顶眺望_

+ **descend from the mountaintop:** _从山顶下来_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **We reached the mountaintop just as the sun was setting.**

**中文翻译:** 我们到达山顶时，太阳刚好落山。

**单词释义:** 山顶

#### 例句2

> **From the mountaintop, you can see the entire city below.**

**中文翻译:** 从山顶上，你可以看到下面的整个城市。

**单词释义:** 山顶

#### 例句3

> **The climbers struggled to reach the mountaintop in the bad
weather.**

**中文翻译:** 登山者们在恶劣的天气中努力到达山顶。

**单词释义:** 山顶

### 背诵小故事

> A brave climber struggled to reach the mountaintop. After many
challenges, he finally stood on the peak, feeling the strong wind and
enjoying the breathtaking view. It was a moment of great achievement.

**一位勇敢的登山者努力攀登到达山顶。经过许多挑战，他终于站在了山顶，感受着强风，欣赏着令人惊叹的景色。这是一个伟大成就的时刻。**
