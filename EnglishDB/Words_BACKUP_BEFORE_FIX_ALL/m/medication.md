## medication

**英音:** _[medɪ\'keɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:** _[\'mɛdɪ\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.药物治疗,药物处理,药物*

### 短语

+ **prescription medication:** _处方药_

+ **over-the-counter medication:** _非处方药_

+ **medication error:** _用药错误_

+ **medication side effect:** _药物副作用_

+ **medication compliance:** _药物依从性_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The doctor prescribed some medication for my headache.**

**中文翻译:** 医生为我的头痛开了一些药。

**单词释义:** 药；药物

#### 例句2

> **She has been on medication for her heart condition for years.**

**中文翻译:** 她因心脏病服药多年。

**单词释义:** 药物治疗

#### 例句3

> **The side effects of this medication can be quite severe.**

**中文翻译:** 这种药物的副作用可能相当严重。

**单词释义:** 药物

### 背诵小故事

> A sick person was in great pain. The doctor prescribed some
medication. With the right medication, the person slowly recovered and
felt better. Remember, medication can heal!

**一个生病的人非常痛苦。医生开了一些药。有了正确的药物治疗，这个人慢慢康复，感觉好多了。记住，medication（药物、药剂）可以治病！**
