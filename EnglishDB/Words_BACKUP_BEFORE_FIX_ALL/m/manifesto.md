## manifesto

**英音:** _[mænɪ\'festəʊ]_ **美音:** _[,mænɪ\'fɛsto]_

**译义:** *n.宣言,声明*

### 短语

+ **political manifesto:** _政治宣言_

+ **election manifesto:** _选举宣言_

+ **manifesto of principles:** _原则宣言_

+ **revolutionary manifesto:** _革命宣言_

+ **party manifesto:** _政党宣言_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The party released its manifesto ahead of the election.**

**中文翻译:** 该党在选举前发布了其宣言。

**单词释义:** 宣言

#### 例句2

> **The manifesto outlines the group\'s goals and strategies for the
next five years.**

**中文翻译:** 这份宣言概述了该团体未来五年的目标和策略。

**单词释义:** 宣言

#### 例句3

> **His manifesto called for radical changes in the education
system.**

**中文翻译:** 他的宣言呼吁对教育系统进行彻底变革。

**单词释义:** 宣言

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends decided to create a manifesto to express their
shared dreams for a better community. They wrote down all their plans
and goals clearly. The manifesto became a guide for their future
actions.

**一群朋友决定创作一份宣言来表达他们对一个更美好社区的共同梦想。他们清晰地写下了所有的计划和目标。这份宣言成为了他们未来行动的指南。**
