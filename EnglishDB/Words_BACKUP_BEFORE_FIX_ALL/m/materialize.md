## materialize

**英音:** _[məˈtɪəriəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[məˈtɪriəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.物化*

### 短语

+ **materialize out of thin air:** _突然出现；凭空出现_

+ **materialize one\'s dream:** _实现某人的梦想_

+ **materialize into reality:** _变成现实_

+ **fail to materialize:** _未能实现_

+ **hope materialize:** _希望实现_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The plan failed to materialize as expected.**

**中文翻译:** 该计划未能按预期实现。

**单词释义:** 实现

#### 例句2

> **His dream of becoming a doctor finally materialized after years
of hard work.**

**中文翻译:** 经过多年的努力，他成为一名医生的梦想终于实现了。

**单词释义:** 实现

#### 例句3

> **The ghost is said to materialize at midnight.**

**中文翻译:** 据说鬼魂会在午夜出现。

**单词释义:** 现身

### 背诵小故事

> A magician was trying hard to materialize a rabbit from thin air.
After several attempts and chants, finally, the rabbit appeared. Just
like things coming into existence, that\'s what \'materialize\' means.

**一位魔术师努力尝试从稀薄的空气中变出一只兔子。经过几次尝试和念咒，最后，兔子出现了。就像东西变为存在，这就是\'materialize\'的意思。**
