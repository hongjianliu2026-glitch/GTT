## memorialize

**英音:** _[mɪ\'mɔːrɪəlaɪz]_ **美音:** _[mə\'mɔrɪəlaɪz]_

**译义:** *v.请愿*

### 短语

+ **memorialize the event:** _纪念这个事件_

+ **memorialize a person:** _缅怀一个人_

+ **memorialize the past:** _铭记过去_

+ **memorialize a moment:** _纪念一个时刻_

+ **memorialize a hero:** _纪念一位英雄_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **They decided to memorialize the fallen soldiers with a special
monument.**

**中文翻译:** 他们决定用一座特殊的纪念碑来纪念阵亡的士兵。

**单词释义:** 纪念

#### 例句2

> **The event was memorialized in a documentary film.**

**中文翻译:** 该事件被记录在一部纪录片中。

**单词释义:** 作为纪念

#### 例句3

> **We will always memorialize the heroes who sacrificed their lives
for our country.**

**中文翻译:** 我们将永远缅怀那些为祖国献出生命的英雄们。

**单词释义:** 纪念

### 背诵小故事

> A famous artist decided to memorialize his hometown by painting a
beautiful landscape. He wanted to make sure people would always remember
its charm. Memorialize means to preserve the memory of something or
someone.
