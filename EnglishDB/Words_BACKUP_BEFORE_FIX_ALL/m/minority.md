## minority

**英音:** _[maɪ\'nɒrɪtɪ; mɪ-]_ **美音:** _[maɪ\'nɔrəti]_

**译义:** *n.少数,少数民族*

### 短语

+ **ethnic minority:** _少数民族_

+ **minority group:** _少数群体_

+ **minority language:** _少数民族语言_

+ **minority opinion:** _少数派意见_

+ **minority shareholder:** _少数股东_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The minority of the students were absent from the class.**

**中文翻译:** 少数学生缺席了课堂。

**单词释义:** 少数

#### 例句2

> **This law is designed to protect the rights of minorities.**

**中文翻译:** 该法旨在保护少数群体的权利。

**单词释义:** 少数群体

#### 例句3

> **In this company, women are in the minority.**

**中文翻译:** 在这家公司里，女性占少数。

**单词释义:** 少数

### 背诵小故事

> In a small village, the minority of people had a unique skill of
making beautiful pottery. They were few but their craftsmanship was
highly valued. The rest of the villagers were the majority. Remember,
minority means a smaller part or number.

**在一个小村庄里，少数人拥有制作精美陶器的独特技能。他们人数很少，但他们的手艺备受重视。其余的村民是多数。记住，minority
意思是少数；少数派。**
