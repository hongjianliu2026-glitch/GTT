## mistreatment

**英音:** _[ˌmɪsˈtriːtmənt]_ **美音:**
_[ˌmɪsˈtriːtmənt]_

**译义:** *n.虐待*

### 短语

+ **suffer mistreatment:** _遭受虐待_

+ **prevent mistreatment:** _防止虐待_

+ **endure mistreatment:** _忍受虐待_

+ **report mistreatment:** _举报虐待_

+ **stop mistreatment:** _停止虐待_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The mistreatment of animals is a serious crime.**

**中文翻译:** 虐待动物是一种严重的罪行。

**单词释义:** 虐待

#### 例句2

> **She suffered years of mistreatment at the hands of her
employer.**

**中文翻译:** 她在雇主手下遭受了多年的虐待。

**单词释义:** 虐待

#### 例句3

> **The report exposed the mistreatment of prisoners in the detention
center.**

**中文翻译:** 这份报告揭露了拘留中心囚犯遭受的虐待。

**单词释义:** 虐待

### 背诵小故事

> A poor little rabbit was constantly subjected to mistreatment by a
mean farmer. It was starved and beaten. But finally, the rabbit was
rescued and the farmer was punished. Remember, mistreatment is bad
behavior!

**一只可怜的小兔子不断遭受一个刻薄农民的虐待。它挨饿又挨打。但最后，兔子被救了，农民受到了惩罚。记住，mistreatment
就是恶劣的对待！**
