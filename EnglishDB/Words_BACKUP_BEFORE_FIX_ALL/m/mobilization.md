## mobilization

**英音:** _[ˌməʊbəlaɪˈzeɪʃ(ə)n]_ **美音:**
_[ˌmob(ə)lɪˈzeʃ(ə)n]_

**译义:** *n.动员*

### 短语

+ **mobilization plan:** _动员计划_

+ **resource mobilization:** _资源动员_

+ **mobilization strategy:** _动员策略_

+ **mobilization effort:** _动员工作；动员努力_

+ **full mobilization:** _全面动员_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The mobilization of resources was crucial for the project\'s
success.**

**中文翻译:** 资源的调动对这个项目的成功至关重要。

**单词释义:** 调动

#### 例句2

> **The military mobilization caused widespread concern among the
citizens.**

**中文翻译:** 军事动员引起了市民们的广泛担忧。

**单词释义:** 动员

#### 例句3

> **The company\'s rapid mobilization of staff to handle the
emergency was impressive.**

**中文翻译:** 公司迅速调动员工来处理紧急情况，令人印象深刻。

**单词释义:** 调动

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, a disaster struck. To deal with it, the mayor
ordered the mobilization of all resources. People came together, moving
cars, equipment, and supplies. This action of bringing everything into
action was called mobilization.

**在一个小镇，一场灾难袭来。为了应对，市长下令动员所有资源。人们聚集在一起，调动汽车、设备和物资。这种让一切行动起来的行为就叫动员。**
