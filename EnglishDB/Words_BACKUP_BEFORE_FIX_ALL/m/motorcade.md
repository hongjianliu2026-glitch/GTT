## motorcade

**英音:** _[\'məʊtəkeɪd]_ **美音:** _[\'motɚked]_

**译义:** *n.汽车行列*

### 短语

+ **presidential motorcade:** _总统车队_

+ **motorcade procession:** _车队游行_

+ **celebrity motorcade:** _名人车队_

+ **motorcade escort:** _车队护送_

+ **political motorcade:** _政治车队_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The president\'s motorcade passed through the crowded
streets.**

**中文翻译:** 总统的车队穿过拥挤的街道。

**单词释义:** 车队

#### 例句2

> **The motorcade of the visiting dignitary attracted a lot of
attention.**

**中文翻译:** 来访贵宾的车队引起了广泛关注。

**单词释义:** 车队

#### 例句3

> **The police escorted the motorcade to ensure its safety.**

**中文翻译:** 警方全程护送车队，确保车队安全。

**单词释义:** 车队

### 背诵小故事

> A motorcade was passing through the town. The shiny cars moved
smoothly. There was a special VIP in the middle car. Everyone was
excited to see the motorcade.

**一支车队正穿过城镇。闪亮的汽车行驶得很平稳。中间的车里有一位特别的贵宾。每个人看到车队都很兴奋。**
