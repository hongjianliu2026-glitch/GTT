## miraculously

**英音:** _[mɪˈrækjələsli]_ **美音:** _[mɪˈrækjələsli]_

**译义:** *adv.奇迹般地；不可思议地*

### 短语

+ **miraculously escape:** _奇迹般地逃脱_

+ **miraculously recover:** _奇迹般地康复_

+ **miraculously survive:** _奇迹般地幸存_

+ **miraculously transform:** _奇迹般地转变_

+ **miraculously succeed:** _奇迹般地成功_

#### 例句1

> **The patient miraculously recovered from a serious illness.**

**单词释义:** 奇迹般地

**单词释义:** 奇迹般地

**单词释义:** 奇迹般地

all was lost, he miraculously found a way out. It was like a miracle had
happened and saved him.
