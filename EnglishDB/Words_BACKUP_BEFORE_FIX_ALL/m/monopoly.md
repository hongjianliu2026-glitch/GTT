## monopoly

**英音:** _[mə\'nɒp(ə)lɪ]_ **美音:** _[mə\'nɑpəli]_

**译义:** *n.垄断,垄断者,专利权,专利事业*

### 短语

+ **monopoly power:** _垄断力量_

+ **natural monopoly:** _自然垄断_

+ **monopoly position:** _垄断地位_

+ **monopoly market:** _垄断市场_

+ **government monopoly:** _政府垄断_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The company has a monopoly on the production of this new
drug.**

**中文翻译:** 该公司拥有该新药的生产垄断权。

**单词释义:** 垄断

#### 例句2

> **The government is trying to break the monopoly of the
telecommunications industry.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在努力打破电信行业的垄断。

**单词释义:** 垄断

#### 例句3

> **He accused them of creating a monopoly in the market.**

**中文翻译:** 他指责他们在市场上形成垄断。

**单词释义:** 垄断

### 背诵小故事

> A brave entrepreneur wanted to start a business, but a big company
had a monopoly. It controlled everything and made it hard for the
entrepreneur. Finally, with hard work and smart strategies, the
entrepreneur broke the monopoly and created fair competition.

**一位勇敢的企业家想要创业，但一家大公司拥有垄断地位。它掌控一切，让企业家举步维艰。最终，通过努力和聪明的策略，这位企业家打破了垄断，创造了公平竞争。**
