### 背诵小故事

> In a faraway land, people were highly polarized. Some strongly
supported one leader, while others were firmly against. This division
caused a lot of conflicts. Remember, polarized means divided into
opposing groups.

**在一个遥远的地方，人们的意见高度两极分化。一些人强烈支持一位领导者，而另一些人则坚决反对。这种分歧导致了很多冲突。记住，polarized
意思是两极分化的、对立的。**
