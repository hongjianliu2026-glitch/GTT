## purification

**英音:** _[,pjʊərɪfɪ\'keɪʃən]_ **美音:**
_[,pjʊrəfə\'keʃən]_

**译义:** *n.净化*

### 短语

+ **water purification:** _水的净化_

+ **air purification:** _空气净化_

+ **purification process:** _净化过程_

+ **purification system:** _净化系统_

+ **purification plant:** _净化厂_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The purification of water is essential for public health.**

**中文翻译:** 水的净化对公众健康至关重要。

**单词释义:** 净化

#### 例句2

> **She is engaged in the purification of metals.**

**中文翻译:** 她从事金属的提纯工作。

**单词释义:** 提纯；纯化

#### 例句3

> **The spiritual purification is a long and challenging journey.**

**中文翻译:** 精神的净化是一段漫长且具有挑战性的旅程。

**单词释义:** 净化

### 背诵小故事

> A dirty river was in great need of purification. Scientists worked
hard and used special methods to remove impurities. Finally, the water
became clear and pure again. Remember, purification means making
something clean and pure!

**一条肮脏的河流急需净化。科学家们努力工作，使用特殊方法去除杂质。最终，水再次变得清澈纯净。记住，purification
意思是使某物变干净和纯净！**
