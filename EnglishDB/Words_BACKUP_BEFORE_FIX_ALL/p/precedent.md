## precedent

**英音:** _[\'presɪd(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[\'prɛsɪdənt]_

**译义:** *n.先例*

### 短语

+ **set a precedent:** _开创先例_

+ **follow precedent:** _遵循先例_

+ **without precedent:** _史无前例_

+ **precedent case:** _判例_

+ **judicial precedent:** _司法判例_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The judge relied on a precedent to make the decision.**

**中文翻译:** 法官依据先例做出了决定。

**单词释义:** 先例

#### 例句2

> **This case sets a new precedent for future legal proceedings.**

**中文翻译:** 该案为未来的法律诉讼开创了新的先例。

**单词释义:** 先例

#### 例句3

> **There is no precedent for such a situation in our company\'s
history.**

**中文翻译:** 这种情况在我们公司的历史上是没有先例的。

**单词释义:** 先例

### 背诵小故事

> A king was about to make a new law. But his wise advisor reminded him
to look at the precedent laws. They found that a similar situation had
been dealt with before. So, they followed that earlier example.
Precedent means an earlier event or action that is regarded as an
example or rule to be followed.

**一位国王即将制定新法律。但他聪明的顾问提醒他查看先例法律。他们发现之前有过类似情况的处理。所以，他们遵循了那个早期的例子。precedent
意思是被视为可遵循的范例或规则的先前事件或行动。**
