## purely

**英音:** _[\'pjʊəlɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'pjʊrli]_

**译义:** *adv.纯粹地,完全地*

### 短语

+ **purely accidental:** _纯属偶然_

+ **purely theoretical:** _纯理论的_

+ **purely commercial:** _纯粹商业性的_

+ **purely personal:** _纯属个人的_

+ **purely fictional:** _纯属虚构的_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The decision was purely based on financial considerations.**

**中文翻译:** 这个决定纯粹是基于财务方面的考虑。

**单词释义:** 纯粹地；完全地

#### 例句2

> **She was purely delighted to see him again.**

**中文翻译:** 她再次见到他纯粹是高兴。

**单词释义:** 十足地；非常

#### 例句3

> **This is purely a matter of personal choice.**

**中文翻译:** 这纯粹是个人选择的问题。

**单词释义:** 纯粹；仅仅

### 背诵小故事

> A girl named Lily always made decisions purely based on her heart.
She never cared about external factors. One day, she chose to help a
stray cat purely out of kindness. This act made everyone admire her pure
heart.
