## pomegranate

**英音:** _[\'pɒmɪgrænɪt]_ **美音:** _[\'pɑmɪɡrænɪt]_

**译义:** *n.[植]石榴*

### 短语

+ **pomegranate juice:** _石榴汁_

+ **pomegranate tree:** _石榴树_

+ **sweet pomegranate:** _甜石榴_

+ **ripe pomegranate:** _成熟的石榴_

+ **pomegranate seeds:** _石榴籽_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I love the juicy pomegranate in the fruit salad.**

**中文翻译:** 我喜欢水果沙拉里多汁的石榴。

**单词释义:** 石榴

#### 例句2

> **The pomegranate tree in our garden bears fruit every year.**

**中文翻译:** 我们花园里的石榴树每年都结果。

**单词释义:** 石榴

#### 例句3

> **She squeezed the pomegranate to get the juice.**

**中文翻译:** 她挤压石榴以获取果汁。

**单词释义:** 石榴

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved a special fruit.
It was red, had many seeds inside, and was called a pomegranate. She
enjoyed eating it and sharing it with her friends. Remember, pomegranate
is this wonderful fruit!

**从前，有一个小女孩喜欢一种特别的水果。它是红色的，里面有很多籽，被叫做石榴。她喜欢吃它并和朋友们分享。记住，pomegranate
就是这种美妙的水果！**
