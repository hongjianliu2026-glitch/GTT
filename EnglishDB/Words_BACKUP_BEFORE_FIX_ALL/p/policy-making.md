## policy-making

**英音:** _[ˈpɒləsi meɪkɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[ˈpɑːləsi
meɪkɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n. 政策制定；决策过程*

### 短语

+ **policy-making process:** _决策过程_

+ **policy-making body:** _决策机构_

+ **policy-making mechanism:** _决策机制_

+ **policy-making power:** _决策权_

+ **policy-making role:** _决策角色_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The government is strengthening its policy-making process to
address economic challenges.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在加强其决策过程以应对经济挑战。

**单词释义:** 决策

#### 例句2

> **Effective policy-making requires comprehensive research and
analysis.**

**中文翻译:** 有效的决策需要全面的研究和分析。

**单词释义:** 决策

#### 例句3

> **She is an expert in the field of policy-making for environmental
protection.**

**中文翻译:** 她是环境保护决策领域的专家。

**单词释义:** 决策

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a kingdom where the king and his wise advisors are gathered
in a room. They are deeply engaged in discussions and decisions to
create new rules for the land. This process is called policy-making.

**想象一个王国，国王和他睿智的顾问们聚集在一个房间里。他们深入地讨论和决定为这片土地制定新的规则。这个过程就叫做政策制定。**
