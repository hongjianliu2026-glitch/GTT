## pancreatic

**英音:** _[,pænkrɪ\'ætɪk]_ **美音:** _[,pæŋkrɪ\'ætɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.胰腺的*

### 短语

+ **pancreatic cancer:** _胰腺癌_

+ **pancreatic duct:** _胰管_

+ **pancreatic juice:** _胰液_

+ **pancreatic islet:** _胰岛_

+ **pancreatic enzyme:** _胰酶_
