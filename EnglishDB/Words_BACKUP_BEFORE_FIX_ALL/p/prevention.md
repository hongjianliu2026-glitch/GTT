## prevention

**英音:** _[prɪ\'venʃn]_ **美音:** _[pri\'vɛnʃən]_

**译义:** *n.预防,防止*

### 短语

+ **disease prevention:** _疾病预防_

+ **prevention measure:** _预防措施_

+ **fire prevention:** _防火_

+ **crime prevention:** _犯罪预防_

+ **accident prevention:** _事故预防_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The prevention of diseases is very important for public
health.**

**中文翻译:** 疾病的预防对于公共卫生非常重要。

**单词释义:** 预防；防止

#### 例句2

> **Regular exercise is a key part of cancer prevention.**

**中文翻译:** 经常锻炼是预防癌症的关键部分。

**单词释义:** 预防；防止

#### 例句3

> **The government is strengthening measures for the prevention of
crime.**

**中文翻译:** 政府正在加强预防犯罪的措施。

**单词释义:** 预防；防止

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a village was threatened by a disease. But the wise
villagers took many actions in advance for prevention. They cleaned the
environment, stored enough medicine, and educated everyone. Finally,
they successfully avoided the disaster.

**有一天，一个村庄受到疾病的威胁。但聪明的村民们提前采取了很多行动来预防。他们清洁环境、储备了足够的药品并教育每个人。最终，他们成功避免了这场灾难。**
