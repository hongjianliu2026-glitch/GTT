## preventive

**英音:** _[prɪ\'ventɪv]_ **美音:** _[pri\'vɛntɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.预防性的*

### 短语

+ **preventive measure:** _预防措施_

+ **preventive maintenance:** _预防性维护；预防性保养_

+ **preventive medicine:** _预防医学_

+ **preventive action:** _预防行动_

+ **preventive detention:** _预防性拘留_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Regular exercise is a preventive measure against many
diseases.**

**中文翻译:** 经常锻炼是预防许多疾病的一种措施。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

#### 例句2

> **The doctor recommended some preventive medicine for the flu
season.**

**中文翻译:** 医生为流感季节推荐了一些预防药物。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

#### 例句3

> **Taking preventive actions can save a lot of trouble in the
future.**

**中文翻译:** 采取预防行动可以在未来省去很多麻烦。

**单词释义:** 预防的；防止的

### 背诵小故事

> A doctor told a patient that taking this preventive medicine
regularly could stop the disease from happening. The patient followed
the advice and stayed healthy. Remember, preventive means to stop
something bad before it occurs.

**一位医生告诉一位病人，定期服用这种预防药可以防止疾病发生。病人听从了建议，保持了健康。记住，preventive
意思是在坏事发生之前加以阻止。**
