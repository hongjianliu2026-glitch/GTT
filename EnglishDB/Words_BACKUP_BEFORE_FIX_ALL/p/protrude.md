## protrude

**英音:** _[prə\'truːd]_ **美音:** _[pro\'trud]_

**译义:** *v.突出*

### 短语

+ **protrude from:** _从\...\...突出_

+ **protrude out:** _突出；伸出_

+ **protrude forward:** _向前突出_

+ **protrude noticeably:** _显著突出_

+ **protrude slightly:** _稍微突出_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The branch protruded from the trunk of the tree.**

**中文翻译:** 树枝从树干上伸出来。

**单词释义:** 伸出；突出

#### 例句2

> **His teeth protruded and gave him an odd look.**

**中文翻译:** 他的牙齿突出了，看上去很奇怪。

**单词释义:** 突出；伸出

#### 例句3

> **The rocks protruded from the surface of the water.**

**中文翻译:** 岩石从水面突出。

**单词释义:** 突出；伸出

### 背诵小故事

> A little mouse was hiding in a hole. When it heard the sound of
cheese, it couldn\'t help but protrude its head to see. \'Protrude\'
means to stick out or extend beyond a surface.

**一只小老鼠躲在洞里。当它听到奶酪的声音时，忍不住把头伸出来看。'protrude'意思是伸出或突出于表面。**
