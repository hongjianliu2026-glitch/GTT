## pediatric

**英音:** _[,pi:dɪ\'ætrɪk]_ **美音:** _[,pidɪ\'ætrɪk]_

**译义:** *adj.小儿科的*

### 短语

+ **pediatric care:** _儿科护理_

+ **pediatric hospital:** _儿科医院_

+ **pediatrician:** _儿科医生_

+ **pediatric department:** _儿科部门_

+ **pediatric medicine:** _儿科医学_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The pediatric department in this hospital is well-equipped.**

**中文翻译:** 这家医院的儿科设备齐全。

**单词释义:** 儿科的

#### 例句2

> **She specializes in pediatric medicine.**

**中文翻译:** 她专攻儿科医学。

**单词释义:** 儿科的

#### 例句3

> **The pediatric nurse was very kind to the children.**

**中文翻译:** 儿科护士对孩子们非常友善。

**单词释义:** 儿科的

### 背诵小故事

> A little boy was ill. His parents took him to the pediatric
department. There, kind doctors specialized in treating children like
him. They made him feel better. Remember, \'pediatric\' is about kids\'
health care.

**一个小男孩生病了。他的父母带他去了儿科。在那里，善良的专门治疗像他这样孩子的医生让他感觉好多了。记住，\'pediatric\'
是关于儿童医疗保健的。**
