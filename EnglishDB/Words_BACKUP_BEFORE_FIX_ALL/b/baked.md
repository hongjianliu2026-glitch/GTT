## baked

**英音:** _[beɪkt]_ **美音:** _[beɪkt]_

**译义:** *adj. 烤的；烘焙的*

### 短语

+ **baked beans:** _烘豆_

+ **baked potato:** _烤土豆_

+ **baked goods:** _烘焙食品_

+ **baked fish:** _烤鱼_

+ **baked bread:** _烤面包_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The baked potatoes smelled delicious.**

**中文翻译:** 烤好的土豆闻起来很香。

**单词释义:** 烤好的

#### 例句2

> **She had a slice of baked bread for breakfast.**

**中文翻译:** 她早餐吃了一片烤面包。

**单词释义:** 烤的

#### 例句3

> **The baked fish is a specialty of this restaurant.**

**中文翻译:** 烤鱼是这家餐馆的特色菜。

**单词释义:** 烤的

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was very hungry. My mom took out some delicious baked
cakes from the oven. The smell was so inviting. I ate them happily and
felt full. Baked means cooked by dry heat in an oven.

意思是在烤箱中通过干热烘烤而成。**
