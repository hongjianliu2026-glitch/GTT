## basketball

**英音:** _[\'bɑːskɪtbɔːl]_ **美音:** _[\'bæskɪtbɔl]_

**译义:** *n.篮球,篮球运动*

### 短语

+ **play basketball:** _打篮球_

+ **basketball game:** _篮球比赛_

+ **basketball court:** _篮球场_

+ **professional basketball:** _职业篮球_

+ **basketball team:** _篮球队_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **I love playing basketball on weekends.**

**中文翻译:** 我喜欢在周末打篮球。

**单词释义:** 篮球

#### 例句2

> **The basketball game was very exciting.**

**中文翻译:** 这场篮球赛非常激动人心。

**单词释义:** 篮球

#### 例句3

> **He is good at shooting basketballs.**

**中文翻译:** 他擅长投篮。

**单词释义:** 篮球

### 背诵小故事

> One day, I was walking in the park and saw a group of kids playing
happily with a basketball. The ball bounced high and they chased it
joyfully. It made me remember the word \'basketball\' easily, which
means the ball used in the sport of playing with a basket as the goal.

**有一天，我在公园散步，看到一群孩子开心地玩着一个篮球。球弹得很高，他们欢快地追逐着。这让我很容易就记住了'basketball'这个单词，它指的是在以篮筐为目标的运动中使用的球。**
