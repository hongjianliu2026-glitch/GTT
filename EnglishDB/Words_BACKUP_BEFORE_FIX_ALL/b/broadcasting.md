## broadcasting

**英音:** _[\'brɔːd,kɑːstɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'brɔdkæstɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.广播*

### 短语

+ **radio broadcasting:** _无线电广播_

+ **television broadcasting:** _电视广播_

+ **live broadcasting:** _现场直播_

+ **broadcasting station:** _广播电台_

+ **broadcasting network:** _广播网络_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The broadcasting of this news program attracts a large
audience.**

**中文翻译:** 这个新闻节目的播出吸引了大量观众。

**单词释义:** 播出

#### 例句2

> **Broadcasting has become an important means of communication.**

**中文翻译:** 广播已成为一种重要的交流手段。

**单词释义:** 广播

#### 例句3

> **He works in the field of broadcasting.**

**中文翻译:** 他在广播领域工作。

**单词释义:** 广播

### 背诵小故事

> A young man dreamed of becoming a star in broadcasting. He practiced
hard, spoke clearly and vividly. Finally, his voice was heard by
millions, achieving his broadcasting dream.

**一个年轻人梦想成为广播界的明星。他努力练习，说得清晰生动。最终，他的声音被数百万人听到，实现了他的广播梦想。**
