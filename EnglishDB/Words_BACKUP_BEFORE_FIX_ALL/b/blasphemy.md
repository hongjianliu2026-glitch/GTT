## blasphemy

**英音:** _[\'blæsfəmɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'blæsfəmi]_

**译义:** *n.亵渎(话)*

### 短语

+ **commit blasphemy:** _犯亵渎罪_

+ **utter blasphemy:** _说出亵渎的话_

+ **blasphemy against religion:** _对宗教的亵渎_

+ **be accused of blasphemy:** _被指控亵渎_

+ **blasphemy law:** _亵渎法_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **His remarks were regarded as blasphemy by the religious
community.**

**中文翻译:** 他的言论被宗教界视为亵渎。

**单词释义:** 亵渎神明

#### 例句2

> **To speak ill of the gods is a form of blasphemy.**

**中文翻译:** 说神的坏话是一种亵渎。

**单词释义:** 亵渎

#### 例句3

> **The book was accused of containing blasphemy and was banned.**

**中文翻译:** 该书被指控含有亵渎神明内容，因而被禁。

**单词释义:** 亵渎言论

### 背诵小故事

> A man was caught making blasphemy in the church. He shouted
disrespectful words against God. Everyone was shocked. Such an act is
considered a serious offense. Remember, blasphemy means speaking
irreverently about sacred things.

**一个男人在教堂里被抓到亵渎神明。他大声喊出对上帝不敬的话。所有人都震惊了。这种行为被认为是严重的过错。记住，blasphemy
意思是对神圣事物不敬的言语。**
