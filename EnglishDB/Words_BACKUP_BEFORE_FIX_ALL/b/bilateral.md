## bilateral

**英音:** _[baɪ\'læt(ə)r(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[,baɪ\'lætərəl]_

**译义:** *adj.有两面的,双边的*

### 短语

+ **bilateral agreement:** _双边协议_

+ **bilateral trade:** _双边贸易_

+ **bilateral relationship:** _双边关系_

+ **bilateral talks:** _双边会谈_

+ **bilateral symmetry:** _双侧对称_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The two countries have signed a bilateral trade agreement.**

**中文翻译:** 两国签署了双边贸易协定。

**单词释义:** 双边的

#### 例句2

> **Bilateral relations between the two nations have improved
significantly in recent years.**

**中文翻译:** 近年来两国双边关系显着改善。

**单词释义:** 双边的

#### 例句3

> **The bilateral meeting was held to discuss economic
cooperation.**

**中文翻译:** 举行双边会议讨论经济合作。

**单词释义:** 双边的

### 背诵小故事

> Two countries were discussing trade. They wanted an agreement that
was fair on both sides. This fair and two-sided deal was called
\'bilateral\'.

**两个国家正在讨论贸易。他们想要一个对双方都公平的协议。这种公平且双边的交易被称为'双边的'。**
