## bloodstream

**英音:** _[ˈblʌdstriːm]_ **美音:** _[ˈblʌdstriːm]_

**译义:** *n. 血流；体内循环的血液*

### 短语

+ **enter the bloodstream:** _进入血液_

+ **circulate in the bloodstream:** _在血液中循环_

+ **drug in the bloodstream:** _血液中的药物_

+ **pollutants in the bloodstream:** _血液中的污染物_

+ **pathogens in the bloodstream:** _血液中的病原体_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The virus entered the bloodstream and spread rapidly.**

**中文翻译:** 病毒进入了血液并迅速传播。

**单词释义:** 血液

#### 例句2

> **Drugs can quickly reach the bloodstream through injection.**

**中文翻译:** 药物通过注射能迅速进入血液。

**单词释义:** 血液

#### 例句3

> **The doctor is monitoring the patient\'s bloodstream for any
abnormalities.**

**中文翻译:** 医生正在监测病人血液中是否有任何异常。

**单词释义:** 血液

### 背诵小故事

> A brave warrior was injured in battle. The enemy\'s arrow pierced his
arm, and the blood flowed into his bloodstream. But his strong will kept
him fighting. Remember, bloodstream is the flow of blood in the body.

**一位勇敢的战士在战斗中受伤了。敌人的箭刺穿了他的手臂，血液流入了他的血流中。但他坚强的意志让他继续战斗。记住，bloodstream
