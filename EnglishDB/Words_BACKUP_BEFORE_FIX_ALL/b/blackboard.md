## blackboard

**英音:** _[\'blækbɔːd]_ **美音:** _[\'blækbɔrd]_

**译义:** *n.黑板*

### 短语

+ **on the blackboard:** _在黑板上_

+ **write on the blackboard:** _在黑板上写_

+ **clean the blackboard:** _擦黑板_

+ **look at the blackboard:** _看黑板_

+ **point to the blackboard:** _指向黑板_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The teacher wrote the lesson on the blackboard.**

**中文翻译:** 老师把课文写在黑板上。

**单词释义:** 黑板

#### 例句2

> **The blackboard was wiped clean after class.**

**中文翻译:** 下课后黑板擦干净了。

**单词释义:** 黑板

#### 例句3

> **Look at the blackboard and answer the question.**

**中文翻译:** 看黑板，回答问题。

**单词释义:** 黑板

### 背诵小故事

> One day, the teacher pointed to the blackboard and said, \'Look at
this blackboard. It\'s like a magic window that shows us knowledge.\'
The black color and its role as a board for writing made it easy to
remember the word \'blackboard\'.

**有一天，老师指着黑板说：'看这块黑板。它就像一个神奇的窗口，为我们展示知识。'黑板的黑色以及它作为书写板的作用，让人很容易记住'blackboard'这个单词。**
