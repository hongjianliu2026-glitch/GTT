### 背诵小故事

> A long time ago, in a small village, there was a special ceremony.
The priest used the word \'eucharistic\' to describe the sacred bread
and wine. Everyone felt a deep connection. Remember this scene and
you\'ll remember \'eucharistic\' means related to the Christian
sacrament of Holy Communion.

**很久以前，在一个小村庄里，有一个特别的仪式。牧师用'eucharistic'这个词来形容神圣的面包和酒。每个人都感受到了深深的联结。记住这个场景，你就会记住'eucharistic'意思是与基督教圣餐有关的。**
