## enzyme

**英音:** _[\'enzaɪm]_ **美音:** _[\'ɛnzaɪm]_

**译义:** *n.[生化]酶*

### 短语

+ **enzyme inhibitor:** _酶抑制剂_

+ **digestive enzyme:** _消化酶_

+ **metabolic enzyme:** _代谢酶_

+ **enzyme activity:** _酶活性_

+ **enzyme catalysis:** _酶催化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The enzyme catalyzes the chemical reaction.**

**中文翻译:** 酶催化化学反应。

**单词释义:** 酶

#### 例句2

> **Digestive enzymes help break down food in the body.**

**中文翻译:** 消化酶有助于分解体内的食物。

**单词释义:** 酶

#### 例句3

> **The lack of a certain enzyme can cause a metabolic disorder.**

**中文翻译:** 缺乏某种酶会导致代谢紊乱。

**单词释义:** 酶

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a tiny factory inside your body. Workers in this factory are
called enzymes. They work very hard to speed up chemical reactions and
keep everything running smoothly. Just like special helpers, enzymes
make important processes happen!

**想象你身体里有一个小工厂。这个工厂里的工人被称为酶。他们非常努力地工作以加快化学反应，让一切顺利进行。就像特殊的帮手一样，酶促使重要的过程发生！**
