## explicitly

**英音:** _[ɪksˈplɪsɪtli]_ **美音:** _[ɪkˈsplɪsətli]_

**译义:** *adv.明白地,明确地*

### 短语

+ **explicitly stated:** _明确陈述_

+ **explicitly prohibited:** _明确禁止_

+ **explicitly mentioned:** _明确提及_

+ **explicitly defined:** _明确界定_

+ **explicitly expressed:** _明确表达_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The contract explicitly states the terms and conditions.**

**中文翻译:** 合同明确规定了条款和条件。

**单词释义:** 明确地

#### 例句2

> **She explicitly refused his invitation.**

**中文翻译:** 她明确拒绝了他的邀请。

**单词释义:** 明确地

#### 例句3

> **The law explicitly prohibits this kind of behavior.**

**中文翻译:** 法律明确禁止此类行为。

**单词释义:** 明确地

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was solving a case. The criminal explicitly confessed his
crime in a letter. The detective was surprised because it was so rare
for someone to be this openly and clearly admitting their wrongdoings.

**一位侦探正在破案。罪犯在一封信中明确地坦白了他的罪行。侦探很惊讶，因为很少有人会如此公开和清晰地承认自己的过错。**
