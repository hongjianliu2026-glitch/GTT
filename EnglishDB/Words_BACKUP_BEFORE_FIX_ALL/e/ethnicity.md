## ethnicity

**英音:** _[eθ\'nɪsɪtɪ]_ **美音:** _[ɛθ\'nɪsəti]_

**译义:** *n.种族划分*

### 短语

+ **ethnicity group:** _族群_

+ **ethnicity diversity:** _种族多样性_

+ **ethnicity identity:** _种族身份_

+ **ethnicity conflict:** _种族冲突_

+ **ethnicity culture:** _种族文化_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The study examines the relationship between ethnicity and
educational attainment.**

**中文翻译:** 该研究探讨了种族与教育程度之间的关系。

**单词释义:** 种族

#### 例句2

> **Her ethnicity has shaped her cultural identity.**

**中文翻译:** 她的种族塑造了她的文化身份。

**单词释义:** 种族

#### 例句3

> **We should respect the diversity of ethnicity in our society.**

**中文翻译:** 我们应该尊重社会中的种族多样性。

**单词释义:** 种族

### 背诵小故事

> A group of friends were discussing their different backgrounds. One
said, \'Our ethnicity makes us unique, like the colors of a rainbow.\'
It helped them understand that ethnicity refers to the characteristics
that make a group of people distinct.

**一群朋友在讨论他们不同的背景。其中一个说："我们的种族特点让我们独一无二，就像彩虹的颜色。"这帮助他们理解了
ethnicity 指的是使一群人与众不同的特征。**
