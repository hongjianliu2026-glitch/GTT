## equivalence

**英音:** _[ɪ\'kwɪv(ə)l(ə)ns]_ **美音:**
_[ɪ\'kwɪvələns]_

**译义:** *n.同等,[化]等价,等值*

### 短语

+ **logical equivalence:** _逻辑等价_

+ **equivalence relation:** _等价关系_

+ **semantic equivalence:** _语义等价_

+ **equivalence class:** _等价类_

+ **chemical equivalence:** _化学等价_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **There is an equivalence between the two theories.**

**中文翻译:** 这两种理论之间存在等价性。

**单词释义:** 对等；相等；等同

#### 例句2

> **The equivalence of these two currencies is constantly
changing.**

**中文翻译:** 这两种货币的等值不断变化。

**单词释义:** 等价；等值

#### 例句3

> **We need to establish the equivalence of these two methods for
solving the problem.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要建立这两种解决问题的方法的等价性。

**单词释义:** 等效；等同性

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working hard to prove the equivalence between two
chemical reactions. He spent days and nights in the lab, and finally
found the key evidence. The equivalence meant the two reactions had the
same result.

**一位科学家努力证明两个化学反应的等价性。他在实验室里日夜钻研，最终找到了关键证据。这种等价性意味着这两个反应有相同的结果。**
