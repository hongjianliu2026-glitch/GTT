### 背诵小故事

> A thief was seen escaping from the jewelry store. He jumped over
fences and ran through alleys, but the police were not far behind.
Finally, he was caught. Remember, \'escaping\' means running away to
avoid being caught or punished.

**一个小偷被看到从珠宝店逃跑。他跳过栅栏，穿过小巷，但警察紧随其后。最后，他被抓住了。记住，'escaping'意思是逃跑以避免被抓住或惩罚。**
