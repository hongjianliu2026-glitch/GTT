## efficiency

**英音:** _[ɪ\'fɪʃ(ə)nsɪ]_ **美音:** _[ɪˈfɪʃənsɪ]_

**译义:** *n.效率,功效*

### 短语

+ **energy efficiency:** _能源效率_

+ **high efficiency:** _高效率_

+ **efficiency improvement:** _效率提高_

+ **operating efficiency:** _运营效率_

+ **thermal efficiency:** _热效率_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The new machine greatly improves the efficiency of the production
process.**

**中文翻译:** 新机器大大提高了生产过程的效率。

**单词释义:** 效率

#### 例句2

> **We need to increase the efficiency of our sales team.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要提高销售团队的效率。

**单词释义:** 效率

#### 例句3

> **Energy efficiency is becoming more and more important in today\'s
world.**

**中文翻译:** 当今世界，能源效率变得越来越重要。

**单词释义:** 效率

### 背诵小故事

> A young engineer was struggling to improve the efficiency of a
factory. One day, he found a smart solution and the production line ran
smoothly. The increase in output showed the power of \'efficiency\' -
making the best use of resources to achieve great results.

**一位年轻的工程师努力提高一家工厂的效率。有一天，他找到了一个巧妙的解决方案，生产线顺利运转。产量的增加展示了"效率"的力量 -
充分利用资源以取得出色成果。**
