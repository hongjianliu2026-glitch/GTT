## engineering

**英音:** _[endʒɪ\'nɪərɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'ɛndʒə\'nɪrɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.工程(学)*

### 短语

+ **mechanical engineering:** _机械工程_

+ **chemical engineering:** _化学工程_

+ **electrical engineering:** _电气工程_

+ **engineering design:** _工程设计_

+ **engineering project:** _工程项目_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She is studying engineering at university.**

**中文翻译:** 她正在大学里学习工程学。

**单词释义:** 工程学

#### 例句2

> **The engineering of this bridge is very complex.**

**中文翻译:** 这座桥的工程设计非常复杂。

**单词释义:** 工程设计

#### 例句3

> **He has a degree in mechanical engineering.**

**中文翻译:** 他有机械工程学位。

**单词释义:** 机械工程

### 背诵小故事

> A young man was passionate about building things. He spent all his
time designing and creating. One day, people called him an \'engineering
genius\' for his amazing skills. Engineering means the work of designing
and building things like machines, structures, and systems.

**一个年轻人热衷于建造东西。他把所有时间都花在设计和创造上。有一天，因为他出色的技能，人们称他为"工程天才"。Engineering
意思是设计和建造诸如机器、结构和系统之类东西的工作。**
