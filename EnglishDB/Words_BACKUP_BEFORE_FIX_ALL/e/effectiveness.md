## effectiveness

**英音:** _[ɪ\'fektɪvnɪs]_ **美音:** _[ə\'fɛktɪvnɪs]_

**译义:** *n.效力*

### 短语

+ **cost effectiveness:** _成本效益_

+ **operational effectiveness:** _运营效能_

+ **relative effectiveness:** _相对有效性_

+ **treatment effectiveness:** _治疗效果_

+ **organizational effectiveness:** _组织有效性_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The effectiveness of the new medicine is still under
investigation.**

**中文翻译:** 新药的有效性仍在研究中。

**单词释义:** 有效性；效力

#### 例句2

> **We need to improve the effectiveness of our marketing
strategies.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要提高营销策略的有效性。

**单词释义:** 有效性；效力

#### 例句3

> **The effectiveness of the training program was evident in the
employees\' improved performance.**

**中文翻译:** 培训计划的有效性体现在员工绩效的提高上。

**单词释义:** 有效性；效力

### 背诵小故事

> A young scientist was determined to prove the effectiveness of his
new medicine. After many experiments and efforts, it finally worked
wonders and saved many lives. The success showed the powerful
effectiveness of his research.

**一位年轻的科学家决心证明他新药物的有效性。经过多次实验和努力，它最终创造了奇迹，拯救了许多生命。这次成功展示了他研究的强大有效性。**
