## embankment

**英音:** _[ɪm\'bæŋkm(ə)nt; em-]_ **美音:**
_[ɪm\'bæŋkmənt]_

**译义:** *n.堤防,筑堤*

### 短语

+ **river embankment:** _河堤_

+ **embankment construction:** _堤岸建设_

+ **embankment reinforcement:** _堤岸加固_

+ **high embankment:** _高堤_

+ **embankment protection:** _堤岸防护_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The embankment prevented the river from flooding the town.**

**中文翻译:** 堤坝阻止河流淹没城镇。

**单词释义:** 堤岸；堤

#### 例句2

> **They are strengthening the embankment to withstand the flood.**

**中文翻译:** 他们正在加固堤坝以抵御洪水。

**单词释义:** 堤岸；堤

#### 例句3

> **The car skidded off the road and hit the embankment.**

**中文翻译:** 汽车滑出路面，撞到路堤上。

**单词释义:** 堤岸

### 背诵小故事

> A man was lost in a storm. He desperately needed a safe place.
Luckily, he found an embankment and took shelter behind it. The
embankment protected him from the strong wind and rain.

**一个男人在暴风雨中迷路了。他急切地需要一个安全的地方。幸运的是，他找到了一个堤岸，并在它后面躲避。堤岸保护他免受强风和大雨的侵袭。**
