## ecclesiastical

**英音:** _[ɪ,kliːzɪ\'æstɪk(ə)l]_ **美音:**
_[ɪ,klizɪ\'æstɪkl]_

**译义:** *adj.教会的,牧师的,神职的*

### 短语

+ **ecclesiastical authority:** _教会权威_

+ **ecclesiastical law:** _教会法_

+ **ecclesiastical history:** _教会史_

+ **ecclesiastical architecture:** _教会建筑_

+ **ecclesiastical decoration:** _教会装饰_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The ecclesiastical ceremony was attended by many devotees.**

**中文翻译:** 许多信徒参加了这次教会仪式。

**单词释义:** 教会的

#### 例句2

> **He has a deep knowledge of ecclesiastical history.**

**中文翻译:** 他对教会历史有很深的了解。

**单词释义:** 教会的

#### 例句3

> **The ecclesiastical law governs the affairs of the church.**

**中文翻译:** 教会事务由教会法管辖。

**单词释义:** 教会的

### 背诵小故事

> Once upon a time, a young man entered an ecclesiastical building. He
was amazed by the sacred and solemn atmosphere. The priests wore special
robes, showing the unique style of this religious place. Remember,
\'ecclesiastical\' means related to the church.

**从前，有个年轻人走进一座教会建筑。他对那里神圣庄严的氛围感到惊讶。牧师们穿着特殊的长袍，展现出这个宗教场所的独特风格。记住，'ecclesiastical'意思是与教会有关的。**
