## efficacy

**英音:** _[\'efɪkəsɪ]_ **美音:** _[\'ɛfɪkəsi]_

**译义:** *n.功效,效验*

### 短语

+ **therapeutic efficacy:** _治疗效果_

+ **clinical efficacy:** _临床疗效_

+ **efficacy evaluation:** _疗效评价_

+ **efficacy study:** _功效研究_

+ **high efficacy:** _高效能_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The efficacy of this new medicine is still under
investigation.**

**中文翻译:** 这种新药的功效仍在研究中。

**单词释义:** 功效；效力

#### 例句2

> **We need to prove the efficacy of our marketing strategy.**

**中文翻译:** 我们需要证明我们的营销策略的有效性。

**单词释义:** 有效性

#### 例句3

> **The efficacy of the vaccine in preventing the disease has been
widely recognized.**

**中文翻译:** 疫苗预防疾病的功效已得到广泛认可。

**单词释义:** 效力

### 背诵小故事

> A scientist was working hard to test a new medicine. After many
experiments, he finally proved its efficacy, which means the ability to
produce a desired or intended result. The medicine worked perfectly and
helped many patients.

**一位科学家努力测试一种新药。经过多次实验，他终于证明了其功效，也就是产生预期或想要的结果的能力。这种药效果极佳，帮助了许多病人。**
