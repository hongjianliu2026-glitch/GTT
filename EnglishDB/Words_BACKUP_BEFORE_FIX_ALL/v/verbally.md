## verbally

**英音:** _[ˈvɜːbəli]_ **美音:** _[ˈvɜːrbəli]_

**译义:** *adv.用言辞地,口头地*

### 短语

+ **verbally abusive:** _言语上的辱骂_

+ **verbally communicate:** _口头交流_

+ **verbally express:** _口头表达_

+ **verbally confirm:** _口头确认_

+ **verbally describe:** _口头描述_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **They communicated verbally to solve the problem.**

**中文翻译:** 他们通过口头交流来解决问题。

**单词释义:** 以口头的方式

#### 例句2

> **The agreement was made verbally, not in writing.**

**中文翻译:** 该协议是口头达成的，不是书面的。

**单词释义:** 口头地

#### 例句3

> **She criticized him verbally in front of everyone.**

**中文翻译:** 她在大家面前口头批评了他。

**单词释义:** 用言语地

### 背诵小故事

> One day, Tom was accused of cheating verbally by his classmates. But
he defended himself verbally and explained the truth. Finally, they
understood and apologized.
