## violin

**英音:** _[ˌvaɪəˈlɪn]_ **美音:** _[,vaɪə\'lɪn]_

**译义:** *n.小提琴,小提琴手*

### 短语

+ **play the violin:** _拉小提琴_

+ **violin concerto:** _小提琴协奏曲_

+ **violin string:** _小提琴弦_

+ **violin case:** _小提琴盒_

+ **violin bow:** _小提琴弓_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **She plays the violin beautifully.**

**中文翻译:** 她小提琴拉得很优美。

**单词释义:** 小提琴

#### 例句2

> **The sound of the violin filled the concert hall.**

**中文翻译:** 小提琴的声音充满了音乐厅。

**单词释义:** 小提琴

#### 例句3

> **He bought a new violin for his daughter.**

**中文翻译:** 他给他女儿买了一把新小提琴。

**单词释义:** 小提琴

### 背诵小故事

> A little mouse dreamed of playing a beautiful violin. One day, it
found a violin in a big house. It learned hard and finally played
wonderful music. The mouse and the violin became good friends.
