## voting

**英音:** _[\'vəʊtɪŋ]_ **美音:** _[\'votɪŋ]_

**译义:** *n.投票,选举adj.投票的,选举的*

### 短语

+ **voting rights:** _投票权_

+ **voting booth:** _投票站_

+ **voting system:** _投票制度_

+ **voting age:** _投票年龄_

+ **voting record:** _投票记录_

### 背诵小故事

> In a small town, the voting day arrived. Everyone was excited to
choose their favorite candidate. People queued up at the polling
stations, carefully marking their choices. The voting was a way to
express their opinions and decide the future of the town.

**在一个小镇上，投票日到了。每个人都很兴奋地选择他们最喜欢的候选人。人们在投票站排队，仔细地做出选择。投票是他们表达意见和决定小镇未来的一种方式。**
