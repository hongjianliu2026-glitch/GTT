## veranda

**英音:** _[və\'rændə]_ **美音:** _[və\'rændə]_

**译义:** *n.阳台,走廊*

### 短语

+ **veranda railing:** _阳台栏杆_

+ **veranda furniture:** _阳台家具_

+ **veranda view:** _阳台景观_

+ **veranda decoration:** _阳台装饰_

+ **veranda access:** _阳台通道_

breeze and beautiful view. Suddenly, a colorful bird landed nearby. The
veranda became a peaceful and pleasant place for me to relax and unwind.
