## veterinarian

**英音:** _[,vet(ə)rɪ\'neərɪən]_ **美音:**
_[\'vɛtərə\'nɛrɪən]_

**译义:** *n.兽医*

### 短语

+ **veterinarian science:** _兽医科学_

+ **veterinarian hospital:** _兽医医院_

+ **licensed veterinarian:** _有执照的兽医_

+ **veterinarian assistant:** _兽医助理_

+ **experienced veterinarian:** _经验丰富的兽医_

### 例句

**单词释义:** 兽医

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little puppy was very sick. Its owner was very worried and
took it to a veterinarian. The veterinarian carefully examined the puppy
and prescribed the right medicine. Soon, the puppy became healthy and
happy again.

**有一天，一只小狗病得很重。它的主人非常担心，带它去看兽医。兽医仔细地给小狗做了检查，并开了正确的药。很快，小狗又变得健康快乐了。**
