## jurisprudence

**英音:** _[,dʒʊərɪs\'pruːd(ə)ns]_ **美音:**
_[,dʒʊrɪs\'prudns]_

**译义:** *n.法学*

### 短语

+ **analytical jurisprudence:** _分析法学_

+ **historical jurisprudence:** _历史法学_

+ **sociological jurisprudence:** _社会学法学_

+ **philosophical jurisprudence:** _哲学法学_

+ **comparative jurisprudence:** _比较法学_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **He is an expert in jurisprudence and has published several
influential papers.**

**中文翻译:** 他是法学方面的专家，并且发表了几篇有影响力的论文。

**单词释义:** 法学；法理学

#### 例句2

> **The study of jurisprudence helps us understand the legal system
better.**

**中文翻译:** 对法理学的研究有助于我们更好地理解法律制度。

**单词释义:** 法学；法理学

#### 例句3

> **She pursued a degree in jurisprudence to become a legal
scholar.**

**中文翻译:** 她攻读法学学位以成为一名法律学者。

**单词释义:** 法学；法理学

### 背诵小故事

> A young lawyer was passionate about jurisprudence. He spent days
studying cases to understand the principles and rules. One day, he
successfully applied his knowledge and won a difficult case, making him
believe in the power of jurisprudence.

**一位年轻的律师对法学充满热情。他花了很多天研究案例以理解其原则和规则。有一天，他成功运用所学知识赢得了一个棘手的案子，让他坚信了法学的力量。**
