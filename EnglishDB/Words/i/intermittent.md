## intermittent

**英音:** _[ɪntə\'mɪt(ə)nt]_ **美音:** _[ˌɪntərˈmɪtənt]_

**译义:** *adj.间歇的,断断续续的*

### 短语

+ **intermittent fever:** _间歇热_

+ **intermittent rain:** _间歇性降雨_

+ **intermittent work:** _间歇性工作_

+ **intermittent current:** _间歇电流_

+ **intermittent noise:** _间歇性噪声_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The intermittent rain made the picnic less enjoyable.**

**中文翻译:** 断断续续的雨让野餐没那么愉快。

**单词释义:** 断断续续的

#### 例句2

> **He has intermittent pain in his right leg.**

**中文翻译:** 他的右腿时有疼痛。

**单词释义:** 时有；间歇的

#### 例句3

> **The power supply was intermittent, causing frequent computer
crashes.**

**中文翻译:** 电源供应断断续续，导致电脑频繁死机。

**单词释义:** 断断续续的

### 背诵小故事

> Imagine a flashlight that sometimes shines bright and sometimes goes
dark. That\'s like intermittent light. Just like this, intermittent
means happening at irregular intervals, not continuous. For example,
\'The rain was intermittent throughout the day.\'

**想象一个手电筒，有时亮有时暗。这就像间歇性的光。正如这样，intermittent
意思是断断续续地发生，不连续。例如：'一整天雨断断续续地下着。'**
