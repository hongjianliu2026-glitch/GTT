## keyboard

**译义:** *n.[计] 键盘*

+ **computer keyboard:** _电脑键盘_

+ **electronic keyboard:** _电子键盘_

+ **keyboard shortcut:** _键盘快捷键_

+ **keyboard player:** _键盘手_
