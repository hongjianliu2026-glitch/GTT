## thirty-three

**英音:** _[ˈθɜːti θriː]_ **美音:** _[ˈθɜːrti θriː]_

**译义:** *num.三十三*

### 短语

+ **thirty-three years old:** _三十三岁_

+ **thirty-three people:** _三十三人_

+ **thirty-three dollars:** _三十三美元_

+ **thirty-three books:** _三十三本书_

+ **thirty-three cats:** _三十三只猫_
