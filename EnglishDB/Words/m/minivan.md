## minivan

**英音:** _[\'mɪnɪvæn]_ **美音:** _[\'mɪnɪvæn]_

**译义:** *n.小型货车*

+ **family minivan:** _家用小型厢式车_

+ **passenger minivan:** _客运小型厢式车_

+ **commercial minivan:** _商用小型厢式车_

not too big but could fit everyone. So, they chose a minivan. It was
