## autobiography

**英音:** _[ɔːtəbaɪ\'ɒgrəfɪ]_ **美音:**
_[,ɔtəbaɪ\'ɑɡrəfi]_

**译义:** *n.自传*

### 短语

+ **write an autobiography:** _写自传_

+ **read an autobiography:** _读自传_

+ **publish an autobiography:** _出版自传_

+ **autobiography of a celebrity:** _一位名人的自传_

+ **memorable autobiography:** _令人难忘的自传_
