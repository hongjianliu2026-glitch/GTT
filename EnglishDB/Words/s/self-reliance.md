## self-reliance

**英音:** _[ˌself rɪˈlaɪəns]_ **美音:** _[ˌself
rɪˈlaɪəns]_

**译义:** *n.自力更生；依靠自己*

+ **achieve self-reliance:** _实现自力更生_

+ **promote self-reliance:** _促进自力更生_

+ **depend on self-reliance:** _依靠自力更生_

+ **encourage self-reliance:** _鼓励自力更生_

+ **cultivate self-reliance:** _培养自力更生_
