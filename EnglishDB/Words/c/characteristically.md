## characteristically

**英音:** _[ˌkærəktərɪˈstɪkəli]_ **美音:**
_[ˌkærəktərɪˈstɪkəli]_

**译义:** *adv. 典型地；表示特性地*

### 短语

+ **characteristically brave:** _具有典型的勇敢_

+ **characteristically calm:** _典型地沉着_

+ **characteristically stubborn:** _典型地固执_

+ **characteristically generous:** _典型地慷慨_

+ **characteristically optimistic:** _典型地乐观_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Characteristically, she had forgotten her keys.**

**中文翻译:** 她一向如此，又忘了带钥匙。

**单词释义:** 典型地；一贯地

#### 例句2

> **He characteristically answered the question with a smile.**

**中文翻译:** 他一如既往地笑着回答了这个问题。

**单词释义:** 典型地；特有地

#### 例句3

> **Characteristically, she took the news calmly.**

**中文翻译:** 她像往常一样平静地接受了这个消息。

**单词释义:** 一贯地；通常地

### 背诵小故事

> A detective was known for solving cases characteristically. He always
observed details carefully and made deductions precisely. His unique
style made him stand out. \'Characteristically\' means in a typical or
distinctive way.

**一位侦探以其特有的方式破案而闻名。他总是仔细观察细节，精确地进行推理。他独特的风格使他脱颖而出。\'characteristically\'意思是以典型的或独特的方式。**
