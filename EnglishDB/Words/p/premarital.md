## premarital

**英音:** _[priː\'mærɪt(ə)l]_ **美音:** _[,pri\'mærɪtl]_

**译义:** *adj.结婚前的*

### 短语

+ **premarital agreement:** _婚前协议_

+ **premarital sex:** _婚前性行为_

+ **premarital counseling:** _婚前咨询_

+ **premarital examination:** _婚前检查_

+ **premarital relationship:** _婚前关系_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **Premarital counseling can help couples prepare for marriage.**

**中文翻译:** 婚前咨询可以帮助情侣为婚姻做好准备。

**单词释义:** 婚前的

#### 例句2

> **She had a premarital relationship that ended before her
wedding.**

**中文翻译:** 她有一段在婚礼前结束的婚前恋情。

**单词释义:** 婚前的

#### 例句3

> **Premarital agreements are becoming more common these days.**

**中文翻译:** 如今婚前协议变得越来越常见。

**单词释义:** 婚前的

### 背诵小故事

> A young couple was very much in love. But they were cautious and
decided not to have too much physical contact before marriage. Their
restraint was called premarital self-discipline.

**一对年轻情侣十分相爱。但他们很谨慎，决定在结婚前不过多进行身体接触。他们的这种克制被称为婚前的自律。**
