## preoperative

**英音:** _[priˈɒpərətɪv]_ **美音:** _[priˈɑːpərətɪv]_

**译义:** *adj.外科手术前的*

### 短语

+ **preoperative assessment:** _术前评估_

+ **preoperative care:** _术前护理_

+ **preoperative period:** _术前时期_

+ **preoperative preparation:** _术前准备_

+ **preoperative examination:** _术前检查_

### 例句

#### 例句1

> **The patient underwent preoperative tests to assess her fitness
for surgery.**

**中文翻译:** 这位患者接受了术前检查，以评估她是否适合手术。

**单词释义:** 手术前的

#### 例句2

> **The preoperative preparation included fasting for several
hours.**

**中文翻译:** 术前准备包括禁食几个小时。

**单词释义:** 手术前的

#### 例句3

> **The doctor reviewed the preoperative notes before the
operation.**

**中文翻译:** 医生在手术前查看了术前记录。

**单词释义:** 手术前的

### 背诵小故事

> A patient was very nervous before the preoperative check. The doctor
comforted him, saying everything would be fine before the operation.
\'Preoperative\' means happening or done before an operation.

**一位病人在术前检查前非常紧张。医生安慰他，说手术前一切都会好的。\'preoperative\'意思是手术前发生的或做的。**
