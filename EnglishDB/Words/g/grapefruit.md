## grapefruit

**英音:** _[\'greɪpfruːt]_ **美音:** _[\'ɡrepfrut]_

**译义:** *n.柚子,柚子树*

### 短语

+ **grapefruit juice:** _葡萄柚汁_

+ **sweet grapefruit:** _甜的葡萄柚_

+ **fresh grapefruit:** _新鲜的葡萄柚_

+ **eat grapefruit:** _吃葡萄柚_

+ **grapefruit tree:** _葡萄柚树_

### 背诵小故事

> One day, a little monkey was very hungry. It found a big fruit tree.
The fruit looked like a combination of grapes and fruits. Later, it
learned that this special fruit was called grapefruit. It was so
delicious!

**有一天，一只小猴子非常饿。它发现了一棵很大的果树。这个水果看起来像是葡萄和水果的结合体。后来，它得知这个特别的水果叫做葡萄柚。它太美味了！**
