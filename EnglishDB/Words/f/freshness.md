## freshness

**英音:** _[ˈfreʃnəs]_ **美音:** _[ˈfreʃnəs]_

**译义:** *n.气味清新地,精神饱满地,刚*

### 短语

+ **keep freshness:** _保持新鲜_

+ **lose freshness:** _失去新鲜度_

+ **maintain freshness:** _维持新鲜_

+ **ensure freshness:** _确保新鲜_

+ **preserve freshness:** _保存新鲜_

### 例句

### 背诵小故事

> A chef was always praised for the freshness of his dishes. One day, a
customer asked the secret. The chef smiled and said, \'I get fresh
ingredients every morning.\' Freshness means the state of being fresh.

**一位厨师总是因其菜肴的新鲜度而受到称赞。有一天，一位顾客询问秘诀。厨师笑着说：'我每天早上都获取新鲜的食材。'Freshness
意思是新鲜的状态。**
