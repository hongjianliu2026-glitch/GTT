\*\*英音:\*\* \_\[ˈɪnfɪlˌtreɪt\]\_ \*\*美音:\*\* \_\[ɪn\'fɪltret\]\_

\*\*译义:\*\* \*v.渗透\*

\### 短语

\+ \*\*infiltrate into:\*\* \_渗入；潜入\_

\+ \*\*infiltrate through:\*\* \_通过\...\...渗透\_

\+ \*\*infiltrate the organization:\*\* \_渗透进该组织\_

\+ \*\*infiltrate enemy lines:\*\* \_潜入敌阵\_

\+ \*\*infiltrate the market:\*\* \_渗入市场\_

\### 例句

\#### 例句1

\> \*\*The spy managed to infiltrate the enemy\'s headquarters.\*\*

\*\*中文翻译:\*\* 间谍成功潜入敌人总部。

\*\*单词释义:\*\* 潜入；渗透

\#### 例句2

\> \*\*Terrorist groups have tried to infiltrate the country\'s
borders.\*\*

\*\*中文翻译:\*\* 恐怖组织试图渗透到该国边境。

\*\*单词释义:\*\* 渗透；混入

\#### 例句3

\> \*\*The new ideas gradually infiltrated the traditional system.\*\*

\*\*中文翻译:\*\* 新的思想逐渐渗透到传统体系中。

\*\*单词释义:\*\* 渗入；逐渐进入

\### 背诵小故事

\> A spy wanted to infiltrate the enemy\'s base. He dressed up as a
soldier and sneaked in quietly during the night. Finally, he reached the
core area successfully.

\*\*一名间谍想要潜入敌人的基地。他装扮成士兵，在夜间悄悄潜入。最终，他成功到达了核心区域。\*\*
